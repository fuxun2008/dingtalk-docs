## How to Enable Beauty Effects?

DingTalk supports beauty effects in video meetings. You can turn the feature on and adjust the effect during or before a meeting.

## Enable Beauty Effects on Desktop

**Method 1:** During a meeting, click the arrow next to **Camera On/Off** in the lower-left corner, select **Virtual Background and Beauty**, then adjust the beauty effect.

**Method 2:** During a meeting, click **More**, go to **Settings**, open the **Virtual Background and Beauty** tab in the sidebar, switch to the **Beauty** tab below the video preview, then adjust the beauty effect.

## Enable Beauty Effects on Mobile

### Before the Meeting Starts

On the pre-meeting screen, tap the icon in the upper-right corner to open the beauty page. Turn the **Beauty** switch at the lower-left on or off and adjust the effect.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01XkAvnQZ2vqF20coq_!!6000000007442-2-tps-990-906.png)

### During the Meeting

During a meeting, tap **More** in the lower-right corner, select **Beauty** to open the beauty page, then turn the **Beauty** switch on or off and adjust the effect.
