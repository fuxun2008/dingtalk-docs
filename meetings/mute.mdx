---
title: "How to Turn Mute On or Off?"
description: "Mute and unmute your microphone in a DingTalk meeting using the toolbar button, the spacebar to push-to-talk, or host controls to mute/unmute participants."
---

During a meeting, the **host** and **all participants** can mute themselves or others as permitted, reducing interruptions and helping the meeting run smoothly.

---

## How Participants Turn Mute On or Off on Desktop

### Before the Meeting Starts

On the pre-meeting screen, click the **Mute** button in the lower-left menu to turn mute on or off.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01KEIyRfrx36C56TKX_!!6000000000013-2-tps-2514-1883.png)

### During the Meeting

On the meeting screen, click the **Mute** button in the lower-left menu to turn mute on or off.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN015lQP06abbPE8Bobj_!!6000000001441-2-tps-4034-2801.png)

## How Hosts Manage Mute on Desktop

Hosts can manage audio in two ways: mute selected members or mute everyone.

*   **Mute selected members:** During the meeting, click **Members** at the bottom >> view the member management sidebar on the right >> select a member and click the **Mute** icon to mute or unmute them.
    
*   **Mute everyone:** During the meeting, click **Members** at the bottom >> click **Mute All** in the lower-right area >> choose whether to allow participants to **unmute themselves**.
    

> If participants are not allowed to unmute themselves, only the host can unmute them. Members can request to be unmuted by the host.
