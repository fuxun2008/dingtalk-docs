Meeting AI supports the following capabilities:

*   During a meeting, Meeting AI can review meeting content in real time. Participants can view transcribed captions, generate summaries, and ask questions about the meeting content, so they do not miss important remarks.
    
*   After each meeting, DingTalk automatically generates meeting minutes and to-dos, helping teams avoid manual note-taking.
    

---

## Feature Overview

### Real-Time Transcription and Q&A During a Meeting

Meeting AI transcribes meeting content in real time, so participants can quickly review and analyze what was discussed.

### Smart Minutes After a Meeting

After the meeting, DingTalk saves the meeting audio and transcript, then sends them to the meeting initiator as AI meeting notes. In AI meeting notes, you can view meeting minutes and suggested to-dos. Participants can also request access to the AI meeting notes from the host through the meeting end page, meeting list, and other entry points.

## How to Use

### Manually Turn AI Meeting Notes On or Off

After AI meeting notes are turned on, meeting content is transcribed in real time. All participants will see a clear **AI Meeting Notes in Progress** notice at the top.

1.  During the meeting, participants can ask open-ended questions based on the transcript.
    
2.  After the meeting, the transcript and audio are saved, and smart minutes are generated and sent to the **meeting initiator** as AI meeting notes.
    

**Desktop:** During the meeting >> click **Meeting AI** at the bottom >> turn **AI Meeting Notes** on or off.

### Automatically Turn On AI Meeting Notes

**Desktop:** During the meeting >> click **More** in the bottom menu bar >> click **Settings** >> click **Meeting AI** >> select or clear **Automatically turn on AI Meeting Notes**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01i4Dswy4qp3I2lCbo_!!6000000001695-2-tps-1360-1200.png)

**Mobile:** During the meeting >> tap **More** in the lower-right corner >> **Settings** >> **Automatically turn on AI Meeting Notes**.

## FAQ

**Q: Who can view the AI meeting notes generated after the meeting?**

A: Only the meeting initiator receives the AI meeting notes automatically from the system. They are not automatically shared with all participants. The host can quickly share them from the meeting end page, meeting details page, and similar entry points.

**Q: What is the difference between AI Meeting Notes and Cloud Recording?**

|  | **AI Meeting Notes** | **Cloud Recording** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How to turn it on** | Click **Meeting AI** >> **AI Meeting Notes** | Click **Record** >> **Cloud Recording** |
| **During the meeting** | You can review earlier discussion at any time and ask questions freely | Not supported |
| **Saved content** | Audio + transcript + summary | Video + audio + transcript + summary |
| **Distribution** | Sent only to the meeting initiator by default | Sent only to the meeting initiator by default |
