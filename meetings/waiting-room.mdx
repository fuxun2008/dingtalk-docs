---
title: "How to Use the Waiting Room?"
description: "Use the DingTalk meeting waiting room to admit participants one by one, screen unknown joiners, and keep sensitive discussions private until the host is ready."
---

After the waiting room is enabled, all participants must wait there before joining. They can enter only after the host or co-host approves them. Members in the waiting room cannot start calls and cannot see the list of people who have not joined.

## Availability

Only the host or co-host can enable the waiting room.

## Enable the Waiting Room

The waiting room is useful for multi-round interviews, performance conversations, business negotiations, and other scenarios where participants need to be separated to protect privacy.

### Enable the Waiting Room on Desktop

**Method 1:** During a meeting, click **Security** at the bottom, then turn on **Waiting Room**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01WDOnb5sT5bFAqDRA_!!6000000003928-2-tps-5340-2732.png)

**Method 2:** During a meeting, click **Members** at the bottom to open the member management panel. Click **Meeting Control** in the lower-right corner, then turn on **Waiting Room**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01oao73o2tFBJ5Q6iY_!!6000000001967-2-tps-2670-1364.png)

After the waiting room is enabled, members who join first enter the waiting room and wait until the host admits them. The host also receives a notification when a user enters the waiting room.

### Enable the Waiting Room on Mobile

During a meeting, tap **More** in the lower-right corner, tap **Security**, then turn on **Waiting Room** in the security settings.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01PtuDcBH3SBJAQnVS_!!6000000000115-2-tps-5138-5056.png)

After the waiting room is enabled, members who join first enter the waiting room and wait until the host admits them. The host also receives a notification when a user enters the waiting room.

## Manage Waiting Room Members as Host

### Admit or Remove Waiting Room Members on Desktop

**Method 1 - From the member management panel**

Prerequisite: The waiting room is enabled and members are waiting.

During a meeting, click **Members** at the bottom to open the member management panel. In the waiting room group, choose **Admit** or **Remove** for the waiting member.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01gmWttf1b0jJ5QMKM_!!6000000008060-2-tps-2672-1366.png)

**Method 2 - From the notification**

When the host receives a notification that a user has entered the waiting room, click **Admit** in the notification.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01egRPPds0E6D5QMKM_!!6000000000057-2-tps-2672-1366.png)

### Admit or Remove Waiting Room Members on Mobile

**Method 1 - From the member management panel**

Prerequisite: The waiting room is enabled and members are waiting.

During a meeting, tap **Members** at the bottom to open the member management panel. In the waiting room group, choose **Admit** or **Remove** for the waiting member.

**Method 2 - From the notification**

When the host receives a notification that a user has entered the waiting room, tap **Admit** in the notification.

### Move an In-Meeting Member to the Waiting Room on Desktop

During a meeting, click **Members** at the bottom to open the member management panel. In the joined members group, click the icon on the right side of the member, then select **Move to Waiting Room**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01PRL0ZpBxN0C5Q6iU_!!6000000000192-2-tps-2670-1360.png)

### Move an In-Meeting Member to the Waiting Room on Mobile

During a meeting, tap **Members** at the bottom to open the member management panel. In the joined members group, tap the member, then select **Move to Waiting Room**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN016wxn1z5p0fJFjbum_!!6000000001856-2-tps-7750-7798.png)
