## Switch Meeting Views

During a meeting, you can switch between different views to focus on the speaker, gallery, or shared content.

### On Desktop

During the meeting, use the view switch control in the meeting window to select the view you need.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01cXjQtS6fhYJ3yjQi_!!6000000002827-2-tps-1960-1364.png)

### On Mobile

During the meeting, tap the view control or swipe as supported to switch the meeting view.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN013CRkorZzCEEF7Kd6_!!6000000000732-2-tps-7446-5194.png)
