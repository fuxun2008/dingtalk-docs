In DingTalk, you can make free voice calls with your contacts. Voice calls include the following benefits:

*   **Protection against eavesdropping**: Call data is protected for security.
    
*   **No call charges**: Use the feature without paying phone charges.
    
*   **HD audio quality**: Audio quality exceeds traditional phone calls, with noise suppression, echo cancellation, and other audio algorithms.
    
*   **Caller business card**: Shows the caller card for colleagues and business partners.
    

## How to Use Voice Calls

### Make or End a Voice Call on Mobile

**Make a call:**

Open a one-on-one chat on mobile >> tap the **+** button on the right side of the input box >> select **Voice Call** >> tap **Voice Call** to call the other person.

You can also open a one-on-one chat on mobile >> tap the icon in the upper-right corner >> select **Voice Call** >> tap **Voice Call** to call the other person.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/AJdl65AXv2da7Oke/img/e797538c-1c15-4a5a-8aee-4c0fce657ebc.png)

**End a call:** When the other person has not answered or after the voice call is connected, tap **Cancel** or **Hang up** at the bottom to cancel or end the call.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/QvjnA3jZEoMrbOXo/img/13d37daa-d073-4df7-88ef-8849ecf2b070.png)

> When you receive a DingTalk voice call, the mobile app displays an incoming call pop-up even if you are not currently on the DingTalk screen, so you will not miss important calls.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/AJdl65AXv2da7Oke/img/a4cd237d-52b3-4459-ab98-e9bc689f25a4.png)

### Make or End a Voice Call on Desktop

**Make a call:** Open a one-on-one chat in DingTalk desktop >> click the icon in the upper-right corner >> click **Voice Call** to call the other person.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/QvjnA3jZEoMrbOXo/img/358ff348-48a2-4ca9-bb9f-b324773e9a42.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_1233,y_0,w_1869,h_2466/ignore-error,1)

**End a call:** When the other person has not answered or after the voice call is connected, click the hang-up icon on the call screen to end the call.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/AJdl65AXv2da7Oke/img/c4b9b047-ec2c-4ad7-918c-1d4b4e5196cf.png)
