Video meetings support network detection, which helps quickly determine whether audio stutter or blurry video is caused by network issues.

## View the Current Network Status During a Meeting

**Entry 1:** Move the mouse over the signal icon in the upper-left corner. A pop-up automatically displays the current network status, uplink and downlink bandwidth, packet loss rate, and latency. Click **Details** to view more detection data.

**Entry 2:** You can also go to **More** > **Settings** > **Quality Monitoring** at the bottom. On mobile, tap the triangle icon at the top of the video meeting and view **Network Detection**. Together with weak-network prompts, this helps you understand the current network status and troubleshoot lag or latency.

## Detect Network Status Before a Meeting

Use the video meeting network detection tool. Select the network node closest to you, click **Start**, and wait for the detection to complete to view the network report.

## Recommended Network Conditions

| Client | Maximum Video Resolution | Maximum Frame Rate | Recommended Bandwidth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile video | 720p | 15 fps | 1.5 Mbps |
| Mobile sharing | 1080p | 5 fps | 1.5 Mbps |
| Desktop video | 1080p | 15 fps | 2 Mbps |
| Desktop sharing | 1080p | 5 fps | 1.5 Mbps |
| Hardware meeting terminal | 1080p | 15 fps | 2 Mbps |
| Hardware meeting terminal sharing | 1080p | 5 fps | 1.5 Mbps |

To ensure meeting quality, internet packet loss should be below 5%, and latency should be below 100 ms.
