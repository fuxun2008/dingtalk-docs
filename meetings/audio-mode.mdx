---
title: "How to Turn On Audio Mode?"
description: "Switch a video meeting to audio-only mode to save bandwidth on weak networks, dial in by phone, or join with audio while the camera stays off."
---

When network quality is poor, turning on Audio Mode helps prioritize audio and shared-screen information during the meeting.

---

## Turn On Audio Mode on Mobile

During the meeting, tap **More** in the lower-right corner >> **Settings** >> turn on **Audio Mode**.
