## 4.6 Is Higher Video Clarity Supported?

## Video Meeting Image Is Not Clear

A smooth video meeting requires at least 2 Mbps of bandwidth. Meetings with more participants require more bandwidth. DingTalk video meeting clarity is automatically adjusted based on network bandwidth. If the network is poor, clarity is automatically reduced to prioritize meeting smoothness.

## How to Enable Ultra HD 1080p Clarity

DingTalk provides plans for teams of different sizes and needs. If you need Ultra HD 1080p clarity, use the relevant DingTalk upgrade or purchase page.

DingTalk Meetings paid plans provide unlimited-time meetings and 10+ benefits. Users can purchase or request consultation from the relevant DingTalk Meetings purchase pages.
