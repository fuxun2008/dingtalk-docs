## End a Video Meeting

During a meeting, the host can end the meeting for everyone, while participants can leave the meeting themselves.

### On Desktop

During the meeting, click **End** or **Leave** in the meeting controls, then confirm the action as prompted.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01XC85MH7vfaC7xiWW_!!6000000001910-2-tps-3922-2934.png)

### On Mobile

During the meeting, tap **End** or **Leave** in the meeting controls, then confirm the action as prompted.
