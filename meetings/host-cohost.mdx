---
title: "How to Set a Meeting Host or Co-host"
description: "Assign meeting host or co-host roles to delegate participant management, mute controls, recording, and screen sharing permissions across one or more attendees."
---

Video meetings support **transferring host permissions** and **setting co-hosts**. Hosts and co-hosts can manage participant microphones, cloud recording, and other controls to maintain meeting order. Co-hosts can help manage the meeting, and when multiple participants have cameras on, one or more specified people can be selected.

## Availability

1.  Free meetings do not support setting co-hosts.
    
2.  Paid Meeting membership supports up to 20 co-hosts. Cloud meeting rooms support up to 50 co-hosts.
    

DingTalk Meetings paid plans provide unlimited-time meetings and 10+ benefits. Users can purchase or request consultation from the relevant DingTalk Meetings purchase pages.

## Set a Host or Co-host on Desktop

During a meeting, click **Members** at the bottom, open the member management panel on the right, click the icon next to the member, then select **Set as Host** or **Set as Co-host**.

## Set a Host or Co-host on Mobile

During a meeting, tap **Members** at the bottom, tap the member, then select **Set as Host** or **Set as Co-host**.
