## How to Set or Cancel Spotlight for Everyone?

During a meeting, when the host wants everyone to focus on a specific speaker, the host can set **Spotlight for Everyone**.

## Set or Cancel Spotlight for Everyone on Desktop

### Set Spotlight for Everyone

During a meeting, click **Members** at the bottom to open the member management panel. Click the icon on the right side of the member, then select **Spotlight for Everyone**.

![截屏2026-06-09 14.52.39.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4j6OJ5jRzA6keq3p/img/eace5669-3f35-4970-87e7-0f9c84a6739d.png)

### Cancel Spotlight for Everyone

During a meeting, click **Members** at the bottom to open the member management panel. Click the icon next to the member who has the **Spotlight for Everyone** label, then select **Cancel Spotlight for Everyone**.

![截屏2026-06-09 14.52.39.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4j6OJ5jRzA6keq3p/img/eace5669-3f35-4970-87e7-0f9c84a6739d.png)

## Set or Cancel Spotlight for Everyone on Mobile

### Set Spotlight for Everyone

During a meeting, tap **Members** at the bottom to open member management. Tap the icon on the right side of the member, then select **Spotlight for Everyone**.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/J9LnW6jvo0e9WlvD/img/47c0ec74-60da-4bb5-b6d9-e07e36c8a2b8.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_197,w_2432,h_5197/ignore-error,1)![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4j6OJ5jRzA6keq3p/img/69fef6fb-41c8-4e66-a501-7c1231058425.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_2459,y_236,w_4959,h_5148/ignore-error,1)

### Cancel Spotlight for Everyone

During a meeting, tap **Members** at the bottom to open member management. Tap the member with the **Spotlight for Everyone** label, then select **Cancel Spotlight for Everyone**.
