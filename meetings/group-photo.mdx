During a meeting, the host can start **Group Photo** to capture an important meeting moment.

## Take a Group Photo on Desktop

During a meeting, click **More** at the bottom, then click **Group Photo**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01DskE8Ew1fxF3vi1c_!!6000000001817-2-tps-1936-1004.png)

## Take a Group Photo on Mobile

During a meeting, tap **More** in the lower-right corner, then tap **Group Photo**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01s8baZSKC9WF33KQW_!!6000000003262-2-tps-1504-1012.png)
