title: "How to Record a Video Meeting?"
description: "Record a DingTalk video meeting to the cloud or locally—covers host permissions, participant notifications, automatic transcription, and post-meeting playback access."
---

To effectively record and archive meeting content, DingTalk video meetings provide **meeting recording** for hosts, making it easy to capture and review meetings later.

---

## Record a Meeting on Desktop

**Start recording:** During the meeting, click **Record** in the bottom toolbar >> select **Cloud Recording** or **Local Recording**.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1GXn45KojmKMdqDQ/img/d924345a-e793-4cff-bb54-18a667a02d01.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_0,w_1965,h_1366/ignore-error,1)

**Stop recording:** During the meeting, click **Stop Recording** in the bottom toolbar.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1GXn45KojmKMdqDQ/img/1409c667-2734-47f2-ac39-3a84c07c9343.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_0,w_1956,h_1364/ignore-error,1)

#### View or Change the Local Recording Save Path

**Method 1:** For local recordings, after you click **Stop Recording**, the system automatically shows a notification saying the recording is complete. Click **Open Folder** to view it.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1GXn45KojmKMdqDQ/img/386b1d02-779f-4e50-aef5-5c6c71f732b9.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_0,w_1963,h_1364/ignore-error,1)

**Method 2:** During the meeting, click **More** at the bottom >> **Settings** >> **Recording** >> view **Recording file path** >> click **Open** on the right to change the save path.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1GXn45KojmKMdqDQ/img/257aba6e-c77b-45d9-bb85-94d1318c125c.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_0,w_2960,h_2490/ignore-error,1)

## Record a Meeting on Mobile

**Start recording:** During the meeting, tap **More** >> tap **Cloud Recording**.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1GXn45KojmKMdqDQ/img/2c000f80-ba1f-467e-b66b-7c85c1a86341.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_235,w_5164,h_5173/ignore-error,1)

**Stop recording:** During the meeting, tap **More** >> tap **Stop Recording**.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1GXn45KojmKMdqDQ/img/0c95639f-6cee-48be-a811-4854da0e3b19.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_226,w_4962,h_5054/ignore-error,1)
