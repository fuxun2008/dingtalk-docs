## How to View Current Meeting Benefits

During a meeting, you can view the benefits used by the current meeting, including the participant limit, duration limit, and other benefit-related information.

DingTalk Meetings paid plans provide unlimited-time meetings and 10+ benefits. Users can purchase or request consultation from the relevant DingTalk Meetings purchase pages.

## View Current Meeting Benefits on Desktop

In the desktop video meeting window, click **More** in the bottom menu bar, click **Settings**, then click **Benefit Description** on the left.

## View Current Meeting Benefits on Mobile

During a meeting, tap **More** in the lower-right corner, tap **Settings**, scroll to the bottom of the list, then tap **Benefit Description**.
