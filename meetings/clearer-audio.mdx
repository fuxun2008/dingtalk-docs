description: "Improve meeting audio quality with noise cancellation, echo suppression, microphone selection, and earphone recommendations for the clearest possible call."
---

To improve meeting quality and make voices easier to hear, DingTalk video meetings provide a **noise reduction** feature. This helps reduce external noise and makes remote meetings more efficient.

## Set Enhanced Noise Reduction on Desktop

In the desktop video meeting window, click **More** in the bottom menu bar, click **Settings**, click **Audio** on the left, then select **Noise Reduction Mode**.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/Q35O851Z5k5rgl9V/img/3e532853-8389-483d-965f-ad1a0ab10fba.png)

## Set Audio Noise Reduction on Mobile

During a meeting, tap **More** in the lower-right corner, tap **Settings**, tap **Background Noise Reduction**, then choose the noise reduction level.
