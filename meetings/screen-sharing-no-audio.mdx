## The “Share Computer Audio” Option Was Not Selected

When you share your screen in a video meeting, if **Share Computer Audio** is not selected in the sharing window, DingTalk does not transmit the sound from videos or audio clips on your computer. Remote participants may see the screen but hear no sound.

## Solution

1.  In the video meeting, after clicking **Share**, first confirm that **Share Computer Audio** is selected in the lower-left corner of the pop-up, then choose the screen or window you want to share.
    
![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01Eda5bw06fUKAqigM_!!6000000000902-2-tps-5344-2834.png)
    
2.  If you are already sharing your screen, click the arrow icon next to **New Share** in the top control bar, then select **Share Computer Audio** in the sharing options.
    

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01jMN07mEuz8LGdF2w_!!6000000000509-2-tps-8192-5818.png)
