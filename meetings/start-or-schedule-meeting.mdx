---
title: "How to Start or Schedule a Video Meeting?"
description: "Two ways to launch a DingTalk video meeting: start an instant meeting for ad-hoc discussions, or schedule one in advance with a reminder and shareable join link."
---

DingTalk Meetings provides two ways to start a meeting: **Start a Meeting** and **Schedule a Meeting**. Starting a meeting is suitable for ad hoc meetings that begin immediately. Scheduling a meeting lets you reserve a time in advance and start the meeting at the scheduled time.

---

## Start a Meeting Guide

## Start a Meeting on Desktop

### Start from the Meetings Page

Open and sign in to the DingTalk desktop client >> click **Meetings** on the left side of the client >> enter the Meetings page >> click **Start Meeting**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01nSMfiCCwCtBAjvNa_!!6000000007716-2-tps-5290-3400.png)

### Start from a Chat Window

Open and sign in to the DingTalk desktop client >> click the **+** icon below the chat input box >> select **Video and Calls**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01fh1LsMNflfH6EDXE_!!6000000003963-2-tps-3068-3572.png)

## Start a Meeting on Mobile

### Start from the Meetings Page

Open and sign in to the DingTalk mobile app >> tap the **Calls** icon in the upper-right corner >> tap **Start Meeting**.

## Schedule a Meeting Guide

## Schedule a Meeting on Desktop

### Schedule from the Meetings Page

**Method 1:** Open the DingTalk desktop client >> click **Meetings** on the left side >> click **Schedule Meeting** >> set the meeting time and participants >> click **Done**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01Xy5q824mN2H4yY2h_!!6000000005731-2-tps-2451-3436.png)

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN012HK9WrFhRrL3eagK_!!6000000004994-2-tps-1800-1336.png)

### Schedule from the Calendar Page

Open the DingTalk desktop client >> click **Calendar** on the left side >> click the **+** button in the upper-left corner >> select the meeting time and participants >> click **Add Video Meeting** >> select **DingTalk Video Meeting** >> click **Done**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01nzB5d69cyFDAxGfw_!!6000000001810-2-tps-5396-3420.png)

### Schedule from a Chat Window

Click the **+** icon below the chat input box >> click **Event** >> select the meeting time and participants >> click **Add Video Meeting** >> select **DingTalk Video Meeting** >> click **Done**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01sIuDMXp8alE6EqWx_!!6000000001398-2-tps-3073-3466.png)

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01eifczTiasEF7JgaG_!!6000000004503-2-tps-3604-2676.png)

## Schedule a Meeting on Mobile

### Schedule from the Meetings Page

Open the DingTalk mobile app >> tap the **Calls** icon in the upper-right corner >> select **Schedule Meeting**.

### Schedule from the Calendar Page

Open the DingTalk mobile app >> tap **Calendar** at the bottom >> tap the icon in the lower-right corner to create a new event >> scroll down and tap **Add Video Meeting** >> select **DingTalk Video Meeting**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01758V01HpY4E3FA4g_!!6000000006726-2-tps-1598-1060.png)

### Schedule from a Chat Window

Open a chat window in the DingTalk mobile app >> tap the **+** icon in the lower-right corner >> tap **Event** to create a new event >> scroll down and tap **Add Video Meeting** >> select **DingTalk Video Meeting**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01MLvu4qM5WUG3XXVU_!!6000000000614-2-tps-1744-892.png)

## Configurable Options for Scheduled Meetings

*   Configure the meeting **title**, **time**, and **participants**. After you finish scheduling, the meeting can be viewed in **Calendar**.
    
*   To set up a **recurring meeting**, click **Does not repeat** >> select a repeat rule >> customize the settings.
    
*   Add a **meeting room**. This feature does not support recurring meetings. If your organization has registered offline meeting rooms in DingTalk Smart Meeting Rooms, you can select or reserve an offline meeting room here.
    
*   Add a **video meeting**. You can add a DingTalk video meeting.
    
*   Set a **meeting reminder**, or choose no reminder.
    
*   After the meeting is created, you can obtain the **meeting ID and join information**.
