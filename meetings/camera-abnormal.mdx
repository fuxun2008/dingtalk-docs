## How to Troubleshoot Camera Image Issues

## Camera Shows a White Slashed Camera Icon

**Symptom:** After turning on the camera in a meeting, the camera window does not show the user and only displays a white slashed camera icon.

**Solutions:**

*   If a PC manager app is installed, it may prevent the camera from being called normally. Temporarily close it to rule out interference.
    
*   The laptop camera may be disabled locally. For Lenovo laptops, open the sliding switch near the front camera. For other laptop brands, press Fn together with the function key that has a camera icon.
    
*   If these steps do not work, contact computer after-sales support and install the latest camera driver.
    

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01mRS2FUjmPxD0eHWm_!!6000000000133-2-tps-320-180.png)

## Camera Shows the DingTalk Avatar

**Symptom:** After turning on the camera, the camera window shows only a circular DingTalk avatar.

**Solution:** The user has disabled their camera. Turn on the camera during the meeting to restore normal display.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN012Lcw3SmSYMJ0eHWm_!!6000000004197-2-tps-320-180.png)

## Camera Image Is Completely Black

**Symptom:** After turning on the camera, the camera window is black.

**Solutions:**

*   Check whether anything is blocking the camera.
    
*   Contact computer after-sales support and install the latest camera driver.
    

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01I2WcvCiPQFK0li0k_!!6000000006506-2-tps-379-219.png)

## Camera Fails to Start

**Symptom:** After turning on the camera, the camera window does not show the user and reports a camera startup exception or failure.

**Solutions:**

*   Close other software that may be using the camera on the computer or phone.
    
*   Exit security or protection software, such as 360 Security, 360 Antivirus, Tencent PC Manager, 2345 Security, Microsoft PC Manager, or Lenovo PC Manager.
    
*   Enable camera permissions:
    
    *   **Windows 10:** Desktop menu > gear icon > **Privacy** > **Camera** > turn on **Allow apps to access your camera**.
        
    *   **Mac:** Grant camera permission, then restart the computer. If already authorized, try reauthorizing and restarting.
        
    *   **iOS:** System Settings > **Privacy** > **Camera** > turn on **DingTalk**.
        
    *   **Android:** Follow the settings for your specific system.
        

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN019c99YriZe8G2T4eC_!!6000000002475-2-tps-1216-836.png)

## Camera Image Is Dim

**Symptom:** After turning on the camera, the image is dark.

**Solutions:**

Improve lighting in the meeting environment.

On desktop, open meeting settings, click **Video** on the left, scroll to the bottom, and select **Automatically improve video brightness in low-light environments**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01SU4Q4CZA3hH0eX8Y_!!6000000002815-2-tps-322-180.png)

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01StxdubJZWdBAqiei_!!6000000007903-2-tps-5344-2732.png)

## Camera Turns Off Automatically

**Symptom:** After the user turns on the camera, it turns off automatically.

**Solution:** This may be caused by a damaged or outdated camera driver. Contact computer after-sales support and install the latest camera driver.

## Camera Image Is Upside Down

**Symptom:** The camera image is upside down.

**Solutions:**

*   **Computer:** Adjust the camera angle.
    
*   **Phone or tablet:** Rotate the device vertically to restore normal display.
    

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01UfXR0EEwlsK0eHWm_!!6000000005148-2-tps-320-180.png)

## Camera Image Is Garbled

**Symptom:** The camera image appears distorted or garbled.

**Solutions:**

*   For an external camera, unplug and reconnect the device or try another USB port.
    
*   If the camera is not physically damaged, update to the latest camera driver. To check whether the camera is damaged, test it in another app such as WeChat or QQ. If it is also garbled there, the camera may be damaged.
    

## Camera Image Is Gray

**Symptom:** The camera image is gray.

**Solution:** The local camera is usually disabled on the laptop. For Lenovo laptops, open the sliding switch near the front camera. For other laptop brands, press Fn together with the function key that has a camera icon.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01nGJ7FblqBoI0nitC_!!6000000002279-2-tps-395-235.png)

## Camera Shows a Laptop Icon

**Symptom:** The camera view shows an icon similar to a laptop.

**Solution:** The camera is usually disabled in the laptop system settings. Re-enable it with the camera switch on Lenovo laptops, or press Fn plus the camera-function key on other laptop brands.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01jryRH8j8MoG0nitC_!!6000000004374-2-tps-395-235.png)

## Camera Shows a Lock Icon

**Symptom:** The camera window shows a lock icon instead of the user.

**Solution:** The local system has disabled the camera, preventing all software from using it. Re-enable it with the camera switch on Lenovo laptops, or press Fn plus the camera-function key on other laptop brands.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01tZSyVphHskK0nitC_!!6000000005841-2-tps-395-235.png)

## Camera Image Is Incomplete

**Symptom:** When using a Huawei phone or tablet to join a meeting, the camera image displays abnormally and part of the image is missing.

**Solution:** Disable Parallel View mode on the device, then rejoin the meeting. Path: phone **Settings** > **Vision** > **Parallel View** > **More Settings** > find DingTalk and turn it off.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01jslrcKMdG3F0eHWm_!!6000000004223-2-tps-320-180.png)
