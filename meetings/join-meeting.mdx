---
title: "How to Join a Video Meeting?"
description: "Join a DingTalk video meeting by tapping a chat invite, dialing the meeting ID, opening a calendar reminder, or scanning the QR code shown on the meeting host's screen."
---

DingTalk video meetings support multiple ways to join. You can join through a **meeting card**, **meeting ID**, **join link**, **meeting list**, **calendar**, or the **quick entry bar**.

---

## Join a Meeting on Desktop

### Join with a Meeting ID

Open and sign in to the DingTalk desktop client >> click **Meetings** on the left >> click **Join Meeting** >> enter the **meeting ID** >> click **Enter Meeting**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN010gskr84YHYIAfe20_!!6000000002096-2-tps-5256-3436.png)

### Join with a Join Link

> To join from a browser, allow camera and microphone permissions on your computer.

Open the **join link** >> enter **your name** >> click **Join Meeting**. To open the DingTalk client and join, click **Open DingTalk** at the bottom of the page.

### Join with a Meeting Card

Click the **meeting card** sent by the other person in a chat window >> click **Join Meeting** at the bottom.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01RTzy4uypSlH1UOii_!!6000000001347-2-tps-734-470.png)

### Join from the Meeting List

Open and sign in to the DingTalk desktop client >> click **Meetings** on the left >> find the meeting you want in the meeting list on the right >> click **Join**.

> Scheduled meetings, ongoing meetings, and meetings you joined and then left are all shown in the meeting list.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN010zaq0eJuuTFAy1VS_!!6000000000548-2-tps-5402-3432.png)

### Join from Calendar

Open and sign in to the DingTalk desktop client >> click **Calendar** on the left >> select the meeting you want >> click **Join Meeting**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01G09Re3zlRLGAedau_!!6000000003552-2-tps-5248-3436.png)

### Join from the Plus Panel in the Upper-Right Corner

Open and sign in to the DingTalk desktop client >> click the **+** icon in the upper-right corner >> click **Join Meeting**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01uU2mY5wBoFJB7y4b_!!6000000000603-2-tps-5481-3436.png)

## Join a Meeting on Mobile

### Join with a Meeting ID

Open the DingTalk mobile app >> tap the **Calls** icon in the upper-right corner >> tap **Join Meeting** >> enter the **meeting ID** to join.

### Join with a Join Link

> To join from a browser, allow camera and microphone permissions.

In a mobile chat, tap the **join link** sent by the other person >> tap **Enter Meeting** >> enter **your name** >> tap **Enter Meeting**. To open the DingTalk client and join, tap **Open DingTalk** at the bottom of the page.

### Join with a Meeting Card

Open the mobile app >> in a chat, tap the **meeting card** sent by the other person >> tap **Join Meeting**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01hBcWrgcXgaF3My3A_!!6000000003040-2-tps-1660-1400.png)

### Join from the Meeting List

Open the DingTalk mobile app >> tap the **Calls** icon in the upper-right corner >> find the meeting you want in the meeting list >> tap **Join**.

> Scheduled meetings, ongoing meetings, and meetings you joined and then left are all shown in the meeting list.

Open the DingTalk mobile app >> tap **Calendar** at the bottom >> tap the meeting you want in Calendar >> select **Join Meeting**.

### Join from the Reminder Bar

For meetings that have already started, you can quickly join from the **reminder bar at the top of the mobile chat list**.
