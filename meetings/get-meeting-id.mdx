---
title: "How to Get the Meeting ID?"
description: "Find or copy the 9-digit DingTalk meeting ID from the in-meeting toolbar, the meeting details card, or your scheduled meeting list—needed when inviting participants by number."
---

A **meeting ID** helps you quickly join a specified video meeting and communicate efficiently across organizations.

---

## Get the Meeting ID on Desktop

### Get the Meeting ID for a Scheduled Meeting

After scheduling a meeting, or after clicking a scheduled meeting from Calendar or the Meetings page, open the meeting details pop-up >> click the **Copy** icon next to the automatically generated meeting ID in the upper-right corner.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01B1UWMFZtGCFAfe1w_!!6000000007272-2-tps-5256-3432.png)

### Get the Meeting ID During a Meeting

During a meeting, hover over **Meeting Information** in the upper-left corner >> click the **Copy** button next to the meeting ID, or click **Copy Join Information**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01uAe6KP9IljF7xafH_!!6000000003096-2-tps-3921-2726.png)

## Get the Meeting ID on Mobile

### Get the Meeting ID for a Scheduled Meeting

After scheduling a meeting, or after tapping a scheduled meeting from Calendar or the Meetings page, open the meeting details page >> tap the **Copy** button next to the meeting ID.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01rUw1ax7HKKD1zcPK_!!6000000003864-2-tps-982-1004.png)

### Get the Meeting ID During a Meeting

Start or join a meeting >> on the meeting screen, tap **Meeting Information** at the top >> tap the **Copy** button next to the meeting ID.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01O3AysN7Ix8H2FTpM_!!6000000005493-2-tps-1108-1144.png)
