Screen sharing is a common meeting feature. Participants can share their desktop, course materials, files, and more, making remote meetings easier.

---

## Share Your Screen on Desktop

During a meeting on desktop, click **Share** in the bottom menu bar to open the sharing panel. Two sharing modes are available:

*   **Share Desktop:** Share the content of your current desktop with participants. You can choose a window, desktop, or whiteboard to share.
    
*   **Share Document:** Share a selected DingTalk Doc. This supports collaborative editing and switching between free browsing and follow mode.
    

Available sharing settings:

*   **Share computer audio:** When sharing your desktop, choose whether to share computer audio so participants can hear sound from your computer.
    
*   **Prioritize video smoothness:** Select this option when shared content includes video playback to improve smoothness.
    

> After desktop sharing is turned on, all members can view the shared content.

### Sharing Toolbar

When sharing your screen, a **toolbar** appears at the top of the screen and supports the following feature:

**Move the toolbar:** Drag the icon on the far left to move the toolbar and prevent it from blocking content.

## Share Your Screen on Mobile

During a meeting on mobile, tap **Share** in the bottom menu bar >> select **Share Screen** or **Share Document**.
