Attendance statistics help track participant attendance in scenarios such as enterprise training, major meetings, and online events. You can export the participant list with one click and efficiently calculate attendance duration.

## Track Attendance After a Meeting Ends

Open and sign in to the DingTalk desktop app. Click **Meetings** in the left sidebar to enter the Meetings page, find **Meeting History** on the right, click a meeting, open **Historical Meeting Details**, then click **Export Participants** at the bottom. The attendance statistics Excel file is sent through **Meeting Assistant**.
