description: "Block unrelated people from joining a DingTalk meeting using meeting passwords, lobby controls, allowed-participant lists, and lock-meeting once everyone has joined."
---

To prevent unrelated people from entering a meeting, DingTalk Meetings provides **security settings** for hosts. Hosts can use these settings to manage participants effectively.

## Availability

Only the host or co-host can open the security menu and configure security settings.

## Configure Security Settings on Desktop

**Method 1:** During a meeting, click **Security** at the bottom, then choose **Lock Meeting**, **Waiting Room**, or **Only members in the organization can join**.

> After the waiting room is enabled, all participants must wait in the waiting room before joining. They can enter only after the host or co-host approves them. Participants in the waiting room can rename themselves or chat privately with the host.

**Method 2:** During a meeting, click **Members** at the bottom to open the member management panel. Click **Meeting Control** in the lower-right corner of the panel, then choose **Lock Meeting**, **Waiting Room**, or **Only members in the organization can join**.

## Configure Security Settings on Mobile

During a meeting, tap **More** in the lower-right corner, tap **Security**, then choose **Lock Meeting**, **Waiting Room**, or **Only members in the organization can join** in the security settings.

> After the waiting room is enabled, all participants must wait in the waiting room before joining. They can enter only after the host or co-host approves them. Participants in the waiting room can rename themselves or chat privately with the host.
