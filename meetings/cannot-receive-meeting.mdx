## What If I Cannot Receive a Video Meeting Call?

If you cannot hear the invitation ringtone for a video meeting, first confirm whether someone has invited you to join the video meeting.

## If You Were Invited but Cannot Receive the Call

This is usually caused by one of the following two situations.

### DingTalk Call Notifications Are Turned Off

Turn new message reminders on or off:

*   **Mobile:** DingTalk mobile homepage > profile picture in the upper-left corner > **Settings** > **New Message Notifications** > **Voice and Video Call Invitation Notifications**.
    
*   **Desktop:** Profile picture in the upper-left corner > **System Settings** > **Notifications** > turn message notification sound on or off.
    

### DingTalk Notifications Are Disabled in Mobile System Settings

You also need to enable DingTalk notification permissions in your phone system settings.

*   **iPhone:** **Settings** > **Notifications** > **DingTalk** > turn on **Allow Notifications**.
    
*   **Android:** Follow the notification settings for your specific phone model.
