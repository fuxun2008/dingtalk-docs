---
title: "How to Invite Others to a Video Meeting?"
description: "Five ways to invite people to a DingTalk video meeting: chat group share, contact selector, meeting ID link, dial-out, and joining via the in-meeting participant list."
---

Before or during a DingTalk video meeting, you can invite others by **sharing a meeting card**, **sending meeting information** including the meeting ID and join link, or **adding participants**.

---

## Invite Others on Desktop

### Invite When Starting a Meeting

On the desktop **Start Meeting** screen, click **Add Participants** >> select the people to invite. You can invite members in your organization or DingTalk friends.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/Lk3lbmb2jg26jOm9/img/ce5d6b71-1260-4eda-b2a6-d2941adfba5d.png)

### Invite When Scheduling a Meeting

On the Meetings page, select **Schedule Meeting** >> in the pop-up, select **Add Participants** >> search for participants by entering text, or use batch add to add multiple participants.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/Lk3lbmb2jg26jOm9/img/ee061cd3-24c5-458c-ae7d-8836ce3e05bf.png)

### Invite from Calendar

**Invite by adding participants:** Click **Calendar** on the left to open Calendar >> view the event and click the meeting event you want >> click **Add Members** at the top of the meeting card >> select and add participants.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/Lk3lbmb2jg26jOm9/img/b69178e9-e67b-4c9a-b24a-893c82a87310.png)

### Invite During a Meeting

**Method 1:** During a meeting, click **Invite** at the bottom of the meeting screen >> choose **Contacts** to call participants through DingTalk, **Meeting Room** to call a meeting room by phone, or **Share to Chat** to send a meeting card in a chat >> select the participants to invite.

**Method 2:** During a meeting, click **Invite** at the bottom of the meeting screen >> click **Copy Join Information** or **Copy Join Link** at the bottom >> notify participants through chat, email, or other channels.

## Invite Others on Mobile

### Invite When Starting a Meeting

Open the DingTalk mobile app >> tap **Meetings** in the lower-right corner >> select **Start Meeting** >> choose to start a video meeting >> tap **Add Participants** at the bottom of the pop-up >> select the participants to invite.

### Invite When Scheduling a Meeting

Open the DingTalk mobile app >> tap **Meetings** in the lower-right corner >> select **Schedule Meeting** >> tap the edit icon in the upper-right corner >> tap the **+** icon to add participants >> select the participants to invite.

### Invite from Calendar

**Invite using join information:** Open the DingTalk mobile app >> tap **Calendar** in the upper-left corner >> tap the meeting you want to enter >> open meeting details >> tap **More Information** next to the meeting ID >> tap **Copy** to copy the join information >> notify participants through chat, email, or other channels.

### Invite During a Meeting

During a meeting, tap **Members** at the bottom >> on the member management page, tap **Invite** at the bottom >> select **DingTalk Contacts** or **Meeting Room** >> select the participants to invite.
