During a meeting, participants can choose to turn their camera on or off when camera use is inconvenient or unnecessary.

---

## Requirements

1.  In the free version, up to 30 participants can turn on cameras at the same time. After purchasing a paid version, there is no such limit.

## Turn the Camera On or Off on Desktop

### Before the Meeting Starts

On the pre-meeting screen, click the **Camera** icon at the bottom and choose to turn the camera on or off.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lRYjNpLJpZO8g/img/62ff6976-234d-40e5-a44e-7bb0d8973dc5.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_430,w_2432,h_1892/ignore-error,1)

### During the Meeting

During the meeting, click the **Camera** icon in the lower-left corner and choose to turn the camera on or off.

![.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lRYjNpLJpZO8g/img/5f4f475d-8885-47b9-aca2-d950ae56a50f.png)

## Turn the Camera On or Off on Mobile

### Before the Meeting Starts

On the pre-meeting screen, tap the **Camera** icon in the lower-right corner and choose to turn the camera on or off.

### During the Meeting

During the meeting, tap the **Camera** icon in the lower-left corner and choose to turn the camera on or off.
