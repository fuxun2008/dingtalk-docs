---
title: "Subscriptions"
description: "Manage your organization subscriptions in the admin console to review subscribed product names, service status, and other billing details."
---

In the left navigation bar of the admin console, click **Billing > Subscriptions** to view and manage the subscription details of your organization, including the subscribed product names, service status, and more.
