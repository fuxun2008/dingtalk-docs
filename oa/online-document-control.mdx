---
title: "Online Document Control"
description: "Manage online document security by controlling copy-paste, export, and sharing actions, and apply policies to specific employees or departments."
---

Organizations can manage operations related to online documents, including controlling actions such as copy-paste, export, and sharing.

---

## II. User Guide

In the left navigation pane, go to **Security & permission > Security > Data Protection > Online Document Control**

### 3. Administrator — Configuring Rules

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preview the card configuration">
    Click the info icon to preview the effect of the card configuration.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01Vp7z1kwIasJ0w21d_!!6000000006287-2-tps-461-352.png" alt="Preview the card configuration" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the feature">
    Toggle the switch and click **Confirm** in the dialog to enable the feature.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01Vwow0hIqZdE1awas_!!6000000006033-2-tps-786-596.png" alt="Enable the feature" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the applicable scope">
    The feature applies to all employees by default.

    Click **Edit** to configure the applicable scope — specify which employees the policy applies to or excludes.

    For example, to apply the policy only to the Engineering Department, select **Specific Employees**, then use the contact picker to select the department.

    The contact picker also supports selection by role, giving administrators flexibility to configure as needed.
