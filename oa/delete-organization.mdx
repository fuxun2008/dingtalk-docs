---
title: "How to Delete an Organization"
description: "Organization creators can delete an organization from Contacts in the DingTalk mobile app. A 3-day cooling-off period lets you cancel; after that, all data and member accounts are permanently deleted."
---

Organization creators can schedule an organization for permanent deletion from **Contacts** in the DingTalk mobile app. You have 3 days to cancel the request before the deletion becomes final.

<Warning>
  **This action cannot be undone**: once the deletion takes effect, the organization and all its data are permanently deleted, and every member account, including your own, is deleted as well.
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Creator Only" icon="user-shield">
    Only the organization **creator** can see and use the **Delete organization** option. Regular members cannot delete an organization.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Back Up Your Data" icon="cloud-arrow-down">
    Back up any docs, files, chat history, or other data you need. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If you are not the creator and only want to leave the organization, contact your admin. If you are the creator but want the organization to keep running, [transfer the creator identity](/contacts/transfer-enterprise-creator) instead.
</Tip>

## Steps

### Open the Deletion Option

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Discover">
    Open DingTalk and tap **Discover** at the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    Tap **Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Organization Details">
    Tap your **organization name** to open the **Organization Information** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Delete Organization">
    Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete organization**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Confirm and Submit the Request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review What Will Be Deleted" stepNumber={5}>
    On the **Delete organization and all accounts?** screen, review what will be deleted, then tap **Confirm and continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Login Password" stepNumber={6}>
    Enter your **login password**, then tap **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish" stepNumber={7}>
    When **Your organization is scheduled for deletion** appears, tap **OK**. The deletion takes effect in 3 days unless you cancel it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Gets Deleted

| Category | Deleted Content |
|----------|-----------------|
| **People** | All member accounts in the organization (including your own), the organization structure, and contacts |
| **Organization assets** | Chats, docs, files, and other app data |

## Cancel the Deletion

The deletion does not take effect immediately. A **3-day cooling-off period** begins when you submit the request, and the system deletes the organization automatically when it ends. During this period, you can cancel the request from either entry:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From Contacts">
    Go to **Contacts** > **Organization Information**, then tap the option to cancel the organization deletion.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From Settings">
    Go to **Settings** > **Account and Security**, then tap the option to cancel the organization deletion.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  After the 3-day period ends, the deletion becomes final and cannot be canceled or undone.
</Warning>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I Delete an Organization if I'm Not the Creator?">
    No. The **Delete organization** option is shown only to the organization creator. Ask the creator to delete it, or have the creator [transfer the creator identity](/contacts/transfer-enterprise-creator) first if the creator needs to change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Data Be Recovered After Deletion?">
    No. Once the deletion takes effect, the organization and all its data are permanently deleted. Back up your data before you submit the request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I Change My Mind After Submitting?">
    Cancel the request during the 3-day cooling-off period using either entry described in **Cancel the Deletion** above. After that, it cannot be canceled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I Still Create or Join Other Organizations?">
    Yes. After deleting an organization, you can still create or join other organizations at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
