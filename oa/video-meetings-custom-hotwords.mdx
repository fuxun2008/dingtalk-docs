---
title: "Subtitles and AI Minutes Support Custom Hotwords"
description: "Configure an enterprise hotword library in the Video Meetings admin console to improve transcription accuracy for meeting subtitles, AI Minutes, and cloud recordings."
---

You can configure an enterprise-specific hotword library through **Hotword Settings** in the Video Meetings admin console. The hotword library applies to meeting subtitles, AI Minutes, and cloud recordings across the organization, improving transcription accuracy.

<Info>
Supported platforms: Android, iOS, Mac, and Windows.
</Info>

## Configuration Entry

Configure the hotword library through either of the following entry points:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Video Meetings">
    **Video Meetings > Meeting settings > Hotword Settings**
