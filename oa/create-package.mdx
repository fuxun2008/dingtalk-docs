# I. Product Overview

Provides customer administrators with the ability to independently package and build exclusive Apps across four platforms (Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac).

**Q2: Can the exclusive App be published on the App Store?**

No. To avoid distribution risks associated with publishing on app marketplaces (especially the Apple App Store) and the risk of telecom resource resale, as well as the requirement for the delivered customer to establish an independent operating entity with full licensing and qualifications, exclusive Apps cannot be published on the App Store.
