title: "Packaging Material Management"
description: "Prepare and submit branding assets like app logos and ringtones for review before packaging and delivering an exclusive DingTalk App."
---

Before the exclusive App can be packaged and delivered, the customer needs to prepare a series of assets. These assets must pass a review process before the exclusive App packaging can proceed.

---

## Admin Console Navigation

In the left navigation pane, go to **Organization settings > App Customization > App Packaging > Packaging Material Management**.

## Prerequisites

### Package Name Confirmation

The customer needs to confirm and provide the following information:

| Name | Content to Provide | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Package name | Android: **com.alibaba.android.rimet.xxxxxx**<br />iOS: **dd.work.exclusive4xxxxxx**<br />(xxxxxx must be lowercase letters, typically an abbreviation of the App's Chinese name. Recommended 6+ characters. Example: dd.work.exclusive4test) | Unique identifier for the exclusive App |
| D-U-N-S Number | The **entity name** associated with the Apple Enterprise Developer account | Example: Alibaba Cloud Computing Ltd. |

### Basic Information Upload

### iOS

- All iOS assets are required, except for the splash animation (optional).
- iOS requires a separate upload of the square logo. See the asset specifications above for format requirements.
- For in-house packaging, the customer needs to upload the packaging certificate and password (optional). For ABM/UAD packaging, contact customer service for assistance.
- After ensuring all assets are filled in, click **Save** first, then **Submit for Review**.

### Windows / Mac

As above, after the customer fills in the image assets for Windows and Mac per the requirements, click **Save** first, then **Submit for Review**.

## Asset Review

- Basic information and assets for all five client platforms are submitted for review separately. Before submitting, click **Save** first.
- After submission, both the basic information and the corresponding client information must pass review before proceeding to the next step of App creation.
- If a review fails, hover over the failure message to view the reason.
