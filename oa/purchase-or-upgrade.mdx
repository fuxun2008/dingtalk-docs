---
title: "Purchase or Upgrade"
description: "Compare DingTalk editions in the Admin Console Billing section and buy or upgrade to the plan that best fits your organization."
---

In the left navigation pane of the Admin Console, go to **Billing > Purchase or upgrade** to view the feature comparison across editions and select the plan that best fits your organization for purchase or upgrade.

---

## Plan Comparison

DingTalk offers 4 plans, with capabilities increasing from left to right.

## How to Purchase / Upgrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the plan you need">
    On the **Purchase or upgrade** page, find the plan you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upgrade">
    Click the **Upgrade** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Follow the instructions to complete the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>
