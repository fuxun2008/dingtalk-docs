# 1 Overview

Signature is a utility for drawing signature images. It is not an electronic signature under any electronic signature law and **has no legal effect** — for record-keeping only.

# 2 Basics

In the upper-right of the title bar, click ➕ Add field — under field types, pick **Signature**.

![](/aitable/fields/field-types/signature/images/全屏截图@1x-8_M0w8LOuqre.png)

**Double-click a cell** to open the signature pad. Click **Clear** in the upper-right to redo, or **Confirm** in the lower-right to save.
