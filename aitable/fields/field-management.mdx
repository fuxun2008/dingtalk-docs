Manage fields centrally from each view's toolbar.

Note: in the gallery view this is called **Gallery settings**; in the kanban view it's **Card settings**.

![](/aitable/fields/field-management/images/image_b82r1jG5rZ.png)

## Reorder fields

Drag the handle in front of a field to reorder it in the view.

![](/aitable/fields/field-management/images/drag_field_v3_2YdBv2WyJ6.gif)

## Show/hide fields

Click the icon next to the field name to **show** or **hide** it in the current view.

![](/aitable/fields/field-management/images/fm_show_hide_zBXaRbovy9.gif)

## Add a field

Click + **Add field** at the bottom of the menu — quickly add a field.

![](/aitable/fields/field-management/images/image_WO43_Zw8Lc.png)

## Manage fields

Click the icon next to a field — the menu offers: move to top, edit, [field grouping](/aitable/fields/field-grouping), delete.

![](/aitable/fields/field-management/images/image_JRG3H5Tjhc.png)
