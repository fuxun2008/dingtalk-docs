The **AI Table usage stats** page gives organization admins a complete picture of **AI Table adoption and activity trends** — at the org level, by member engagement, and ranked by individual table usage.

From this page, admins can:

- Quickly assess AI Table penetration and activity inside the organization.
- Identify the core departments and the most important tables.
- Inform rollout, permission configuration, and commercial decisions with data.

## How to get there

Open the DingTalk client > **More** > **Admin Console**, or visit [https://oa.dingtalk.io/#/welcome](https://oa.dingtalk.io/#/welcome "https://oa.dingtalk.io/#/welcome") directly. In the left-hand menu, open **AI Table** > **Usage stats**.

## Top-line metrics

The cards at the top of the page surface the key usage metrics for the organization:

### Active departments (last 7 days)

> Measures **departmental reach** within the organization.

Definition: the number of departments where at least one member was active in AI Table (creating, editing, viewing, sharing, etc.) in the last 7 days.

### Active users (last 7 days)

> Reflects **actual usage scale** within the organization.

Definition: the total number of users in the organization who were active in AI Table (creating, editing, viewing, sharing, etc.) in the last 7 days.

### Total AI Tables

> Reflects the **asset base** the organization has built in AI Table.

Definition: the total number of AI Tables successfully created in the organization that haven't been permanently deleted.

## Usage trends

### Usage trend chart

A line chart of three core user metrics over the selected window (7 / 30 / 90 days):

1. **Active users** — total users who created, edited, viewed, commented on, or shared an AI Table during the window.
2. **Editors** — total users who edited an AI Table during the window.
3. **Viewers** — total users who viewed an AI Table during the window.

Switch the time range to compare short-term vs medium-term trends.

## AI Table leaderboard

### What it shows

The most active AI Tables in the selected window — switchable across 7 / 30 / 90 days.

### **Why it's useful**

1. Quickly locate the core business tables in the organization.
2. Identify high-collaboration, high-reuse AI Table scenarios.
3. Inform permission governance, template curation, and rollout strategy.
