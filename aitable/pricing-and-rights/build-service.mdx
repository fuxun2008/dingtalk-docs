## Purchase and delivery flow

### How AI Table build delivery works

**First contact**: after you submit a purchase inquiry, a dedicated rep reaches out by phone or another channel within 2 working days.

- **Requirements call**: deep-dive call at a mutually agreed time to confirm requirements.
- **Purchase**: fill in the order form and pay to complete the purchase.
- **Schedule confirmation**: once requirements are locked, the DingTalk side confirms the delivery schedule.
- **Implementation and delivery**: with the schedule set, the DingTalk side executes the build.
- **Acceptance and go-live**: after the build is complete, you sign off and take it live.
- **Post-launch support**: post go-live, the DingTalk side provides one free training session and 7 working days of dedicated support.

## FAQ

- Q: After acceptance, can I still make changes?

  A: There's a 7-working-day post-acceptance support window. Within that window, the partner can help with light tweaks (no rebuilds). After the window closes, further changes require a new build service order.
- Q: We're worried about the result before paying — can you provide a demo?

  A: We can share simple past builds related to your scenario, or point you at the build template gallery, but those won't necessarily match your exact case.
- Q: Is the build service refundable?

  A: Service-type products are non-refundable.
- Q: Can I build first and pay later, or pay only part upfront?

  A: No.
- Q: Can I get an invoice for the build service?

  A: Yes.
- Q: When can the build start once I've purchased?

  A: Within 2 working days after purchase, the build expert kicks off a deep-dive call to confirm requirements and schedule the implementation.
