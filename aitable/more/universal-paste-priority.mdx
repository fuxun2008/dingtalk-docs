## What is Universal Paste?

In short: anything on your clipboard, try pasting into AI Table. No setup screen — just create a blank table and paste. Whatever can be recognized as table data goes straight in. No extra steps.

> Note: Universal Paste is desktop-only.

## Where to use it

### Paste an image into AI Table

You're often pulling data out of Excel screenshots, photos of paper forms, screenshots of online data, BI reports, dashboard kiosks, PDFs, PPT slides — and you need to get that data back into a table for a chart or analysis.

Before: a 4×10 table screenshot took 10 minutes plus repeated proofreading.

Now: screenshot → `Ctrl` + `V` into AI Table → done.

### Paste text into AI Table

Sales, HR, and ops folks often get a chunk of structured text (signup info, customer lists, event feedback, product lists, etc.) and have to clean it up into a table for analysis. With hundreds of rows, that's tedious and error-prone — fields get missed, columns drift.

Now: `Ctrl` + `C` the text → `Ctrl` + `V` into AI Table → done.
