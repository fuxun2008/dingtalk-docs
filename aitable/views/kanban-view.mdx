Kanban view groups records by a field — pick a field and records with the same value land in the same column.

Drag a card to update its field value; changes sync to all views of that table in real time.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01ZhGRO31m6XqZCQvvv_!!6000000004905-2-tps-1740-1234.png)


## Customize Cards


1. Click **Customize cards**.
2. Pick an attachment field for the cover.
3. Show or hide the field title.
4. With lots of fields, click the icon to hide some.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01UBupqO1lQrnyB8q2U_!!6000000004814-2-tps-1164-1280.png)


## Stack-by fields

Eligible stack-by fields: Member (excluding "Created by" / "Modified by"), Single select, Multi-select, Checkbox, Flow, Rating, One-way link, Two-way link.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01EmyZWf1mc9AwL7Zp0_!!6000000004974-2-tps-1646-598.png)
