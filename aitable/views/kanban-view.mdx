Kanban view groups records by a field — pick a field and records with the same value land in the same column.

Drag a card to update its field value; changes sync to all views of that table in real time.

## Card config

1. Click **Card config**.
2. Pick an attachment field for the cover.
3. Show or hide the field title.
4. With lots of fields, click the icon to hide some.

## Group-by fields

Eligible group-by fields: Member (excluding "Created by" / "Modified by"), Single select, Multi-select, Checkbox, Flow, Rating, One-way link, Two-way link.
