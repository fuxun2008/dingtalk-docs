Gallery view visualizes attachment fields — images, PDFs, and files at a glance. Use it for product showcase, storefronts, personal albums, travel logs, and photo journals.

## Gallery Config

Click **Gallery config**, pick an attachment field as the cover.

Cover defaults to **Crop**; you can switch to **Fit**.
