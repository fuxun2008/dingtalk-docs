# Overview

When creating an **AI Minutes** data sync, you can configure AI fields. Once enabled, the sync auto-generates **AI minutes summary**, **AI speaker viewpoint extraction**, and **AI meeting QA**.

For details, see the AI Minutes data sync handbook.

# AI field quota rules

**Consumption**: every AI field generation consumes AI field quota. When a data sync first runs, or whenever scheduled refresh updates content, AI fields auto-update and regenerate.

**Quota cap**: standard AI Table caps apply.

**Disable AI field auto-update**: double-click the AI field to open its config panel; uncheck auto-update.
