## Overview

AI Table teamed up with Taobao Business Advisor (Shengyi Canmou) to ship a smart data-analytics solution — one-click auto-sync of merchant ops data + dynamic visualizations.

Bring Business Advisor traffic data into AI Table — products, services, traffic, customer profiles (Phase 1 mainly opens shop-traffic data). Merchants can use preset templates (daily / weekly reports, competitor analysis, etc.) to auto-generate charts and KPI dashboards — sharply cutting the time spent manually downloading & cleaning data, and getting timely alerts via DingTalk groups or messages to drive better decisions.

Multi-shop management, cross-team collaboration, complex reports — all driven by "templated config + auto-update" for cost-down / efficiency-up. Data value flows to business growth.

## Sync Business Advisor data

### Link your AI Table to Business Advisor

1. In the AI Table left sidebar bottom, click **Data sync center**.

2. Under Sales management, find **Business Advisor**.

3. Click **Sync from Business Advisor**.

4. Sign in to Business Advisor.

5. Go to Self-serve analytics → AI Table page → click **Link now**.

For more on **Business Advisor primary / sub-account linking** & **account management**:

### Sync data from Business Advisor to AI Table

1. Sign in to Business Advisor → **Traffic** → **Shop sources** → top-right **Download** → **Sync to AI Table**.

2. Sync settings:

- Check **Auto-update** — future data syncs into AI Table automatically.
- Daily / weekly / monthly data must each be synced separately.
- After sync, view in DingTalk → **My docs**.

## FAQ

#### Why does my dashboard show 0 after linking?

After linking your shop account to DingTalk, you need to go to **Traffic → Shop sources → Download → Sync to AI Table** — only then does dashboard data appear.

#### Why can I only sync Traffic → Shop sources?

Phase 1 mainly opens shop-traffic data. Shop, product, service modules will be opened in later phases.
