## Overview

The DingTalk Log connector syncs Log data from DingTalk to AI Table — for structured management, data analytics, and team collaboration.

---
## Steps

### Step 1: add the data source

1. In AI Table, click **Add data source**.
2. In the source list, find the **DingTalk Log** plugin.
3. Click to enter the config page.

### Step 2: data source settings

Under **Data source selection**, pick the data type to sync.

- **Submitted & received logs**

  Sync DingTalk logs you submitted and received.
- **Logs under templates I manage**

  Sync logs under templates you manage.

Each source needs its own creation.

### Step 3: pick a log template

If you sync "Logs under templates I manage", pick the log template to sync — fuzzy search is supported.

⚠️ Note: if you have manage permission for a template but the template list is empty, confirm the org allows employees to export logs under templates they manage.

How to enable:

1. **Open Log.**
2. **In the left nav bottom, click the «···» button which contains the «Settings» entry; click Settings.**
3. **Toggle on «Export logs under templates I manage».**

### Step 4: configure the time range

Two range modes:

- Recent time (default)
  - Last 30 days
  - Last 6 months (180 days)
  - Last year (365 days)
- Fixed range
  - Custom start / end dates
  - ⚠️ Span can't exceed 1 year

### Step 5: confirm and sync

Click **Next** to finish config — the system auto-starts the sync.

## Limits

| Limit | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Data table permission | When you pick "Logs under templates I manage", you need manage permission for the templates, and the org must allow exporting managed-template logs |
| Time range | In fixed-range mode, span ≤ 1 year |
| Read-only fields | Synced fields are read-only — can't write back to logs after editing in AI Table |

## Typical use cases

1. Bug tracking board: sync Aone bug data to AI Table, build trend / severity / resolution analytics dashboards.

3. Team workload analysis: sync all work items, analyze assignee workload, identify bottlenecks, optimize resource allocation.

## FAQ

- Q: After picking "Logs under templates I manage", the list is empty — what do I do?

  A: Check whether you have template manage permission. Also confirm that the org admin has enabled "Allow exporting managed-template logs".
- Q: Will sync be slow with a large dataset?

  A: The system uses paginated loading. For large datasets, narrow the time range to speed up sync.
- Q: Why can't I uncheck the "Log link" field?

  A: The log link is the unique identifier per log — used for deduplication and incremental sync matching, so it's required.
- Q: Can I edit the synced log data?

  A: Synced fields are all read-only — no write-back to DingTalk. You can add custom fields in AI Table for extended analysis and personal notes.
