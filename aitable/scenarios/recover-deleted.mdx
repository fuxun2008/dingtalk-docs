## ✅ Who can delete

- The AI Table owner
- An AI Table admin

  ![](/aitable/scenarios/recover-deleted/images/image_cYq37CODLT.png)

## 📋 Recovery paths

| Location | Deleted by | Who can recover | Recovery path | Illustration |
| ---- | ---- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| My documents | Owner | Owner | Owner's **My documents** or **Knowledge base** recycle bin | ![](/aitable/scenarios/recover-deleted/images/image_4niTm4efmr.png)  ![](/aitable/scenarios/recover-deleted/images/image_5n-uCEsWFf.png) |
| My documents | Admin | Owner | Owner's **My documents** recycle bin | ![](/aitable/scenarios/recover-deleted/images/image_Byj0g59Vb5.png) |
| Knowledge base | Owner | Owner | Owner's **Knowledge base** recycle bin | ![](/aitable/scenarios/recover-deleted/images/image_1QTr2FgFe5.png) |
| Knowledge base | Admin | Owner or the person who deleted it | The recycle bin of the knowledge base where the deleted AI Table lived. Note: if the person who deleted it is doing the restore, they need access to that knowledge base. | ![](/aitable/scenarios/recover-deleted/images/image_P-F_akArOA.png) |

## FAQ

**Q: I just realized I deleted an AI Table by accident — what should I do?**

A: Follow one of the paths above and act fast. Items in the recycle bin are kept for 30 days only — after that, recovery is no longer possible.

**Q: Where does the AI Table show up after restore?**

A: It returns to the location it was in when it was deleted.

**Q: What if the owner has left the organization — how can I recover the deleted table?**

A: In that case, the only option is the enterprise recycle bin in the admin console of the Office management suite.
