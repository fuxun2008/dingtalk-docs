## ✅ Who can delete

- The AI Table owner
- An AI Table admin

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01iYMnd31FJw6hVWuWu_!!6000000000467-2-tps-726-790.png)


## 📋 Recovery paths

| Location | Deleted by | Who can recover | Recovery path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| My documents | Owner | Owner | Owner's **My documents** or **Knowledge base** recycle bin |
| My documents | Admin | Owner | Owner's **My documents** recycle bin |
| Knowledge base | Owner | Owner | Owner's **Knowledge base** recycle bin |
| Knowledge base | Admin | Owner or the person who deleted it | The recycle bin of the knowledge base where the deleted AI Table lived. Note: if the person who deleted it is doing the restore, they need access to that knowledge base. |

## FAQ

**Q: I just realized I deleted an AI Table by accident — what should I do?**

A: Follow one of the paths above and act fast. Items in the recycle bin are kept for 30 days only — after that, recovery is no longer possible.

**Q: Where does the AI Table show up after restore?**

A: It returns to the location it was in when it was deleted.

**Q: What if the owner has left the organization — how can I recover the deleted table?**

A: In that case, the only option is the enterprise recycle bin in the admin console of the Office management suite.
