## Live course: Fields & formulas

### What you'll learn

1. How to use everyday field types in AI Table (Text, Single Select, Formula, etc.) to quickly stand up a business system that fits your scenario.

2. How to use advanced fields like Lookup and One-Way / Two-Way Link to connect multiple tables — so you can see how data flows across the whole system and keep your processes clear.

3. How to combine AI tools like DeepSeek R1 with field configurations — process large volumes at once, analyze data automatically, and generate visualizations directly. A real productivity boost on complex work.
