## Voice Input

You used to type out forms. Now, just say it — AI Table parses your speech and populates the form for you.

Less typing for long answers. Less hassle filling forms on a phone, outdoors, on the go.

> Note: Voice Input works on mobile and desktop.

## A quick example

Inspector Xinge from Mengniu fills forms by speaking. In an AI Table form, voice input is supported directly: AI parses what you said and fills the form.

You "say a few words", and AI matches the content to the field structure — no typing.

## How to use Voice Input

**Talk continuously — it all gets filled.**

For example: "I visited Beijing Xingchen Co., the visit was for customer co-creation and to learn about product features."

![](/aitable/ai-assistant/voice-input/images/image_xWkaSLmkqe.png)

**Or fill one field at a time.**

Tap a field and say: "I visited 5 customers."

![](/aitable/ai-assistant/voice-input/images/image_Vr6Tu1F9xP.png)

## Switch between voice and tap any time

Done dictating but want to tweak the result? Want to type a single-select instead of speaking it? Forgot what the multi-select options were and want to peek? Voice Input lets you switch freely between voice and tap whenever you want.
