## I'm your assistant — what can I help with?

**Build**: describe what you need in a sentence or two and the AI Table Assistant builds the scenario for you — pro-grade data management and team collaboration with zero learning curve. Talk, and the system follows.

**Q\&A**: What's a view? How do I use automation? Where do I configure field permissions? Just ask.

## Getting the most out of the AI Table Assistant

### A sentence builds more than a table

### Start from a local Excel file — it can run with that too

### Complex automation workflows? Just ask the assistant.

When you need automatic overdue-task reminders, or to sync approval results into a table, ask the AI Assistant to generate the workflow — cross-scenario notifications, data hand-offs, and process automation, made easy.

Prompt: "Set up an automation that pings the project manager three days before the project ends."

## FAQ

#### Q: Where do I find the table I built from the AI Assistant page?

A: Click the expand icon at the top-left to find the new file's location.

![](/aitable/ai-assistant/ai-assistant/images/image_Ka8VKXnbvl.png)

#### Q: My prompt is fuzzy — can the AI still build something?

A: Yes. The AI keeps context and asks back for missing details. When your prompt is vague, it builds a baseline table and refines through a few rounds of conversation, so the final table actually fits your scenario.

#### Q: What's a script plugin?

A: See AI Table Plugins — Script Plugins for the details.
