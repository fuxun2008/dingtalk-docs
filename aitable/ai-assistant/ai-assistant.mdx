## I'm your assistant — what can I help with?

**Build**: describe what you need in a sentence or two and the AI Table Assistant builds the scenario for you — pro-grade data management and team collaboration with zero learning curve. Talk, and the system follows.

**Q\&A**: What's a view? How do I use automation? Where do I configure field permissions? Just ask.

## Getting the most out of the AI Table Assistant

### A sentence builds more than a table

### Start from a local Excel file — it can run with that too

### Complex automation workflows? Just ask the assistant.

When you need automatic overdue-task reminders, or to sync approval results into a table, ask the AI Assistant to generate the workflow — cross-scenario notifications, data hand-offs, and process automation, made easy.

Prompt: "Set up an automation that pings the project manager three days before the project ends."

### Out of ideas for a dashboard? Just tell it what you want.

When you want a clear view of project progress or sales trends, ask the AI Assistant to build a dashboard — it aggregates the key metrics and renders the charts for visual analysis and live monitoring.

Prompt: "Add a countdown card to the dashboard for the most recent open task — make project progress easier to feel at a glance."

## How to find the AI Assistant

#### When creating something new — pick AI build

From the home page, choose **Build with AI**.

The AI Assistant interface opens.

Type your request, press Enter ↩︎, and the build starts.

#### Already have a table — let the AI Assistant keep building

Click the AI Assistant entry above. Click again to close.

Shortcut: Ctrl/⌘ + /[^note1]

### Tangled-up complex fields? Hand them off.

#### Create: AI fields

When you want to summarize a few fields' worth of data, extract attachment info, or similar, ask the AI Assistant to generate an AI field.

Prompt: "Build me an AI-generated field that turns the task description and known pitfalls into preventive actions."

#### Create: link fields

Want to link records from another table into the current one — and click to see that record's full row without switching tables? Just ask, and the AI Assistant creates a link field for you.

Prompt: "In my project master table, link members from the team roster table."

#### Create: lookup fields

When you need to quickly look up and reference data across tables, ask the AI Assistant to generate a lookup field — instant filtering and aggregation.

Prompt: "In the resource table, look up effort hours from the task table by project."

## FAQ

#### Q: Where do I find the table I built from the AI Assistant page?

A: Click the expand icon at the top-left to find the new file's location.

#### Q: My prompt is fuzzy — can the AI still build something?

A: Yes. The AI keeps context and asks back for missing details. When your prompt is vague, it builds a baseline table and refines through a few rounds of conversation, so the final table actually fits your scenario.

#### Q: What's a script plugin?

A: See AI Table Plugins — Script Plugins for the details.
