## Universal Paste

Anything on your clipboard, you can try pasting into AI Table. No special UI, no extra steps — create a blank table and paste. As long as it parses as tabular data, it lands in AI Table directly.

> Note: Universal Paste is desktop-only.

## Where to use Universal Paste

### Paste an image into AI Table

You're often working from screenshots — Excel grabs, hand-filled paper turned into photos, web data captures, BI reports, big-screen dashboards, PDF reports, slides — and you need to feed those numbers into a table for downstream dashboards or analysis.

Before, a 4 × 10 chart took 10 minutes — and needed a second pass to verify.

Now: screenshot → copy → `Ctrl` + `V` into AI Table → done.

### Paste text into AI Table

Sales, HR, and ops teams often deal with a long block of structured-ish text — sign-up rosters, customer lists, event feedback, product lists — and need to organize it into a table for analysis. With a few hundred rows, doing that by hand is mind-numbing and error-prone — fields get missed, columns shift.

Now: `Ctrl` + `C` to copy the text → `Ctrl` + `V` into AI Table → done.
