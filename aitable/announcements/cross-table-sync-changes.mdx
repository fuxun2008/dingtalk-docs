Dear AI Table users,

Thank you for your continued support.

Over the past few years, AI Table has been part of countless moments of efficient digital transformation across organizations. To keep cross-AI-Table sync running smoothly at scale, we've kept investing in the feature.

To make the service sustainable long-term and deliver an even sharper experience, the **time-limited free access to cross-AI-Table sync ends on February 2, 2026** — it becomes a Business / Enterprise capability.

**What's changing:** only the AI-Table-to-AI-Table sync inside the Data Connector center is affected. Sync from other data sources is unchanged.

**Existing tasks:** sync tasks already created keep running — updates and syncs continue as configured.

**Recommendation:** after February 2, 2026, Free plan users can't create new **cross-AI-Table sync** tasks. Upgrade to [**AI Table Business or Enterprise**](https://table.dingtalk.io/pricing?spm=a2q1e.24441682.0.0.4b2c765f8bYzFB "AI Table Business or Enterprise") as needed.

### FAQ

**Q: Will my existing cross-AI-Table sync tasks be affected?**

A: No. Existing sync tasks are not affected — you can still view them, and they continue to sync per their configuration.

**Q: Can I modify the data-source settings on existing cross-AI-Table sync tasks?**

A: Yes. Existing sync tasks remain editable — reconfiguring rules and modifying data-source settings still work.

**Q: Will sync from other data sources be affected too?**

A: No. This change only adjusts cross-AI-Table sync; other rules stay the same.

**Q: Can Free plan users create new cross-AI-Table sync tasks?**

A: No. Upgrade to AI Table Business or Enterprise as needed to use cross-AI-Table sync.

**Q: Is the pricing per user or per organization?**

A: Per organization. Available capabilities depend on the organization's plan; the seat count follows the total seats purchased for AI Table.
