Open the Template Center from the AI Table home page to browse industry templates and best-practice setups, and apply any of them with a single click.

## Custom templates

Save any AI Table you've built as a personal template so you can re-create it later. You can also save it as a shared template for everyone in your organization (this option is rolling out shortly).

### Save a table as a personal template

1. In your AI Table, click the menu icon in the top-right corner.
2. In the dropdown, choose **Save as template**.
3. Click **Save as personal template**.

### Use a personal template

1. On the AI Table home page, click the **+ New AI Table** button in the bottom-left corner.

2. In the create panel, switch to the **My templates** category.
3. Hover over the template you want and click **Use**.

### Publish a table to the Template Center

You can publish your AI Table to the Template Center. Once we review and approve the content, it goes live in the center, where any DingTalk user can spin up a new AI Table from your template in seconds.

Make sure to scrub any sensitive data before publishing.

Steps:

1. In your AI Table, click the menu icon in the top-right corner.
2. In the dropdown, choose **Publish to Template Center**.

3. Fill in the template details.
4. Click **Submit**.

> 💡 Note: publishing requires approval from your manager, followed by approval from the Template Center team. Only after both checks pass does your template go live.
