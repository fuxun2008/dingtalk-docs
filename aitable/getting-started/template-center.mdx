Open the Template Center from the AI Table home page to browse industry templates and best-practice setups, then apply any template to your own AI Table with a single click.

## Custom templates

Save an AI Table as a personal template so you can reuse it later. You can also save it as a shared template for your organization. This feature will be available soon.

### Save a table as a personal template

1. In your AI Table, click the menu icon in the top-right corner.
2. In the dropdown, choose **Save as template**.
3. Click **Save as personal template**.

### Use a personal template

1. On the AI Table home page, click the **+ New AI Table** button in the bottom-left corner.

2. In the create panel, switch to the **My templates** category.
3. Hover over the template you want and click **Use**.

### Publish a table to the Template Center

You can publish your AI Table to the Template Center. Once the content is reviewed and approved, the template becomes available in the Template Center, where DingTalk users can create a new AI Table from it.

Make sure to remove any sensitive data before publishing.

Steps:

1. In your AI Table, click the menu icon in the top-right corner.
2. In the dropdown, choose **Publish to Template Center**.

3. Fill in the template details.
4. Click **Submit**.

> 💡 Note: Publishing requires approval from your manager and then from the Template Center team. Your template becomes available only after both approvals are completed.
