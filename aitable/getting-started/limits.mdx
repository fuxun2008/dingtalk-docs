#### Q: How Many Rows Can a Single Table Hold?

A: Up to 10,000,000 rows per table. By default, the Free plan allows 20,000 rows, the Business plan 30,000 rows, and the Enterprise plan 50,000 rows.

Note: 10,000,000 rows is available as a paid upgrade. [Request more capacity](https://docs.dingtalk.io/notable/share/form/v01eLbnj1bgP0ARrlaN_hERWDMS_BbIMDo9?dontjump=true "Request more capacity").

---
#### Q: How Many Free AI Field Calls Do I Get?

A: Free plan — up to 10,000 AI field calls per person per day. Business and Enterprise plans — up to 50,000 per person per day.

These free limits may be adjusted from time to time based on actual usage, cost, and policy.

#### Q: What Are the Per-Cell Limits for Different Field Types?

A: See the table below.

| Field type                          | Per-cell limit                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Text                                | Up to 10,000 characters         |
| Member (when multiple select is on) | Up to 1,000 members             |
| Attachment                          | Up to 100 attachments           |
| One-Way / Two-Way Link              | Up to 500 linked records        |
| Group                               | Up to 10 groups                 |
