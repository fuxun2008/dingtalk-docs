title: "AI Table limits"
description: "Per-table and per-account limits for rows, AI field calls, attachments, automations, and more — across the Free, Business, and Enterprise plans."

#### Q: How many rows can a single table hold?

A: Up to 10,000,000 rows per table. By default, the Free plan allows 20,000 rows, the Business plan 30,000 rows, and the Enterprise plan 50,000 rows.

Note: 10,000,000 rows is available as a paid upgrade. [Request more capacity](https://docs.dingtalk.io/notable/share/form/v01eLbnj1bgP0ARrlaN_hERWDMS_BbIMDo9?dontjump=true "Request more capacity").

---
#### Q: How many free AI field calls do I get?

A: Free plan — up to 10,000 AI field calls per person per day. Business and Enterprise plans — up to 50,000 per person per day.

These free limits may be adjusted from time to time based on actual usage, cost, and policy.

#### Q: How many people can read a single AI Table at the same time?

A: No limit.

A: 200.

#### Q: How many attachments can I upload to a table?

A: No limit.

#### Q: How many attachments can a single cell hold?

A: 100 per cell.

#### Q: What's the maximum size of a single attachment?

A: 3 GB.

A: `.xlsx` and `.csv`.

#### Q: What's the maximum file size for importing a spreadsheet into AI Table?

A: 20 MB.

A: Up to 20,000 rows per copy.

A: Up to 20,000 rows per paste.

#### Q: How many options can a Single Select or Multiple Select field have?

A: 1,000.

#### Q: How many roles does Advanced Permissions support?

A: Free plan — 3. Business plan — 50. Enterprise plan — 100.

---
#### Q: How many members can I assign to an Advanced Permissions role?

A: 100, including organization units, groups, and departments.

---
#### Q: How many automations can a single AI Table have?

A: 100.

---
#### Q: How many automation node runs are included with each plan?

A: Free plan — 1,000 / month. Business plan — 50,000 / month. Enterprise plan — 500,000 / month.

#### Q: What are the loop limits in an automation?

A: Free plan — 10 iterations per loop. Business plan — 100 iterations per loop. Enterprise plan — 500 iterations per loop.

#### Q: What are the per-cell limits for different field types?

A: See the table below.

| Field type                          | Per-cell limit                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Text                                | Up to 10,000 characters         |
| Member (when multiple select is on) | Up to 1,000 members             |
| Attachment                          | Up to 100 attachments           |
| One-Way / Two-Way Link              | Up to 500 linked records        |
| Group                               | Up to 10 groups                 |
