#### **Why can't I delete, move, or hide the first column?**

The first column in AI Table is the **primary field** — it's the unique identifier for each record. It can't be deleted, moved, or hidden.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01xZQBU11rt9xxsn2EK_!!6000000005688-2-tps-2326-1264.png)


#### **Why can't I edit data in a form view?**

A form view is for designing the layout and configuration of a shareable form. To collect data, share the form's link — respondents fill it out from there.

#### **Why does my view's data suddenly look different?**

AI Table is a real-time collaborative product. Other collaborators may change a view's filters or sort order, and those changes propagate to everyone using the same view.

#### **How do I convert an AI field template back to a regular field?**

Double-click the field name to open its config, hover over the AI field template that's currently in use, and click **Cancel** on the right. Then choose the field type you want and click **Confirm** — your AI field is now a regular field.

**Note:** after conversion, the field adapts to its new type's format and keeps only the AI's output content.

#### **How do I create a Button field?**

Click the **+** icon to the right of the column headers to add a new field. Pick the **Button** type, then set its name, label text, color, action to run, and success message. Click **Save and enable** when you're done.

#### **What's the difference between a button automation and a conditional automation?**

A **button automation** is triggered manually — the user clicks the button and the workflow runs. A **conditional automation** is triggered automatically by the system whenever the configured condition is met, with no user action required.

#### **How many levels of subrecords can I create?**

Up to 4 levels of subrecords under a single parent record.

#### **How do I prefill default values in a form?**

Append parameters to the form's URL — for example, `?prefill_FieldName=DefaultValue`. Supported field types include text, number, single select, and multiple select.

#### **How do I hide a question in a form?**

Append `&hide_FieldName=1` to the form's URL — for example, `?prefill_Reviewer=Kato&hide_ProjectOwner=1`.

#### **How do I freeze a column?**

Right-click the field header and choose **Freeze up to this column** from the menu.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01ZgoIYP1tqzJtBqd7H_!!6000000005954-2-tps-2030-1260.png)


You can also drag the freeze line to adjust the freeze range. Note: the primary field is frozen by default.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01rsuWEP1cSOaKDJUjy_!!6000000003599-2-tps-1772-880.png)


#### **Can I duplicate a Button field?**

Yes — but only the title, label text, and color carry over. The automation configuration does not.
