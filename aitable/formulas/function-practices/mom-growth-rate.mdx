**Scenario**: calculate MoM (month-over-month) growth for orders.

**Data**: [Month] field: 1-12; [Order count] field.

**Steps**

1. Add helper field [MoM helper] with formula `[Month]-1`.

![](/aitable/formulas/function-practices/mom-growth-rate/images/全屏截图@1x_副本_bS7m_ulb9c.png)

2. Use lookup to add [Order count_lagged] referencing [Order count]; lookup condition: [Month] = [MoM helper]; aggregation: original value.

![](/aitable/formulas/function-practices/mom-growth-rate/images/图2_5H2zB0Omhw.png)

3. MoM growth rate formula: `IFERROR(([Order count]-[Order count_lagged])/[Order count_lagged],"")`.

![](/aitable/formulas/function-practices/mom-growth-rate/images/图3_ckpPYcA6_-.png)
