Revision history shows every edit on the table — who changed what, and when.

## View whole-table history

In the AI Table, click the save-state label at the top-right.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01Qepb2M1u66OEYCtiV_!!6000000005987-2-tps-742-774.png)


## Restore from history

Right now, AI Table only supports manual copy-paste to restore.

1. Pick the version you want from the right panel.

2. Select the rows to restore, right-click and pick **Copy** — or use `cmd/ctrl + c`.

3. Back in the editor, paste with `cmd/ctrl + v`.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01Fe9awN1QDwhSQ0eg8_!!6000000001943-2-tps-1794-1640.png)


## View the history of a single record

**Method 1**:

1. Right-click any cell in the row.
2. Pick **View record history**.

**Method 2**:

1. Click **View** in the first cell to open the record detail.

2. In the top-right of the detail, click the icon and pick **Field / column history**.

## FAQ

#### Q: **Who can view whole-table history and restore versions?**

A: With Advanced Permissions off — Editors and above. With Advanced Permissions on — only file admins.

#### Q: **Who can view single-record history?**

A: With Advanced Permissions off — anyone with **Edit** or above. With Advanced Permissions on — users with **Full access** on the table see all fields' history; users with **Edit** see history only for fields they have access to. Other roles can't view it.

#### Q: **Why doesn't my edit show in single-record history?**

A: Either the field type doesn't track history, or the action didn't change cell content.

#### Q: **Can I roll back just one table or view?**

A: Not yet.

#### Q: **Can I see share activity in history?**

A: Not yet.
