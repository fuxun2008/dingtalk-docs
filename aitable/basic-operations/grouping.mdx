In a grid view you can group records by a field — up to **3 levels** of nesting.

## Add a group rule

1. Click **Group** in the view's top toolbar.
2. Click **Add group rule**.

A group rule has two parts: pick the **field** to group by, then the **sort order** within each group.

## Remove a group

Click the icon to the right of a group rule.

## Expand / collapse a group

Click the small triangle next to a group name to **expand / collapse** it.

You can also **Collapse all** or **Expand all** from the group config panel in the toolbar.

# FAQ

#### Q: **If I set grouping in the current view, do other views inherit it?**

A: No — view config applies only to the **current view**, saved independently.

#### Q: **Will grouping reset on refresh or reopen?**

A: Public views: unsaved changes are temporary; saved changes persist. Private views: changes save automatically.

#### Q: **Can I set multiple group rules?**

A: Yes — up to 3.

#### Q: **Can I drag groups to a custom order?**

A: No — manual ordering isn't supported. You can sort by option order / reverse, A → Z / Z → A, 0 → 9 / 9 → 0, earliest → latest / latest → earliest, etc.
