## Overview

AI Table dashboards offer powerful AI analysis — surface insights faster and deeper.

1. **Single-chart AI analysis**:
   - Run AI explanation on a single chart (column, line, etc.) — surface meaning and trends behind the data.
   - Quickly grasp the business condition reflected in the chart.
2. **AI summary**:
   - Use AI to summarize the whole dashboard — generate insight commentary.
   - Combine with automation: when scheduling the dashboard image push, attach the AI summary — recipients see the takeaway at a glance.

## Steps

### Single-chart AI analysis

1. **Trigger**: hover over a chart, click **AI analysis** in the upper-right.

2. **Get the result**:
   - AI generates the analysis instantly.
   - Toggle **Detailed mode** in the upper-right of the result card for more depth.
3. **Act & give feedback**:
   - **Upvote**, **downvote**, or **copy** the result — feedback improves AI quality. Click **Regenerate** for fresh output.

### Push the AI summary (via automation)

1. **Select & preview**

   On the dashboard, click **Scheduled push** in the upper-right. In the popup, find **AI summary** — toggle to include or skip the AI summary in the scheduled push.

   Click **Preview** to see the AI summary for the whole dashboard.

2. **Configure the push**

   Configure schedule and recipients directly in the popup, or click **More config** to open the full automation editor.

## FAQ

#### Q: Which chart types support single-chart AI analysis?

A: Column, line, pie, bar, radar, scatter, combo, funnel, word cloud, metric card.
