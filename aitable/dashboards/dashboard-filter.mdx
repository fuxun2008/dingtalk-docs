---
title: "Filter Dashboard Data"
description: "Apply global filters to an AI Table dashboard to slice data and charts by region, time, group, or other dimensions — all collaborators see the same filtered view, making it ideal for market, project, and CRM analysis."
---

Set filters in a dashboard to view data and charts from different angles. Common scenarios:

• **Market analysis**: in a market analytics dashboard, different teams or regions can filter to see trends for their own region.
• **Project management**: in a project tracking dashboard, the PM can pick a specific time range or phase to inspect task completion and team performance.
• **CRM**: in a customer dashboard, sales reps can filter by customer group or industry to refine retention strategy.

## Basics

### Add Filters

Click **Filter** in the upper-left of the dashboard. In the popup, click **+** **Add condition** to add one or more filters. Click **Save** to apply. All collaborators see the filtered result.

### Delete a Filter

In the filter panel, click the icon to delete a filter. Click **Save** to apply.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01colAOH1rKoGD60Alp_!!6000000005613-0-tps-618-355.jpg)


## FAQ

#### Q: Who Can Filter Dashboard Data?

A:

| Without advanced permissions | The AI Table owner, managers, and editors can filter. Readers can't yet. |
| --- | --- |
| With advanced permissions on | Only the owner and managers can. Others can't. |

#### Q: If I Filter, Does It Affect What Others See?

A: Once saved, all collaborators see the filtered chart.

#### Q: Can Shared Dashboards Be Filtered?

A: Not yet. The shared dashboard shows only the filtered data and charts — the recipient can't change filters.

#### Q: When Duplicating a Dashboard or AI Table, Are Filters Preserved?

A: Yes.

#### Q: Can I Filter on Mobile?

A: Not yet — mobile is view-only on the filtered dashboard.
