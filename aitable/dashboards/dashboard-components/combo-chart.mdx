---

A combo chart combines column, line, and scatter chart types in a single view. Pick one field as X-axis (e.g. "Date"), then plot different Y-axis fields with different chart types. For example, in sales data: column for daily volume, line for growth rate trend.

Combo charts let you analyze data across multiple dimensions — surface trend changes and reveal hidden correlations, deepening analytical depth and efficiency.

## Add a Combo Chart

In the upper-left, click **Add chart**. Pick **Combo chart**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN015CU3aX21QtgQfhlXD_!!6000000006980-0-tps-383-391.jpg)


## Configure the Combo Chart

Configure type/data and custom style:

**Data**: pick source — table and data range. **All data** enables filters.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN0192W3T029GpuQRixyB_!!6000000008041-0-tps-301-315.jpg)


**Chart**: pick a color theme.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01yBgrup1kNpZ0ftnEZ_!!6000000004672-0-tps-300-124.jpg)


**X-axis**: pick the field, sort basis (by X value, Y value, or record order), and order (ascending/descending).

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01TfDRN11yTErRSK4tm_!!6000000006579-0-tps-291-272.jpg)


**Y-axis**: count of records, or aggregated field value. With "count of records": Y auto-counts records per X value. With "aggregated field value": multiple Y fields, each with its own aggregation (count, sum, average, max, etc.), Y position (left or right), and chart type (line, column, or scatter). Remove/move up/down per Y field.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01GkPuPV22GC2iGWzIz_!!6000000007092-0-tps-295-283.jpg)


**Note**: sum, max, min, average only apply to numeric fields.

**Custom style**: background color, legend, axes — all configurable.

## Manage the Combo Chart

Hover over the chart — find the icon in the upper-right. Click for: configure, rename, copy, add to dashboard center, copy as image, export as image, delete.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01nCatGW1Td5HoilZ0h_!!6000000002404-0-tps-573-329.jpg)


Drag the lower-right corner to resize. Drag the top to reposition.

## FAQ

#### **Q: How Do I Set Different Chart Types per Series in a Combo Chart?**

A: In the combo chart config → Type & data → Y-axis. For each Y field, pick column, line, or scatter.
