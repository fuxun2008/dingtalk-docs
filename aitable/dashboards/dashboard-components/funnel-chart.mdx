A funnel chart visualizes the **data and conversion at each stage** of a process. In marketing, sales management, and user behavior analysis, it surfaces stage-to-stage conversion rate clearly — making bottlenecks easy to find.

## Add a funnel chart

In the upper-left, click **Add chart**. Pick **Funnel chart**.

## Configure the funnel chart

Configure type/data and custom style:

**Data**: pick source — table and data range. **All data** enables filters.

**Chart**: pick a color theme.

**Funnel type**: regular funnel or conversion funnel. Regular funnel doesn't compute conversion or overall conversion.

**Conversion rate**: ratio of one stage to the prior stage. Example: in the chart above, the bottom stage is 3 and the top stage is 10 — the end-to-end conversion is 3/10 ≈ 30.00%.

Toggle **roll up current and lower stages**. When on, each stage aggregates its own data plus all lower stages. When off, each stage shows only its own data.

**Funnel stages**: the stages shown in the chart. In an AI Table dashboard, funnel stages are generated from the selected field's values — each stage shows that value and its data.

**Custom style**: background color.

## View funnel chart data

Anyone with read access can browse the funnel data. Hover over a stage — the count/value and conversion rate appear.

Click a stage — open **Data details** to see the records behind it.

## Manage the funnel chart

Hover over the chart — find the icon in the upper-right. Click for: configure, rename, copy, add to dashboard center, copy as image, export as image, delete.

Drag the lower-right corner to resize. Drag the top to reposition.
