---

AI Table lets you share a single dashboard chart with AI-generated analysis to a DingTalk group or DM in one click. Recipients see the chart and AI insights right in chat — no need to open the dashboard.

---
## Step 1 · Start sharing from the dashboard

Open AI Table, navigate to the **dashboard** with the chart you want to share. Hover over the chart — an action toolbar appears in the upper-right.

Click **Share to chat** in the toolbar to start.

---
## Step 2 · Preview the share

The **Share chart** panel opens with a preview:

- **Top**: visualization preview (matches the dashboard)
- **Bottom**: AI-generated analysis summary covering data trends and outliers

Not happy with the AI analysis? Click **Regenerate** at the bottom — AI re-analyzes the data and produces a fresh interpretation.

---
## Step 3 · Configure share permissions (optional)

Click **Share settings** in the lower-left of the panel — open the permissions page.

Available options:

| Option | Description | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share chart | Master switch — controls whether the chart can be viewed via the link | On |
| Copy link | Copy the chart's share link — paste anywhere | — |
| Invite viewers | Search and add specific users, groups, or departments as viewers | — |
| Public access | When on, anyone with the link can view; when off, only invited people can view | Off |
| Allow returning to source dashboard | Whether recipients can jump from the detail page to the full dashboard | Off |

> ⚠️ If **Public access** is off and no viewers are invited, recipients may not be able to view the chart. Confirm permissions before sharing.

---
## Step 4 · Send to chat

After confirming the preview and permissions, click **Send to chat** in the lower-right — pick a group or contact to send.

Recipients receive a rich card in DingTalk chat with:

- Chart visualization thumbnail
- AI analysis summary (title + body excerpt)
- **Continue viewing** button

---
## Step 5 · Recipient views details

Recipients see the card in chat and can open the detail page two ways:

| Action | Location | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Click the magnifier icon | Upper-right of the card | Open the chart + AI analysis detail page |
| Click **Continue viewing** | Bottom of the card | Same as above |

The detail page is split into two columns:

- **Left** — full chart visualization, data updates in real time
- **Right** — AI-generated in-depth analysis report

The detail page has an **Open in original dashboard** entry at the top — click to jump to the full dashboard for filtering, drill-down, and deeper analysis.

> 💡 The **Open in original dashboard** entry depends on whether the sharer enabled **Allow returning to source dashboard** in Step 3.

---
## FAQ

#### **Q: What if the AI analysis is inaccurate?**

A: In Step 2's share panel, click **Regenerate** — AI re-analyzes the chart data and produces fresh interpretation.

#### **Q: Can recipients jump to the full dashboard?**

A: Depends on the **Allow returning to source dashboard** setting. When on, recipients can click **Open in original dashboard** at the top of the detail page; when off, they see only the shared chart and AI analysis.

#### **Q: Can I share multiple charts at once?**

A: Currently, charts are shared one at a time. To share the entire dashboard, use **Share dashboard** in the upper-right of the dashboard.
