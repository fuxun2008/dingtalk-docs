# Weather query

Look up the **current and future weather** of a region by **adcode**. For higher precision or advanced metrics (waterlogging, snow), [submit a ticket](https://console.amap.com/dev/ticket/create/66 "submit a ticket") to inquire about commercial offerings.

The `parameters` field includes both required and optional parameters. All parameters are separated by an ampersand (`&`). The list below enumerates the parameters and their usage.

1. **Prerequisite: get an adcode**

   Visit the [adcode table](https://lbs.amap.com/api/webservice/download "adcode table").
2. **Workflow configuration**

   Enter the adcode and weather type.
