DingTalk YiDA offers five ways to create an app. This article explains how to create a blank app.

## Steps

Follow these steps to create a blank app.

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench.
2. In the upper-right corner, click **Create app**.
3. On the **Create app** page, click **Create blank app**.
4. In the upper-left corner of the page, rename the app.

<Note>
- The default app name is Untitled app.
- The app name is saved automatically when the input box loses focus.
</Note>

Once the app is created, select a page to start building.
