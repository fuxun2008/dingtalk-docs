This article explains what approval comments are.

## Feature Overview

Approval comments apply when an approval process has been initiated and reaches an approver or executor node. If the approver or executor has questions or opinions about the process, they can add approval comments and send notifications within the approval.

- One-click notification to all approvers, CC recipients, and participants in the workflow.
- Quick reply supported. Quick reply phrases include Confirm, Received, Confirmed, and Thanks for your effort.
- Emojis, images, and attachments can be added to comments. Each image or attachment can be up to 20 MB in size.

| **Name** | **Definition** |
|---|---|
| **Workflow approver** | Users who actually performed the **Accept** or **Decline** action on approval tasks before the workflow reached the current node. |
| **Workflow CC recipient** | Users who received a CC before the workflow reached the current node. |
| **Workflow participant** | All users who participated before the workflow reached the current node, including:<br />• The workflow initiator<br />• Workflow approvers<br />• Workflow CC recipients<br />• Users generated by transfers, returns, sign requests, and similar actions up to the current approval node.<br />**Note**: Workflow participants do not include approvers currently in progress at the current node. |

## Case Analysis

- At node ①, Visitor 888 performed the Accept action, while Visitor 119 and Visitor 59 took no action. Therefore, Visitor 119 and Visitor 59 are neither workflow participants nor workflow approvers.
- At node ②, Visitor 105 transferred the approval to Visitor 111. Visitor 105 is a workflow participant but not a workflow approver, while Visitor 111 is both a workflow participant and a workflow approver.
- At node ⑥, since none of the approvers took any action, they are not workflow participants.
- At node ⑦, since the workflow has not yet reached node ⑦, its users are neither workflow participants nor workflow approvers.
