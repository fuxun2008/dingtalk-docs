Processes you submit appear not only in DingTalk Chats and DingTalk To-Do, but also in the Yida task center and the process task center. This article covers the process task center.

## About the process task center

After you create an app and open it, the process task center appears on the left.

The process task center on the left includes the following categories:

- To-Do: Processes awaiting action by the current signed-in user.
- Done: Approval tasks completed by the current signed-in user.
- Created by me: Approval tasks created by the current signed-in user.
- CC to me: Approval processes CC'd to the current signed-in user.

The process task center is grayed out by default and does not appear on the app's access page. You must set it to visible manually.

After you unhide the task center, the To-Do, Done, Created by me, and CC to me task records appear on the app's access page.
