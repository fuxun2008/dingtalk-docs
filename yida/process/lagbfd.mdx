## Initiation Permissions

You can configure initiation permissions and Field permissions for the initiation page. Adjust the permission group to Redirect To the Settings page of the workflow form.

## Node Field Permissions

- Field permissions for approvers and handlers

Approver and handler nodes support three permission settings: **Edit**, **Read-only**, and **Hidden**.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01fn56i43iDMC2BS5c_!!6000000005405-2-tps-1076-1184.png)
</Frame>

- Field permissions for CC nodes: CC nodes support configuring the View Permission of CC recipients, with two options available: Read-only and Hidden.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01M29m19DZTmE2BxKA_!!6000000000523-2-tps-1080-1246.png)
</Frame>
