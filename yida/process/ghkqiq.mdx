The CC recipient has permission to view the Approval form at their node, and you can also set the Form Content that the CC node can view, enabling some simple Permissions control.

<Note>
CC nodes are not shown in the flow chain of the Approval form.
</Note>

**Use cases for CC nodes**: After an Approval process is initiated, the corresponding Users need to view or be aware of the Approval form, but no Approval is required.
