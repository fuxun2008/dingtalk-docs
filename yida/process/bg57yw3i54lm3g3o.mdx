## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Feature Overview

Unified process creation streamlines how your organization creates, approves, and executes business processes. With this feature, users can quickly design, configure, and launch new workflows so that every task is handled efficiently within a standardized framework.

## 2. Steps

### 2.1 Entry Point

**Entry point**: To-Do Center — **View all tasks**.

### 2.2 Create a Process

1) Click **Create process**. The app list on the left shows all apps you currently have permission to access.

Click

 to add this process to your favorites. **You can add up to 12 processes**, as shown below:

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01GIuqPCXe3uI4iYIe_!!6000000003072-2-tps-2324-1304.png)
</Frame>

2) Click an app, then click on the right to view **all forms in the app that you have permission to submit**, as shown below:

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01dcmL2N9KL7L4yfIP_!!6000000005161-2-tps-2452-1317.png)
</Frame>

After filling in the form, click Submit to launch the corresponding process with a single click.
