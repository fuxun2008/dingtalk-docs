Official connectors are provided by DingTalk to link various business modules. This article explains how to use DingTalk official connectors.

## Steps

Follow the steps below to use an official connector in Integration & Automation or a workflow form.

1. Sign in to the Yida workbench.
2. Select the target app and go to the **Integration & Automation** page.
3. Select an existing integration & automation flow and click **Edit**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hover over the connecting line of a Step. When the + button appears, click it and select Connector.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the connector configuration panel on the right, select DingTalk Official, and then choose the connector you want to use.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

For example, use the **Start instance** connector under OA Approval.

6. Configure the execution action for the connector.

For example, configure the execution action for the Start instance connector.

7. When finished, click **Save**.
