## 1. Overview

The YiDA Video Meeting connector is one of the official connectors provided by DingTalk. It simplifies the video meeting workflow — from scheduling and creation (initiation) to querying information about ongoing video meetings. By covering every step of the full lifecycle from multiple angles, it helps enterprises accelerate their digital transformation.

## 2. Actions

1. **Schedule a video meeting**: Fill in and submit a YiDA form to create a meeting Event for the selected Members, send reminders, and complete the booking.
2. **Create a video meeting**: Submit a YiDA form to trigger the Create Video Meeting action. DingTalk automatically initiates the video meeting for the organizer. Once the organizer confirms, DingTalk invites the Members selected in the form to join the conference and start the video meeting.
3. **Query video meeting information in bulk**: Submit a YiDA form containing the IDs of ongoing video meetings to retrieve meeting and participant details, including the meeting name, start time, meeting status, and participant List.
