The steps in Integration & Automation are largely the same as those in an Approval process. The difference is that Integration & Automation cannot start an Approval directly, because the Approval start step relies on a workflow form. This topic describes the steps available in Integration & Automation.

- Data step
- Manual step
- Chats step
- Card step
- Branch step
- Connector step
- Script step
