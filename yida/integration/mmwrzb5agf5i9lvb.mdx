title: "Data nodes"
description: "Data nodes play a core role in Integration & Automation, supporting actions such as creating, updating, deleting data, and retrieving single or multiple records."
polished: true
---

Data nodes are the core feature of Integration & Automation. They act on data generated by apps to produce different execution results. The following nodes are currently available:

- **Create data**
- **Update data**
- **Get single record**
- **Get multiple records**
- **Delete data**
