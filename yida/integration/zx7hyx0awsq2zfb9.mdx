But have you ever reached the agreed Meeting Time only to realize that, as the organizer, you forgot to start the Meeting? Or, under time pressure, mis-selected or missed Users while inviting Members? Now, with the second action of the Video Meeting Connector — New Meeting — these pain points are a thing of the past. Work happily, and avoid embarrassment!

## Effect

<Note>
When starting a Video Meeting, the meeting initiator is invited to join the conference first. Once they accept, the Members are invited to join the conference. If the initiator declines, the Members are not invited.
</Note>

## Step 2: Create and configure the Connector

<Note>
**Further reading**: For an introduction to Connector features, see [Integration & Automation - Connector](https://docs.aliwork.com/docs/yida_support/zevvr1).
</Note>

**Create the Connector**: Click the "Integration & Automation" navigation at the top of the admin console to enter the feature section. Click the "Create Integration & Automation" Button and select "Create from Blank". In the dialog that appears, name the Connector "Create Video Meeting", and in the Form event trigger, select the "New Video Meeting" Form. Click Confirm.

Create Integration & Automation

**Configure the Form Trigger Event**: Set the trigger event to "Create Success", set the Data filter to "All Data", and click Save.

Configure the Form Event Trigger

**Configure the Connector**: Add a Connector node, use Search to locate the Connector with the action "Create Video Meeting", and click it to configure the Connector action. Map the Connector's required Fields to the Form Fields one by one, then click Save. Finally, click "Save" and "Publish" in the upper-right corner of the Page.
