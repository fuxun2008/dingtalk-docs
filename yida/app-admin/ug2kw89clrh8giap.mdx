After creating an app, refer to this article to modify its basic information, description, theme, navigation link, access URL, and other settings.

## Steps

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and open the **My Apps** page.
2. Select the target app, then choose **···** > **App settings**.

3. In the left-hand menu, select **Basic settings**, then edit the app's basic information as needed.
