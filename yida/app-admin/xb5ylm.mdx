This article walks through five steps: the service provider distributes the app, the customer purchases it, the app is sent from the order center, the customer enables the app, and the project is closed.

## Prerequisites

Before using the YiDA app distribution feature for sales, complete the following prerequisites:

- Become a DingTalk partner.
- Apply for app distribution permission.
- (Optional) Become a YiDA Premium partner.

## Workflow Overview

Service providers can distribute apps by following the steps below.

## Step 2: Customer Purchases the App

1. Send the app distribution link to your customer.
2. The customer opens the link in a browser and signs in to the organization that will purchase the app.
3. After confirming that the app is correct, the customer clicks **Purchase app** at the bottom.

## Step 5: Close the Project

Once the project is completed, the service provider must return to the YiDA workbench to close it.

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and go to the platform management page.
2. Click **Service provider** > **Project management**, select the completed project, and then click **View details**.

## Notes

When using app distribution to deploy apps to a customer's organization, note the following:

- App distribution helps YiDA partners quickly copy apps to a customer's organization. After distribution, the app's feature entitlements are verified against the entitlements of the target organization. We recommend that the source organization and the target organization use the same YiDA paid edition whenever possible, to avoid issues where advanced features cannot be enabled or used in the target organization.

## FAQ

- Q: After creating a project, I found that the project information was entered incorrectly. How do I modify it? Modification is not currently supported. Before the project is approved by YiDA, click **Undo** in the Actions column to cancel the initiated workflow.
- Q: Can an app be distributed multiple times for a single project?

![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01WuFhDpSrYrC3th7P_!!6000000007305-2-tps-1920-879.png)

For the same customer, you can obtain distribution links multiple times to distribute the same or different apps, from project approval until the project is closed.

- Q: Does "Project contract amount" refer to the total project amount or only the YiDA-related amount?

"Project contract amount" refers to the amount related to YiDA delivery. If the contract covers the entire project, enter the YiDA-related amount and provide additional context in "Project description".

- Q: What if I need to distribute an app to a customer in advance under special circumstances, but the contract has not been signed yet?

To protect your interests, this is not recommended. If special circumstances apply, provide a detailed explanation in "Project description". You can upload a quotation as the project contract, and the project amount must match the quotation.

- Q: Why is "View details" grayed out and unavailable?

By default, platform admins can view the list of projects created by all users and obtain distribution links, but they cannot view project details. To view details, contact the project creator.

- Q: Why does the message "This organization cannot make a purchase. Please contact the service provider" appear when a customer tries to purchase the app?
