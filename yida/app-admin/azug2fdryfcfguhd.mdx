This article uses the **Online Image Generator** template as an example to walk through the steps of selecting and enabling a template to generate an app.

## **Procedure**

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench.
2. Click **Template Gallery** in the top menu bar.
3. In the filter bar in the middle of the page, select **Quick Start** and **Form**, then select the **Online Image Generator** template.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01PWi6cUZBnxC3U0zb_!!6000000007308-2-tps-1716-951.png)
</Frame>

4. Click **Enable Template**. In the dialog box that appears, enter the **App name** and click **Confirm**.

Once the template is enabled, you are automatically redirected to the app's **Page Management** page.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01kkIhbskD9yF3Ue0f_!!6000000001014-2-tps-1721-928.png)
</Frame>

You have now created an app from a template. To edit the app, click **Edit** in the upper-right corner.
