The YiDA platform lets you customize the app access URL, making it easier to promote and share your app. You configure this on the app settings page. This article explains how to set a custom access URL for an app.

## Procedure

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and click **My Apps**.
2. Locate the target app, then click **···** > **App Settings** in the lower-right corner to open the app settings page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the Basic Settings page, go to the Access URL section and enter your custom URL in the Custom App Access URL field.">
    <Note>
    The custom URL supports only uppercase and lowercase English letters, numbers, underscores (_), hyphens (-), and forward slashes (/). If your preferred URL is already taken, add a suffix to make it unique—for example, `your_personal-address/enjoy/01`.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Once configured, click Save at the bottom of the page.">
  </Step>
</Steps>
