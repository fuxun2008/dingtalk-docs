In addition to using Templates, Excel, AI, and creating a blank app, you can also customize an app.

## Steps

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench.
2. Click Create App in the upper-right corner.
3. In the Create App dialog box that appears, click I want to consult on customization in the lower-left corner of the dialog box.
