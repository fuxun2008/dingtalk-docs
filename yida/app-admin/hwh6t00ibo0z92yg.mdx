## Entitlements

To help developers get started, intelligent apps created through the AI channel are currently exempt from the 10-user license cap of the Free plan. (However, under the standard 10-user entitlement of a Free plan organization, users beyond the cap can only update and modify the current intelligent app, and still cannot view or change other apps within the organization.) A "智" (AI) badge appears on apps after they are successfully created.
