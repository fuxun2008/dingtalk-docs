## Get a System-Generated Link

Follow these steps to get the app URL in a browser.

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and click **My Apps**.
2. Select the target app and click it to enter the app editor. Then click **Visit** in the upper-right corner.

  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01MuAoiAIZlTE2UKda_!!6000000000695-2-tps-1226-620.png)
</Frame>

3. On the app access page, copy the browser URL of the page.

## Get a Custom Link

Follow these steps to get a custom URL for the app.

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and click **My Apps**.
2. Select the target app and click it to enter the app editor.
3. Click **App settings** to open the settings page.
4. On the **Basic settings** page, in the **URL settings** section, select **Copy app link** or **Download app QR Code** as needed.

## Get the App Link in the DingTalk Client

Getting the app link in the DingTalk client is similar to getting it in a browser — you need to open the app details page first. Follow these steps to get the app URL from the DingTalk workbench.

1. Open the **DingTalk workbench** and select **YiDA** to open the YiDA home page.
2. Click **My Apps**, then select the target app to open the app details page.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01XgG0laoc43D1uhvS_!!6000000001548-2-tps-943-615.png)
</Frame>

3. Click **Share** in the upper-right corner, then select **Open in browser** or **Copy link**.

## Share a Link to a Single Record from the Data Management Page

To share a single record from a form, follow the steps below.

**My Apps** > select the app > select the page > **Data management** > select a single record > right-click to copy the link

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN0195JyjB9HYIC2f9qh_!!6000000001237-2-tps-1312-671.png)
</Frame>

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and click **My Apps**.
2. Select the target app and click it to enter the app editor.
3. Select the form whose data you want to share, and click **Data management**.
4. Select the record you want to share, and click **Details** in the Action column.

5. In the Source records sidebar, click **···** > **Share** in the upper-right corner, then click **Copy link** in the share dialog to get the link to the current record.
