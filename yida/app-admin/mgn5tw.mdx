Yida lets you publish an app, or an individual form within an app, to your organization's workbench. You can also install Yida app plugins in Group Chat. This chapter explains how to publish a Yida app.

In this chapter:

- Publish an app
- Publish a silent login page
- Publish a form page publicly
- Add an app to a group
