This article explains how to create a dataset from a database.

## Prerequisites

Before creating a dataset from a database, add a database Data Source to your App. For detailed steps, see [Create a Data Source](https://www.yuque.com/yida/ng4ubn/ap3zy8h89xpzgfbt).

## Steps

1. Sign in to the Yida workbench and go to the App settings page.
2. Click **Data Factory** > **Dataset**.
3. Click **Create Dataset** > **From Database**.
