Filtering apps means selecting, screening, or excluding certain apps based on specific criteria or conditions. The goal is to find the apps that best match user needs or serve a specific purpose, and remove those that do not meet the requirements. This streamlines the app list and improves the user experience.

## Steps

1. Enter a keyword to run a fuzzy search on app names.

2. Use App Management groups to quickly filter apps.

3. Click **Manage groups** to sort the current apps, add a new group, delete a group, or configure the apps within a group.

4. Select an app on the left to add it to the selected app list on the right.
