Silent login pages in YiDA apps can be published individually to your organization's internal workbench, or converted into surveys for external distribution. This article explains how to publish a form page individually.

## Procedure

Publish a form page using either of the following methods.

### Method 1: Publish via a DingTalk Open Platform Web App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [YiDA workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click **My apps**, then select and open the target app to view its details page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    Select the page to publish, then click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> > **Publish page**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

4. Select **Publish within organization** and copy the **access URL** of the page.
5. Sign in to the DingTalk Open Platform developer backend, create an internal app, and enable the web app capability.

<Note>
For steps to create an app, see the [Open Platform documentation](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/orgapp/create-an-application).
</Note>

6. Enter the page link in the web app home URL field, then click **Save**.

7. Click **Publish app** > **Version management and publish** > **Create new version**.
8. Configure the version information and visibility scope, then click **Save** to complete publishing.

### Method 2: Create a Form Shortcut on the Workbench

Create a shortcut to the form page on the DingTalk workbench from the DingTalk mobile app.

1. Get the access URL of the form page. See Method 1 for details.
2. Open the workbench in the DingTalk mobile app and tap **Add**.
3. Select **Custom app**, then tap **Connect to workbench**.
4. Enter the app information, then tap **Add** to finish.
