## Overview

Unlike reports, which offer a full analytics workflow, YiDA data cards let you quickly assemble fragmented, scenario-based charts for use in a YiDA portal. Cards also include a built-in broadcast capability, so you can share data snapshots directly to groups.

## Use Cases

### Present Key Metrics

Combine data cards with a portal to surface key metrics inside your app portal and bring the portal to life with live data.

| Traditional portal | Portal enhanced with data cards |
|---|---|

### Push Daily Insights

Push key metrics configured in a data card to a group as a snapshot. This works well for scenarios such as **daily data briefings** and **weekly progress updates**.

Data cards can push key metrics to DingTalk groups.

| Client-side view | Mobile view |
|---|---|
