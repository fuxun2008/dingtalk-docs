## 1. Introduction

### 1.2 Use Cases

- Financial reporting and analysis: Finance teams regularly generate reports such as income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements. QBI automates data collection and report generation, improving both accuracy and efficiency.
- Sales and marketing analysis: Sales teams track market trends, customer behavior, and sales patterns to refine their strategies. QBI helps them access this information quickly and perform in-depth analysis.
- Operational performance monitoring: Operations teams need real-time visibility into metrics such as production efficiency, inventory levels, and supply chain status. QBI provides real-time dashboards and alerts so teams can spot issues promptly and take action.

## 2. Procedure

### 2.1 Enable Premium Data Reports

#### 2.1.1 Enable for Existing Apps

1. After the customer places an order in the Entitlement Center, the YiDA support team manually enables or disables premium data reports in the backend.
2. Location: Platform Management > Basic Information > Premium Features.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01Fz3YD8yHCqH6ivMk_!!6000000000879-2-tps-3312-1654.png)
</Frame>

#### 2.1.2 Enable When Creating an App

1. Open the YiDA App Management backend and click **Create App**.
2. Enter the basic app information in the dialog box.
3. In the Create dialog, click **More App Settings** and enable App-Dedicated Storage, App-Dedicated Physical Columns, App-Dedicated Environment, and Premium Reports Isolated Database.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01iMjGSJ4YUnK5wpxk_!!6000000007277-2-tps-2930-1522.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
- Once App-Dedicated Storage is enabled, app data is stored in dedicated storage mode and cannot be reverted.
- Once App-Dedicated Physical Columns is enabled, app releases support physical columns.
- Once App-Dedicated Environment is enabled, app releases support independent environment deployment and multi-version sandbox capabilities.
- Once Premium Reports Isolated Database is enabled, the isolated database independently supports premium report capabilities.
</Note>
