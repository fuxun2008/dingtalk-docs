DingTalk Data Asset Platform dPaaS (Data Platform as a Service) is a data platform service launched by DingTalk, a product of Alibaba Group. The platform provides organizations with a unified data processing and management solution, helping them use data resources more efficiently to drive business growth and optimize organizational governance.

## Overview

As a low-code app building platform, YiDA integrates seamlessly with dPaaS data assets and turns complex data into clear, visual information. Connect YiDA directly to dPaaS datasets and apply the data to business scenarios such as report building, data analysis, and decision support. This improves work efficiency and decision accuracy, and further strengthens organizational competitiveness and innovation.

The core advantages and features of DingTalk Data Asset Platform include:

- Low cost: Optimized data processing and storage technologies reduce the cost of building and maintaining data assets.
- Scalable: The flexible platform design scales with data volume growth and changing business requirements.
- Easy to maintain: User-friendly interfaces and tools simplify data asset management and streamline daily data operations.
- Data inclusion: Promotes data sharing and utilization, and drives internal data circulation and value discovery within your organization.
- Unified data capabilities: Provides one-stop data integration, management, and consumption services that help organizations centralize data management and use data efficiently.
- Data-driven decisions: Delivers data support for organizational governance and business operations through data analysis and mining, enabling a data-driven decision-making model.
- New ways of consuming data: Encourages innovative data consumption scenarios and applications to improve the value conversion of data.

## Entitlements

The **Data Asset service** is currently available only in YiDA Professional edition and above.

|  | **YiDA Free plan**<br />**YiDA Standard / Basic edition** | **YiDA Premium / Professional edition** | **YiDA Dedicated edition** | **YiDA Data Asset service** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Data item usage** | Not available | 100 calls/month | 1,000 calls/month | 20,000 calls/month, stackable |
| **Data volume per call** | Not available | 5,000 records | 10,000 records | 10,000 records |
| **Data item level** | Not available | Offline statistical data<br />Offline detail data | Offline statistical data<br />Offline detail data | Offline statistical data<br />Offline detail data<br />Real-time statistical data<br />Real-time detail data |
| **Data asset access period** | Not available | 180 days | 180 days | 1 year |

- Data freshness note: The data in DingTalk Data Asset Platform currently covers the most recent year.

## Steps

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench, select the app for which you want to enable the dataset, and click to open the app details page.
2. Click **App Settings**, and then choose **Data Factory** > **Dataset**.
