YiDA low-code apps support five creation methods, one of which lets you build an app directly from an Excel file.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the YiDA [Workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click **Create App** in the upper-right corner, and then click **Create App from Excel** in the dialog box that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    On the **Create App from Excel** page, **select an Excel file**.
    You can either **select from Excel templates** or **upload from local** to add the Excel file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4">
    In the field settings step, configure the form fields based on the following information, and then click **Next**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
