YiDA low-code apps support five creation methods. This article describes how to create an app using Excel.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the YiDA [Workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click **Create App** in the upper-right corner, and then click **Create App from Excel** in the dialog box that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    On the **Create App from Excel** page, **select an Excel file**.
    You can either **select from Excel templates** or **upload from local** to add the Excel file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4">
    In the field settings step, configure the form fields based on the following information, and then click **Next**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Steps>
  <Step title="In the workflow design step, choose whether to turn on the approval process">
    <Note>
    - If new data requires approval, turn on the process and complete the flowchart design. For details about the workflow design step, see workflow design.
    - For apps created from Excel, up to three approvers can be added in the workflow design.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the app settings step, configure the app name and permissions, and then click Create App to complete the creation">
  </Step>
</Steps>
