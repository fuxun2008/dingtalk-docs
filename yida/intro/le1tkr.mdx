## **My Apps**

On the My Apps page, you can view the apps that the currently signed-in user has created or has edit permission for within the current organization. Platform Admins can view and manage all apps within the organization, with support for sorting, filtering, and other actions.

## **Template Gallery**

The Template Gallery provides app templates from YiDA officials, service providers, and low-code competitions, covering almost every everyday business need. With these templates, you can quickly build apps that fit your organization's actual situation.

Templates in the Template Gallery serve a variety of purposes, such as HR and administration, financial reimbursement, manufacturing, and project management, and are suitable for organizations of all sizes. Select the template that fits your needs. Using templates from the Template Gallery saves you the time and effort of building pages from scratch and helps you meet business needs more efficiently. To learn how to create an app from a template, see [Create an app](/yida/app-admin/oncnoy).

If you cannot find a template that fits your needs in the Template Gallery, click the **Product Experience Feedback** button on the page to send us your feedback. While using YiDA, if you run into any issues, you can consult us and share feedback through the smart service assistant on the right.

## **FAQs**

**Q: How do I modify a template?**

A: Before modifying a template, make sure you understand the business relationships between the forms in the template app, such as whether business association rules or data linkage have been configured between forms.

**Modification steps**:

1. Sign in to the web version of the YiDA workbench and click to open the My Apps page.
2. Select the app you want to modify and open its details page. Then locate the form you want to modify and click the Edit button in the upper-right corner.
3. On the form editing page, modify the corresponding form or components.

**Q: Is there a limit on the number of permission groups within a Premium app's forms? What is the maximum number of permission groups that can be configured?**

In a Premium app, all pages except workflow forms and regular forms support up to 100 permission groups. Workflow forms and regular forms each support up to 100 submit permission groups and 100 view permission groups.
