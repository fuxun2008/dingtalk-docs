## About the Broadcast Portal

The YiDA broadcast portal—a portal home page that can broadcast announcements and key updates to members—can serve as the home page of an app. With portal templates, out-of-the-box business components, and the editor, organizations can build a dedicated, personalized app portal home page that provides one-stop workbench navigation.

- Organization portal: business dashboards, government and public-sector services, customer service, and more.
- App portal: customer management, quality inspection management, procurement management, CRM, and more.

## About the Editor

The broadcast portal editor consists of the following four areas.

- ① Component library list: select from a range of out-of-the-box components. For details, see portal components.
- ② Central canvas: lay out components and arrange their order.
- ③ Attribute panel (right side): configure attribute settings for individual components and style settings for the entire page.
- ④ Top menu bar: preview the portal page on mobile and PC, undo changes, view the activity log, and adjust the page style.

## Ways to Create a Broadcast Portal

After you create a YiDA app, you can create a broadcast portal in any of the following ways.

- Option 1: Create a portal from a blank page.
- Option 2: Create a portal from a template.
- Option 3: Create a portal with YiDA AI.
