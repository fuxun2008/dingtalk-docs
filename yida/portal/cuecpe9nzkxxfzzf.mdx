Once your business app is built, you can use out-of-the-box business components and editor capabilities to design a personalized, broadcastable portal home page. It provides one-stop workbench navigation and better showcases your app's core content. This article explains how to create a portal.

There are three ways to create a portal:

- Create from scratch: Build a broadcastable portal from the ground up.
- Create from a template: Quickly build a broadcastable portal based on an official template.
- Create with AI: Use the Yida AI Assistant to generate a broadcastable portal automatically.

## Create a portal from scratch

1. Sign in to the Yida workbench and open the **My apps** page.
2. Select the app in which you want to create the portal, and open the app details page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click ➕ in the upper-left corner, and then select Create broadcastable portal.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create blank page, and then set the portal page name in the upper-right corner.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a portal from a template

1. Sign in to the Yida workbench and open the **My apps** page.
2. Select the app in which you want to create the portal, and open the app details page.

3. Click **➕** in the upper-left corner, and then select **Create broadcastable portal**.

4. Select a portal template that fits your business needs and click **Use**.
5. Set the portal page name in the upper-left corner of the page.

<Note>
If none of the existing portal templates meet your needs, you can [submit a request here](https://docs.aliwork.com/docs/yida_support/bp2rhh).
</Note>

Start from a portal template and choose a suitable reference to quickly kick off your broadcastable portal design.

## **Create a portal with AI**

The Yida AI Assistant supports one-click creation of a broadcastable portal. For details on how to use the AI Assistant, see [Yida AI](https://docs.aliwork.com/docs/yida_support/_4/_12/cizng12zapm1kbaf).
