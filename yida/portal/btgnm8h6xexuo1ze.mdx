## Brand-new portal building

In the past, building a polished portal Page required writing code. Users needed coding Skills and experience, which posed a challenge for those without a technical background.

Now, YiDA introduces the brand-new "portal" Page concept. Users can quickly generate a polished App Home Page from a portal Template, delivering a personalized, dedicated App or Organization portal Home Page and providing one-stop workbench navigation.

(YiDA officially provides 9 scenario-based portal Templates.)

With the brand-new low-threshold, zero-code portal design tool, even Users with zero experience can build a new portal from scratch in just three steps: Component drag-and-drop, Data configuration, and Subject configuration. This approach makes building a portal Page simpler and more efficient. Without professional coding knowledge, anyone can easily Create their own portal Page.

The portal also supports building Data Cards to visualize Data Content. Data can be broadcast end-to-end, Cards can be quickly added to groups, and business insights are no longer a hassle.

## Broadcastable Data Card

The YiDA **Data Card** is a lightweight Data visualization element. Unlike the full analysis scenarios of reports, Data Cards can quickly combine fragmented, scenario-based Charts, and are currently supported in YiDA portals. In addition, YiDA Data Cards come with built-in broadcasting capability. Data Cards can be scheduled to broadcast in DingTalk group scenarios as Data snapshots, automatically sharing Important Data in the group Daily.
