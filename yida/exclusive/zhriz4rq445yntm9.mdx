As the number of low-code apps in an organization grows, admins and developers in different roles find it harder to search for and categorize apps. To help organization admins manage apps more effectively, the Yida Dedicated edition introduces the App groups feature. Yida platform management admins can create and manage App groups, and App Management admins can select a group when creating or editing an app.

<Note>
Each organization can create up to 10 groups, and each group can contain up to 100 apps.
</Note>

### Manage App groups

Follow these steps to manage App groups.

1. Sign in to the Yida workbench and go to the My Apps page.
2. Click Manage groups. In the dialog box, click Add group and enter a group Title.

3. Click **+** on the right to add apps to the group, then click Confirm to Complete creation.

### Yida To-Do groups

DingTalk To-Do tasks sync Yida App groups to the Task list, and the Yida To-Do task list appears in OA Approval based on this group information.

When you create an app or open App Settings, you can flexibly adjust and Configure the business category and group information of an app. Group relationships are maintained by the **platform management Admin**, while regular app developers **can adjust the group an app belongs to**.
