## Overview

- Compared with other editions, YiDA Dedicated edition offers stronger business performance and big data processing capabilities.
- Supports up to one million records per form and tens of millions of records across the overall solution (paired with the database architecture design of dedicated storage).

## Dedicated Database Index Acceleration

- Accelerates indexing on business fields within forms, enabling high-speed retrieval on forms with up to one million records in scenarios such as search and filter recognition.
- Improves retrieval efficiency of the dedicated PostgreSQL storage and reduces data RT (response time) in multi-cloud and complex network environments.

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Retrieval performance on high-frequency business fields in the dedicated database was poor and required a professional DBA to tune database performance indexes. | For hot business query fields on a form, the YiDA platform management admin of an organization can improve retrieval performance by configuring field indexes. |
