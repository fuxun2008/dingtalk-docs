The Space Tabler evolves from the App Tabler. Unlike a regular Tabler, it can be configured with data sets from all Apps in the Space, enabling intelligent data analysis across Forms and Apps.

## Prerequisites

Before creating a Space Tabler, first create a workspace. For details, see [Create workspace](/yida/exclusive/ugxt8b6935q7znbe).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the YiDA [workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform) as an Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    In the top-right corner, click **Platform management** to open the **Platform management** Page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    In the left menu, go to **Platform-exclusive customization** > **Space management**, select the Space you want to edit, and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure Space Tabler Capabilities

<Note>
Editing the Space Tabler and enabling its capabilities work essentially the same as for the App-level Tabler. For detailed steps, see YiDA Tabler.
</Note>

Main differences between the Space Tabler and the App-level Tabler:

- The App-level Tabler's data scope is limited to the current App.
- The Space Tabler's data scope covers all Apps in the current Space, enabling cross-App Q&A.
