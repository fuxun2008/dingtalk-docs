## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Feature overview

The workspace component visibility feature delivers a personalized experience for every user. It gives organizations a more granular and flexible visibility management tool that safeguards data security while improving efficiency and user experience. With custom visibility settings, organizations can precisely control which users or departments can view specific components. This protects sensitive information and prevents unauthorized access.

### 1.2 Feature benefits

- Precise access control: Custom visibility settings let organizations precisely control which users or departments can view specific components. This protects sensitive information and prevents unauthorized access.
- Reduced risk: Minimize unnecessary information exposure, lower the risk of information leakage, and ensure that only relevant personnel can see important or sensitive data.
- Personalized portal pages: Employees in different departments or roles see different components, helping them find the information and tools they need faster and boosting productivity.
- Simplified workflows: For specific business scenarios, visibility settings streamline workflows and prevent unrelated personnel from joining unnecessary approvals or reviews.
