Once a workspace is created, use the Quick Entry component in the Space portal to build a personalized App Center that meets diverse collaboration needs across your organization. This article describes how to configure the App Center in the Space portal.

## Procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [Yida workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform) as an Admin, then click **Platform Management** in the upper-right corner to open the platform management page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    In the left-hand menu, choose **Platform Dedicated Customization** > **Space Management**, select the target Space, and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    Choose **Space App Management** > **Manage Associated Apps**, select the Apps to add to the Space, and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Space Portal Settings, then click Edit Portal">
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the left-hand component panel, select the Quick Entry component and drag it onto the central canvas">
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the right-hand attribute panel, choose Content > Batch Edit">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4">
    In the dialog box, set Entry Type to App, set App/Page/Link to the target App, set Entry Name to the App name, and set Auxiliary Description to the App description. Once all settings are complete, click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After configuration, click Save in the upper-right corner.">
  </Step>
</Steps>
