This topic describes how to manage the apps associated with a space in the YiDA workbench.

## Procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [YiDA workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform) as an Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click **Platform Management** in the upper-right corner to open the platform management page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    In the left-side menu, select **Platform Dedicated Customization** > **Space Management**, choose the space you want to edit, and then click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4">
    Click **Space App Management**, and then click **Manage Associated Apps**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
