To update basic information such as the space name, access URL, or watermark, follow the steps in this article to change the basic space settings.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [YiDA workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform) as an Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click **Platform Management** in the upper-right corner to open the platform management page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    In the left-side menu, select **Platform Dedicated Customization** > **Space Management**, choose the space you want to edit, and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

4. Click Basic Space Settings to modify the workspace's basic information, space navigation link information, and space access URL.
