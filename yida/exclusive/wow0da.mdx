To offer a more intuitive view and analysis of business digitalization results at the organizational level, the **Enterprise efficiency** data in the YiDA Dedicated edition supports more granular data views (click a card to see details).

## What You Can View

- Analyze report data from the perspectives of apps, users and departments, and instance submissions.

## What You Can See

- Common information such as app name, app creator, creation time, usage analytics (visit count, number of visitors), visitor names within your organization, user IDs, department information, and instance submission details—all displayed at a glance.
- Filter each report by the criteria you need. Table details can also be exported and downloaded as Excel files for further data mining and analysis on your local machine.

## What You Can Discover

- Reports automatically highlight top-performing apps, departments, and individuals within your organization. See the quantified economic benefits that low-code delivers to your organization through cost reduction and efficiency gains.
- Benchmark against industry averages to see how your organization compares to industry peers in leveraging low-code for digitalization (based on YiDA low-code usage).

## Who Can View

- Platform admins of the organization can run quantitative analysis of the return on IT investment via YiDA **Workbench** > **Platform settings** > **Enterprise efficiency** > **Efficiency data**.

## FAQ

### Q1: How Are the Estimated Savings and Development Time Saved for the Organization Calculated?

- App development budget: Calculated based on the number of months your organization has used YiDA in the past 12 months, the number of YiDA app developers, and the average efficiency improvement in the industry.
- Development time: The total time spent by all developers in your organization building low-code apps over the past 12 months. Durations shorter than one day are rounded.
- Note: The evaluation data is for reference only and is intended to help quantify general digitalization efficiency results.
