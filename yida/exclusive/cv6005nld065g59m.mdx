Efficient work handover positively impacts every aspect of organizational operations. It addresses task owner changes across scenarios such as department transfers, team restructuring, extended leave, and onboarding or offboarding. From improved efficiency to higher employee and customer satisfaction, it is a key part of an Organization's digital transformation.

YiDA Dedicated further strengthens work handover efficiency in an Organization's data-driven initiatives.

## Work handover

Retrieve and transfer in-progress tasks assigned to a designated User in batch.

- Available to platform Admins (within the management scope authorized to the primary or sub platform Admin). For in-progress Approval tasks, in-progress workflows are shown in a List and can be transferred to a designated User in batch.
- Combined with task management in the Delegation Center, you can selectively transfer long-standing, low-efficiency tasks of the delegator with precision.
- In-progress task retrieval is currently supported for active Employees. Task handling for offboarded Employees will be available in a future release.
- Currently limited to the current Organization. Cross-Organization and upstream or downstream Organization handovers are not yet supported.

When an Employee changes roles, the responsibilities and in-progress items of the current role can be transferred to the successor in batch.

|---|
| Step 2: Transfer the tasks to the successor in batch and send a task reminder. |
