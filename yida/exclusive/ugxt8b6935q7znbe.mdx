Once you understand the basic concepts of workspaces, follow this article to create your own dedicated workspace.

## Create a Workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [YiDA workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform) as an Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click **Platform Management** in the upper-right corner to open the platform management page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    In the left-side menu, choose **Platform Customization** > **Space Management**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4">
    Follow the steps below to create a Space.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN0109x7gwlEM6G34aRp_!!6000000007116-2-tps-1514-915.png)
</Frame>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td>**Setting**</td>
    <td>**Description**</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>**Space Name**</td>
    <td>Enter a name for the Space. Keep it short.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>**Default Language**</td>
    <td>Defaults to the system language.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>**Space Icon**</td>
    <td>Select an icon for the Space. Custom icons are not supported during creation. After the Space is created, you can switch to a custom icon on the Space settings page.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>**Space Description**</td>
    <td>Enter a description for the Space. Up to 100 characters.</td>
  </tr>
</table>

## Edit a Workspace

1. In the left-side menu, choose Platform Customization > Space Management. Select the Space you want to edit, then click Edit.
