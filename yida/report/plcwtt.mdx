## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Feature Overview

The administrative division map component supports the China map at the province, city, and county levels, with drill-up and drill-down capabilities across these tiers.

**Download the map data:**

[📎China Provinces, Cities, and Districts (New).xlsx](https://www.yuque.com/attachments/yuque/0/2021/xlsx/21842187/1639454220961-4f0b8f7b-ea20-46ec-86c0-3b1e2640bb8b.xlsx)

Highlight: Freely control the size of the component and move its position (left-aligned, center-aligned, or right-aligned).

### 1.2 Use Cases

Statistical analysis often requires examining how data is distributed across geographic regions:

- Analyze customer counts and new customer counts by region
- Analyze sales figures by region

- Analyze store counts by region

### 1.3 Basic Requirements

| **Region division** | **Primary metric (required)** | **Secondary metric** |
|---|---|---|
| Required, up to 30 fields | Up to one field | Multiselect supported, up to 30 fields |

## 3. FAQ

### 3.1 Why Does the Map Component Appear Blank on Windows PCs?

The chart data currently displays normally on Mac PCs and in browsers. If the China map component in a YiDA report appears blank on a Windows PC, resolve the issue with the following configuration:

**Note: After configuring, sign out of DingTalk and sign in again.**

Path: DingTalk desktop client >> Profile Photo in the upper-left corner >> Settings >> Enable WebGL support and SVG support >> Sign out of DingTalk and sign in again.
