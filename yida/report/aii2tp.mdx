Data collected through forms or workflow forms can be viewed, analyzed, and processed using reports.

Reports support multiple chart styles. Use Details tables and Pivot Tables to view detailed and summarized data from forms or workflow forms. Use bar charts, Line charts, and other visualizations to analyze trends, compare categories, and surface insights. Use Pie charts to show the proportion of each part within the Data.
