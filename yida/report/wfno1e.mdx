## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Use Cases

A column chart provides an intuitive view of differences across data groups and emphasizes comparison between individuals.

A line chart analyzes sales trends of a product category over time to help forecast future sales.

A column-line combo chart displays a column chart and a line chart at the same time.

Component types: Column-line combo, Line-column combo, and Dual-axis line (all available under style settings).

Highlight: Freely adjust the component size and move the component to any position (left, center, or right).

### 1.2 Basic Requirements

The basic requirements for the column-line combo chart are as follows:

| **X-Axis** | **Left (primary) Y-Axis** | **Right (secondary) Y-Axis** | **Reference line** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 1 field | Multiselect supported, up to 30 fields | Multiselect supported, up to 30 fields | Multiselect supported, up to 30 fields |
