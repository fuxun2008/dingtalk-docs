## 1. Introduction

Containers are mainly used for layout and can be combined with other components.

Note: You can adjust the size of the components inside, but you cannot move the container's position (Left Align, Center Align, or Right Align).

## 2. Steps

### 2.1 Select a Component

Select the Container component and drag it into the designer. You can then drag other components into it.
