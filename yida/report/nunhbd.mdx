## 2. FAQ

### 2.1 Why does the year line break not take effect?

It currently applies only to **Column charts and combination line charts**. Other chart components are not supported.

If the X-Axis of a Column chart or combination line chart uses a Date field, the year wraps to a new line once year line break is set to On, as shown below:

### 2.2 Fields supported by date formatting

Date fields are supported by default. Specific formats of Text fields (such as 20000204) are also supported.
