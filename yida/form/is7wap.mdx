| **Feature** | **Free plan** | **Basic edition** | **Professional edition** | **Dedicated edition** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Print | Supported | Supported | Supported | Supported |
| Custom print template | Not supported | Supported | Supported | Supported |

<Note>
Batch printing supports up to 100 records, and the total data size must not exceed 200 MB.
</Note>

The YiDA low-code platform lets you output visible form data to paper or other physical records through a printer. The YiDA Basic edition, Professional edition, and other paid editions support custom print template settings. The YiDA Free plan does not.
