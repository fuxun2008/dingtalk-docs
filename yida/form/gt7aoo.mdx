Basic edition and Professional edition

The Free plan supports basic bulk import. For details, see [Bulk import](/yida/form/andrke).

By default, bulk import does not trigger validation rules, associated business rules, or third-party service callbacks.

## 1. Overview of enhanced bulk import

### 1.1 Trigger validation rules, associated business rules, and third-party callbacks during import

During import, select the option to trigger validation rules, associated business rules, and third-party service callbacks at the same time. Once selected, the corresponding rules are triggered.
