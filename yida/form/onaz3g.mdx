Data management page

## Procedure

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and open the page management page of the app.
2. Locate the target workflow form or form, and click **Data management**.
3. Click **More** > **Activity log**.
4. Click **Import records**, select the data to delete, and click **Delete data added in this batch**.

## Batch Deletion Rules

You can batch delete data from both batch submissions of the current workflow form and batch imports of the form.
