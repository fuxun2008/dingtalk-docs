In app distribution scenarios, service providers can accumulate and reuse the Custom Components they develop, and distribute them to different customer organizations.

## 1.1 Page entry

**Path**: Platform management > Service provider > Order center > Send App / Send Custom Component

## 1.2 Feature overview

### Initial distribution

1. For the initial app distribution, click Send App. If the App contains Custom Components, the Custom Components are distributed to the customer organization at the same time.

Figure: App distribution page

Figure: Component list on the customer side after distribution

### Component version upgrades after distribution

1. If the original App (the Component list installed under the current App) has new Custom Components added, or existing Custom Components have new versions, the ISV can select specific Components and push the app distribution update to the customer side.

Figure: Custom Component version upgrade
