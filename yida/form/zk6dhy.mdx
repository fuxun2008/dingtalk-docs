## Component Overview

Premium components are advanced components used to configure a form. They include:

- Image and Text Display
- Department
- Country/Region
- Address
- Rich Text
- Layout Container
- Group
- Associated Form
- Location
- Signature
- Electronic Seal
- Association Query
- Serial Number
- Word Template
- Smart Invoice
