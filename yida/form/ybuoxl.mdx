## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Feature Overview

Once you have confirmed the data to collect, create a form and design it to match your requirements.

### 1.2 Use Cases

Two form types are available. A regular form includes collaboration features and supports permission-based data management. A workflow form includes workflow features and supports workflow design.

There are three ways to create a form:

- **Create from blank**
- **Create from a template**
- **Create a form from Excel** (uses Excel headers as components)

## 2. How to Create a Form

**Path**: Open the YiDA workbench >> Click My Apps or Create new >> Enter the app editor.

### 2.1 Steps

(1) On the current App Center page, click Create blank form.

(2) At the top of the left-hand navigation pane of the app, click Create page, then select Create form from the drop-down menu.

<Warning>
Note: After a workflow ends, workflow form data cannot be edited. Plan accordingly.
</Warning>
