## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Feature Overview

In Select, Multi-select, Dropdown select, and Dropdown multi-select fields, assign specified components to each option. The specified component appears only when the option matches the selected value. This applies to scenarios such as item requisition and contract management.

**Note: The Linked option settings button is not available when the component is placed inside a subform.**

### 1.2 Use Case

Design a Party member information platform for a company. Employees with different identities fill in different information. For example:

- Party member: Enter the date of joining the Party, the location of joining, and the introducer.
- Probationary Party member: Enter the date of becoming a probationary Party member.
- Activist: Enter the date of becoming an activist.
- General public: No additional information required.

## 2. Rules for Linked Option Settings

If a component is assigned to an option but no option is selected, the assigned component is hidden and does not appear on the form.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01nP5ud3B5wKC2ETCQ_!!6000000005926-2-tps-1100-410.png)
</Frame>

For example, a Multi-line text component is assigned to Option 1. When Option 1 is not selected on the form, the Multi-line text component is hidden by default:

Option 1 is linked to a Multi-line text component

If the assigned component is set to hidden in the form permission group, it remains hidden even when the option is matched.
