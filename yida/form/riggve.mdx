## 1. Basic Introduction

### 1.1 What Is the Signature Component

A Component that enables quick electronic signatures on mobile devices.

### 1.2 Use Cases

- Submit Data on a mobile phone or iPad with an electronic Signature as a Form Credential for business entries, such as customer information Confirmation at car dealerships.
- Official document application systems, such as PCR test information submissions and return-home applications during the pandemic.
