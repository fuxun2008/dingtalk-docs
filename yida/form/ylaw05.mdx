## 1. Country/Region

### 1.1 What Is the Country/Region Component

A component for selecting countries or administrative regions worldwide.

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01KN2QRisfZhI3i75V_!!6000000006237-2-tps-1828-861.png)

Add the Country/Region component.

### 1.2 Use Cases

- A logistics company selects the origin or destination for a shipment.
- An employee selects the departure or destination city when booking airfare for reimbursement.
