YiDA lets you output visible data such as form content to paper or other physical media through a printer. For example, print expense reports or application forms and hand them to your manager or finance team for record keeping.

## Print a Single Record

Follow these steps to print a form.

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and open the app details page.
2. Locate the form you want to print, and click **Data management**.
3. Locate the form record you want to print, and click it to open the details page.
4. In the upper-right corner, click **···** > **Print**, and then select a print template.

5. Select **Sign file**, **Print directly**, or **Download**.

## View Print Records

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and open the app details page.
2. Select the target form, and click **Data management**.
3. On the **Data management** page, click **More** > **Activity log**.

4. Click **Print records** to view them.

## Batch Print

Differences between batch printing and printing a single record:

- Print multiple form records at once.
- Downloading a single printed file returns one PDF file by default.
- Downloading batch-printed files returns a ZIP archive containing all printed files by default.
- You can select up to 100 records per batch print.

To batch print, follow these steps:

1. Sign in to the YiDA workbench and open the app details page.
2. Locate the form you want to print, and click **Data management**.
3. Select the records to print, and click **More** > **Print selected**.

**Note:**

The **All data** tab contains four sub-tabs: **To do**, **Handled by me**, **Created by me**, and **CC to me**. The page data types available for filtering under each sub-tab are listed in the table below. Switch between different forms or workflows to print data on the corresponding pages.

| **Sub-tab** | **Filtered page type** |
|---|---|
| To do | Workflow instance |
| Handled by me | Workflow instance |
| Created by me | Form instance, workflow instance |
| CC to me | Workflow instance |
