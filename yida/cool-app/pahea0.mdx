Millions of custom apps built on YiDA now support half-screen display, custom cool card sending and updates, and low-code logic orchestration. Cool capabilities make future app forms simpler to build. Low-code apps produced on YiDA include Cool App capabilities by default.

**After a custom Cool App or template Cool App built on YiDA is developed, submit it to the DingTalk Chat Custom Cool App Center or the DingTalk Chat Cool App Marketplace. Users must enable it in a chat before they can use it.**

**Prerequisites:**

1. In DingTalk Department Chats and All-staff Chats, only Admins can install and enable Cool Apps (custom Cool Apps and template Cool Apps) by default.
2. In your organization's Internal Groups, all chat members have permission to install and enable Cool Apps (custom Cool Apps and template Cool Apps).

1. Click **Enable** to use it out of the box, or click a Cool App icon to view its details.

## FAQ

**Q1: How do I launch a Cool App across DingTalk?**

<Frame>
  ![](https://gw.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN0110WRADXF48F1CWfo_!!6000000002155-2-tps-592-1280.png)

- Each Cool App now has its own dedicated Cool App link (replace the coolappcode with that of the target Cool App). You can launch it anywhere within DingTalk with one click. Cool Apps are no longer limited to the Cool App Center Marketplace or the main app details page—they can now be distributed throughout DingTalk.
- ISVs can replace the coolapp code with their own Cool App code and distribute their Cool Apps to customers across different channels to boost reach. See the result below. (Currently, the link can only be opened on mobile.)
- Note: If the chat list is empty when you select a chat, confirm whether the organization currently signed in on your mobile has any Internal Groups. If not, create one first before using the Cool App in the chat.
