Today, millions of organization-built Apps on YiDA support capabilities such as half-screen opening, custom Cool Card sending and updates, and low-code logic orchestration. Cool capabilities make future App development simpler. Low-code Apps built on YiDA include Cool App capabilities by default.

**YiDA supports both organization-built Cool Apps and first-party/third-party template Cool Apps, and both can be published to the DingTalk Cool App Marketplace.**
