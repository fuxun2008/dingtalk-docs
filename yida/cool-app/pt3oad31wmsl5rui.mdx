## Prerequisites

Complete self-testing of the SaaS app in a sandbox test organization before submitting the product for listing.

## App Publishing

- Publish SaaS products to DingTalk Open Platform > Third-party app unified management.
- Publish SaaS products to the DingTalk App Marketplace, with independent sales leads, transactions, self-operation, and service capabilities.
- Publish SaaS products to the DingTalk Cool App Marketplace for efficient collaboration in group chats.

After a standard SaaS app service product is published on DingTalk, promotion typically takes one of two forms:

- App Marketplace promotion: if you already have an app product or plan to build one, and your business model is clear and meets the DingTalk App Marketplace listing standards, your product can be listed for promotion in the marketplace after review. Once listed, customers can search, view, install, and purchase the product from the DingTalk App Marketplace.
- Self-owned channel promotion: if you already have an app product or plan to build one, use the interfaces provided by DingTalk to generate offline promotion QR Codes and promote the product through your own channels or channels outside DingTalk (for example, external ad campaigns or third-party official websites). Customers can install the app by scanning the QR Code or clicking the link.

### 2 Publish to the DingTalk Cool App Marketplace

- Join the DingTalk partner organization, search for "Cool App listing application" in OA Approval, complete the required materials in the form, and submit for review. DingTalk Open Platform operators review the request. Once approved, the listed app appears in the Cool App Marketplace.
