In Yida, a liaison acts as a department owner responsible for handling specific tasks within a business process. Liaisons typically serve in key roles such as approver or operator. You can configure liaisons through the permission management scope to streamline the management and execution of business processes and improve efficiency. This article explains how to configure a liaison.

## Procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [Yida workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform) as an Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click **Platform management** in the upper-right corner to open the platform management page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Steps>
  <Step title="In the left-side navigation pane, click Role management, select Yida liaison, and then click Add liaison.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the Add liaison dialog box, enter the name, maintainer, and description, and then click Confirm to create the liaison.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

| **Attribute** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name of the liaison. |
| **Maintainer** | The manager of the liaison, who can view, modify, and delete liaison members. |
| **Description** | Information that describes the purpose or other functions of the liaison. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the liaison you just created and click Add user.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the Add liaison dialog box, set the Liaison and Owning department, and then click Confirm to add the user.">
  </Step>
</Steps>
