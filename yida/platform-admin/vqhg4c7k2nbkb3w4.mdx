This topic describes how the integration works, which editions support it, and how to configure and manage the shared storage capacity for your organization.

| Feature | Free plan | Basic edition | Professional edition (Premium) | Dedicated edition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YiDA integrated with DingTalk organization storage | Not supported | Not supported | Supported | Supported |

<Note>
The DingTalk base version must be Professional edition or Dedicated edition.

- YiDA orders placed through the Alibaba Cloud platform do not currently support self-service activation. The self-service activation entry is hidden by default. Contact your account manager to convert and upgrade the order manually (Alibaba Cloud orders cannot be linked).
- If you have purchased a YiDA attachment capacity package separately, the self-service activation entry is hidden by default. Contact your account manager for manual activation.
- After the integration with organization storage is enabled, any future capacity expansion must be ordered through the organization storage side.
</Note>

## Introduction

Customers who have DingTalk Professional edition and have purchased YiDA Professional edition or Dedicated edition on the DingTalk platform are entitled to three key benefits, including the upgrade to share the 1 TB extra-large organization storage capacity granted with DingTalk Professional edition.

|---|---|
| After upgrading to integrated organization storage | **Entry visibility:**<br />• After YiDA is integrated with DingTalk organization storage capacity, both **Super Admins and Sub-Admins** of the organization can see the **Go to organization storage management** entry.<br />Attachment capacity meaning:<br />• Numerator: The organization's **real-time** total attachment usage.<br />**Note**: After the upgrade, the numerator may be slightly smaller than the value before the upgrade because some statistical system optimizations were applied during synchronization.<br />• Denominator: The shared organization storage (including the 1 TB space granted with the Professional edition).<br />**Configure storage capacity in the DingTalk Admin Console:**<br />• Go to [DingTalk Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > **Organization data** > **Organization storage** to view and modify the storage capacity allocated to YiDA.<br />**Purchased plans:**<br />• View historical attachment order details.<br />**Dedicated capacity:**<br />• Configure dedicated organization storage capacity for YiDA. Once configured, this portion of the capacity is used exclusively by YiDA. |
