The Order Center is a YiDA feature built for app service providers to view app sales. Use it to review historical sales orders for your apps. Orders support fuzzy search by organization name and app name.

<Note>
Only YiDA certified service providers can use this feature.
</Note>

## Procedure

Follow these steps to view historical app sales orders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [YiDA Workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click the **Platform Management** button in the upper-right corner to open the platform management page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3">
    In the left-side menu, select **Service Provider** > **Order Center** to open the Order Center.
    In the Order Center, you can view details such as **Purchasing Organization Name**, **Purchased App Name**, **Operator**, **Order Time**, **App Delivery Status**, **Page Delivery Status**, **Edit Permission**, **Order Details**, and **Remote Maintenance**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When there are many orders, use one of the following two methods to quickly locate a specific order. Search by operator is not currently supported.

- Search by purchasing organization name

In the search box, select the purchasing organization name field and enter the organization name to quickly locate the corresponding order.

- Search by purchased app name

In the search box, select the purchased app name field and enter the app name to quickly locate the corresponding order.
