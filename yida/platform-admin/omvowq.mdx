YiDA platform permissions management is available only to the organization's Super Admin and Sub Admins. It is used primarily to manage platform Sub Admins and App Admins.

## Manage Contacts permissions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [YiDA workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform) as an Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click the **platform management** Button in the upper-right corner to open the platform management page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

3. In the left-hand menu, click Platform Permissions Management, then decide whether to enable the following two permissions.

<Note>
The contact visibility scope for Organization Users on the YiDA platform is synced by default from the DingTalk Contacts visibility scope. To adjust the visibility scope, sign in to the DingTalk admin console and make the changes there. For details, see [How to configure Contacts management permissions](https://alidocs.dingtalk.com/i/p/Y7kmbokZp3pgGLq2/docs/2X3LRMZdxkAJpdaDzrykJGgrBYeOq5Ew).
</Note>

To revoke the App Admin permission from a User, click the **Remove User** Button next to that User.

<Note>
To grant App Admin permissions to Users in the Organization who already have YiDA access—encouraging full participation in YiDA App development—you can also enable auto Authorization on the page. Once auto Authorization is On, every Organization User with YiDA access is granted App Admin permissions.

</Note>

## Manage platform Admins

Follow the steps below to manage platform Admins.

1. Go to the Platform Permissions Management page and click Platform Admins.
2. Click Add User, select the corresponding User, then click Confirm to complete the platform Admin setup.

<Note>
DingTalk Organization admins are automatically synced as YiDA platform Admins and can manage the YiDA platform. To change this Configuration, go to the [DingTalk admin console](http://oa.dingtalk.io) and Undo their YiDA-related Permissions.
</Note>

## FAQ

**Q: Why do new Users see only one Admin when applying for YiDA Permissions, even though the Permissions center shows that the Organization has multiple Admins?**

A: To avoid disturbing Organization executives, platform Admins are shown first when selecting an Admin. Organization admins appear only when no platform Admin exists.

**Q: Why don't I have YiDA Permissions even though I'm the Super Admin or Sub Admin of the DingTalk Contacts?**

A: Check whether YiDA platform Admins have been set within the YiDA App.

- If YiDA platform Admins have been set, YiDA platform Admin permissions take precedence.
- If no YiDA platform Admin has been set, YiDA automatically inherits the Super Admin and Sub Admins from the DingTalk Organization Contacts.
