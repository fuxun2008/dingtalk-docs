The Yida organization performance module helps you better understand the benefits of enterprise digitalization. It compares the industry average of your organization's sector with your organization's actual usage of Yida, and then forecasts the resulting cost reduction and efficiency gains. The data is for reference only. This article describes the metrics available in organization performance.

## View organization performance data

To view organization performance data, follow these steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1">
    Sign in to the [Yida workbench](https://www.yidaapps.com/workPlatform).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2">
    Click the (**Platform management**) button in the upper-right corner, and then click **Organization performance**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Performance data overview

The organization performance overview shows the total app development budget and development time that Yida has saved for your organization. This data is calculated based on the average usage across the low-code industry and is for reference only.

## User learning outcomes

In this section, you can view the number of Users in your Organization who have completed low-code training, along with the number of certified low-code developers. You can also click to notify Users to complete training and certification.

<Note>
- Only data from before 24:00 the previous day is shown. Users who complete training and certification on the current day are counted starting the next day.
- The number of certified low-code developers includes all Users in your Organization who hold a basic, intermediate, or advanced certification.
- Certifications are tied to individual Users in your Organization. If a User has completed certification but is not reflected in the data, click the Smart Service Assistant in the lower-right corner of the page for help.
</Note>

## Performance data

In the performance data section, you can review your Organization's usage of Yida, including the weekly daily-average UV, number of apps, number of workbench apps, number of custom-built apps, number of template apps, number of app creators, and cumulative form instances.

<Note>
- Performance data reflects the level of digitalization your Organization has achieved with low-code products.
- Some latency in the statistics for each performance metric is normal.
- The industry reference value refers to the corresponding metric for Organizations of a similar workforce size, and is for reference only.
</Note>

<Note>
Yida Dedicated edition also supports viewing details for each performance metric. For more information, see [Yida Dedicated](/yida/exclusive/rzpggcnrelq9iyku).
</Note>
