## Langkah-langkah

Buka aplikasi desktop DingTalk > klik Email di bilah sisi kiri > klik ikon <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01ouT8VhDeJ7I04WyO_!!6000000000868-2-tps-36-28.png" alt="imag.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} /> di sudut kanan atas > Kontak Email > klik ... di samping Kontak > Grup baru.
