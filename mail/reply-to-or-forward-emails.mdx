---
title: "Reply to or Forward Emails"
description: "Open an email on mobile or desktop and choose Reply or Forward to respond quickly or pass the message on to other recipients."
---

DingTalk Mail supports quick email replies and forwarding on both desktop and mobile, making it easier to handle email anytime, anywhere.

## Steps

### Mobile

Open the DingTalk mobile app > More > Mail > tap the email you want to manage > tap Reply or Forward at the bottom.

### Desktop

Open the DingTalk desktop app > click Mail in the left sidebar > open the email you want to manage > click ... in the upper-right corner > choose Reply or Forward.
