---
title: "Set Up Automatic Email Replies"
description: "Configure a vacation auto-reply on the DingTalk desktop app so DingTalk Mail responds automatically while you are away, with custom content and active dates."
---

DingTalk Mail on desktop supports automatic email replies.

## Steps

Open the DingTalk desktop app > click Mail in the left sidebar > click Settings in the lower-left corner > Vacation reply settings.
