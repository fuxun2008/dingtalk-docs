Mail calendar on desktop supports exporting calendars as PDF files.

## Steps

Open the DingTalk desktop app > click Mail in the left sidebar > click the ![imag.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/a/RyGqoJqeOT9Aj7mb/1f6f1ab52bcd4ec0a73302765f2eba640521.png)icon in the upper-right corner > Mail Calendar > click the![imag.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/a/6EKGbX5oPIe8y0g2/c4af2ddb2f7148ac90f6bd93b9e1f76d0521.png)icon in the upper-right corner > Export PDF calendar.
