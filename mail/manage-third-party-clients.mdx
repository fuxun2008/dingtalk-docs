Email administrators can control whether members can access their DingTalk Enterprise Mail accounts and send emails through third-party clients.

## What Are Third-Party Clients?

Third-party clients are software applications developed by third parties for managing and using email. They typically offer a range of email management features and customization options to meet different user needs.

Common third-party email clients include Outlook, Foxmail, Apple Mail, and the built-in email apps on mobile phones. These clients typically connect to different email service providers using SMTP, IMAP, or POP, allowing users to manage emails from multiple accounts in one place.

<Info>
To help protect DingTalk Enterprise Mail accounts, **Allow Third-Party Clients** is turned off by default. This means that signing in through third-party clients is not allowed by default. You can turn this setting on or off to meet your business needs.
</Info>

## Procedure

Follow the steps below to enable and configure **Allow Third-Party Clients**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Third-party Client Management">
    Sign in to the DingTalk admin console as an administrator. Go to **App Management > Mail > Security Policy > Third-party Client Management**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01PLOaylOe67B2pEDf_!!6000000008120-2-tps-1392-671.png" alt="Third-party Client Management under Security Policy" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Allow Third-Party Clients">
    Turn on **Allow Third-Party Clients**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01P0A2UkZhnxK35LDn_!!6000000002276-2-tps-1520-707.png" alt="Turn on Allow Third-Party Clients" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the application scope">
    Under **Application Scope**, select the option that meets your needs.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="All">
        You can add exceptions in the **Add Exception** section. Click **Add from Contacts** and select the accounts to exclude from the organizational structure or email contacts. These accounts will not be able to sign in to DingTalk Enterprise Mail through third-party clients.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Department and Account Only">
        Click **Add from Contacts** and select the accounts that are allowed to use third-party clients from the organizational structure or email contacts.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01MesQEtNssBC38jzx_!!6000000003699-2-tps-1547-906.png" alt="Select the application scope and add accounts from contacts" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the settings">
    Choose whether to select **Include sub-departments when adding a department**, then click **Save**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01M6v1747XDOK2qzU1_!!6000000007297-2-tps-1406-667.png" alt="Include sub-departments when adding a department and save" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
