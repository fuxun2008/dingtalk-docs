---
title: "Add Labels to Emails"
description: "Create custom labels on the desktop app to categorize emails by project, customer, priority, or any other dimension that helps you filter and find them later."
---

DingTalk Mail supports email labels, helping you categorize different types of emails.

## Steps

Open the DingTalk desktop app > click Mail in the left sidebar > click the + icon next to Labels in the lower-left area > New label > enter a custom label name in the pop-up input box.
