---
title: "Freeze or Unfreeze a DingTalk Enterprise Mail Account"
description: "Learn how email administrators freeze or unfreeze a member's DingTalk Enterprise Mail account by toggling Account Frozen in the Enterprise Email advanced settings as business needs change."
---

Freeze or unfreeze a DingTalk Enterprise Mail account as your business needs change. Email administrators can follow these steps to update the settings for the relevant account.

## Procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member's information editor">
    Sign in to the DingTalk admin console with a DingTalk Enterprise Mail administrator account. Go to **Directory > Members**. For the member whose account you want to update, click **More > Edit information**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01xbHFjsbuonK2yANz_!!6000000004699-2-tps-1463-816.png" alt="Click More > Edit information for a member" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Advanced Settings">
    Under **Enterprise Email**, open **Advanced Settings**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01bWOgMC9L6YL38UOv_!!6000000007544-2-tps-1545-952.png" alt="Advanced Settings below the Enterprise Email field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn Account Frozen on or off">
    Turn **Account Frozen** on to freeze the account, or off to unfreeze it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01bDVzU25QJMJ1At0d_!!6000000001881-2-tps-579-496.png" alt="Account Frozen toggle in account settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
