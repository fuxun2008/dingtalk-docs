---
title: "Import or Export Contacts"
description: "Import contacts in CSV or vCard format into Mail Contacts, or export your existing contacts for backup or migration to another mail client."
---

## Steps

Open the DingTalk desktop app > click Mail in the left sidebar > click the<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01ouT8VhDeJ7I04WyO_!!6000000000868-2-tps-36-28.png" alt="imag.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />icon in the upper-right corner > Mail Contacts > click ... next to Contacts > Import contacts or Export contacts.
