---
title: "Assign Enterprise Email Accounts to Members"
description: "Learn how admins manually assign DingTalk Enterprise Mail accounts to team members from Directory > Members, handle reassignment prompts, and configure account settings and aliases."
---

After inviting members to join the team, admins can assign them DingTalk Enterprise Mail accounts.

<Info>
DingTalk Enterprise Mail accounts can be created and assigned in two ways:

- Manually through **Directory > Members** in the DingTalk admin console. This article explains this method.
- Automatically in bulk by the system, based on rules configured in mail management. For details, see [Bulk Account Assignment](/mail/bulk-assign-email-accounts).
</Info>

## Prerequisites

Sign in to the admin console with an administrator account or a sub-administrator account with the required permissions, then go to **App Management > Mail**. Verify domain ownership before assigning accounts.

<Tip>
If you do not want to verify domain ownership yet, click **Trial Domain** at the bottom of the page to switch to a trial domain and continue.
</Tip>

## Procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member's information editor">
    Sign in to the DingTalk admin console as an admin and go to **Directory > Members**. Find the member who needs a DingTalk Enterprise Mail account. The **Enterprise Email** field initially shows **Unassigned**. Open the action menu on the right and click **Edit information**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01HE0BdZAxKwE3RsJ7_!!6000000001204-2-tps-1699-926.png" alt="Click Edit information for a member in Directory > Members" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the email prefix and confirm">
    Enter the email address prefix (the part before "@") in **Enterprise Email**. Check that the information is correct, then click **OK** to create the DingTalk Enterprise Mail account.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01bCQpqYWK19K0zBDS_!!6000000005608-2-tps-486-640.png" alt="Enter the Enterprise Email prefix" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
    - If the email account is already assigned to another employee, a message appears: "This email account is already assigned to employee (name). Reassign it to the current member?" To reassign it, click **OK**. The data in the mailbox is retained, and the email account is linked to the new DingTalk account.
    - If the email account is currently used as a shared mailbox, a message appears: "This email account is currently used as a shared mailbox. Reassign it to the current member?" To reassign it, click **OK**. Members who previously used the shared mailbox can no longer access it. The data in the mailbox is retained, and the email account is linked to the new DingTalk account.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The employee receives a work notification">
    Once the account is created, the employee receives a work notification in DingTalk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the new email address">
    On the **Members** page, admins can view the newly created DingTalk Enterprise Mail address in **Enterprise Email**.

    <Note>
    For an account with a DingTalk Enterprise Mail address activated for the first time, refresh the entire page to display the address.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Configure advanced settings">
    For members whose DingTalk Enterprise Mail accounts are already activated, admins can click **Advanced Settings** below **Enterprise Email** on the member information page to configure account settings and aliases.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01bWOgMC9L6YL38UOv_!!6000000007544-2-tps-1545-952.png" alt="Advanced Settings below the Enterprise Email field" />
    </Frame>
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01BxChh2mtqlG1BGQK_!!6000000003009-2-tps-582-498.png" alt="Account settings and alias settings in Advanced Settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
