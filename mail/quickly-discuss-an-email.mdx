---
title: "Quickly Discuss an Email"
description: "Forward an email from DingTalk Mail on mobile to a chat or to its participants, so you can discuss the message instantly in DingTalk."
---

In DingTalk Mail on mobile, you can quickly start a chat from an email and discuss it with email participants at any time.

## Steps

Open the DingTalk mobile app > More > Mail > tap the email you want to discuss > tap ... in the lower-right corner > Forward to chat > forward it to a group or select email participants.
