Both DingTalk mobile and desktop support moving and deleting emails. You can delete invalid or unnecessary emails at any time.

## Steps

### Mobile

Open the DingTalk mobile app > More > Mail > long press and select the emails you want to manage > tap Move to or Delete at the bottom.

### Desktop

Open the DingTalk desktop app > click Mail in the left sidebar > select the emails you want to manage > click the Move or Delete icon at the bottom.
