---
title: "Create a Contact Group"
description: "Group your Mail Contacts on the DingTalk desktop app so you can send to multiple recipients at once and keep contacts organized by team, project, or relationship."
---

## Steps

Open the DingTalk desktop app > click Mail in the left sidebar > click the![imag.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/a/RyGqoJqeOT9Aj7mb/1f6f1ab52bcd4ec0a73302765f2eba640521.png) icon in the upper-right corner > Mail Contacts > click ... next to Contacts > New group.
