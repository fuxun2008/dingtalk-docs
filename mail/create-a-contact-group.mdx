---
title: "Create a Contact Group"
description: "Group your Mail Contacts on the DingTalk desktop app so you can send to multiple recipients at once and keep contacts organized by team, project, or relationship."
---

## Steps

Open the DingTalk desktop app > click Mail in the left sidebar > click the<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01ouT8VhDeJ7I04WyO_!!6000000000868-2-tps-36-28.png" alt="imag.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} /> icon in the upper-right corner > Mail Contacts > click ... next to Contacts > New group.
