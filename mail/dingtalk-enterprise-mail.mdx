DingTalk Enterprise Mail is a native enterprise email product built on DingTalk. It works with many DingTalk apps as part of a collaborative office suite, helping users work more efficiently.

DingTalk Enterprise Mail provides secure, high-performance, and highly stable email infrastructure. It is designed with high-volume throughput, load balancing, dynamic resource scheduling and scaling, and multi-copy redundant data storage.

| One-click migration of dingtalk.com mailbox data | Supports one-click migration of historical emails from a personal dingtalk.com mailbox. Future received emails can also be synced to DingTalk Enterprise Mail. |

| Bulk send with individual display | When sending bulk emails, each recipient receives an individual email, which feels more personal and protects recipient email addresses. |
