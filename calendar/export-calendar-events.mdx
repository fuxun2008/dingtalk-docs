### Calendar events can be exported

The creator can export events from DingTalk on desktop by going to **Calendar > My**, clicking **...** on the right, selecting **Export**, choosing a time range, and selecting an export method. Events can be exported to AI Sheet or online sheet, or downloaded locally.
