## Mobile

Open DingTalk on mobile, go to **Calendar**, tap the search icon in the upper-right corner, and enter event or meeting keywords to search quickly.

## Desktop

Open DingTalk on desktop, go to **Calendar**, click the search icon in the upper-left corner, and enter event or meeting keywords to search quickly.
