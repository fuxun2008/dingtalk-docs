DingTalk Calendar is a time management tool that connects chats, mail, and To Do. It helps reduce communication costs and makes team collaboration smoother and more efficient.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4jKqm0by9a1kbnw1/img/a2455749-067e-47c5-8689-3be2a0b4d439.png)

## Precise event and meeting notifications

Create meeting events with one click. Invitations are sent directly to each participant in chat, so everyone receives the information reliably.

## Automatically create a meeting group

Events can automatically generate a meeting group, making organization, communication, and collaboration before and after the meeting more efficient.

## Multiple calendar views

In addition to day, week, and month views, DingTalk Calendar also provides a three-day view that is better suited for mobile. This makes it easier and more efficient to view schedules and plan time.
