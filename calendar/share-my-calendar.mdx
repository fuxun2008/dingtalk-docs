---
title: "Share My Calendar"
description: "Share your calendar with teammates so they see your availability—choose view-only or grant create / edit rights from Calendar Settings on mobile or desktop to avoid interruptions."
---

DingTalk Calendar supports calendar sharing. You can share your calendar with teammates or project members for daily collaboration. Shared users can view your availability by default, helping you avoid interruptions during meetings or breaks. If you grant create and edit permissions, they can also add important events for you.

## Instructions

### Mobile

Go to **DingTalk Calendar**, tap ... in the upper-right corner, open **My > My Calendars**, tap..., tap **Share My calendar**, add the people you want to share with, and set the permission scope.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01zj9TrofcU9C3NDX0_!!6000000003937-2-tps-1662-908.png)

### Desktop

Go to **DingTalk Calendar**, find **My Calendars** on the left, click ..., choose **Set sharing permissions**, add the people you want to share with, and set the permission scope.
