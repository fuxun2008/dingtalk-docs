Subscribed calendars support color changes. You can update calendar colors with one click based on your preferences, making your calendar easier to read and more visually organized.

## Instructions

### Desktop

Go to Calendar from the left navigation bar on desktop. In **My Calendars**, find the target calendar, click the **...** icon next to it, choose **Change color**, and select the color you want.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/3BMqYyb9DjpGEqwZ/img/8f8d27d6-6516-4470-807b-1f60f560d6f1.png)

The calendar will display with the new color.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/3BMqYyb9DjpGEqwZ/img/c4f67ee1-f0e2-4aa6-a8e4-69e1921d5a1b.png)

Open DingTalk on mobile,tap **Calendar / Schedule** at the top, tap **...** in the upper-right corner, go to **My Calendars**, tap **...** next to the target calendar, tap **Change color**, select the color you want, and tap **OK**. The new color will take effect.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/3BMqYyb9DjpGEqwZ/img/62f5c131-2000-484a-98a8-52eaca0e7a1f.png)
