---
title: "Change Calendar Colors"
description: "Update the display color of a subscribed calendar on desktop or mobile—open My Calendars, tap the … icon next to a calendar, choose Change color, and pick the color you want."
---

Subscribed calendars support color changes. You can update calendar colors with one click based on your preferences, making your calendar easier to read and more visually organized.

## Instructions

### Desktop

Go to Calendar from the left navigation bar on desktop. In **My Calendars**, find the target calendar, click the ... icon next to it, choose **Change color**, and select the color you want.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01W2NHDUHLwiE5VtjE_!!6000000006678-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

The calendar will display with the new color.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN014S9WeLGwrBF5VtjE_!!6000000000639-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

Open DingTalk on mobile,tap **Calendar**, tap ... in the upper-right corner, go to **My Calendars**, tap ... next to the target calendar, tap **Change color**, select the color you want, and tap **OK**. The new color will take effect.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01nZl4CD8VqNE3fb0w_!!6000000006478-2-tps-1808-934.png)
