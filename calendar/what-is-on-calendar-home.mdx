## Detailed operations

Please check out the actions supported on the Calendar home page!

● Quickly Create a New Event:

You can click the blue plus sign (+) to the right of the input box and select "New Event."

● View My Calendar:

You can toggle the checkboxes to display "My Calendar".

● Calendar Settings

Tap to access your personalized settings.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ABmOoWbNDa92WOaw/img/1bf3b04c-50f3-4ed4-82bd-bb82413f9ff7.png)

● Calendar Views

DingTalk Calendar supports List View, Day View, Week View, and Month View.

It also includes a 3-Day View on mobile \[Right Arrow\].

![日历4.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ABmOoWbNDa92WOaw/img/3ef2eac5-6998-4ed0-9c65-85c80111e339.png)

● View Meeting Minutes

Tap to quickly access meeting minutes.

● Search Events

You can enter keywords in the search bar to quickly find the event information you need.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ABmOoWbNDa92WOaw/img/97363efb-4520-4ce4-bc4d-086cc5a42bb0.png)
