## Switch calendar views with one click

To meet different viewing habits, DingTalk Calendar supports multiple views that you can switch between with one click.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN0194BT4NFncVH5VtjE_!!6000000004331-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

## Desktop

### List view

Shows more events in a list so you can scan schedules at a glance.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01BKEWBE6G4kI5VtjE_!!6000000005497-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

### Day view

Shows the day's schedule on a timeline, with each event mapped to a specific time slot.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01IicQ0y1GH8C5VtjE_!!6000000003456-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

### Week view

Shows one week of events on one screen, making it easy to plan the work week.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01Dwo7NHY2GCH5VtjE_!!6000000004417-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

### Month view

Shows the full month's plan on one screen, so you can preview important items anytime.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01XFb2XfnNzeL5VtjE_!!6000000007888-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

## Mobile

On mobile, Calendar also provides a three-day view that is easier to read on smaller screens.

Go to Calendar, tap ... in the upper-right corner, and select a view.

## Customize the first day of the week

You can customize the **First day of week** setting in Calendar view to match different usage habits.

## Desktop

Click **Calendar** in the left navigation bar, click **Calendar Settings** in the lower-left corner, go to **View**, and select **First day of week**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN013YxRtFQMf1L5VtjE_!!6000000004564-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

## Mobile

Go to **Chats**, tap **Calendar / Schedule** at the top, tap ... in the upper-right corner, select **Calendar Settings**, scroll down, and tap **First day of week** to customize it.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01JsKaIWfHIpG3iMiU_!!6000000007103-2-tps-1830-936.png)

## Set a secondary time zone

Calendar Home supports multiple secondary time zones, making cross-border collaboration easier to view.

## Setup path

On desktop, click **Calendar** in the left navigation bar, open **Calendar Settings**, go to **View**, and enable **Show secondary time zone**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01Dkvnu6GtXcG5VtjE_!!6000000005792-2-tps-2716-1356.png)

## View the result

You can view different time zones in **Day** or **Week** view.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01tTMHcYFiOXG5VtjE_!!6000000005643-2-tps-2716-1356.png)
