After setting up DingTalk Calendar, you can view upcoming events on both mobile and desktop, making work arrangements more organized.

## How to access Calendar

## On mobile

Open DingTalk on mobile, Open the DingTalk mobile app and tap the second icon in the bottom menu,  tap **Calendar**.

## On desktop

Click **Calendar** in the left navigation bar.
