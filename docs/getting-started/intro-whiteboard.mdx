## 1. Getting started with DingTalk Whiteboard

DingTalk Whiteboard is an online collaborative whiteboard that supports real-time editing by multiple users. Built on a boundless, freely scalable canvas, it lets every collaborator create together using **brushes, text, shapes, images, and many other elements**, **breaking the barriers of time and space and sparking the collision of ideas**. It also supports the full workflow from brainstorming to content management, building an **inspiration space** for your team.

### Rich elements, unlimited creation

The built-in templates in DingTalk Whiteboard offer a wide range of options, letting you reuse industry best practices with a single click and boost your team's creation and collaboration efficiency. In addition, rich elements such as brushes, text, shapes, images, notes, and audio/video bring every creative idea to life on the whiteboard.

### Spark inspiration, collaborate without limits

DingTalk Whiteboard supports real-time online collaboration for 50+ users. Every collaborator can brainstorm, create, or share suggestions through comments, greatly improving team collaboration efficiency and engagement.

### Optimized for iPad, paperless office

The moment inspiration strikes, pick up your iPad and capture it. With a stylus, you can effortlessly express creative ideas with both visuals and text. Multi-device data sync ensures that the collision of ideas is never limited — record your thoughts anytime, anywhere.

### What's the difference from the legacy whiteboard?

|  | **Before: legacy whiteboard** | **After: new whiteboard** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Boundless canvas** | The canvas resembled a blackboard, supported only three pages, and had limited drawing and expression capabilities. | Breaks away from the traditional "blackboard" canvas. The **infinite canvas** supports **multiple subject** color schemes for a fresher visual experience. |
| **Flexible editing** | Supported only three basic editing functions: brush, shape, and text — limited creative capabilities. | Adds more editing capabilities, including **brushes, text, shapes, images, notes**, and more — enabling more flexible creation. |
| **Rich elements** | Limited shapes and line assets with few color options — making expression less vivid. | A **far richer variety** of lines, shapes, and colors, plus **beautiful templates** you can reuse with one click — letting you express your ideas more fully and vividly. |
| **Multi-user collaboration** | Only supported creation on the same canvas, with no interactive exchange. | Supports synchronized online collaboration for **50+ users**, with instant **comments and interaction** for easier exchange of ideas. |

### How to start creating

**Desktop entry**: Open DingTalk, click **Docs** in the left navigation, then click **Create** and select **Whiteboard**.

**iPad**: Open DingTalk, tap **More** > **Docs**, tap the blue **+** at the bottom, and select **Whiteboard**.

## 2. Basic operations in DingTalk Whiteboard

### 🎨 Creation features

Select an element to edit it: change color, resize, move, modify attributes, and more.

Drag to move or zoom the canvas.

With the brush and eraser, you can freely draw on the canvas, and drag to adjust line size.

✅ Take notes and annotations

✅ Highlight key points during discussions

✅ Draw fun content

Insert a Text Box to enter text, and freely configure its border, background, Font Size, color, and more.

Insert a variety of shapes. The shape library includes basic shapes, mathematical symbols, callouts, buttons, and more — making it easy to create flowcharts, business architecture diagrams, milestones, and other common charts.

Insert connectors to create associations between basic shapes. Switch between different arrow styles and precisely adjust the connector path.

Insert images and flexibly resize them, fill colors, change border styles, and more.

Insert emoji icons to highlight important information and make canvas content more lively and engaging.

Insert frames. A frame can be thought of as a "shot" or "scene" — select canvas elements to form a frame. In presentation mode, each frame corresponds to one slide of the presentation.

### 🙌 Collaboration features

*   **Real-time collaborative editing**

Supports real-time online collaboration for 50+ users. Every collaborator can brainstorm, create, comment, and interact, using tools such as brushes, text, and icons to mark up content and express ideas.

*   **Interaction and comments**

Whether in a Video Meeting or an online discussion, sharing a DingTalk Whiteboard bridges the distance — bringing the meeting room whiteboard online. It lets users communicate freely, accelerate decision-making, and collaborate more efficiently.

### 🔒 Manage permissions

Just like other DingTalk Docs, click **…** > **Manage permissions** in the upper-right corner of the whiteboard to set user permissions. Four permission types are supported: **Can edit, Can view/download, View only, and Hide chat permission**.

### 🌠 Export and download

Click **…** > **Download to local** in the upper-right corner of the whiteboard to export the whiteboard content as an image.

### 🌟 Presentation feature

*   Click **Present** in the upper-right corner, and all frames on the canvas will be presented in the order they were inserted.

*   To adjust the order, click the arrow next to the **Present** button to view the presentation path, and drag with the mouse to reorder.

## 3. Use cases

### Organization training

With DingTalk Whiteboard, you can launch organization training or an online team-building session anytime, anywhere. The boundless canvas with unlimited pages, combined with fun content elements and interactive features, sparks user engagement and enthusiasm even across screens. Real-time collaboration and interaction add a layer of fun to your training.

### Brainstorming

Online whiteboards make it effortless to run productive brainstorming sessions, inspiring team members to generate more ideas. Real-time multi-user collaboration sparks the collision of ideas, while inline comments let participants share suggestions instantly — keeping the brainstorming process organized.

**Meetings and proposal discussions**

The new whiteboard makes online meetings far from boring. Post comments in real time to share thoughts on proposal details, and add emoji to give whiteboard content attitude and warmth. When presenting a proposal, use one-click presentation to instantly turn the whiteboard into a slide deck — vividly showcasing content, focusing attendees' attention, and driving proposals to completion more efficiently.

**Employee office relocation seating chart**

When the company moves and the seating chart is confusing, how do you find your desk? DingTalk Whiteboard makes it easy. It supports real-time multi-user collaboration, lets you insert various lines, background colors, notes, and more. Team members can collaborate online to draw the desk layout — fun and lively.

**Time capsule**

DingTalk Whiteboard supports inserting images. User wishes and team photos can be added to the whiteboard to capture unforgettable moments. In presentation mode, you can customize the presentation path and logic frame by frame — turning a static whiteboard into a dynamic video and bringing personal and team creativity to life.
