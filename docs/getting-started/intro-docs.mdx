## Overview | What is DingTalk Docs

DingTalk Docs is an enterprise collaboration suite developed by **Alibaba Group**'s DingTalk. It includes online creation and document management tools such as **Docs, Spreadsheets, Mind Maps, AI Tables, Whiteboards, Slides, and Knowledge Base**. No need to download documents—edit and view directly on your computer, phone, or tablet with real-time saving. DingTalk Docs is **deeply integrated with DingTalk**, enabling efficient collaboration with colleagues based on your organizational structure. Daily work scenarios such as **information collection, project management, meetings, business reporting, and onboarding** can all be completed efficiently with DingTalk Docs.

## 📝 Docs

Docs supports real-time collaborative editing by multiple users, helping you quickly complete content creation and proposal discussions. Docs also lets you insert images, tables, videos, locations, and more. Deeply integrated with DingTalk, you can insert business cards, projects, to-dos, and group chats into your documents. **Connect everything intelligently and bring your documents to life.**

*   Insert rich content such as images, videos, files, business cards, Amap locations, and Youku videos to gather all information in one place, with smart dynamic updates that bring your documents to life.

*   @ colleagues within a document to alert them for a quick review.

*   For action items generated during meetings, insert "To-Do" items in the document to remind colleagues to complete them.

*   Insert project Kanban boards into documents to visualize project progress.

*   Comment directly on the entire document or specific paragraphs to handle most details before meetings.

*   Enable presentation mode with one click to turn your document into a slideshow instantly.

*   Generate long images for sharing—turn your document into a poster within minutes for more efficient content distribution.

*   Voice notes capture the entire meeting discussion in real time and convert it to text for easy minute compilation.

## 📊 Spreadsheet

Spreadsheet also supports real-time collaborative editing by multiple users. Powerful formula editing and chart drawing capabilities enable your team to collaboratively complete the entire process of data collection, analysis, and presentation within DingTalk.

*   Full formula and function capabilities for better business data analysis.

*   Supports common column, line, and pie charts—simple configuration makes data presentation more intuitive.

*   Independent filter views ensure that multiple users can filter data without interfering with each other, enabling personalized data customization.

*   Lock cells so only specified users can view or edit them, while others are blocked from viewing, ensuring data privacy and security.

*   Link to addresses, cells, or worksheets—open shortcuts in your spreadsheet to reach target data with one click.

*   Use zebra stripes to instantly apply table styles and make your data visually appealing.

*   Highlight duplicates to quickly spot repeated entries and reduce duplicate input.

*   Insert checkboxes, dropdown lists, and date-times with one click, combined with Conditional Formatting, to easily build task lists and track task statuses.

## 🐙 Mind Map

Mind Map is an effective graphical thinking tool developed by DingTalk for expressing divergent thinking. It is both practical and efficient for learning, life, and work. Also known as mind maps or thinking maps.

*   Supports multi-user collaborative editing.

*   Switch between outline notes and mind maps with one click to organize structure faster.

## 👨💻 Knowledge Base

Knowledge Base is a professional and efficient enterprise knowledge management platform for building corporate knowledge repositories and internal document collaboration. With simple and convenient operation, after the Admin adds users, multiple people can share content and sync in real time. Team members can record all internally produced collaborative content here.

Whether you're a small team or a large enterprise, create a Knowledge Base, add team members, and a group of people can collaborate to build the knowledge base and accumulate organizational knowledge:

*   Writing project documents and product requirement documents.

*   Team task management and knowledge accumulation.

*   Company rules, regulations, and processes.

*   Creating help manuals, product instructions, and API guides.

*   Asynchronous communication within teams.
