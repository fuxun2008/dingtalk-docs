## [Magic wand] Docs home

---
<Note>
## Knowledge Base supports batch download | Say goodbye to repeated downloads and archive files in a snap!

Knowledge Base now supports batch download. Download multiple files with one click and skip the tedious repetitive steps for easier, more efficient knowledge management.

**😊** **Recommended scenarios:**

Project archiving: Download all related documents at once, including reports, designs, code, and Meeting Notes, for archiving and backup.

Compliance audit: Batch download contracts, policy documents, and audit reports to support compliance reviews and record keeping.

Customer service team: Batch download product FAQs, troubleshooting guides, and user feedback to respond quickly to customer needs and improve service quality.

---

## Mobile Group Knowledge Base supports activity notifications | Stay informed on the go!

Knowledge Base Admins can enable automatic syncing of Knowledge Base activity to Group Chats from mobile. Document creation, updates, and Comments are pushed instantly, so team members always receive the latest information in real time and team alignment is improved.

**😊** **Recommended scenarios:**

Business travel: Even without your computer on the road, configure Knowledge Base activity notifications and stay updated on every change instantly.

Sales representatives: When visiting clients in the field, enable Group Knowledge Base activity notifications on mobile to ensure clients always get the most accurate information.

Human resources: Turn it on anytime during job fairs or external Meetings to receive real-time feedback from internal Employees and policy document updates, keeping processes running smoothly.

</Note>
[Bulb] Available on: Android / iOS / Mac / Windows

[Notebook] How to use:

1. Enable Group Knowledge Base in the group, or bind a Group Chat under Knowledge Base > Premium Settings.

2. Turn on the push toggle under "Push & Contribution List" settings, and configure the push Content and format.

## [Magic wand] Calendar

---
<Note>
## Calendar supports Employee care sync | Smart reminders + AI-tailored wishes for heartfelt care!

Calendar can now sync Employee work anniversaries and birthdays! Anniversaries are automatically reminded three days in advance, so you can plan ahead for Important Dates. AI-generated wishes are also supported—Employees receive unique greetings on their special day.

**😊** **Recommended scenarios:**

Human resources: HR teams can use Calendar sync to track each Employee's work anniversary and birthday, ensuring gifts are prepared and celebrations are arranged in advance.

Project managers: Use this feature to avoid scheduling Important Meetings or deadlines on Employee birthdays or work anniversaries, showing respect for their personal life.

Administrative management: Use Calendar reminders to manage all Important anniversaries across the Company and coordinate celebrations and budgets.

</Note>
[Bulb] Available on: Android / iOS / Mac / Windows

[Notebook] How to use:

Admin: Work anniversary reminders—click here to enable. Birthday reminders—click here to enable.

Employee Users: Calendar Settings > Sync > Sync work anniversaries and birthday wishes (On by default).
