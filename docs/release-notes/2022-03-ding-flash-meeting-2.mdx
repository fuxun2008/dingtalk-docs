## 1. Add log templates to Chat Quick actions

Add frequently used log templates to Chat Quick actions for one-click use, making work reporting faster and more efficient.

## 1. Row and column grouping supported

Spreadsheet data can be grouped on demand and expanded or collapsed across multiple tiers, helping you organize structured data and keep your spreadsheets neat.

### Quick Meeting

## 1. Quickly create agendas and submit topics in advance

Start a Quick Meeting directly from the Create Event entry. When sending the Invitation, you can submit topics in sync and @ the relevant owners, so pre-meeting preparation is more thorough and efficient.

[View the attachment "Quick Meeting: Quickly create agendas and submit topics in advance.mp4" in DingTalk Docs.](https://docs.dingtalk.io/i/nodes/3Pl7jXNw6dBWx9AaP7RyVvKAGqOzY0D9?iframeQuery=anchorId%3DX02l10iuhzdpm2lvqsu4qk)

## 2. Up to ten meeting templates in Quick Meeting for easier onboarding

Quick Meeting provides templates for several common meeting types and supports saving Custom templates. For recurring meetings, you can create a recurring Quick Meeting to help you start every Meeting with ease.

[View the attachment "Quick Meeting: Multiple built-in templates for easier onboarding.mp4" in DingTalk Docs.](https://docs.dingtalk.io/i/nodes/3Pl7jXNw6dBWx9AaP7RyVvKAGqOzY0D9?iframeQuery=anchorId%3DX02l10iuhzde2oivrnxais)

## 3. Three-column Layout for stronger Collaboration and control

Quick Meeting documents now feature a three-column Layout: meeting controls, topics, and minutes. Operate freely and stay focused throughout the meeting.

[View the attachment "Quick Meeting: Three-column layout for stronger collaboration and control.mp4" in DingTalk Docs.](https://docs.dingtalk.io/i/nodes/3Pl7jXNw6dBWx9AaP7RyVvKAGqOzY0D9?iframeQuery=anchorId%3DX02l10iuhzdbkh3ffu5sr9)

## 4. Start a meeting Group Chat to keep communication flowing

Quick Meeting documents support starting a meeting Group Chat, so meetings and communication run in parallel without constant switching. Meeting Content is centralized for easy traceability after the meeting.

[View the attachment "Quick Meeting: Start a meeting group chat to keep communication flowing.mp4" in DingTalk Docs.](https://docs.dingtalk.io/i/nodes/3Pl7jXNw6dBWx9AaP7RyVvKAGqOzY0D9?iframeQuery=anchorId%3DX02l10iuhzd4vh3wrkijty)

## 5. Post-meeting Data Kanban to quantify efficiency gains

Review post-meeting metrics such as time spent on each topic, the number of in-meeting quick notes, and the number of post-meeting To-Dos on the Data Kanban. Efficiency improvements are clear at a glance.

[View the attachment "Quick Meeting: Post-meeting data dashboard to quantify efficiency gains.mp4" in DingTalk Docs.](https://docs.dingtalk.io/i/nodes/3Pl7jXNw6dBWx9AaP7RyVvKAGqOzY0D9?iframeQuery=anchorId%3DX02l10iuhzd7abszpfloao)
