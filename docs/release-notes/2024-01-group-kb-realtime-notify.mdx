**😊** **Recommended scenarios:**

Financial reporting: When organizing monthly income and expense reports, finance staff can easily integrate pasted new Data into fine-grained categories.

Student management: When school administrators paste student information databases, the system automatically expands rows for hassle-free pasting, keeping Data neatly organized.

Scientific experiments: When researchers consolidate experiment results and paste Data, rows are automatically expanded, accurately separating information into each Group.
