## AI PPT now available | Generate professional presentations with a single prompt

AI PPT creation has been added to AI Compose! With just one sentence, AI generates a professional presentation deck (in true .pptx format!). You can also adjust the PPT outline through DingTalk Mind and freely fine-tune every detail, turning inspiration into smooth delivery and ensuring precise execution of your instructions.

💡 Availability: Mac and Windows users

📓 Instructions: On the document home page, click AI Compose — Create PPT, enter your creative prompt, click Create to generate and adjust the outline, then select a template to generate the PPT.

## Background settings supported in text documents | Make your documents more visually appealing

Easily choose a personalized background through "Document style" to enhance the reading experience while showcasing your personal and team identity, making documents more pleasant to read.

📓 Instructions: Click the three-dot menu in the upper-right corner, select Document style, and choose a background.

## Documents now support icons | Visually appealing and easy to recognize

Set icons via "Add icon" above the title, when renaming a document, or when renaming items in the Knowledge Base directory tree, making documents more visually appealing and easier to find.

💡 Availability: Mac and Windows users (mobile is read-only)

📓 Instructions:

1. In a text document, click "Add icon" above the title to set an icon.

2. For all online document types, click the document name in the upper-left corner of the top bar to rename and add an icon.

3. Renaming items in the Knowledge Base directory tree supports adding icons.

Add emojis when inserting a divider. Choose your favorite emoji to make documents more lively, combining productivity with fun.

💡 Availability: Mac and Windows users (mobile is read-only)

📓 Instructions: In a document, insert a divider, select it to bring up the floating toolbar, click the emoji icon, and choose an emoji.

Insert tags with one click to quickly categorize and label document content, making information organization clearer and content retrieval faster.

## Print now supports auto-fit table width | Precisely control your print layout

No more limitations when printing tables! Select "Fit to paper page" when printing to ensure data is complete and clear, presenting table data perfectly.

## Form

## Login-free submission supported | Enjoy a lightning-fast submission experience

Forms now support "Login-free submission". Collect data freely outside DingTalk and manage it securely within DingTalk, making information collection seamless.

💡 Availability: Android, iOS, Mac, and Windows users

📓 Instructions:

To enable login-free submission, the form admin first clicks Settings in the upper-right corner of the form and selects "Anyone can submit" under "Who can submit".

Then enable "Login-free submission" below. Respondents can submit the form without signing in to a DingTalk account, but submitter UIDs and other information will not be available.

Unlock diverse question types with one click to easily collect customer feedback and precisely drive ongoing service improvements, turning every piece of feedback into a step forward.

💡 Availability: Android, iOS, Mac, and Windows users

📓 Instructions: When designing a form, drag the NPS component from the right panel into the form and configure it as needed.
