## DingTalk Whiteboard | Real-time collaboration for your dedicated brainstorming space

DingTalk Whiteboard is an online collaboration tool that supports real-time editing by multiple users. Built on an infinite, freely zoomable canvas, every collaborator can create together using pens, text, shapes, images, and more. Break down barriers of time and space, and let ideas spark off one another.


💡 Supported on | Desktop (read-only on mobile)

📖 How to use:

Desktop entry: Open DingTalk, click the left sidebar to enter "DingTalk Docs", click "Create" and select "Whiteboard" to finish creation.

iPad: Open DingTalk, tap "Collaboration" - "Docs", tap the blue "+" at the bottom and select "Whiteboard".


## DingTalk Docs | Customize page style for a more personalized document experience

The default minimalist interface is clean, but sometimes you can't find the tools you need. DingTalk Docs now **lets you set your preferred interface and the document styles you want**, lowering the learning curve for new users and making document creation more intuitive.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use: On PC — DingTalk Docs — top-right "···" — Page Style.

---

## DingTalk Docs | New reading mode prevents accidental edits while browsing

When browsing documents during collaboration, it's easy to accidentally click and change content. DingTalk Docs adds a "Reading Mode". Switch to it for an immersive reading and commenting experience — click anywhere without worrying about accidentally modifying the document.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use:

1. Entry: Document — top-right — toggle between Edit/Reading mode.

2. Action: Click to switch.

3. Note: Applies only to the current document, not as a user preference.

---

## DingTalk Docs | Home Recent list supports cross-filtering for easier document discovery

DingTalk Docs experience optimization: documents from Drive and chats are now included in the Home "Recent" list, and you can cross-filter by type, source, creator, and sort order.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use:

1. Open the DingTalk Docs home page to view documents recently opened or edited in Knowledge Base, Drive, and chats in the "Recent" list.

2. Quickly filter by type, source, creator, and sort options at the top to find what you need faster.

---

## DingTalk Docs | Quick Share follow on mobile to stay in sync with discussions

Away from your computer during an ad-hoc project discussion and unable to follow on your phone in real time? DingTalk Docs **now supports Quick Share follow on mobile**. Open the document and tap follow to stay with the discussion location — boosting collaboration efficiency.


💡 Supported on | Mobile

📖 How to use:

PC: Start Quick Share — hover over the Quick Share icon in the top bar — click Share Now.

Mobile: Follow view — open the document — tap the blue bar at the top — tap Follow.

---

## DingTalk Docs | Mobile supports adding IM chat files to Knowledge Base

When working on mobile, files you receive can only be added to Drive? DingTalk Docs experience optimization: on mobile, you can now save IM files to Drive or Knowledge Base, making work more convenient.


💡 Supported on | Mobile

📖 How to use: Update to the latest client, long-press the file extension menu, or tap "Add to" on the card to save it to DingTalk Docs.

## DingTalk Docs | Table of contents column mode improvements

After inserting a table of contents into a document, **you can choose to display heading levels up to N** for a consistent format. The column mode now looks even cleaner.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use: From the insert table of contents panel, or type "+ml" or "/ml" — find [Table of contents] — click insert — top-right "..." of the table of contents — choose [Column mode]. You can select which heading levels to display.

## DingTalk Docs | "Starred" renamed to "Favorites"

DingTalk Docs experience optimization: "Starred" has been renamed to "Favorites" to better match user habits.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use: The "Starred" label in the document main navigation has been changed to "Favorites".

## DingTalk Docs | Contacts can be configured to display department handbooks or department Knowledge Groups

Employees can't find an entry point to view department knowledge? You can now configure Contacts to display the department handbook or Knowledge Group, making it easy for employees to learn about their department and access its knowledge.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use:

1. From the DingTalk main navigation, click "Contacts" and enter a department node.

2. (Department head) Click "Add department link" to configure.

3. (Department members) Click the link displayed at the top to access.


## Spreadsheet | Insert multiple images in a single cell for more complete form content

When filling out various forms, a single image per cell isn't enough? Spreadsheet **now supports "inserting multiple images in a single cell"**, making your descriptions more complete and clear — no more missing details.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

Bringing local spreadsheet capabilities, Spreadsheet experience optimization **now supports "Insert/Delete cells"** for fast local editing.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use:

Entry: Insert images into a cell — multiple images can now be uploaded.

**Note**: Currently only supported via upload.

## Spreadsheet | Mobile supports generating an image from a selected range to share

The Spreadsheet mobile experience has been upgraded. When you select a cell range, **a new "Generate image" option appears in the floating menu**, letting you share data while preserving its original appearance.


💡 Supported on | Mobile

📖 How to use:

On phone or iPad, select a cell range and bring up the floating menu — the new "Generate image" option is now available.

Tap to generate an image of the selection, forward and share it directly, or save it to your phone album.

---

## Spreadsheet | Mobile supports smart screenshots — clearly view all data, free from screen size limits

When sharing screenshots on mobile, your spreadsheet is too long to capture in full and constrained by screen size? Spreadsheet mobile now supports smart screenshots: **detect the screenshot gesture and generate a data image with one tap** for easier sharing.


💡 Supported on | Mobile

📖 How to use:

On phone or iPad, when using the screenshot gesture, the app automatically detects the data area in the current worksheet and generates a data image.

You can forward and share it directly, or save the generated image to your phone album.


## AI Table | New visual design released for clearer operations (dual premium benefit)

DingTalk AI Table's visual interface has been fully upgraded for clearer and more convenient editing operations, with new additions: @ People, Comments, and date reminders.


💡 Supported on | Desktop


## Quick Meeting | Inserted DingTalk Docs are automatically granted permissions for easy reference during meetings

Need to grant document permissions on the fly during a meeting? When you insert a meeting-related DingTalk Docs link in Quick Meeting, permissions are granted automatically — click anytime during the meeting to view, making meetings more efficient.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use: After inserting or copying a DingTalk Docs link directly, permissions are automatically granted to event participants.


## Quick Notes | View remaining Quick Notes storage and admin storage configuration

How much storage do you have left for Quick Notes? Will it still work? You can now view remaining Quick Notes storage, and admins can configure Quick Notes capacity — stay in control and make adjustments any time to avoid running out of space.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use: Open the Quick Notes home page — view storage — expand capacity.

---

## Quick Notes | Voiceprint recognition to distinguish speakers for clearer review

Still manually tagging each speaker when converting voice to text? Quick Notes can automatically distinguish speakers by voiceprint and segment Quick Notes content by speaker — freeing your hands and making meeting reviews easier.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use: Quick Notes — filter by speaker — smart speaker identification.

---

## Quick Notes | Backend management with data analytics

Each meeting or topic discussion can feel like one-off content — but what if you want comprehensive analytics on every discussion? Enable Quick Notes during each meeting, and check the analytics in the Quick Notes backend: usage data, discussion keywords, word frequency, and more — all at a glance.


💡 Supported on | Desktop

📖 How to use: Quick Notes — Admin Console — Data Analytics.


## DingTalk Calendar | Multi-day events support tile/pinned customization

Previously, multi-day events tiled on the calendar took up a lot of space and were inconvenient to view. The DingTalk Calendar experience has been upgraded — choose to display them as tiles or pinned to the top, based on your needs.


💡 Supported on | All four clients

📖 How to use: Calendar settings — multi-day events of 24 hours or longer can be tiled or pinned, fully customizable.
