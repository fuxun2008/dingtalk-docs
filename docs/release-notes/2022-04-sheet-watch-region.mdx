**Update version: 6.5.10**

## DingTalk Docs: Auto-adjust spacing between Chinese and English

> Availability: Desktop

    **Value**

Smart detection of Chinese and English text automatically adjusts spacing, removing tedious manual steps and making document editing more standardized.

    **How to use**

Open page settings and turn on **Automatically add spaces between Chinese, English, and numbers** in editing behavior. Once enabled, spaces are automatically inserted when you mix Chinese, English, and numbers in the document.

    **Use case**

Ding Sanduo is a content operator in the marketing department who uses DingTalk Docs daily to edit various company materials such as promotional copy, announcements, and administrative documents. With this feature, spaces are automatically added between Chinese characters and adjacent English or numbers, eliminating the need to proofread formatting one by one. This makes article formatting more standardized and reduces the workload of format proofreading.

## Spreadsheet: Range watch updates

> Availability: Full functionality on desktop; mobile only supports receiving message notifications

    **Value**

The new range watch feature provides timely alerts for additions, deletions, and changes to data within a selected range, making team collaboration smoother and more responsive.

    **How to use**

In the new **Collaboration** menu at the top — select **Watch range** — then set the **range to watch** and the **users to notify**.

    **Use case**

Ding Sanduo is an office administrator who uses Spreadsheet daily to consolidate office supply requests from different departments. With "Watch range" enabled, when each department head submits office supply requests via Spreadsheet and a cell is updated, Ding Sanduo receives an instant DingTalk notification. No separate reminders are needed, which lowers communication costs and improves collaboration efficiency.


_Note: Only users with **View only** permission or **higher** to the spreadsheet will receive notifications. Former employees will no longer receive notifications. Notifications are delivered through the DingTalk Docs message account._

## Spreadsheet: Spreadsheet information upgrade

> Availability: Desktop

    **Value**

View readers, editors, and data content of a spreadsheet at a glance.

    **How to use**

In the top navigation bar of the spreadsheet, click the spreadsheet name and select View spreadsheet information.

    **Use case**

Ding Sanduo is a product manager at an internet company who uses Spreadsheet daily to track the launch progress of new product features and collaborates with R&D and design colleagues to fill in information. With "View spreadsheet information," he can clearly see how colleagues are using the spreadsheet, including the most recent reading and editing dates and times.

## DingTalk Log: Log supports spreadsheet component

> Availability: Desktop / Mobile

    **Value**

Log templates now support creating spreadsheet components, making data collection more standardized and data organization more efficient.

    **How to use**

Click Log — enter **Template management** — select **Create / Edit template** — and choose **Spreadsheet component**.

    **Use case**

Ding Sanduo is a regional manager at Freshippo who uses DingTalk Log to consolidate daily work reports from store managers, including key information such as sales data, event status, and after-sales issues. With the "Spreadsheet component" feature, she can flexibly combine attachments, text, images, multiselect, and other components to create log templates, standardize the content of log submissions, and efficiently support reporting between managers and team members.

## DingTalk Project: "My tasks" quick creation supports selecting "project"

> Availability: Web / Desktop

    **Value**

Distribute tasks across different projects more conveniently and shorten the task creation path.

    **How to use**

Go to the "My tasks" page and click the create box at the top to quickly create a task. You can directly select "Set project."

With this feature, task creation is much more convenient, cutting the time in half.
