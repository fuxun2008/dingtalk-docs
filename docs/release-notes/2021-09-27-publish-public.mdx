## 🆕 [Team space] Custom sorting for the team space list

Sort and hide spaces with custom settings to find files in your space more efficiently.

## 🆕 [Team space] Public publishing now available

Publish team space documents publicly to build product user guides and share company business content externally, boosting product and content exposure.

_(Note: Only authenticated organizations can enable public publishing.)_

[Learn more about public publishing >>](https://docs.dingtalk.io/i/team/nb9XJoPQ6OKPmyAp/docs/nb9XJKMB6LMQVmyA?corpId=dingd8e1123006514592&iframeQuery=#)

## 🆕 [Team space] Bulk file import

DingTalk Docs and team spaces now support bulk import of files (Word, Excel) and folders, improving file management efficiency.

## 🆕 [Team space] Team templates

Save team templates in your team space. Admins can customize collaboration templates for the team space to standardize the content and style of collaborative documents.

## 🆕 [DingTalk Mind] Create mind maps on mobile

Create mind maps on your phone to capture ideas and thoughts anytime, anywhere, and enhance your structured thinking.
