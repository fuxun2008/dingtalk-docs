## How to switch layout, subject, and line style

*   Desktop: In Mind Map View mode, click the button at the top left of the screen to open the panel, then switch to your preferred layout, line style, and subject.

*   Mobile: In Mind Map View mode, tap the button at the bottom right of the screen to open the panel, then switch to your preferred layout.
