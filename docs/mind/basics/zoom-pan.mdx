In Mind Map View mode, zoom and scroll the entire canvas to switch between details and the overall picture.

## Zoom the Canvas on Desktop

*   Four methods are currently supported:

1.  Ctrl + mouse scroll.

2.  Two-finger gesture on a trackpad (not yet supported in the DingTalk Mac client; open in a browser for a better experience).

3.  Ctrl + +/- shortcut.

4.  Use the zoom tool in the bottom-right corner to control canvas zoom.

## Move the Canvas on Desktop

*   Five methods are currently supported:

1.  Scroll the mouse wheel to move the canvas.

2.  Use a two-finger gesture on a trackpad.

3.  Hold down the right mouse button and drag to move the canvas.

4.  Click the hand icon on the left to move the canvas.

5.  When there is a lot of content, drag the scroll bar to move the canvas.

## Zoom the Canvas on Mobile

*   In Mind Map View mode, pinch with two fingers on a blank area of the canvas to zoom.

*   After a successful pinch-to-zoom, the current zoom ratio is displayed in the center of the screen.

## Move the Canvas on Mobile

*   In Mind Map View mode, swipe with one finger on a blank area of the canvas to move it.
