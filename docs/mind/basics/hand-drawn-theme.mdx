## Value

Mind Map now supports hand-drawn subjects, making content more engaging and visually appealing.

## Use Case

Ding Sanduo is the head of the event planning department at an advertising agency and uses DingTalk Mind daily to create marketing plans and event proposals. The new hand-drawn subject not only helps quickly express ideas and creativity, but also makes Mind Maps more visually appealing, adds fresh energy, and brings proposals to life.

## How to Use

On the Mind Map editing page, click the icon in the upper-left corner. Under **Subject**, select a hand-drawn subject from the bottom of the list.
