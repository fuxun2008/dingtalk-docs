DingTalk Mind presentation templates support one-click generation of transition animations and layouts, making even simple logic diagrams shine in your presentations.

It also supports features such as comment bullet screens and a minimap.

## Presentation Mode Entry

Click the presentation icon in the top-right corner to enter presentation mode.
