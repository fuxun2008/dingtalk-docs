The History feature displays the operation history of a document and supports rollback. To restore a previous version, view the History and roll back to any earlier version.

## How to access History

*   Click [...] in the upper-right corner of the Mind Map, and then select [History] from the menu.

*   Currently available on desktop only. Mobile support is coming soon.

## How to use History

Click [Restore] next to the desired Version.
