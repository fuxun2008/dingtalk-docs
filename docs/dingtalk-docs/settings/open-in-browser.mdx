## Feature update: DingTalk now supports opening documents in a browser by default

*   While using DingTalk, you can set documents to open, view, and edit in a web browser based on your personal preferences.

*   When using DingTalk Docs in a browser, save documents to your browser's favorites with one click for more efficient retrieval and viewing.

*   The setup is simple and intuitive—edit documents in your browser in just 3 seconds, with no change to your existing habits.

*   Upgrade DingTalk to version 6.0.15 or later to access this setting.

## How to set up

*   **Windows:** Open **DingTalk** **\>** click your **Profile Photo** in the top-left corner **> Settings & Privacy > General > DingTalk Docs**, then enable "Always open documents in browser" to complete the setup.

*   **Mac:** Open **DingTalk** **\>** click your **Profile Photo** in the top-left corner **> Settings & Privacy > General > DingTalk Docs**, then check "Always open documents in browser" to complete the setup.
