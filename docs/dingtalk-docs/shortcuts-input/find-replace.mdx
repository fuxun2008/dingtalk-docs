Find and Replace helps you quickly locate the content you need and make batch edits to your document while editing or reading a document.

## Instructions

Click **Find and Replace** on the right side of the toolbar, or use the **shortcut Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on Mac)** to find and replace specific fields in the document. Search results are highlighted with a yellow background. Use the arrows at the bottom right to navigate through all matched results.
