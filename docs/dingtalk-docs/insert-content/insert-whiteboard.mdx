---

DingTalk Whiteboard, designed for real-time multi-user collaboration and creative brainstorming, can now be inserted into DingTalk Docs. It supports flowcharts, discussion boards, brainstorming, and graphic drawing.

## Instructions

In DingTalk Docs, click **Insert** on the top toolbar, or type `/bb` in the document. In the insertion panel that appears, locate **Whiteboard** and click it to insert a whiteboard.
