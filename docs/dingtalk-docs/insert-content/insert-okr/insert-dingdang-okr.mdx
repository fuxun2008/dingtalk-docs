## Overview

In DingTalk Docs, you can insert third-party OKR apps to display team and personal OKR content directly with synchronized updates. After enabling the DingDing OKR and Polaris OKR apps, use / or + to quickly select and insert the corresponding OKR app.

## 🎯 How to insert DingDing OKR

## 1. Copy the OKR share link

## 2. Insert into a DingTalk Doc

### Option 1: Paste the link directly

In the document, press Ctrl + V or right-click to paste the OKR link. The link is recognized automatically, and you can display it as a text link, icon text link, summary card, or preview card.

### Option 2: Insert from the Insert menu

Click the Insert menu in the document, find DingDing OKR in the list, enter the OKR link in the link input box, and click Confirm to generate the OKR preview card.
