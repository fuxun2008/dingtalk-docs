## Overview

In DingTalk Docs, you can insert third-party OKR apps to directly display team and personal OKR content with synchronized updates. After activating the Dingdang OKR and Polaris OKR apps, you can quickly select and insert the corresponding OKR app using "/" and "+".

## 🎯 How to insert Polaris OKR

## Copy an objective link in Polaris OKR

1. Hover over the objective you want to share, then click **Details**.

2. On the details page, click the **Share icon** in the upper-right corner, then select **Copy to DingTalk Docs**.

**II. Insert the objective in DingTalk Docs**

Method 1: In the document, press Ctrl + V or right-click to paste the OKR link. The link is automatically recognized, and you can choose to display it as a text link, icon text link, summary card, or preview card.

**Method 2:** Click the Insert menu within the document, find "Polaris OKR" in the list, enter the objective link in the link input box, and click Confirm to generate the objective preview card.

**III. How to use the objective card**

The inserted card offers rich display features to meet a variety of business scenarios.
