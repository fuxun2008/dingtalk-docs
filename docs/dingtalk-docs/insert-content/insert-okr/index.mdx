## Overview

In DingTalk Docs, you can insert third-party OKR apps to display team and personal OKRs and keep them in sync. Once you have enabled the DingDang OKR and Polaris OKR apps, use the / or + shortcut to quickly insert the corresponding OKR app.

## How to insert DingDang OKR

## 1. Copy the OKR share link

In DingDang OKR, open the OKR you want to share and copy its share link.

## 2. Insert into a DingTalk Doc

### Method 1: Paste the link directly

In the document, press Ctrl + V or right-click to paste the OKR link. The link is recognized automatically, and you can choose to display it as a text link, icon text link, summary card, or preview card.

### Method 2: Use the Insert menu

Click the Insert menu in the document, find DingDang OKR in the list, enter the OKR link in the input box, and click Confirm to generate an OKR preview card.

## How to insert Polaris OKR

## Copy the objective link in Polaris OKR

1. Hover over the objective you want to share, then click Details.

2. On the details page, click the **Share icon** in the upper-right corner and select **Copy to DingTalk Docs**.

**2. Insert the objective into a DingTalk Doc**

Method 1: In the document, press Ctrl + V or right-click to paste the OKR link. The link is recognized automatically, and you can choose to display it as a text link, icon text link, summary card, or preview card.

**Method 2:** Click the Insert menu in the document, find Polaris OKR in the list, enter the objective link in the input box, and click Confirm to generate an objective preview card.

**3. How to use the objective card**

The inserted card offers rich display features to meet a variety of business scenarios.
