DingTalk Docs supports inserting dates, eliminating the hassle of manual entry and standardizing date formats. Inserted dates can also include a specific time.

## Instructions

Select the location where you want to add the date, click **Insert > Date** in the top navigation bar, and then select the target date from the calendar that appears. The inserted date is displayed in **blue**. To modify it, click the date and reselect.

## Additional notes

*   Click the **left and right arrows** `<` `>` on the calendar to switch between months.

*   Click the year in the upper-left corner of the calendar to switch years.

*   Select the **Time** option at the bottom of the calendar to display a specific time after the date. The time can be adjusted as needed.
