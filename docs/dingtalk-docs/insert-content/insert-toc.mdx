## Value

Insert a table of contents in your document with one click to make the structure clear at a glance and improve content lookup efficiency.

## Use case

Ding Sanduo is an administrative staff member who uses DingTalk Docs daily to edit various company materials, such as event copy, meeting notes, and administrative documents. With this feature, she can insert a table of contents into a document with one click while drafting. For text-heavy documents, the table of contents makes the structure clear at a glance, eliminating the need to flip back and forth when looking up content—simple and convenient.

## Instructions

Click the Insert panel on the top navigation bar, then select Table of contents to insert one into the document. Headings in the document (font size 14 and above) are automatically generated into the table of contents.
