Documents are often used in daily meetings. Switching a document to Presentation mode delivers the best presentation experience.

## Instructions

## Start a presentation

Click the **presentation icon** in the upper-right corner of the document, or use the **shortcut Ctrl + Shift + P (Mac: Cmd + Shift + P)** to enter Presentation mode.

## Switch the presentation style

Presentation mode offers two display styles: scrolling playback and Slides paging mode.

Scrolling playback presents the document in a continuous scrolling stream. In Slides paging mode, the document is paginated and presented by document node.

**In Presentation mode, hover over any spot at the bottom of the screen and click once to bring up the presentation settings panel, then make your selection.**

## Presentation style settings

### Day/Night mode

Presentation mode supports both day and night display modes.

**In Presentation mode, hover over any spot at the bottom of the screen and click once to bring up the presentation settings panel, then switch the mode.**

### View zoom ratio

Presentation mode supports three view sizes: large, medium, and small.
**At the bottom of Presentation mode, click the icon to switch between view ratios.**

## Exit the presentation

In the Presentation mode settings, click **Exit presentation**, or press **Esc** to exit the presentation.
