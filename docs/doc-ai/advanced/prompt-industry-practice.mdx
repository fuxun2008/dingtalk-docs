## New Retail

**Industry: Consumer goods, retail**

## New Media

* **** Writing an official account article for a cosmetics brand "xx Diary"

* **** You are an experienced copywriter. Write an article for an official account on the topic of Valentine's Day gift promotion. The subject is the Perfect Diary Valentine's Day gift box. Describe the advantages of the gift box in terms of design, effect, and price. The article should appeal to young readers, exclude promotional information, convey enthusiasm, attract Customers, and remain concise.

**Text-to-text Prompt formula**

Prompt = Role + Task goal + Context and background information + Detailed requirements and specifics + Content to exclude + Format and form of the response
