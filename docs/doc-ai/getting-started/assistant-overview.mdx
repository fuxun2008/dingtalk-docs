</Note>

**Assisted writing makes document creation simple**

Use "/Assisted writing" in your document and let AI quickly generate marketing plans, creative stories, promotional copy, summary reports, competitive analyses, and more to boost your productivity.

**Rewrite content for richer expression**

Need a different tone? Let AI rewrite your content. Generate summaries, adjust tone, polish wording, and easily transform your content into multiple styles of expression.

**Generate images and create artwork in any style**

Unleash your creativity and imagination. Use "/Generate images" to let AI produce vivid, varied image assets, saving creation time and improving efficiency.

### Generate Tables and Create Visual Tables in One Click

Information overload? Use "/Generate tables" to let AI quickly build structured tables that present your information clearly.

**Document-based generation: distill and analyze work documents**

Use "/Add document" when asking a question, and let AI analyze, summarize, distill, and create content based on your work documents. Generate more accurate, evidence-based content intelligently.
