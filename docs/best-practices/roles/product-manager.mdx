> One Knowledge Base brings product, design, R&D, and operations together for efficient cross-department Collaboration.

Product managers must strike the right balance between user experience, business, and technology. This requires close Collaboration with Colleagues across many functions, such as designers, R&D engineers, and operations staff. Making sure everyone clearly understands where product requirements come from, the product roadmap, specific designs, and Important progress is no small challenge . With a Knowledge Base, everyone collaborates in the same space, accessing the latest information in one place and working together efficiently.

## Discover opportunities

Product managers must continuously Discover new opportunities. These opportunities often start with Data insights but still call for deeper customer research and co-creation. Capturing these findings in the Knowledge Base and sharing them with the Team helps everyone understand where requirements come from and why they matter. Inviting technical Team members to take part in the product Discovery process also boosts Team engagement .

## Product planning

Based on the early research reports and co-creation Documents stored in the Knowledge Base, the Team can discuss iteration priorities together, draft a 3-6 month product roadmap, keep it updated, and Share it with everyone. This gives Team members a clear view of both mid-term and short-term goals.

## Requirements management

Requirements need to be brought to life by R&D, and a clearly written requirements Document is the start of every great outcome. Use Presentation Mode in the Knowledge Base to kick off reviews quickly, capture Team Comments in real time, and collaborate seamlessly — even remotely.
