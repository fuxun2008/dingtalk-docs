## Background

Designers often need to walk others through their design proposals, which involve large numbers of images and videos. Designers also frequently need to send these proposals to external partners.

## Pain Points

When presenting a design proposal, designers often have to switch back and forth between PowerPoint slides and design drafts. Demonstrating long images is especially inconvenient, since the only option is to open the design software to display them.

Additional note: When sending design proposals to external partners, designers need to convert them into image files to prevent tampering.
