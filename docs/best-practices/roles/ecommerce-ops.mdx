## Scope of application

*   A complete set of templates covering every scenario in daily e-commerce operations—from store operation plans, design schedules, product selection, new launches, customer service, inventory, and returns to daily data reviews—helping beginners quickly master standard store operation processes and knowledge.

*   This solution comes with a variety of Spreadsheet Functions and data Quote features, enabling e-commerce managers to efficiently manage daily store operations from a single, unified workspace.

## 1. Store operation plan

To run Tmall and Taobao stores successfully, it is essential to set up a solid operation plan in advance. Without well-defined sales targets and campaign plans, execution becomes aimless and business performance cannot be guaranteed. For e-commerce operations, a practical "E-commerce store operation plan" Spreadsheet Template boosts work efficiency. Simply enter the store's operational target Data, and the Spreadsheet will automatically generate Data. It also supports breaking down targets by month and by day, making goal management both clear and efficient.

Store decoration and design are critical aspects of e-commerce operations. In addition to major sales events such as the 618 and Double 11 promotions, monthly campaigns are needed to enhance the store atmosphere. Consolidate design requirements in the "Store design request scheduling Table" Template to efficiently categorize and plan design tasks. Work together with designers to manage store design requirements during campaigns, strengthen collaboration between operations and design Teams, and ensure clear scheduling and Progress tracking for every request.

Use the "E-commerce product selection General Table" Template to consolidate and deeply analyze and monitor store product Data, helping improve store sales conversion.
