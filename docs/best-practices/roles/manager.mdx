As a manager in an organization, you serve as the bridge between leadership and staff, shouldering critical management responsibilities. You often face situations that require coordination and agenda planning:

*   Regularly attending team weekly meetings, monthly meetings, and business planning sessions, and organizing various types of meetings.

*   Efficiently organizing scattered documents generated within the team and organization.

*   Managing busy schedules with proper planning and reminders.

On DingTalk, managers can use Quick Meeting, DingTalk Docs Team space, and Event to effectively address common pain points such as time-consuming meetings without outcomes, scattered knowledge accumulation, and low team collaboration efficiency. These tools help managers achieve efficient team collaboration, freeing up more focus for business thinking, delivering business results, and enhancing workplace competitiveness.

## 01. Unclear meeting topics? No meeting records? No follow-up on conclusions?

**Quick Meeting helps your team run every meeting effectively.**

Meetings are the most expensive business activity for an organization, but inefficient meetings make many people feel that meetings are a waste of time. The all-new **Quick Meeting** product suite delivers truly efficient team meetings that are **prepared, focused, agenda-driven, outcome-oriented, and action-tracked**.

**Before the meeting**

Schedule the event and set the topic.

**During the meeting**

Communicate and manage time.

**After the meeting**

Document outcomes and follow up.

**Learn more:** [Run effective meetings: Host efficient meetings with Quick Meeting](/docs/best-practices/scenarios/ding-flash-meeting)

## 02. Are your organization's core document assets scattered and hard to manage?

**Team space: Accumulate organizational knowledge efficiently and share it quickly.**

To help team members collaborate more efficiently on documents across the organization, you can build a **knowledge management library** with DingTalk Team space. All team outputs—Meeting Minutes, project files, training materials, and more—sync in real time, and all accumulated knowledge is shared among team members. Multiple creation formats are supported, including documents, spreadsheets, and mind maps.

## 03. Managers handle numerous daily tasks with packed schedules

**Event Reminder: Plan your schedule wisely and never miss important events.**

DingTalk Calendar integrates products such as **Chat, Mail, Meeting, and Notes**, and supports one-tap sync with your phone calendar. Get a clear overview of your daily work schedule. You can also view team members' availability to reduce communication costs, adjust schedules as needed, and never miss any meeting or arrangement.
