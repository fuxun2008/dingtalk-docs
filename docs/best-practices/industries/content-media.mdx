Vivian is a producer in Ximalaya's Innovative Content Production Department, mainly responsible for thematic content on suspense, film, and TV works, planning dozens of themed projects each year. Her daily work involves content planning, audio content outsourcing and production, quality control, supervision, project management, and summary reports—all of which require multi-party communication and coordination. Vivian uses DingTalk Docs to ensure both efficiency and quality in her daily work.

## 1. Plan thematic content and make cross-department material collection simple and efficient

Audio content production is one of Vivian's most important responsibilities. Popular audio dramas on the platform, such as *The Age of Awakening* and *Twilight*, are produced under the lead of her department. A thematic content project—from planning and production to launch—often requires cross-department collaboration. IP selection, subject content, audio materials, author information, copyright information, and thematic copy involve different teams. With DingTalk Docs, information from different departments can be consolidated in a single document, enabling efficient collection of audio content in the early stages. In the past, producing and launching an audio content theme typically took a week. With DingTalk Docs collaboration, it now takes only 2 to 3 days.

Use DingTalk Docs to plan the launch of the theme *Three Lives, Three Worlds, A Lotus Step at a Time*, which quickly climbed the listener hot list.

## 2. Coordinate ad slot applications and gather operational promotion requirements to boost collaboration efficiency

A newly launched thematic project needs promotion. To reach more listeners, teams need to apply for ad slots within the company. The Ximalaya app offers various types of channel pages, carousel banners, and card promotion slots for content produced by different teams to gain exposure. When the operations team applies for ad slots, they need to consolidate multiple sets of information such as the applying department, theme type, launch date, and sales targets. The department managing ad slots uses DingTalk Docs to enable everyone to collaborate. After collecting information, they not only coordinate the content promotion schedule but also sync the specific launch times with the operations team. A single DingTalk Docs file enables efficient two-way collaboration between cross-department teams.

A single spreadsheet enables two-way synchronization of ad slot management for efficient collaboration.

## 3. Manage popular IP projects with team spaces to consolidate project content for easy review

Popular IP series planning is a key content initiative at Ximalaya. Hit titles currently online, such as *High-IQ Crime* and *Who Is the Killer*, are all carefully produced by the team. Packaging a successful IP project involves collaboration with multiple parties inside and outside the company, including copyright, business, marketing, operations, and R&D. As the producer and project owner, Vivian uses DingTalk Docs Team spaces to create Knowledge Bases for different IPs. Within each Team space, she organizes documents, materials, project progress, and data reports by function, so project members can review them in real time and share through collaboration.

Multiple functional members collaborate and review project content in real time.

## 4. Accumulate weekly reports and team knowledge to promote member growth

Vivian uses DingTalk Docs and Team spaces to consolidate and accumulate team weekly reports, monthly reports, and annual summary plans. Every team member also receives weekly and monthly reports from their manager, aligning information across levels, clarifying business goals, and sharing work issues and solutions. New department hires can quickly understand the business by reviewing summary documents accumulated by team members in the Team space, reducing the learning curve and accelerating onboarding.

Accumulate work summaries; team knowledge helps members grow.

## Customer profile

Ximalaya is a beloved online audio sharing platform dedicated to sharing human wisdom through sound and serving a better life with sound, providing lifelong spiritual nourishment for the whole family. Today, Ximalaya has built a healthy, balanced, and vibrant ecosystem of content—covering everything from top IPs to long-tail content. Its online audio services cover rich content needed by users at every life stage from -1 to 100 years old, with 340 million audio pieces accumulated across 98 categories.

Ximalaya has driven the "ear economy" featuring audio. In 2020, the platform reached 5.2 million active content creators. With the philosophy that "everything makes a sound," Ximalaya connects and serves hundreds of millions of users through sound. It launched China's first all-content smart AI speaker, Xiaoya, opening a new era of Voice Interaction in communication. Meanwhile, Ximalaya has strengthened its intelligent ecosystem and expanded its footprint across hardware terminals such as cars, smart homes, smart speakers, and wearables, making sound and knowledge as ubiquitous and on-demand as water and electricity. Throughout 2021, the platform's average monthly active users across all scenarios reached 268 million.

Rapid business growth has driven Ximalaya to continually attract more talent. Faced with an increasingly large product and R&D business line and a growing team, Ximalaya chose DingTalk Docs as its company content creation platform. By introducing new collaborative workflows for product, R&D, operations, and other departments, DingTalk Docs has effectively improved the direction and production of thematic content.
