## Make Work Reporting Truly Effective

As a manager: Use logs to understand your team members' work progress and thinking, and share your own thoughts with them in return.

As an Employee: Use logs to record your work and accumulate insights. During annual or quarterly reviews, you can clearly see your career trajectory and let your achievements be recognized.
</Note>

DingTalk Log covers common workplace reporting scenarios: daily reports, weekly reports, Monthly reports, quarterly reports, annual reports, and more. Organization managers can flexibly configure reporting methods and Customize a wide range of work report Templates. Employees can submit work reports anytime, anywhere, helping them organize their work with greater structure and purpose. Leaders can stay informed about Employee work status and outcomes in real time, even when they are away, and offer encouragement and guidance by liking and commenting on Employee reports.
