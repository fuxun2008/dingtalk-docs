## 01

**How to quickly collect return-trip information?**

**"Collect information from hundreds of people in 1 second—saving time and effort"**

After the holiday, employees usually return to their posts from across the country one by one. Knowing each "Employee return-trip information" promptly becomes especially important, so the Organization can plan work assignments accordingly.

Sending Direct Messages to ask when each person is coming back, then copying, pasting, and saving everything into a Spreadsheet—the workload is huge and time-consuming. With DingTalk Docs, "Employee return-trip registration" can be Completed in just 1 minute. Spreadsheet supports real-time online Editing by multiple users, eliminating repeated manual Copy and Paste. Simply wait for Employees to fill in their entries, and you'll get a complete information Spreadsheet.

① Open DingTalk Docs, click the Featured Template "Employee return-trip registration form," then select Use.

② Right-click and Select Comments, then @ all Collaboration Users with one click.

## 02

**How to set annual goals for 2022?**

**"Define goals and create OKRs—simple and efficient"**

At the start of a new year, it's worth planning out what you want to do in 2022—covering work, future plans, income, lifestyle, and more.

**The first step is to set an achievable flag for the year.** Open DingTalk Docs > Featured Template > "SMART goal-setting principle" to learn how to set goals correctly.

One of my goals is to get a promotion and a raise. Following the SMART principle, I'd set it like this: a Monthly content push with 100K+ reads.

S: The goal is to hit the KPI.

M: 100K+ reads.

A: Achievable.

R: Highly relevant to a promotion and raise.

T: Once a month.

## 03

**How to break down goals and execute them Monthly?**

**"Prioritize tasks to boost work efficiency"**

Once you have clear goals, break them down by month. Open DingTalk Docs > Featured Template, then Enable "2025 Monthly work plan" or "R&D work plan" to achieve your goals month by month.

Use the "2025 Monthly work plan" to record tasks anytime, so everything is at a glance. Note:

① Prioritize urgent and important tasks first—focus on efficiency, but care more about results.

② Set deadlines and take them seriously, like commitments to yourself.

③ Be specific. If a task is too large, break it down into simple steps. This reduces memory load, prevents forgetting, helps you start work quickly, and makes good use of fragmented time.
