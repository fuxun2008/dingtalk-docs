At work, you often need to connect with colleagues, partners, and customers during holidays or project milestones.

Do you struggle with how to express your feelings on these occasions?

**Today, learn three tips to create a vivid audio greeting card with DingTalk Docs.**

Grab a seat and pay attention — you might need this skill tomorrow!

**Key features used:** **Page settings, Insert, and Signature**

**Step-by-step tutorial:**

<Note>
1. Create a new DingTalk Docs document.

2. **Hide document information.** Hover over the title, click "Page settings", and turn off the title and info bar.

3. **Insert an attractive image.** Insert an image you like (GIFs are also supported), then fill the page with a background color that matches the image in Page settings.

4. **Insert your favorite music or video.** At the same time, enter the main text content.

5. **Add a handwritten signature.** To show extra sincerity, add a Signature at the signing area. Click Insert, then select Signature.

Follow the steps above, and your beautiful audio greeting card is complete. Simple, isn't it?

Got it? Give it a try now!
