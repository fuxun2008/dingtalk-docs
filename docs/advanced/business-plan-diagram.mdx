we are expected to master every tool out there—PPT, PS, Word, Excel, and more.

It can get a little exhausting, right?

The new fiscal year is just around the corner,

and it's time to kick off **business roadmaps and operational plans** again.

**Are you still using PPT to draw your 2025 business roadmap?**

Aligning frames, dragging, snapping—does it leave your eyes strained?

Today, let's share some of the hidden features recently launched in DingTalk Docs.

By mastering the **Highlight block** and **Column layout** features in DingTalk Docs,

_**anyone can easily create a polished, professional-looking roadmap diagram!**_

Pair it with the **Presentation mode (Ctrl+Shift+P)** in DingTalk Docs and the result is simply stunning — colleagues may even ask if you made it in PPT 🤭

**Key features you'll need: Column layout and Highlight block**

**Step-by-step tutorial:**

<Note>
1. Create a new DingTalk Docs document.

2. Click "Quick column layout" under the Insert menu and choose the number of columns based on your content.

3. If your diagram includes content that spans the full width, insert a "Highlight block".

4. Hover over the colored block and click the **color palette icon** to set your preferred background and font colors for the Highlight block and columns.

(Gradient font colors are highly recommended—they look absolutely amazing!)

5. To create nested layers within a block, simply insert another column layout or Highlight block and apply your color settings.

And there you have it—a roadmap diagram created entirely within your document! No more drawing frames, aligning, or dragging in PPT. Switch to Presentation mode when sharing for an even better experience!

Did you get the hang of it? Give it a try!
</Note>
