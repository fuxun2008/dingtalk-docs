"Hi DingTalk, make me a short video on how to redeem coupons!"

In daily work, you often need to create simple short videos.

There are plenty of video editing tools out there, but using them is far from easy.

Finding background music, adding captions, writing voiceovers...

It's just too much hassle!

Today, we're sharing a method to create simple videos using DingTalk Docs.

It's easy to get started and solves your urgent needs effortlessly!

**Key feature you'll use:** **Generate Video**

**Step-by-step tutorial:**

<Note>
1. Create a new DingTalk Docs document and add the video materials, arranging them in a text + image layout.

2. Click the **...** icon in the top-right corner, then select **Download to local** and **AI Generate Video**.

3. Customize the video's **voice, volume, speed**, and **background music** to your preference. You can also choose the display position of the digital avatar.

4. After configuring the settings, click the **"Start Generating Video"** button at the bottom. Once complete, the finished video will be sent to your chat via the DingTalk Docs Assistant.

5. Here's a quick tip: to align the images with the voiceover, structure your content using **one paragraph** (the voiceover text for the image) + **one image** when adding materials in the document. This makes the final video much smoother.

And just like that, your simple video created with DingTalk Docs is ready! Pretty easy, right?

Got it? Give it a try now!
</Note>
