---
title: "Share Meeting Conclusions: How to Send to Multiple Groups or Colleagues in Bulk"
description: "After a Quick Meeting ends, the organizer and attendees can share the full meeting content to multiple groups or colleagues in one click, with flexible permission settings for viewing or editing."
---

After running an efficient meeting with [Quick Meeting](https://docs.dingtalk.io/i/p/lPDmrrpqjBklJmxd/docs/bxgzX5wq4YoJPQej3m6p8Ry2OB79ALPD?dontjump=true), valuable materials and conclusions are automatically captured. The meeting Organizer and Members can share the entire Quick Meeting with the whole Team for reading and learning.

➡️ In the upper-right corner of the Quick Meeting, click **Share** > **Send to Chat**. In the share dialog, select the recipients or Group Chats.

➡️ If the recipient or Group Chat does not have access, the system prompts **Add the corresponding permission**. Four permission levels are available: **Can manage, Can edit, Can view/download, Can view only, No access**.

➡️ Once shared, the recipient receives a Card in DingTalk Chat. Click the Card to open it.
