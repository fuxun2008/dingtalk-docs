---
title: "Meeting Time Eaten up by Reading Materials? Master This Method to Shorten Meetings Effortlessly!"
description: "Use Quick Meeting's reminder feature to prompt attendees to read and comment on materials before the meeting, eliminating in-meeting explanations so discussion can start from a shared foundation."
---

Still spending meeting time explaining or reading through materials? It drags out meetings and colleagues may lose focus. Use **Quick Meeting** to invite Members to **read and comment on meeting materials in advance**.

➡️ In the top-right **Reminder** menu of the Quick Meeting page, select the **Read and comment** reminder and confirm the recipients.

➡️ The system automatically sets all Event participants as recipients. You can also remove them manually. Recipients can be added by **Contacts, Organization Structure, or Group Chat**.

➡️ Once confirmed, the system sends a reminder message to the recipients via **DingTalk Chat**.
