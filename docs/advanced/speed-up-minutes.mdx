Still manually editing and organizing meeting minutes after every meeting? Skip the hassle. With **Quick Meeting**, you can fill in meeting minutes directly after the meeting ends, while keeping task follow-ups on track—boosting your efficiency in an instant.

➡️ When creating a Quick Meeting, you can see **Meeting Minutes** in the left-hand Table of contents. Write conclusions and to-dos in the Meeting Minutes section to make it easier to summarize and follow up after the meeting.

➡️ To assign tasks, @ the person in charge in the to-do, enter the corresponding to-do content, and select the priority level from the **[…]** menu on the right side of the to-do. Once editing is complete, the to-do is created automatically.

➡️ To view the details of a to-do, click the **[arrow icon]** on the to-do and select **View details**.
