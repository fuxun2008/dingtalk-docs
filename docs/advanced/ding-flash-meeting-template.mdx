Preparing meeting materials can be time-consuming, and sometimes the meeting is about to start before everything is in place. To run a meeting efficiently, **Quick Meeting** templates are here to help.

[Arrow right] In DingTalk Calendar, create an event and click **Select Template** in the meeting description. Click **More Templates** to browse all options. Once created, the template is applied directly to the meeting.

[Arrow right] You can also use templates **inside Quick Meeting**. On the right side of the page, click **Meeting Templates**, choose the template you need, and click **Use**.

[Arrow right] You can **change the meeting template** even after entering content. Click **...** in the upper-right corner of the page, then select **View All Templates** to switch. **Note that this clears existing content and applies the new template.**
