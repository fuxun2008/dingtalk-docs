## Still worried about your year-end report? DingTalk Docs offers 10+ free templates!

➡️ In the DingTalk desktop client, click **Document** > **Knowledge Base** > **Create Knowledge Base** in the left panel, then select **Year-End Report** to access **10+ templates**, including **writing tips, general templates, and industry-specific templates**.

➡️ In the Year-End Report Knowledge Base, templates come pre-built with DingTalk Docs components such as **Insert Table, To-Do, Highlight blocks, Columns, and Roadmap**. View and edit any template freely to create an impressive year-end report with ease — and become the year-end productivity star of your team!
