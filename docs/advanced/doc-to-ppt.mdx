Still using PowerPoint for reports and presentations?

Every time you build a deck,

you spend ages aligning, centering, and resizing—

your eyes go blurry before you're done!

The bigger problem is that creating a deck

often means spending half your time on layout instead of content.

Sometimes, when content runs short, you end up padding it with formatting!

Today, DingTalk Docs shows you a simple trick

that lets you focus on content creation

while still delivering a slideshow-style presentation when it's time to share.

**Key feature you'll use:****DingTalk Docs -****Presentation - Edit slide breaks**

**Step-by-step guide:**

<Note>
1. After preparing your content in DingTalk Docs, click the dropdown next to "Present" in the top-right corner, then select "Edit slide breaks".

2. Based on your presentation flow, use split and merge actions to organize the document Content into the slide groupings you want for sharing.

3. Click "Play" in the top-right corner to Preview the result.

4. Want to capture your audience's attention even better? Try these tips:

Wrong presenter name showing during your report? In presentation mode, click the bottom toolbar - [Presentation settings] to change the presenter name, or hide the presenter entirely.

Worried your audience will miss the key points? While presenting, click the bottom toolbar to switch the Laser Pointer style and highlight what matters most.

When you start presentation mode from a Document, you can "View document comments" to discuss and analyze topics around the Comments, resolve disagreements efficiently, and reach consensus.

Say goodbye to PowerPoint—your Document does it all!

Got it? Try it out now!
</Note>
