Do you ever struggle with work documents like this?

A document contains several sections,

and you have to scroll up and down endlessly just to locate one part of the content.

Why is it so hard to grasp the framework of a document at a glance?

You probably didn't know that DingTalk Docs has a clever trick to handle this!

With this feature, doubling your efficiency in locating document content is no longer a dream.

Listen carefully! Today's lesson guarantees you'll master it!

**Key features you can use:** **Set titles, collapse titles, and Outline view.**

**Step-by-step tutorial:**

<Note>
[Priority: 1] Find a DingTalk Doc that contains text content.

[Priority: 2] Based on your document content, format the title sections using the **heading fonts** in the navigation bar (font size can be customized).

[Priority: 3] Once all titles are set, click the **Menu** in the upper-left corner, select **View**, and turn **On** the **Outline**. The outline of the entire document will appear on the right. To quickly navigate, click any title within the outline to jump directly to the corresponding section.

[Priority: 4] In addition, titles within the document support content collapsing for easier viewing and editing. Hover over the front of a title row, and a triangle icon will appear. Click it to collapse the content under that title, making the document structure clear at a glance.

After these steps, isn't your document interface clean, organized, and well-structured? It's exactly the kind of document every boss loves!

Got it? Try it out now!
</Note>
