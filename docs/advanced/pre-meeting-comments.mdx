---
title: "Have Questions About Pre-Meeting Materials? Comment Directly for Efficient, Centralized Answers"
description: "Read pre-meeting materials in Quick Meeting, select any text to leave a comment and mention colleagues directly, so questions are resolved before the meeting starts rather than interrupting the discussion."
---

[Received meeting materials through Quick Meeting](/docs/advanced/shorten-meeting-time) before the meeting but have questions or thoughts about the content? In Quick Meeting, you can **read and leave comments** with the same interactive experience as DingTalk Docs.

➡️ As a meeting participant, after [receiving the Quick Meeting event invitation](/docs/advanced/schedule-meeting-tip), click **View minutes** on the event card to enter Quick Meeting.

➡️ Select the text, choose **Comment** from the pop-up toolbar, enter your content, and @mention the people to notify. The comment will be delivered through the Chats window.
