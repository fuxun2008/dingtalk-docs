[Received meeting materials through Quick Meeting](/docs/advanced/shorten-meeting-time) before the meeting but have questions or thoughts about the content? In Quick Meeting, you can **read and leave comments** with the same interactive experience as DingTalk Docs.

[Right arrow] As a meeting participant, after [receiving the Quick Meeting event invitation](/docs/advanced/schedule-meeting-tip), click **View minutes** on the event card to enter Quick Meeting.

[Right arrow] Select the text, choose **Comment** from the pop-up toolbar, enter your content, and @mention the people to notify. The comment will be delivered through the Chats window.
