description: "Nanjing Maiwan, a children's products e-commerce company, rolled out DingTalk company-wide and adopted DingTalk Docs for real-time collaborative editing and Work Logs for transparent reporting, significantly boosting team coordination and management visibility."

Founded in 2016, Nanjing Maiwan Children's Products Co., Ltd. upholds and practices the values of "Customer First, Integrity, Growth, and Collaboration," committed to providing quality products at affordable prices for families with children. The company operates a highly responsive supply chain system, with self-built warehousing to ensure product supply cycles and meet customers' personalized needs. It also partners with multiple cloud warehouses across the country to ensure efficient product circulation and deliver goods to customers at the fastest possible speed.

The Company adopts a flat management structure and the Amoeba management model. After rolling out DingTalk for all employees, internal communication efficiency improved dramatically. Features such as DingTalk Docs and Work Logs help the Organization boost Collaboration efficiency and unleash the creativity of Team members.

## 1. Online collaboration with DingTalk Docs — Boost efficiency and increase work flexibility

"After adopting DingTalk for daily work, communication and Interaction between Colleagues have become much more convenient," said Ms. Cheng, Operations Specialist at the Company. For Ms. Cheng, the biggest change brought by DingTalk is work clarity. Previously, when using other Apps, messages could easily be forgotten if not handled promptly. Collaboration between Colleagues also lacked transparency, and managers could not stay informed about ongoing work in a timely manner. Now, with DingTalk, Collaboration through DingTalk Docs is highly convenient: team members can co-edit online at any time, Document Content updates in real time, and Important items can be followed up by @mentioning Colleagues directly within the Document. This reduces the risk of forgetting tasks due to a busy workload and helps Employees work more efficiently.

## 2. Efficient work reporting with DingTalk Work Logs — Scheduled reminders and automatic statistics

In the past, work reporting at Maiwan was not timely. Employees reported through multiple management layers, which prevented executives from gaining direct visibility into frontline operations. They had to rely on supervisors to filter and relay information, resulting in opaque management and delayed awareness of work status. Compiling Data was also cumbersome, and work results could not be acknowledged in time.

After deploying DingTalk, the Company found that the Work Logs feature not only solved the issue of consolidating Employee reports, but also allowed published Work Logs to be commented on, liked, and shared. This made every Employee's strengths visible to leaders and provided learning opportunities for other Colleagues.

Employees can write daily reports through DingTalk Work Logs and sync them with the mobile App, enabling them to review Data anytime, anywhere and resolve issues promptly. This streamlines managers' workflow, and Work Logs allow quick traceability of reporting history, making work fully trackable. The feature meets the Company's needs for daily, weekly, and monthly reports. Reminder rules can be flexibly configured to automatically prompt Employees to Submit reports on time, with submission status displayed at a glance. Reported Data is aggregated automatically, and reports can be exported with one click on either mobile or desktop — an excellent option for knowledge retention and review.

## 3. End-to-end collaboration: DingTalk and Maiwan moving forward together

"DingTalk is easy to integrate and unifies our workplace tools. Everyone uses the same communication App, which works very well overall. DingTalk is not just used in e-commerce — it's adopted across many industries, so it offers a broad range of features. For some personalized requirements, DingTalk also provides Plugins that meet our needs." — This is how Mr. Wu Shilin, Chairman of Maiwan, recognizes DingTalk's value for daily work.

Indeed, in today's fully digital environment, collaboration tools not only digitize people, tasks, and assets within an Organization but also make communication and management more intelligent. Over the past few years, DingTalk and Maiwan have moved forward hand in hand, working to build comprehensive collaboration across multiple internal scenarios and grow together.
