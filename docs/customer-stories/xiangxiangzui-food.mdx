## Food and beverage industry

DingTalk helps organizations streamline internal processes, reduce costs, and improve efficiency. It provides a free platform for corporate culture development and an ongoing vehicle for talent cultivation. With DingTalk, we have accelerated information exchange with the market, enabling the company to quickly capture industry leadership. In just a few years, the company has grown rapidly at a yearly sales growth rate of 40%, with products earning unanimous recognition from the industry and consumers.

| **Pain point** | **Solution** | **Outcome** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment maintenance knowledge was not preserved, and new employees relied on verbal handover from senior staff. | Knowledge Base | Upload equipment maintenance information to the Knowledge Base, generate a QR Code, and attach it to the equipment. Employees can scan the code on the workshop equipment at any time to view the information. Permissions can be configured to prevent information leakage. |
