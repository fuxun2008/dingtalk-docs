## DingTalk Docs permission management safeguards document content

### 😖 Pain points

Many important documents lacked sufficient privacy controls, and adding multiple collaborators was cumbersome.

### 💡 Value

Document privacy is now guaranteed. Edit or view-only permissions can be assigned to specific users.

## Multi-role document permissions safeguard document Security

Many documents are used in daily work. To ensure Security, some documents should not be editable or viewable by everyone. With the Share feature, the right users can be selected for editing and viewing, preventing unauthorized modifications or leakage of Important matters, and greatly safeguarding the privacy and Security of each Document. In addition, when a Document requires simultaneous Edit by multiple Users, the Share button makes it easy to locate a User's Team and Add all members at once, eliminating the need to Add them one by one. This saves time and boosts efficiency.

## Online organization enables efficient Meeting coordination

Departments hold many Meetings on a daily basis, especially during the pandemic when employees work from home. Confirming schedules in advance and ensuring the effectiveness of Meetings both incur significant costs. With DingTalk, you can create a DingTalk Event to schedule attendance and send Notifications with one click. You can clearly see whether Members have scheduling Conflicts, and you can set Weekly, daily, or other recurring Event Reminders to avoid forgetting Repeated tasks or Weekly Meetings. Colleagues can Share Events with each other so others can easily view their schedules, greatly improving Meeting coordination efficiency. DingTalk Video Meeting and Audio Meeting features allow remote Colleagues to participate in Meetings in real time even when they are not in the Company. Group To-Do items and Group announcements also greatly facilitate information delivery for remote Meetings.
