---
title: "Zoom view"
description: "Adjust zoom to review spreadsheet formatting and preview how large content fits on a printed page."
---

When editing spreadsheets in your daily work, especially for formal files, the zoom view feature lets you review all formatting and layout consistently. Zoom view also helps when printing large content or content spanning multiple pages on a single page.

## How to use zoom view

Select any cell in the spreadsheet, click **Menu —> View —> Zoom**, and choose the desired ratio. Alternatively, click the **+** or **-** ratio buttons in the bottom-right corner of the spreadsheet for a more efficient experience.
