---
title: "Hide gridlines"
description: "When working with spreadsheets, hide the gridlines outside the worksheet to keep your spreadsheet clean and tidy."
---

When working with spreadsheets, use the **Hide gridlines** feature to temporarily hide the spreadsheet gridlines outside the worksheet, keeping the presentation clean and tidy.

## How to hide gridlines

Select any cell in the worksheet, then click **Menu > View > Hide gridlines** in the top navigation bar.

## How to show gridlines

Select any cell in the worksheet, then click **Menu > View > Show gridlines** in the top navigation bar.
