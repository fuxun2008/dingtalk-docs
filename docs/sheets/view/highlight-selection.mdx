---
title: "Highlight Selection"
description: "Highlight the active row and column in Sheets to prevent misalignment when viewing large reports."
---

Highlight the active row and column to clearly show the position relationship between them, preventing misalignment when viewing large reports.

## Highlight Selection

Select any cell in the Spreadsheet, then click **View > Highlight active row and column**. When you click any data in the Worksheet to view it, the row and column where the data is located will be highlighted in the **selected color** to avoid misalignment.

## How to Cancel Highlight Selection

Click **Menu > View > Cancel highlight active row and column** at the top.
