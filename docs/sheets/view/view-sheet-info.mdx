## Value

View readers, editors, and data content of a spreadsheet at a glance.

## Use case

Ding Sanduo is a product manager at an internet company. She uses DingTalk Spreadsheet to track the launch progress of new product features and collaborates with colleagues from the R&D and design departments to fill in information. With View spreadsheet details, she can clearly see how colleagues collaborate on the spreadsheet, including the dates and times of recent reads and edits.

## Steps

On the top navigation bar, click Spreadsheet > Document info > View spreadsheet details.
