To make a Spreadsheet look clean and tidy, it's important to unify or adjust the text alignment within the Spreadsheet. In daily Spreadsheet work, you typically select an entire Record, Column, or the whole Spreadsheet to apply alignment changes in one step—simple and efficient.

## Instructions

## Vertical alignment

### Align top

Text is aligned to the top of the Cell.

Select the target Cell, click **Menu > Format** at the top, then choose **Align > Align Top** from the drop-down menu. Alternatively, select the target text and click **Align Top** in the toolbar for quick adjustment.

### Align middle

Text is aligned to the vertical center of the Cell.

Select the target Cell, click **Menu > Format** at the top, then choose **Align > Align Middle** from the drop-down menu. Alternatively, select the target text and click **Align Middle** in the toolbar for quick adjustment.

### Align bottom

Text is aligned to the bottom of the Cell.

Select the target Cell, click **Menu > Format** at the top, then choose **Align > Align Bottom** from the drop-down menu. Alternatively, select the target text and click **Align Bottom** in the toolbar for quick adjustment.

## Horizontal alignment

### Align Left

### Center Align

Text is aligned to the horizontal center of the Cell.

Select the target Cell, click **Menu > Format** at the top, then choose **Align > Center Align** from the drop-down menu. Alternatively, select the target text and click **Center Align** in the toolbar for quick adjustment.

### Align Right

Select the target Cell, click **Menu > Format** at the top, then choose **Align > Align Right** from the drop-down menu. Alternatively, select the target text and click **Align Right** in the toolbar for quick adjustment.
