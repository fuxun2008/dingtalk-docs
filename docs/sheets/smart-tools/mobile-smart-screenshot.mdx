## Value

Capture smart screenshots on mobile to break free from screen limits and view all data clearly.

## Use cases

When sharing a screenshot on mobile, the Spreadsheet may be too long to capture in full, leaving you constrained by the screen size. The DingTalk Spreadsheet mobile app supports smart screenshots: **trigger the screenshot gesture to generate a data image with one tap** for easier sharing.

## Instructions

1. On a mobile phone or iPad, trigger the screenshot gesture to automatically detect the data area in the current Worksheet and generate a data image.

2. Forward and Share directly, or save the generated Image to your phone Album.
