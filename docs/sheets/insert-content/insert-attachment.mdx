Spreadsheet supports inserting attachments such as local files, images, audio, and video. Build your team's online resource library with ease.

## Instructions

1. Entry point: Insert > Attachment.

2. The attachment size limit is 251 MB for DingTalk Standard and 1 GB for DingTalk Professional.

3. Attachment formats that support online preview: documents, spreadsheets, presentations, images, video, and audio. Other formats must be downloaded and cannot be previewed.
