In DingTalk Spreadsheet, convert existing data into visual charts for intuitive presentation and analysis.

Spreadsheet currently supports multiple chart types, including **Column, Pie, Line, Area, Scatter, and Bubble**.

## Instructions

### Create a Chart

Select the data range you want to use for the chart, then click **Chart** in the toolbar to insert it. By default, the data is displayed as a **Column** chart.

### Edit a Chart

In the **Settings** tab of the chart editor panel, switch between chart types such as **Line, Pie, and Bar**, and configure options such as **chart color** and **data range**.

You can also apply **Custom** settings to the inserted chart, including the **chart title, axes, legend, series style, and rendering**. Available customizations vary by chart type, so adjust them based on your needs.

### Modify a Chart

To re-edit an existing chart, click **...** on the chart and select **Edit chart**. You can also **Delete** the chart or **Download** it as an image.
