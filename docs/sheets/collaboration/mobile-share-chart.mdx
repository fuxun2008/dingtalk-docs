## Value

Mobile devices support generating images from selected ranges for sharing.

## Use cases

For mobile work across various scenarios, share Chart Data easily from your phone. When you select a Cell range, ++**use the "Generate image" option in the floating menu**++ to share Data while preserving its original appearance.

## Instructions

1. On your phone or iPad, select a Cell range, open the floating menu, and tap "Generate image".

2. After tapping, generate an image of the selected range and Share it directly to a Chat, or Save it to your phone's Album.
