## Q: Why can't I edit a spreadsheet online in DingTalk?

Answer: Encrypted Excel files cannot be edited online.

## Q: How do I make filtered data visible only to myself?

Answer: Use the filter view feature on the toolbar. Path: Click **Filter** on the toolbar > **Create filter**. When filtering data, select **Only visible to me** for the filter results.

## Q: How do I change the permissions so that all users can view the spreadsheet?

Answer: If the permission scope is set to "Entire spreadsheet", click **Share** in the top-right corner to set user permissions.

## Q: How do I @ someone outside my organization in a spreadsheet?

Answer: Mentioning Contacts outside your organization is supported. Enter @ in a Cell to quickly select users outside your organization.

## Q: Where can I view the version History of a spreadsheet?

Answer: Click the navigation bar in the top-left corner, then go to **Menu > Spreadsheet >** **History**.

## Q: How do I download a DingTalk Spreadsheet?

Answer: Open the file, then click **Menu > Spreadsheet > Download as** in the top navigation bar to quickly save it locally.

## Q: What is the maximum number of Worksheets that can be created in an online Spreadsheet?

Answer: Up to 1,000 Worksheets are currently supported. For a better experience, we recommend not creating too many Worksheets in a single Spreadsheet.

## Q: How do I add Annotations to a specific spreadsheet?

Answer: Click a Cell, then right-click to add a comment.

## Q: Can the Find feature in a spreadsheet search across all Workbooks?

Answer: The Find feature currently only searches within the current Worksheet.

## Q: Can I print a Spreadsheet directly?

Answer: Yes. Click **Menu > Spreadsheet > Print** in the top navigation bar to print quickly.
