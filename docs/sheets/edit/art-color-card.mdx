## Value

Diverse color options to meet a variety of scenarios.

## Use cases

In DingTalk Spreadsheet, switch between Standard and artistic color palette styles to access a variety of new color schemes. Change the color of fonts, cell background colors, and worksheet tags at will to create visually appealing and diverse spreadsheet documents.

## Instructions

Font color, cell background color, and worksheet tag color all support switching.
