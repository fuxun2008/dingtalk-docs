元旦、春节、元宵节、五一、端午、中秋、国庆……节日扎堆，你是不是又在思考🤔，该如何给同事、客户送些新祝福呢？不用担心，**接下来三期内容，手把手教你用钉钉文档制作超有新意的有声贺卡。**

## 节日扎堆，用文档也能“花式”送祝福？简单有新意，一学就会

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/28a90523-e0c5-4b1e-a7c5-480192478434.png" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="image.png" />

##  第①期，贺卡背景设置

[向右]新建一篇钉钉文档，点击右上角【┇】**\>** **偏好设置 > 新建样式**

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/ae0bd997-ff85-4f43-aa17-bb794144d066.gif" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="/Users/wenshuang/Library/Application Support/iDingTalk/ScreenCapture/阿里钉录屏_2025-09-05 150732.gif" />

[向右] 再点击左上角 **菜单 > 视图> 文档背景** 添加 **背景颜色**。还可以点击更多颜色，输入色号，随心设置你想要的背景颜色哦。

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/5c31835f-292f-4641-bf9f-1420ed4291b1.gif" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="/Users/wenshuang/Library/Application Support/iDingTalk/ScreenCapture/阿里钉录屏_2025-09-05 150946.gif" />

## 第②期，内容排版设计

[向右] 鼠标放置于空白行，点击左端【+】或输入【/】添加分栏；再将鼠标悬浮于对应分栏，点击上方【颜色】按钮修改边框和背景颜色。

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/297db7b6-6067-4694-af7d-202b0911bb65.gif" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="/Users/wenshuang/Library/Application Support/iDingTalk/ScreenCapture/阿里钉录屏_2025-09-05 151913.gif" />

[向右] 在一侧分栏内输入**“/tp”**插入图片或动图，另一侧则输入祝福语，最后按住拖动分栏中间竖线调整布局。

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/db62f84f-fd8d-4f51-86b1-92286201b1a9.png" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="image.png" />

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/7fa4e878-bc6c-4960-894b-166cb7d16154.png" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="image.png" />

[向右] 同样，空白行点击左端【+】或输入【/】添加分割线，可以修改样式及粗细，进一步对内容进行排版美化哦。

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/55fd6aae-f7c2-46fe-a0d9-84088247d585.png" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="image.png" />

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/0bab2ceb-bad7-4ec9-b639-a65bbd5ac428.png" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="image.png" />

## 第③期，插入音乐 完成制作

[向右] 内容排版完成之后，点击右上角...-下载到本地-高清长图，根据喜好勾选设置后【保存图片】，一份简单又有创意的静态贺卡就做好啦。

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/6eb365c9-04ed-481d-8fdb-4091915ff41b.gif" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="/Users/wenshuang/Library/Application Support/iDingTalk/ScreenCapture/阿里钉录屏_2025-09-05 154502.gif" />

[向右] 如果想要有声效果，在文档内输入“**/yy**”添加背景音乐，复制粘贴单曲页地址，即可完成添加哦。

<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/MeYVOLgQxkPwqpz2/img/8b336f67-8fee-4969-8291-2bb900dcc21c.png" referrerPolicy="no-referrer" alt="image.png" />
