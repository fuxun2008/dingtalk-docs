---
title: "如何设置/取消全员看TA？"
description: "把单个参与者视频固定在主视图，对全员可见或仅对自己可见，用于聚焦当前发言人或演讲者。"
---

会议中，当主持人希望全员看某个参会发言人时，可设置 **全员看TA**。

---

## 电脑端设置/取消全员看TA

### 设置全员看TA

会议进行中界面>>单击底部<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN018na9fvhBuKK062dM_!!6000000008141-2-tps-48-48.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**成员** 按钮打开成员管理面板>>单击成员右侧的<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01TnwqvmPYscH062dM_!!6000000005209-2-tps-48-48.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**图标**\>>选择 **全员看TA**

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01MUcVp4FP9nL5Q6ia_!!6000000001014-2-tps-2670-1366.png)

### 取消全员看TA

会议进行中界面>>单击底部<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN018na9fvhBuKK062dM_!!6000000008141-2-tps-48-48.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**成员** 按钮打开成员管理面板>>单击带有全员看TA标签成员右侧的<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01TnwqvmPYscH062dM_!!6000000005209-2-tps-48-48.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**图标**\>>选择 **取消全员看TA**

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01VLijDQBxOtF5Q6iY_!!6000000004260-2-tps-2670-1364.png)

## 手机端设置/取消全员看TA

### 设置全员看TA

会议进行中界面>>单击底部<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN018na9fvhBuKK062dM_!!6000000008141-2-tps-48-48.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**成员** 按钮打开成员管理页面>>单击成员右侧的<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01TnwqvmPYscH062dM_!!6000000005209-2-tps-48-48.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**图标**\>>选择 **全员看TA**

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01TVCWsqSfwOE9sJ2I_!!6000000005153-2-tps-4864-5050.png)

### 取消全员看TA

会议进行中界面>>单击底部<img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN018na9fvhBuKK062dM_!!6000000008141-2-tps-48-48.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**成员** 按钮打开成员管理页面>>单击带有全员看TA标签成员>>选择 **取消全员看TA**

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01JvHquKtfYsB9sJ2I_!!6000000001885-2-tps-4864-5050.png)
