---
title: "如何设置/取消全员看TA？"
description: "把单个参与者视频固定在主视图，对全员可见或仅对自己可见，用于聚焦当前发言人或演讲者。"
---

会议中，当主持人希望全员看某个参会发言人时，可设置**全员看TA**。

---

## 电脑端设置/取消全员看TA

### 设置全员看TA

会议进行中界面>>单击底部<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ABmOoRYVaDjzqawZ/img/54cf843c-953d-49a5-9a86-fb4351d16699.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**成员**按钮打开成员管理面板>>单击成员右侧的<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/8K4nyawwm2rYnLbj/img/0101593f-a567-482d-8507-aa28035a76e7.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**图标**\>>选择**全员看TA**

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4j6OJ5jRzA6keq3p/img/de4ada1e-379e-4426-9fa3-6235630f8e26.png)

### 取消全员看TA

会议进行中界面>>单击底部<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ABmOoRYVaDjzqawZ/img/54cf843c-953d-49a5-9a86-fb4351d16699.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**成员**按钮打开成员管理面板>>单击带有全员看TA标签成员右侧的<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/8K4nyawwm2rYnLbj/img/0101593f-a567-482d-8507-aa28035a76e7.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**图标**\>>选择**取消全员看TA**

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4j6OJ5jRzA6keq3p/img/f1d2086b-4cd3-474f-889f-c390393d5f65.png)

## 手机端设置/取消全员看TA

### 设置全员看TA

会议进行中界面>>单击底部<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ABmOoRYVaDjzqawZ/img/54cf843c-953d-49a5-9a86-fb4351d16699.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**成员**按钮打开成员管理页面>>单击成员右侧的<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/8K4nyawwm2rYnLbj/img/0101593f-a567-482d-8507-aa28035a76e7.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**图标**\>>选择**全员看TA**

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4j6OJ5jRzA6keq3p/img/f7b0c665-f5b6-46f5-96d4-64525048671b.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_230,w_4864,h_5050/ignore-error,1)

### 取消全员看TA

会议进行中界面>>单击底部<img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ABmOoRYVaDjzqawZ/img/54cf843c-953d-49a5-9a86-fb4351d16699.png" alt="image.png" style={{display:'inline-block',height:'1.2em',width:'auto',verticalAlign:'-0.2em',margin:'0 2px'}} />**成员**按钮打开成员管理页面>>单击带有全员看TA标签成员>>选择**取消全员看TA**
