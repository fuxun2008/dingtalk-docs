## 第三方 AI 应用开发流程概览

### **前提条件**

1. 已完成[产品方案商](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/become-an-application-service-provider)入驻。
2. 已获得开发者权限。详细信息，参考[获取开发者权限](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/get-developer-permissions#)。

### **使用限制**

目前 AI 应用开发平台处于邀请测试阶段，只开发 AI 应用可以走测试组织进行体验，但不能直接上架。如果需要上架 AI 应用，必须联合开发小程序应用或网页应用。详细信息，参考[（可选）测试应用](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/optional-test-application#)和[自检应用](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/self-test-application#)。

### **流程概览**

1. [创建应用](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/create-an-application#)
2. [添加 AI 应用能力](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/add-ai-application-capabilities#)
3. [（可选）添加开发人员](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/optional-add-developers#)
4. 开发应用

   1. [搭建 AI 能力](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/build-a-magic-wand-skill#)
   2. [配置魔法棒应用](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/configure-the-magic-wand-application#)
   3. [配置 AI 助理](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/optional-configure-digital-workers#)
5. 发布 AI 应用

   1. [监控应用](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/monitoring-applications#)
   2. [部署应用](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/deployment-method-introduction#)
   3. [发布 AI 应用](/zh/open/development/publish-application)
   4. [配置 AI 助理](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/optional-configure-digital-workers#)
   5. [自检应用](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/self-test-application#)
