**Senario**: mengira pertumbuhan MoM (bulan-ke-bulan) untuk pesanan.

**Data**: Medan [Month]: 1-12; Medan [Order count].

**Langkah**

1. Tambah medan bantu [MoM helper] dengan Formula `[Month]-1`.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN0145lI4y1Gx9da0aPq8_!!6000000000688-2-tps-1836-1016.png)

2. Guna Carian untuk menambah [Order count_lagged] merujuk kepada [Order count]; Syarat carian: [Month] = [MoM helper]; pengagregatan: nilai asal.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01DRQ0YI1MfeBzKrROy_!!6000000001462-2-tps-1836-1016.png)

3. Formula kadar pertumbuhan MoM: `IFERROR(([Order count]-[Order count_lagged])/[Order count_lagged],"")`.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01mBqyUv1lje2klUX9Q_!!6000000004855-2-tps-1836-1016.png)
