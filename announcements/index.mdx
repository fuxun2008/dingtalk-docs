<Update label="2026-10-21" tags={["Plan changes"]}>
  ### [Notice of Changes to DingTalk Plans for International Customers](/announcements/international-plan-changes-2026)

  Beginning October 21, 2026, DingTalk introduces four plans for international customers: Free, Basic, Standard, and Premium. New orders for the legacy Standard and Professional editions will no longer be accepted, and active contracts are not affected.
