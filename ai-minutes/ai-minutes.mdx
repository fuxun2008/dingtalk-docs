AI Minutes is an intelligent note-taking tool launched by DingTalk and powered by the Tongyi large model. It can be used for meeting minutes, course training, interviews, customer visits, and other scenarios. It supports real-time transcription, translation, smart speaker recognition, minutes summaries, To Do summaries, and more, helping improve work and learning efficiency.

---

  <Card title="Online Meetings: Keep Meeting Minutes" icon="video">

  <Card title="Live Training: Turn Live Content Into Retained Knowledge" icon="tower-broadcast">
    Convert live-stream content into AI Minutes with one click, then quickly view the transcript, smart chapters, and smart summary.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Insert AI Minutes Into Docs With One Click" icon="file-pen">
    Text transcribed from recordings can be exported as text or inserted directly into a document, making it easier to edit and organize.
