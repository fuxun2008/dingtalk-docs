AI Minutes is an intelligent note-taking tool launched by DingTalk and powered by the Tongyi large model. It can be used for meeting minutes, course training, interviews, customer visits, and other scenarios. It supports real-time transcription, translation, smart speaker recognition, minutes summaries, To Do summaries, and more, helping improve work and learning efficiency.

---

# Feature Overview: Supported Scenarios

## Online Meetings: Keep Meeting Minutes

Enable cloud recording during an online meeting. After the meeting, you can view AI Minutes to generate a transcript, smart chapters, and post-meeting minutes.

## Live Training: Turn Live Content Into Retained Knowledge

Convert live-stream content into AI Minutes with one click, then quickly view the transcript, smart chapters, and smart summary.

## Local Videos: Convert Content Into Digital Assets

Import local videos into AI Minutes with one click, export AI Minutes transcripts to a knowledge base, and build enterprise digital assets.

## Insert AI Minutes Into Docs With One Click

Text transcribed from recordings can be exported as text or inserted directly into a document, making it easier to edit and organize.

## Real-Time Recording: A Handy Offline Communication Tool

Record audio in real time so no recording opportunity is missed. After recording ends, quickly generate summaries and To Dos.

## Hardware Recording: Easier Offline Meetings

Use office hardware devices such as DingTalk A1 and meeting room displays to capture high-quality audio, improve transcription accuracy, and support offline team meetings.
