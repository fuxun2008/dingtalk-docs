## 1. Overview

DingTalk supports four attendance methods: **location-based clock-in, Wi-Fi clock-in, Zhidian Bluetooth clock-in**, and **Time Clock clock-in**. Admins can refer to this article to learn the use cases, advantages, and disadvantages of each method, and select the option that best fits their Organization.

## 2. Details

| **Clock-in method** | **Use case** | **Advantages** | **Disadvantages** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Location-based clock-in** | Suitable for Organizations with a **fixed Work Location** that allow users to clock in within a designated range (for example, 3,000 meters). | 1. **Supports mobile clock-in**: Users can clock in without waiting in line, improving efficiency. <br />    <br />2. | **Wi-Fi clock-in** | The Work Location **has office Wi-Fi**, allowing users to connect to the Company Wi-Fi to clock in, and provides a **relatively complete MAC address**. | 1. **Supports mobile clock-in**: Users can clock in without waiting in line, improving efficiency. <br />    <br />2. Supports clock-in via **facial recognition**. <br />    <br />3. **Enables field attendance management**: Users can also perform field clock-in when working outside or from home. | 1. **Multiple inconsistent MAC addresses**: Users may be marked as field clock-in even when they are within the designated range. <br />    <br />2. **Mobile clock-in only**: Users can only clock in from a mobile device. |

## 3. FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can I do if the Time Clock does not support field clock-in?">

To work around the fact that the Time Clock does not support field clock-in, Organizations can use location-based clock-in and Time Clock clock-in together to cover both on-site and field attendance. Go to **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global Settings > Add attendance group**, or edit an existing attendance group, then go to **Clock-in method > Location-based clock-in** and turn **Location-based clock-in** On.
