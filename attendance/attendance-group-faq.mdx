<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="q1" title="How do I set up a shift-based attendance group?">

When employees in the same attendance group have different working hours, choose **shift-based attendance** to customize their shifts.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step>

As an Admin, open DingTalk on desktop > Workbench > Attendance > Attendance Group Management > Add Attendance Group.

<Frame caption="Example of the Add Attendance Group page on desktop">
![Add Attendance Group page on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01tGXiwDA6kWL1yU2a_!!6000000000842-2-tps-973-523.png)
</Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step>

Click Attendance Time Settings > Shift-based (clock in and out by shift).

<Frame caption="Example of the shift-based attendance setup page on desktop">
![Shift-based attendance setup page on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01yyBIZGknsMC1xLnV_!!6000000002140-2-tps-964-517.png)
</Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step>

Fill in the basic information for the attendance group and select the applicable shifts. Configure the remaining options according to the on-page instructions, and click Save when done.

<Frame caption="Example of selecting shifts under shift-based attendance on desktop">
![Selecting shifts under shift-based attendance on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01OTC6Ol3LbWB1uCgX_!!6000000001541-2-tps-939-530.png)
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="q2" title="How do I add a new shift?">

The shift owner can **delete** or **modify** their own shift. By default, the **shift creator** can invite other Admins to co-manage the shift.

As an Admin, open **DingTalk on desktop > Workbench > Attendance > Shift Management > Add Shift**, and create shifts for shift-based attendance based on your company's needs.

<Frame caption="Example of the Add Shift page on desktop">
![Add Shift page on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01T2QamzxRulE28o21_!!6000000000906-2-tps-1055-566.png)
</Frame>

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q3" title="How do I schedule shifts?">

Admins, attendance group owners, and scheduling owners can schedule shifts for attendance users. DingTalk currently supports the following scheduling methods: **daily scheduling, cycle-based scheduling, and Excel bulk scheduling**.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

  </Step>
</Steps>
## Daily scheduling

1.  As an Admin, open **DingTalk on desktop > Workbench > Attendance > Scheduling Management > the corresponding attendance group** to schedule shifts. On the schedule Table, click the **attendance date** for the **attendance user** and select a shift.

<Frame caption="Example of daily scheduling on desktop">
![Daily scheduling on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01yd2dZRnUWJJ2310o_!!6000000007053-2-tps-1009-541.png)
</Frame>

1.  Click a specific date to assign the same shift to **all users** in the attendance group with **one click**.

<Frame caption="Example of the shift scheduling page on desktop">
![Shift scheduling page on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01GR3LnSk2RLK21d9w_!!6000000001375-2-tps-998-534.png)
</Frame>

## Cycle-based scheduling

1.  If no scheduling cycle has been set, add one by following these steps: **Scheduling Management > Scheduling Cycle Settings > Add Scheduling Cycle**. You will then be redirected to the setup page.

<Frame caption="Example of setting a scheduling cycle on desktop">
![Setting a scheduling cycle on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01C92vxYqtBrJ28o1y_!!6000000005761-2-tps-1055-563.png)
</Frame>

2.  Once a cycle is set, click the employee's name and select **cycle-based scheduling** to **schedule an entire month's shifts in one go**.

<Frame caption="Example of cycle-based scheduling on desktop">
![Cycle-based scheduling on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01GkxXAQQDwjE1zrtP_!!6000000001869-2-tps-984-527.png)
</Frame>

## Excel bulk scheduling

Click **Excel Bulk Scheduling > Download Schedule Table >** schedule shifts for employees in the downloaded schedule Table **>** then **import the schedule Table** and click **Save**.

<Frame caption="Example of Excel bulk scheduling download/import on desktop">
![Excel bulk scheduling download and import on desktop](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01oelKz4DXDPK24eTR_!!6000000001276-2-tps-1022-547.png)
</Frame>

<Frame caption="Example of an Excel schedule Table">
![Excel schedule Table](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01G02JVnhJNfI1yMEe_!!6000000002237-2-tps-972-520.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
**Note:**

Be sure to **add attendance shifts in advance** before downloading the schedule Table. Otherwise, the downloaded Excel file will not include any shifts.
</Note>
