---

## 1. Use cases

To export attendance reports for multiple months (or a full year) at once, upgrade to the DingTalk Professional edition and use the Annual Summary feature.

**Example scenarios:**

- Export attendance data from January to June at once for a summary.
- Export the full year of 2023 attendance data at once for a summary.
- And so on.

## 2. How to use the annual summary report

### Step 1: Create an annual summary report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Attendance app">
    Open and sign in to DingTalk on desktop, select "Workbench," and open the "Attendance" app.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lR4aj1GgJq8g5/img/01cdb811-f235-4017-8779-c5c7a8ef9303.png" alt="Open the Attendance app" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Report management">
    Select "Report management" under attendance statistics.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lR4aj1GgJq8g5/img/40e47bb5-259b-4cc3-86a5-8d2ee0262fd9.png" alt="Select Report management" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an annual summary report">
    Click "Add report," select "Annual Summary," and create an annual summary report. Standard edition DingTalk users must upgrade to the Professional edition first.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lR4aj1GgJq8g5/img/b096745f-819c-4dfe-a990-b8a605ee0006.png" alt="Create an annual summary report" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add statistics columns">
    After creating the annual summary report, use "Add column" to add the data columns you want to track to the report.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lR4aj1GgJq8g5/img/8c0b5b04-d6e3-4517-bf49-4d745775b09f.png" alt="Add statistics columns" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Export the annual summary report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report details page">
    Open "Attendance," select "Report management" under attendance statistics, find the "Annual Summary" report, and click "View" to open the report details page.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lR4aj1GgJq8g5/img/d36444c0-dfae-4f54-b5a2-43b38fd0479f.png" alt="Open the report details page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the span to export">
    Now you can select a time span longer than one month to export, **up to a maximum of 12 months**.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lR4aj1GgJq8g5/img/b9ccc3fd-3476-47a0-b1f7-c618a44daab2.png" alt="Select the span to export" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
