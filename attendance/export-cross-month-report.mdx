## 1. Use cases

To export attendance reports for multiple months (or a full year) at once, upgrade to DingTalk Pro and use the Annual Summary feature.

**Example scenarios:**

- Export attendance data from January to June at once for a summary.
- Export the full year of 2023 attendance data at once for a summary.
- And so on.

## 2. How to use the annual summary report

### Step 1: Create an annual summary report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Attendance app">
    Open and sign in to DingTalk on desktop, select **Workbench**, then open the **Attendance** app.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN010b7yyApenGC3xTNs_!!6000000004294-2-tps-1950-1366.png" alt="Open the Attendance app" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Report management">
    Under attendance statistics, select **Report management**.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01SjAG8tqv7yF4GbiK_!!6000000007188-2-tps-2102-1466.png" alt="Select Report management" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an annual summary report">
    Click **Add report**, select **Annual Summary**, and create an annual summary report. Standard edition users must first upgrade to DingTalk Pro.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01AR6KL6EamkB4GbiK_!!6000000004009-2-tps-2102-1466.png" alt="Create an annual summary report" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add statistics columns">
    After creating the annual summary report, click **Add column** to add the data columns you want to track.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01dVFswGRWQ9L4GbiK_!!6000000001771-2-tps-2102-1466.png" alt="Add statistics columns" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Export the annual summary report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report details page">
    Open **Attendance**, select **Report management** under attendance statistics, locate the **Annual Summary** report, and click **View** to open the report details page.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN011JcgValSX1B4GbiK_!!6000000000014-2-tps-2102-1466.png" alt="Open the report details page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the span to export">
    Select a time span longer than one month to export, **up to a maximum of 12 months**.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01PR3bL0eZaxH5JoQO_!!6000000002557-2-tps-2620-1128.png" alt="Select the span to export" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
