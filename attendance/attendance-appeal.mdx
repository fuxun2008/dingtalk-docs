Appeals are a capability of Attendance Security Enhanced Edition. After you purchase the Enhanced Edition and enable **Detect cheating and block clock-in**, any cheating clock-in is blocked. If an employee believes the block is a mistake, they can file an appeal. **Once the admin approves it, the clock-in takes effect.**

## Employee action path

After a clock-in is blocked, the employee taps **Appeal**, takes a photo, enters the reason for the appeal, and selects an admin to send it to.

## Admin action path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Handle from the card">
    After receiving the card sent by the employee, tap **Handle** to open the details page, then **Accept** or **Decline** the appeal.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01fKkeZkttlaE1o2M6_!!6000000006298-2-tps-890-904.png" alt="Admin handling an appeal from the card" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="View all appeal records">
    Go to **Attendance → Statistics → Anti-cheating → Appeal records** to view all appeal records, including pending and previously handled ones.
