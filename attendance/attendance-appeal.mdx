Appeals are a capability of the Attendance Security Enhanced Edition. After you purchase the Enhanced Edition and enable **Detect cheating and block check-in**, any cheating check-in is blocked. If an employee believes the block is a mistake, they can file an appeal. **Once the admin approves it, the check-in takes effect.**

## Employee operation path

After a check-in is blocked, the employee can tap the **Appeal** button, take a photo, enter the reason for the appeal, and select an admin to send it to.

## Admin operation path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Handle from the card">
    After receiving the card sent by the employee, tap **Handle** to open the details page, then select **Accept** or **Decline** the appeal.
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/Mp7ld7b1KP0XAOBQ/img/bababa82-41f0-4fba-9a13-87b5fc01a03b.png" alt="Admin handling an appeal from the card" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="View all appeal records">
    Go to **Attendance → Statistics → Anti-cheating → Appeal records** to view all appeal records, including pending and previously handled ones.
    <Frame>

    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
