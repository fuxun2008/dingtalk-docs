## Background and pain points

In daily attendance management, more and more organizations use "photo check-in" to verify whether employees have actually arrived on site or completed store inspection tasks. However, the following common problems occur in real use:

- Employees upload invalid images such as ceilings, floors, black screens, or blank walls.
- Photos do not include a face, so they cannot prove the person was present.
- During store inspections, photos of shelves or displays are blurry or taken from the wrong angle and do not reflect the real situation.
- Special job requirements (such as wearing a safety helmet, wearing a reflective vest, or complying with grooming standards) lack effective inspection methods.
- Admins must manually review hundreds or thousands of photos one by one, which is time-consuming, labor-intensive, and inefficient.

## Product solution

To solve these management challenges, we launched the new "AI error detection for photo check-in" feature. Using **large models and image understanding technology**, it automatically identifies non-compliant check-in behavior and helps organization managers achieve efficient, accurate remote supervision.

- **Access point**: Attendance app → Statistics → More statistics → Check-in photo statistics.
- **Content**: After entering, view the analysis results for today's and historical check-in photos, including the total number of check-in photos, the number of qualified photos vs. unqualified photos, and the error type breakdown. Click View details to verify each photo.
- **Generation time**: This currently runs as an asynchronous post-event detection mode. Analysis usually completes and results are generated on the morning after check-in.
