"Show the dedicated anti-cheating label" is a capability of the Attendance Security Enhanced edition. After you buy the Enhanced edition and turn on this feature, employees see the **Enhanced attendance security protection is on** prompt on the check-in Home page.

## Turn on the setting

<Info>
Path: **Attendance → Statistics → Anti-cheating → Show the dedicated anti-cheating label**
</Info>
