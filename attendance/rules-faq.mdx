## Attendance group

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="q1" title="How do I set up an attendance group?">

You can set up three types of attendance groups: fixed shift attendance, shift-based attendance, and flexible working hours. The setup paths are as follows:

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
### How to set up a fixed shift attendance group

1.  **Mobile:** As Admin, go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Add attendance group > Attendance type**, and select **Fixed clock-in and clock-out time**. Fill in the remaining fields as prompted on the page.

2.  **Desktop:** As Admin, go to **Desktop DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Attendance group management > Add attendance group > Attendance time > Settings > Attendance type**, and select **Fixed clock-in and clock-out time**. Fill in the remaining fields as prompted on the page.

### How to set up a shift-based attendance group

1.  **Mobile:** As Admin, go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Select the attendance group > Scheduling** to open the scheduling page. Select day-by-day scheduling, choose the corresponding shift, and save the settings.

2.  **Desktop:** As Admin, go to **Desktop DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Scheduling management > Select the attendance group > Scheduling**, follow the on-page prompts to schedule shifts, and save the settings.

<Note>
If your shift pattern is fixed (for example, two days on and one day off, or five days on and one day off), you can configure a scheduling cycle to schedule shifts more efficiently. For details, refer to **Scheduling cycles**.
</Note>

### Flexible clock-in and clock-out time

1.  **Mobile:** As Admin, go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Add attendance group > Attendance type**, select **Flexible clock-in and clock-out time**, fill in the remaining fields as prompted, and save.

2.  **Desktop:** As Admin, go to **Desktop DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Attendance group management > Add attendance group > Attendance type**, select **flexible working hours**, fill in the remaining fields as prompted, and save.

<Note>
You can set the daily attendance start time, which defines when a new attendance day begins.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="q2" title="Office staff work from 8 AM to 5 PM daily, but a group of field employees have irregular hours and locations. How do I set this up?">

Set up a **fixed shift** attendance group for office staff, and set up a separate **flexible clock-in and clock-out time** attendance group for field employees. Enable **field clock-in** in the field employees' attendance group.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q3" title="Our work cycle is three days on and one day off. How do I configure scheduling?">

You can set up a scheduling cycle. As Admin, go to **Desktop DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Scheduling management > Scheduling settings > Cyclic scheduling > Add cyclic scheduling > Days per cycle**, and configure it as needed.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q4" title="Different departments in our company work morning, midday, or evening shifts. Do I need to set each one up individually?">

As Organization Admin, go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Select the attendance group > Scheduling** to open the scheduling page. Select day-by-day scheduling, choose the corresponding shift, and save the settings. If scheduling shifts in advance is inconvenient, enable **Let employees choose a shift when unscheduled** and preset a few shifts for employees to pick from. Path: **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings >** tap the shift-based attendance group **> Edit attendance group > Scheduling settings >** turn **When unscheduled, let employees select a shift to clock in** on or off.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q5" title="Different departments in our company work morning, midday, or evening shifts. Do I need to set each one up individually?">

Desktop: First, set up your attendance shifts (create one shift per specific work time; for details on shift configuration, refer to how to set up shifts in **2. Shifts**). After completing the shift setup, follow this path: As Admin, go to **Desktop DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Attendance group management > Add attendance group > Attendance time settings > Attendance type > Shift-based**, complete the attendance details, save, then open scheduling and finalize the schedule.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
## Shifts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="q6" title="How do I set up shifts (add or edit)?">

Follow these paths to set up shifts:

1.  **Mobile:** As Admin, go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Shift management > Add shift**, or tap the **Edit** button next to an **existing shift** to **edit** it. If you already have an **attendance group**, tap **Enter attendance group**, then tap **Attendance time** to **edit the shift**.

2.  **Desktop:** As Admin, go to **Desktop DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Shift management > Add shift** to add a new shift (click **Edit** next to a shift to **modify** it, or click **Delete** to **delete** it).

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q7" title="How do I add or modify shift clock-in times?">

Go to **Desktop DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Shift management > Add or edit existing shift.**

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q8" title="How do I set up day shifts and night shifts (morning, midday, and evening shifts)?">

Day shifts and night shifts are simply different shifts with different times. Set up the shifts first, then adjust the times. Shift setup path: Go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Shift management > Add shift.**

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q9" title="I had a rest day on the 10th of last month but forgot to schedule it as rest. How do I update the shift now?">

For shift-based attendance, the primary owner of the attendance group or a secondary owner with scheduling permissions can reschedule shifts and mark the 10th of last month as a rest day. Path: Go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Select the attendance group > Scheduling**, and change it to a rest day.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q10" title="Under shift-based attendance, how do I change the working hours for a specific day?">

Create a new shift in shift settings, then add it to the current attendance group that needs the change. Path to create a new shift: As Admin, go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Shift management > Add shift**, then tap the corresponding shift-based attendance group **> Scheduling** and reassign the shift.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
## Scheduling

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="q11" title="Why can't I schedule shifts for a fixed shift attendance group?">

Attendance groups fall into three types: **fixed shift attendance, shift-based attendance, and flexible working hours**. Fixed shift attendance groups do not support scheduling. You can either **change** the attendance group type or **add** a new shift-based attendance group. To change the **attendance group type**, follow these paths:

1.  **Mobile:** As Admin, go to **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > the attendance group > Attendance type** to review and switch the type.

2.  **Desktop:** As Admin, go to **Desktop DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Attendance group management > Edit the attendance group > Attendance time > Settings > Attendance type** to review and switch the type.

<Note>
After **changing the attendance type**, previously configured **special clock-in date data cannot be restored**.
</Note>

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q12" title="After setting up attendance groups and shifts, how can department managers schedule shifts on their own?">

Set the department manager as the attendance group owner. They can then schedule shifts via this path: **Mobile DingTalk > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Global settings > Select the attendance group > Scheduling**. Open the scheduling page, select day-by-day scheduling, choose the corresponding shift, and save the settings.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
