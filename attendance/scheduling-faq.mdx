  <Accordion id="q1" title="How can attendance group owners schedule shifts? I've already enabled the scheduling permission for attendance group sub-owners, but it still doesn't work. Why?">

Sub-owners can only perform this action on the mobile app.

Open **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Select the target attendance group > Scheduling**.

## Scheduling management on the mobile app

Open **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Settings**, then tap **View All > Select a shift-based attendance group to schedule shifts**.

<Frame caption="Example of the scheduling management page on the mobile app">
![Scheduling management page on the DingTalk mobile app](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01rDHb42T5yrI2WyY4_!!6000000005708-2-tps-1247-673.png)
</Frame>

## Scheduling management on the desktop

Go to [**Admin Console**](https://oa.dingtalk.io) **> Workbench > App Management > Attendance > Enter > Scheduling Management**.
