## 1. Overview

Attendance notifications include **personal attendance statistics notifications** and **team attendance statistics notifications**. Customize the delivery method and schedule for daily, weekly, and monthly reports, and the system sends notifications based on your settings.

## 2. Use cases

Suitable for organizations that need to push attendance statistics notifications.

## 3. Procedure

### Personal attendance statistics notifications

<Steps>
  <Step>

Admins: go to DingTalk Mobile > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > More Settings > Notification Management > Personal Attendance Statistics Notifications for Employees, and turn the personal daily, weekly, or monthly attendance reports on or off.

<Note>
**Note: For details about personal attendance statistics notifications, see the related documentation.**
</Note>

  </Step>
  <Step>

Employees: go to DingTalk Mobile > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Personal Attendance Statistics Notifications, turn the daily, weekly, or monthly reports on or off, and choose Work Notifications or DingTalk Mail as the delivery channel.

</Steps>
### Team attendance statistics notifications

At the start of each week and each month, the attendance system automatically sends team attendance statistics to the Super Admin, organization owner, attendance Admins, and supervisors. The Content includes **overall attendance data and errors**. Notifications can be delivered via **Work Notifications and DingTalk Mail**, and recipients can choose whether to receive them.

Settings path: **DingTalk Mobile > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > Personal Settings > Team Attendance Statistics Notifications**

## 4. FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="q1" title="Why isn't there a daily attendance report for fixed shift attendance with mobile clock-in?">

Currently, only Time Clock clock-ins trigger daily attendance reports.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q2" title="How can I resubscribe to weekly attendance report emails after unsubscribing?">

You cannot resubscribe to weekly attendance report emails after unsubscribing. Proceed with caution.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q3" title="Our organization has more than 5,000 employees. Why aren't personal daily or weekly attendance reports being pushed?">

Personal daily and weekly attendance reports are only available for organizations with fewer than 5,000 employees. If the employee count exceeds this limit, daily and weekly attendance reports are no longer pushed.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q4" title="Our organization has more than 500 employees. Why aren't team daily or weekly attendance reports available?">

Team daily and weekly attendance reports are only available for organizations with fewer than 500 employees.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q5" title="I'm an employee. Why can't I turn on personal attendance statistics notifications?">

Personal attendance statistics notifications can only be enabled or disabled centrally by Admins on the mobile app. Once the Admin turns the feature on, Users in the organization can toggle their own personal attendance statistics notifications. Admins with the required Permissions can follow this path: Open **DingTalk Mobile > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > More Settings > Notification Management > Personal Attendance Statistics Notifications for Employees >** turn **the personal daily, weekly, or monthly attendance reports** on or off.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
