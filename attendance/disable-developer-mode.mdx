---
title: "How to turn off developer mode"
description: "DingTalk attendance anti-cheating requires developer mode to be turned off before clock-in. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for disabling developer options on Huawei/Honor, Xiaomi/Redmi, OPPO, vivo, and other Android devices."
polished: true
---

To keep attendance data accurate and maintain a fair working environment, turn off developer mode before you clock in. Select your phone brand below to view the corresponding steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Huawei / Honor">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open System & updates">
        Open the **Settings** screen on your phone, then tap **System & updates**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01dAAAUdc7VfH2BxaZ_!!6000000000661-2-tps-1080-2263.png" alt="Open System & updates" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find Developer options">
        Find **Developer options** under **System & updates**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01EohEd0jX6AF2BxaZ_!!6000000005014-2-tps-1080-2263.png" alt="Find Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn off Developer options">
        Open the **Developer options** page, then turn off Developer options.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN019QGWAYwDXBD2Bxaa_!!6000000001332-2-tps-1080-2264.png" alt="Turn off Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Xiaomi / Redmi">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Additional settings">
        Open the **Settings** screen on your phone, then tap **Additional settings**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN010zePbsVRUfL2Bxb4_!!6000000005653-2-tps-1080-2294.png" alt="Open Additional settings" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find Developer options">
        Find **Developer options** on the **Additional settings** screen.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01UL3dbXcHmzG2Bxb8_!!6000000006370-2-tps-1080-2298.png" alt="Find Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn off Developer options">
        Open the **Developer options** page, then turn off Developer options.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01bHf1yVWhXmG2Bxb3_!!6000000002695-2-tps-1080-2293.png" alt="Turn off Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN017ADWW9d6F2G2Bxar_!!6000000003407-2-tps-1080-2281.png" alt="Open System management" />

  <Tab title="General method">
    The following example uses a Huawei phone. Other phones work in a similar way (this method may not apply to some older models).

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Search for Developer">
        On the **Settings** page of your phone, search for **Developer**. An entry similar to **Developer options** appears.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN012nDsHAvtIjC2Bxbo_!!6000000004392-2-tps-1080-2340.png" alt="Search for Developer" />

        <Frame>
          <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01APhnJib4xHB2Bxbo_!!6000000007329-2-tps-1080-2340.png" alt="Turn off Developer options" />

    </Steps>
  </Tab>

<Note>
Because Android phones are highly fragmented, the steps to turn off developer options may vary slightly even between two units of the same model. Follow the actual steps shown on your device.
