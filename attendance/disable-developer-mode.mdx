---
title: "How to turn off developer mode"
description: "DingTalk's attendance anti-cheating feature requires you to turn off developer mode before checking in. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for turning off developer options on Huawei/Honor, Xiaomi/Redmi, OPPO, vivo, and other brands."
---

To ensure attendance data is accurate and to maintain a fair working environment, turn off developer mode before checking in. Select your phone brand below to view the corresponding steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Huawei / Honor">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open System & updates">
        Open the **Settings** screen on your phone, then tap **System & updates**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/a5c6fbe6-5fed-4cbf-b236-75a9a0be8e73.png" alt="Open System & updates" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find Developer options">
        Find **Developer options** under **System & updates**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/7fae9d71-23c7-423b-a96a-d35357ebc292.png" alt="Find Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn off Developer options">
        Open the **Developer options** page, then turn off Developer options.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/faea2e7a-46ff-4c87-ba65-cecd1624a61c.png" alt="Turn off Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Xiaomi / Redmi">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Additional settings">
        Open the **Settings** screen on your phone, then tap **Additional settings**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/c16728bf-f6a5-4c27-81a0-b529695a019b.png" alt="Open Additional settings" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find Developer options">
        Find **Developer options** on the **Additional settings** screen.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/25148194-676a-4787-b313-5dfc228358f7.png" alt="Find Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn off Developer options">
        Open the **Developer options** page, then turn off Developer options.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/d6b95389-75aa-4d45-9885-a4cc2537f4dd.png" alt="Turn off Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="OPPO">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open System & updates">
        On the **Settings** screen of your phone, find **System & updates**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/228f39c3-3354-4692-a2fc-75099a70a7a1.png" alt="Open System & updates" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find Developer options">
        On the **System & updates** screen, find **Developer options**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/2fbbdef6-3408-4cdc-900d-a29431a4bf5b.png" alt="Find Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn off Developer options">
        On the **Developer options** screen, turn off Developer options.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/4af82f56-bcb2-4e8b-9b6f-35618a936532.png" alt="Turn off Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="vivo">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open System management">
        On the **Settings** screen of your phone, tap **System management**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl0wmbDmXZO34Y/img/1c5f3178-bca1-4559-a505-f4f83056bc79.png" alt="Open System management" />
        </Frame>

        <Frame>

      </Step>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="General method">
    The following uses a Huawei phone as an example. Other phones work in a similar way (this method may not apply to some older models).

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Search for Developer">
        On the **Settings** page of your phone, search for **Developer**. You will see an entry similar to **Developer options**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/Q35O8LpPg35BO9Vb/img/cdced6e1-9f68-4419-af72-7ad960bebc9a.png" alt="Search for Developer" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn off Developer options">
        Tap to open the Developer options screen, then turn off Developer options.
        <Frame>
          <img src="https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/Q35O8LpPg35BO9Vb/img/4b1bdb34-b531-4397-8251-05b4c663b2a5.png" alt="Turn off Developer options" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

<Note>
Because Android phones are highly fragmented, the steps to turn off developer options may vary slightly even between two units of the same model. Follow the actual steps on your device.
</Note>
