title: "How to fix inaccurate location?"
description: "Troubleshooting and fixes for inaccurate GPS location in DingTalk Attendance: update the App, turn on Wi-Fi and location service switches, check location permissions and phone security software restrictions, with step-by-step guidance for background location settings on Huawei phones."
---

Due to the current limitations of positioning technology and the complex, changing positioning environments, location errors and issues are unavoidable. After confirming that the inaccurate location is not caused by your surroundings (such as being in an enclosed environment like a basement or elevator), try the following steps.

## 1. General troubleshooting steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update the DingTalk App to the latest version">
    Update to the latest version for the best location experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Wi-Fi">
    You do not need to connect to a wireless network—just turn on the switch. Wi-Fi scanning can correct minor deviations based on nearby hotspots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the location service switch">
    <div style={{display: 'flex', gap: '12px', justifyContent: 'center', flexWrap: 'wrap', margin: '16px 0'}}>
      <img src="https://gw.alicdn.com/tfs/TB1rO8qRXXXXXaSaXXXXXXXXXXX-1080-835.jpg" alt="Turn on the location service switch" style={{width: '52%', minWidth: '260px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
      <img src="https://gw.alicdn.com/tfs/TB18U4gQXXXXXXMXFXXXXXXXXXX-300-534.png" alt="Location service switch settings" style={{width: '26%', minWidth: '160px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check whether the DingTalk App has location permission">
    Go to Phone Settings → App Management → Manage permissions → Get location, and check whether location access is allowed.
    <div style={{display: 'flex', gap: '12px', justifyContent: 'center', flexWrap: 'wrap', margin: '16px 0'}}>
      <img src="https://gw.alicdn.com/tfs/TB1elFmQXXXXXbMXpXXXXXXXXXX-540-960.png" alt="Check the DingTalk App location permission" style={{width: '30%', minWidth: '180px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check whether security management software disables location permission">
    Check whether the security management software installed on your phone has disabled the App's location permission. Such software is usually preinstalled, as on Huawei, OPPO, vivo, and Xiaomi phones.
    <div style={{display: 'flex', gap: '12px', justifyContent: 'center', flexWrap: 'wrap', margin: '16px 0'}}>
      <img src="https://gw.alicdn.com/tfs/TB1EWXuQXXXXXa3XXXXXXXXXXXX-246-437.png" alt="Check the location permission settings in security management software" style={{width: '30%', minWidth: '180px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Background location settings on Huawei phones

<Note>
Recently, background location has been found to be restricted on some Huawei phones (even a foreground service cannot perform background location). The main cause is that Huawei's security software, Phone Manager, restricts the app's background activity. The specific fix is as follows.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Huawei's Phone Manager app">
    <div style={{display: 'flex', gap: '12px', justifyContent: 'center', flexWrap: 'wrap', margin: '16px 0'}}>
      <img src="https://a.amap.com/lbs/static/locSDK/%E5%8D%8E%E4%B8%BA%E5%90%8E%E5%8F%B0%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/11.png" alt="Open Huawei Phone Manager" style={{width: '30%', minWidth: '180px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the launch management screen">
    <div style={{display: 'flex', gap: '12px', justifyContent: 'center', flexWrap: 'wrap', margin: '16px 0'}}>
      <img src="https://a.amap.com/lbs/static/locSDK/%E5%8D%8E%E4%B8%BA%E5%90%8E%E5%8F%B0%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/22.png" alt="Go to the launch management screen" style={{width: '30%', minWidth: '180px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the manual management screen">
    Check whether the App is set to auto management. If so, tap the toggle (or the App name) to go to the manual management screen.
    <div style={{display: 'flex', gap: '12px', justifyContent: 'center', flexWrap: 'wrap', margin: '16px 0'}}>
      <img src="https://a.amap.com/lbs/static/locSDK/%E5%8D%8E%E4%B8%BA%E5%90%8E%E5%8F%B0%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/33.png" alt="Go to the manual management screen" style={{width: '30%', minWidth: '180px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Allow background activity">
    <div style={{display: 'flex', gap: '12px', justifyContent: 'center', flexWrap: 'wrap', margin: '16px 0'}}>
      <img src="https://a.amap.com/lbs/static/locSDK/%E5%8D%8E%E4%B8%BA%E5%90%8E%E5%8F%B0%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/44.png" alt="Turn on Allow background activity" style={{width: '30%', minWidth: '180px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the manual management status">
    After you tap Confirm, the App is set to manual management.
    <div style={{display: 'flex', gap: '12px', justifyContent: 'center', flexWrap: 'wrap', margin: '16px 0'}}>
      <img src="https://a.amap.com/lbs/static/locSDK/%E5%8D%8E%E4%B8%BA%E5%90%8E%E5%8F%B0%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/55.png" alt="App is set to manual management" style={{width: '30%', minWidth: '180px', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)', margin: 0}} />
    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>
