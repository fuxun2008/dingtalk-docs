---

Automatic shift matching offers two methods: Match by clock-in time and Match by clock-in and clock-out time. See the examples below.

## 1. Match by clock-in time

Match the shift based on the employee's **first clock-in**. This works for rotating shifts and overnight shifts.

<Note>
**Example:** An attendance group has a day shift from 8:00 to 20:00 and a night shift from 20:00 to 8:00 the next day, with the automatic matching window set to 2 hours before the shift starts. If an employee clocks in between 06:00 and 08:00, the automatic matching result is the day shift. If they clock in between 18:00 and 20:00, the result is the night shift.
</Note>

## 2. Match by clock-in and clock-out time

Match the shift based on the employee's **first and last clock-in**. This works for same-day shifts that share the same start time.

<Note>
**Example:** An attendance group has a long day shift from 8:00 to 20:00 and a regular day shift from 8:00 to 17:00. If an employee clocks in at 7:50 and out at 18:30 on the same day, the automatic matching result is the regular day shift.
</Note>
