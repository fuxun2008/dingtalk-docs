## Overview

Admins can **turn message notifications on or off by default for employees**, including clock-in reminders, personal daily, weekly, and monthly attendance reports, and missed clock-in reminders. These defaults apply only to employees who have not previously adjusted the related settings themselves. Once the defaults take effect, employees can still change the settings in the Attendance app.

## Use cases

This feature is designed for Organization Admins who want to configure default clock-in message notifications for employees.

## Steps

### Clock-in reminders

#### Personal clock-in reminders

Go to **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Personal Settings > Clock-in Reminder** to configure **DING Reminder / Work Notifications / System Alarm Reminder.**

<Note>
**Notes:**

1. DING Reminder: sends scheduled notifications with a continuous ringtone.

2. Work Notifications: sends scheduled app notifications.

3. System Alarm: triggers the phone's alarm as a reminder.
</Note>

#### Enabling clock-in reminders as an admin

Admins with the required permissions can go to **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > More Settings > Notification Management > Clock-in Reminders for Employees** to turn the reminders **On/Off**.

### Personal daily, weekly, and monthly attendance reports

Admins with the required permissions can go to **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > More Settings > Notification Management > Personal Attendance Statistics Notifications for Employees** to turn personal daily, weekly, and monthly attendance reports **On/Off**.

<Note>
Notes:

1. Personal daily attendance report: reminds employees to review their statistics after work each day.

2. Personal weekly attendance report: reminds employees to review their statistics every Monday. It can be delivered via Work Notifications or DingTalk Mail.

3. Personal monthly attendance report: reminds employees to review their statistics on the first working day of each month.
</Note>

### Missed clock-in reminders

Admins with the required permissions can go to **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > More Settings > Notification Management > Missed Clock-in Reminders for Employees** to turn the reminders **On/Off**.

## What happens when defaults are enabled

### Clock-in reminders

Once an admin enables clock-in reminders for employees, those who do not change the settings receive reminders 10 minutes before their shift starts.

### Personal weekly and monthly attendance reports

When enabled, employees receive a monthly clock-in summary on the first working day of each month and a weekly clock-in summary every Monday by default.

### Missed clock-in reminders

When enabled, missed clock-in reminders for the previous day are pushed as Work Notifications after 10:00 each day.
