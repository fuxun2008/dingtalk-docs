DingTalk International attendance is the **Standard edition**, designed for simple attendance scenarios. It supports scheduling and working hours calculation for most organizations free of charge, and covers the following core capabilities:

- Attendance rules configuration
- Employee scheduling
- Mobile clock-in and Time Clock clock-in
- Attendance approval
- Attendance statistics and reports
- Attendance confirmation

## Standard edition feature list

The table below lists the features supported by the Standard edition. Some capabilities have usage limitations under the Standard edition (noted in the "Description" column).

| Module | Feature | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic settings | Work calendar | Automatically syncs national statutory holidays such as New Year's Day and Spring Festival (the Standard edition does not support custom holidays) |
| Basic settings | Working hours system | Supports the standard working hours system (the Standard edition does not support the comprehensive or non-fixed working hours systems) |
| Basic settings | Attendance approval | Handles attendance errors through approvals such as leave request, going out, business trip, and makeup clock-in |
| Attendance group management | Attendance members | Quickly creates attendance groups by user or department; new employees joining a department are synced automatically |
| Attendance group management | Attendance time | Supports fixed shift attendance and shift-based attendance, as well as flexible working hours (as long as the required working hours are met) |
| Attendance group management | Attendance methods | Supports multiple methods, including location-based clock-in, Wi-Fi clock-in, Bluetooth clock-in, and Time Clock clock-in |
| Attendance group management | Association rules | Configures overtime rules, makeup clock-in rules, field clock-in, and going out / business trip rules per attendance group |
| Shift management | Fixed shift | Supports fixed shifts, with one or multiple clock-in/out sessions per day |
| Shift management | Flexible shift | Supports early arrival with early leave, late arrival with late leave, and late leave allowing late arrival the next day |
| Shift management | Error rules | Configures late arrival and early leave errors based on shift clock-in checkpoints |
| Scheduling management | Scheduling by user | Supports scheduling by user |
| Scheduling management | Scheduling by cycle | Sets one or multiple cycles for recurring scheduling |
| Employee clock-in | Time Clock clock-in | DingTalk Time Clock and access control devices support fingerprint and facial clock-in |
| Employee clock-in | Mobile clock-in | Supports precise GPS clock-in, company Wi-Fi, and Time Clock Bluetooth clock-in |
| Makeup clock-in management | Makeup clock-in count | Sets the monthly makeup clock-in count |
| Makeup clock-in management | Makeup clock-in type | Configures whether errors such as late arrival, early leave, and missed clock-in can be corrected via makeup clock-in |
| Makeup clock-in management | Makeup clock-in time limit | Sets a time limit for makeup clock-in requests |
| Overtime management | Overtime type | Configures overtime rules independently for workdays, rest days, and holidays |
| Overtime management | Overtime period | Configures whether time before or after a shift counts as overtime |
| Overtime management | Overtime duration calculation | Calculates by clock-in duration, approval duration, or clock-in duration within the approved period |
| Overtime management | Overtime duration rounding | The Standard edition only supports rounding down |
| Overtime management | Deducting break time | Deducts by time period or by overtime duration |
| Overtime management | Overtime compensation | Converts to overtime pay or compensatory leave, or lets employees choose |
| Overtime management | Risk alerts | Sets overtime alert thresholds by day, week, or month |
| Leave management | Manual leave grant | Grants leave balance manually |
| Leave management | Scheduled leave grant | Grants leave balance on a yearly or monthly basis according to rules |
| Leave management | Overtime to compensatory leave | Automatically converts overtime duration to compensatory leave |
| Attendance statistics | View team statistics by day/week/month | Displays employee attendance records and results in real time |
| Attendance statistics | View employee statistics by day/week/month | Lets employees view their own attendance records and results |
| Attendance report | Original clock-in records | Includes original clock-in records, clock-in time, and records of suspected cheating |
| Attendance report | Summary statistics report | Exports summary reports by day or month |
| Attendance report | Modify attendance results | Lets Admins modify attendance results individually or in bulk |
| Attendance report | Attendance confirmation | Sends attendance confirmations so employees can confirm their attendance data promptly |
