DingTalk Attendance offers multiple verification methods to confirm that employees clock in themselves, prevent buddy punching, and help organizations build a fair and impartial work environment. The following section describes three common verification methods that prevent buddy punching.

## Three anti-buddy-punching verification methods

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clock-in phone recognition">
    Employees can only clock in with their own phones. If an Employee uses their own phone to clock in on behalf of someone else, the action is recorded and triggers a security check. Admins can review and confirm the record in the attendance history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Photo clock-in">
    When Employees clock in—especially when visiting clients off-site—they can upload a photo taken with the client to confirm that they have arrived at the work location and started working. Admins can then review the photo in the attendance records for confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Facial recognition">
    During clock-in, facial recognition quickly verifies whether the Employee is clocking in themselves. Anyone attempting to clock in on behalf of another person will fail the facial recognition check.
  </Step>
</Steps>
