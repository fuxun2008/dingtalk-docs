## 1. Feature overview

DingTalk Attendance provides organizations with a simple, efficient, and smart employee attendance management solution. With the Attendance app, employees can easily clock in, submit overtime requests, and complete other actions based on the rules set by their organization, helping the organization improve attendance management efficiency.

## 2. Use cases

*   Employees who take part in daily attendance clock-in within the organization.

*   Employees who submit leave requests, business trip requests, overtime requests, makeup clock-in requests, or other actions.

## 3. Workflow

### 3.1 Attendance clock-in

*   Clock-in path: Open **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and go to the **Attendance** app. Based on your attendance schedule, tap the **Clock-in** button to complete the clock-in.

*   View attendance rules or the attendance group owner: Open **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and go to the **Attendance** app, then tap **View Rules** at the top.

![Sample of the employee clock-in page](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01zSMFnAivMLE1IRKu_!!6000000002310-2-tps-639-685.png "(Sample of the employee clock-in page)")

### 3.2 Submit overtime, makeup clock-in, and other approval requests

*   Attendance request path: Open **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and go to the **Request** page in the **Attendance** app. Submit requests as needed, including makeup clock-in, leave request, overtime, out-of-office, and more.

*   View historical request records: Open **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and go to **Attendance > Request > Request Records**.

### 3.3 Data statistics

*   View your attendance: Go to **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and open the **Attendance** app, then tap **Statistics** to view your attendance, including late arrivals, early leaves, missed clock-ins, and other records. (This path is visible only if the Admin allows employees to view attendance data.)

*   Handle your attendance errors: Go to **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and open the **Attendance** app, tap **Statistics >** tap the error **attendance date > Handle Error**.

### 3.4 Personal settings

Go to **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and open the **Attendance** app **> Settings**. Customize options such as clock-in reminder, Quick Check-in, clock-in result notification, missed clock-in reminder, personal attendance statistics notifications, face management, and more.
