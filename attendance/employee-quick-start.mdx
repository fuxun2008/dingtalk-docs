## 1. Feature overview

DingTalk Attendance provides organizations with a simple, efficient, and smart employee attendance management solution. With the Attendance app, employees can easily clock in, submit overtime requests, and complete other actions based on the rules set by their organization, helping the organization improve attendance management efficiency.

## 2. Use cases

*   Employees who take part in daily attendance clock-in within the organization.

*   Employees who submit leave requests, business trip requests, overtime requests, makeup clock-in requests, or other actions.

### 3.1 Attendance clock-in

*   Clock-in path: Open **DingTalk mobile > Discover > Attend**. Check the organization, shift, required times, and attendance range. Connect to the specified office Wi-Fi or enter the configured location range before tapping the relevant clock-in/out button. Confirm the successful result. **Unable to clock in/out** indicates that a prerequisite has not been met; the example below is not connected to the required Wi-Fi.

*   View rules: Open **Attend > Statistics > My > View Rules** to check your attendance rules. The clock-in page may also show this link beside the attendance group name.

### 3.2 Submit overtime, makeup clock-in, and other approval requests

*   Submit a request: Open **DingTalk mobile > Discover > Approval**, then choose the template configured by your admin, such as leave request, overtime, business trip, or makeup clock-in. The current Attendance bottom bar contains **Attendance**, **Statistics**, and **Settings**; use the separate Approval app for requests.

*   View your request records: Open **Approval > I initiated**. This entry shows requests you have submitted and their approval status.

<Note>
**Notes:**

The admin controls which approval templates are available. If a template is missing, contact your admin. Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved.
</Note>

### 3.3 Data statistics

*   View your attendance: Open **Discover > Attend > Statistics**. Admins should switch from **Team** to **My**. Select the day, week, or month to review your own records. Available data depends on your attendance group, schedule, and data visibility settings.

*   Review an exception: Select the affected date in your personal statistics and check the records and rules. If a correction is needed, open **Approval** and use the appropriate template enabled by your admin. Available actions and updates depend on the rules and approval outcome.

### 3.4 Personal settings

Go to **DingTalk mobile > Discover > Attend > Settings** to review your personal settings, including **Clock-in/out Reminder**, **Express Clock-in/out**, **Clock-in/out Result**, **Missing Clock-ins/outs Reminder**, **Schedule synced to calendar**, and **My Attendance Devices**. Admins should select **My** for personal settings; **Global** contains organization settings such as attendance groups. Available options depend on the client and organization configuration.
