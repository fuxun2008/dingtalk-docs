## 1. Feature overview

DingTalk Attendance provides organizations with a simple, efficient, and smart employee attendance management solution. With the Attendance app, employees can easily clock in, submit overtime requests, and complete other actions based on the rules set by their organization, helping the organization improve attendance management efficiency.

## 2. Use cases

*   Employees who take part in daily attendance clock-in within the organization.

*   Employees who submit leave requests, business trip requests, overtime requests, makeup clock-in requests, or other actions.

## 3. Workflow

<Note>
Select the correct organization in Workbench before opening Attendance. Your admin must add you to the appropriate attendance group and configure its rules. Available requests, statistics, and settings depend on your organization's configuration and your permissions.
</Note>

### 3.1 Attendance clock-in

*   Clock-in path: Open **DingTalk mobile > Workbench > Attendance** in the correct organization. Check the displayed shift, clock-in time, and attendance range, then tap the clock-in or clock-out button for the required session. Confirm that a successful clock-in result appears.

*   View attendance rules or the attendance group owner: In **Attendance**, tap **View Rules** near your attendance group information to check the rules and group owner.

![Sample of the employee clock-in page](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01zSMFnAivMLE1IRKu_!!6000000002310-2-tps-639-685.png "(Sample of the employee clock-in page)")

### 3.2 Submit overtime, makeup clock-in, and other approval requests

*   Attendance request path: In **Attendance**, tap the **Request** entry to open the request page. Submit requests as needed, including makeup clock-in, leave request, overtime, out-of-office, and more.

*   View historical request records: Open **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and go to **Attendance > Request > Request Records**.

<Note>
**Notes:**

The admin controls whether the Request entry is available and which request templates you can use. If an entry or template is missing, contact your admin. Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved.
</Note>

### 3.3 Data statistics

*   View your attendance: Go to **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and open the **Attendance** app, then tap **Statistics** to view your attendance, including late arrivals, early leaves, missed clock-ins, and other records. (This path is visible only if the Admin allows employees to view attendance data.)

*   Handle your attendance errors: Go to **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and open the **Attendance** app, tap **Statistics**, select the date with an attendance exception, then tap **Handle Error**. Follow the available options to submit a makeup clock-in or another relevant request. Whether it can be submitted and how the result is updated depend on the attendance rules and approval outcome.

### 3.4 Personal settings

Go to **DingTalk mobile > Workbench >** and open the **Attendance** app **> Settings**. Review the settings available to your account, such as clock-in reminders, Quick Check-in, result notifications, missed clock-in reminders, and personal attendance statistics notifications. Additional options, including face management, depend on the client version and organization settings.
