The statistics rules for Clocked In and Expected but Not Clocked In in clock-in details are as follows.

## Clocked in

The total number of employees who actually clocked in on the day. This **includes**:

- Employees with a normal clock-in, field clock-in, late clock-in, early leave clock-in, or clock-in records that include a missed clock-in;
- Employees on leave, business trip, or out-of-office status who have clocked in;
- Employees who were not scheduled but have clocked in.

## Expected but not clocked in

The total number of employees who were required to attend on the day but did not clock in. This **includes**:

- Employees who were scheduled but did not clock in;
- Employees who did not clock in during a business trip or while out of office.

<Note>
The following cases are **excluded**: employees on all-day leave, rest, or not scheduled. If clock-in is not required for out-of-office or business trips, employees on all-day out-of-office or business trips are also excluded.
</Note>
