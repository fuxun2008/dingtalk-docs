---
title: "Rules for check-in details statistics"
description: "This article explains the DingTalk attendance check-in details statistics rules. It clarifies the inclusion and exclusion scope of the two metrics, Checked in and Expected but not checked in, to help admins understand attendance statistics accurately."
---

The statistics rules for Checked in and Expected but not checked in in check-in details are as follows.

## Checked in

The total number of employees who actually checked in on the day. This **includes**:

- Employees with a normal check-in, field check-in, late check-in, early leave check-in, or check-in records that include a missed check-in;
- Employees on leave, business trip, or out of office status who have checked in;
- Employees who were not scheduled but have checked in.

## Expected but not checked in

The total number of employees who were required to attend on the day but did not check in. This **includes**:

- Employees who were scheduled but did not check in;
- Employees who did not check in during a business trip or when out of office.

<Note>
The following cases are **excluded**: employees on all-day leave, rest, or not scheduled. If check-in is set as not required for out of office or business trips, employees on all-day out of office or business trips are also excluded.
</Note>
