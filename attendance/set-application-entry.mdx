## 1. Feature overview

The DingTalk **Attendance request** entry is a module that lets employees submit various attendance-related requests (such as leave, overtime, and makeup clock-in) through the DingTalk platform. This entry integrates multiple types of attendance request forms and supports custom configuration to meet the specific needs of different organizations. Employees can complete a request in just a few steps, and managers can respond to and process these requests quickly, enabling an efficient and transparent attendance management process.

## 2. Applicable scenarios

Quickly submit requests such as leave, overtime, and makeup clock-in.

## 3. Procedure

Open **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Settings > More settings > Attendance approval > Enable quick request entry**.

<Note>
Once enabled, a **Request** entry is added to the employee's **Attendance home page**, making it easier to submit approvals. The visible scope matches the setting configured in the approval admin console.
</Note>

<Note>
**1. When enabled**: The **Request** entry is **displayed** in the Attendance app, letting employees easily submit requests such as leave, overtime, and business trips.

**2. When disabled**: The **Request** entry is **hidden** in the Attendance app, and employees cannot submit requests from the Attendance app.
</Note>

## 4. FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="q1" title="Will employees be notified when the quick request entry is disabled?">

Whether the quick **Request** entry on the Attendance page is enabled or disabled, employees are notified via Work Notifications.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="q2" title="Why doesn't the attendance quick request entry display the approval template?">

Approval form templates shown under **Request** on the Attendance page require the Attendance Suite to be enabled before they appear in the request entry, so that employees can submit approval forms.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
