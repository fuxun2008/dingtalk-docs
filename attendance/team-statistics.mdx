At the start of each week and month, DingTalk Attendance automatically sends team attendance statistics to the Super Admin, Organization owner, attendance administrators, and managers. The content covers overall attendance data and errors. Statistics are delivered via **Work Notifications** and **Mail**, and recipients can choose whether to receive them.
