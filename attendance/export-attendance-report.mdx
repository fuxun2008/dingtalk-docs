## 1. Feature overview

In daily organization management, attendance reports are essential references for evaluating employee attendance, calculating payroll, and conducting performance reviews. DingTalk provides powerful **attendance statistics and export capabilities**, allowing you to generate detailed attendance reports by department, user, or time range.

## 2. Workflow

**Organization owner / Sub Admin / Admin: View and export attendance data**

### View and export attendance data of active employees

Open **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Statistics > Export report >** select the **desired report > Confirm >** select the **start date / end date > user scope > Export report**.

<Note>
**Report notes:**

1. Daily statistics: The mobile app supports online viewing of daily data only within the last 6 months. To view data older than 6 months, you must export the report.

2. Summary statistics: The mobile app supports online viewing of data only within the last 3 months. To view data older than 3 months, you must export the report.

3. Suspected cheating records, clock-in records, and raw records can only be viewed by exporting reports.
</Note>

### Attendance certificate

Open **DingTalk desktop app > Workbench > Attendance > Attendance statistics > Attendance certificate**, select the **attendance period** and the corresponding **former employees > Export certificate**.

<Note>
**Export notes:**

1. An attendance administrator can export attendance records of former employees within their managed departments.

2. Each export can cover a time range of up to 12 months.

**Tips:**

Under applicable laws and regulations, when determining employment relationships or handling voluntary overtime pay for employees, the employer bears the burden of proof for attendance records. If the employer refuses to provide such records, the employer shall bear the adverse consequences.
</Note>

Open **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Statistics > Team statistics >** select **Daily / weekly / monthly statistics** to view, or tap **Export report** at the top **>** select the **date, report type, and user scope > Export report**. ### Department managers viewing and exporting attendance data

<Note>
A department manager can view attendance data of employees in their own department and its subordinate departments, but cannot view attendance data of all employees in the company.
</Note>

Open **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Statistics > Team statistics >** select **Daily / weekly / monthly statistics** to view, or tap **Export report >** select the **date, report type, and user scope > Export report**. The exported report is delivered via Work Notifications.

### Employees viewing and exporting attendance data

#### Active employees

Open **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Statistics**. You can view attendance data within the last **6 months** by **day / week / month**. Employees cannot export their own attendance data for the past 6 months. To request an export, contact an Admin, attendance group owner, or department manager.

<Frame caption="Example of active employees viewing attendance data on the mobile app">
![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4jKqm0bvdx7oDnw1/img/ba36402a-63b8-4575-a81c-d8d95633a0c8.png)
</Frame>

Open **DingTalk mobile app > Workbench > Attendance > Statistics > View by month**, then tap a **month older than 6 months > Go to export >** select the **query time range** (each export can span up to one month) **> Export report**.

<Note>
1. Attendance statistics older than 6 months must be exported to view; online viewing is not supported for records older than 6 months.

2. Historical data exports take 1–3 days to complete. Once ready, the report is delivered via Work Notifications.
</Note>

<Frame caption="Example of active employees exporting attendance data on the mobile app">
![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4jKqm0bvdx7oDnw1/img/06e94cbb-d13c-4d4c-a980-d485e0fce642.png)
</Frame>

#### Former employees

*   If you have left the company and exited the organization, you cannot view or export attendance data yourself. We recommend contacting an Admin at your former company who has attendance permissions to help export the data.

*   If the company does not cooperate, we recommend using a legal notice to request the records from your former company.

## 3. FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I export half a year of attendance data at once on the mobile app?">

Because only one month of data can be displayed per view, exporting half a year of data requires multiple batches.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How far back can I export attendance data on the Free plan?">

DingTalk Standard supports exporting attendance data within the last 180 days (including former employees). We recommend upgrading to DingTalk Pro / Exclusive / Attendance Enterprise plan, or exporting and backing up your attendance data promptly.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How far back can I export attendance data on DingTalk Pro?">

Pro and Exclusive editions support exporting attendance data within the last 2 years (upgrade now for a limited-time offer to export data within the last 5 years).

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if I need to export attendance data older than 2 years?">

The DingTalk Attendance Enterprise plan supports exporting attendance data within the last 5 years.

  </Accordion>
