DingTalk Attendance has built a comprehensive attendance security protection strategy that intelligently identifies attendance behaviors with security risks and protects your company's attendance data security in real time. The following detection rules help prevent attendance cheating.

## Anti-cheating detection rules

<Steps>
  <Step title="Phone identification to prevent proxy check-in">
    Employees can only check in with their own phones, and each phone generates a unique identifier. When an employee checks in with someone else's phone, a security check is triggered. The employee must take a photo to confirm they have arrived at the attendance location, and the Admin can view the photo in the attendance records to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Malware identification to block virtual positioning">
    Malware includes virtual positioning software, phone emulators, parallel spaces, and other software that obtains or tampers with phone information. When an employee uses malware to check in with a virtual location, an attendance security check is triggered. The employee is prohibited from checking in or required to take a photo to confirm, and the Admin can view the photo in the attendance records to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DingTalk version identification to keep the latest version">
    The attendance anti-cheating strategy is continuously updated and iterated. When an employee checks in, the DingTalk version is identified. Versions that are too low (below Version 5.0) are blocked, and the employee is reminded to update in time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Any behavior that violates the above security rules is recorded in the security protection statistics, which you can view on mobile. Related information is also recorded in the raw attendance records table and the employee's monthly attendance calendar.
</Note>

<Info>
DingTalk Attendance continuously iterates its product to improve compliance. In line with the requirements for protecting sensitive personal facial information under the Personal Information Protection Law and other relevant laws and regulations, the DingTalk platform iterates its product in time. For organizations that enable facial attendance check-in scenarios, it upgrades to require separate personal Authorization for entering facial base images and clarifies the scenarios and scope of Data use.
