Before enabling Quick Check-in, make sure you understand and agree to how Quick Check-in works.

## 1. Turn Quick Check-in on or off freely

DingTalk Quick Check-in is developed and provided by DingTalk as an automatic attendance check-in service. You choose whether to enable, use, and manage it on your own, **without being restricted by the Admin authorized by your Organization**.

## 2. Never locates you in the background

To perform the automatic check-in of Quick Check-in, within a certain time window, Quick Check-in accesses your location once each time DingTalk is opened from the background or the network is switched while using DingTalk. It then compares locally on your phone whether you have arrived within the attendance range.

**Time windows:**

- **Clock-in**: from "2 hours before start time" to "1 hour after start time"
- **Clock-out**: from "end time" to "4 hours after end time"

**In any of the following cases, Quick Check-in will not access your location:**

- DingTalk is not open on your phone.
- Check-in is already complete (unless you have turned on Auto-update for the clock-out record).
- The time is outside the time windows.

## 3. Protecting your location privacy

<Check>
Quick Check-in compares your location locally on your phone. If your location matches the attendance range, the system records and uploads your current location and checks you in automatically. After a successful check-in, it no longer accesses your location.

If your location does not match the attendance range set by your Company, Quick Check-in stops. Your location is neither recorded nor uploaded. Quick Check-in will not upload any location outside the attendance range.
</Check>
