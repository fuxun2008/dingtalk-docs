DingTalk Attendance has built a comprehensive attendance security protection strategy that intelligently identifies attendance behaviors with security risks and protects company attendance data security in real time.

Limiting the number of frequently used phones for attendance is one of DingTalk Attendance's anti-cheating features. Attendance admins can set the number of frequently used phones for employees to prevent employees from cheating check-in with multiple phones, or to restrict check-in on behalf of other employees.

---

## 1. Admin settings

Admins can set the number of frequently used phones for employees and the verification method after the limit is exceeded.

**Number of frequently used phones:**

- **No limit**: Any phone can check in, with no security verification.
- **Limit**: Up to 3 phones, 1 recommended.

**Verification method when the limit is exceeded:**

- **Take photo confirmation (default)**: Employees must take a photo to confirm they have arrived at the attendance location. Admins can view the confirmation photo in the attendance records.
- **Facial recognition (recommended)**: Employees must complete facial recognition. Check-in succeeds after recognition passes. If no face has been enrolled, employees are prompted to enroll their face before checking in.
- **Block check-in**: Employees cannot check in. They must switch to a phone bound as their frequently used phone, or contact the admin to delete a bound frequently used phone.

## 2. Employees bind their phones

When employees check in for the first time or use a new phone to check in, that phone is automatically bound as a frequently used phone. When the number of bound phones exceeds the limit set by the admin, no more phones can be bound.

<Note>
**FAQ:** If an employee has already reached the number of frequently used phones set by the admin, and their phone breaks so they buy a new phone to check in, can it be bound?

If the DingTalk Attendance system detects that the current phone is a new phone, it can be bound as a new frequently used phone. During binding, the employee must unbind the old phone.
</Note>

---

## 3. Employee check-in verification

When employees use another person's frequently used phone, or exceed the number limit set by the admin, security verification is triggered. To continue checking in, employees must take a photo with an alias, complete facial recognition, or are not allowed to check in, according to the requirements set by the admin.

<Info>
Any behavior that violates the above security rules is recorded in the security protection statistics, which you can view on mobile. Related information is also recorded in the attendance raw record table and the employee attendance monthly calendar.

In addition, DingTalk Attendance continues to iterate on its product and improve compliance. In line with the requirements of the Personal Information Protection Law and other relevant laws and regulations for protecting sensitive personal information such as facial data, the DingTalk platform iterates its product in a timely manner. For organizations that enable facial recognition check-in, it upgrades the separate individual authorization for enrolling a base face image and clarifies the scenarios and scope of data use. For existing facial photos (including facial check-ins triggered by attendance errors), it optimizes data retention and performs deletion to protect sensitive personal information in accordance with the law.
</Info>
