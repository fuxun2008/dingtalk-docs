DingTalk Attendance has built a comprehensive attendance security protection strategy that intelligently identifies risky attendance behavior and protects your company's attendance data in real time.

Limiting the number of frequently-used phones is one of DingTalk Attendance's anti-cheating features. Attendance admins can set the number of frequently-used phones per employee to prevent employees from cheating with multiple phones or clocking in on behalf of others.

---

## 1. Admin Settings

Admins can set the number of frequently-used phones per employee and the verification method applied once that limit is exceeded.

**Number of frequently-used phones:**

- **No limit**: Any phone can clock in, with no security verification.
- **Limit**: Up to 3 phones; 1 is recommended.

**Verification method when the limit is exceeded:**

- **Photo confirmation (default)**: The employee must take a photo to confirm they have arrived at the attendance location. Admins can review the photo in the attendance records.
- **Facial recognition (recommended)**: The employee must pass facial recognition before clocking in successfully. If no face is enrolled, the employee is prompted to enroll first.
- **Block clock-in**: The employee cannot clock in and must switch to one of their bound frequently-used phones, or contact the admin to remove a bound frequently-used phone.

## 2. Employee Phone Binding

When an employee clocks in for the first time or clocks in with a new phone, that phone is automatically bound as a frequently-used phone. Once the number of bound phones exceeds the limit set by the admin, no further phones can be bound.

<Note>
**FAQ:** If an employee has already reached the admin-set limit of frequently-used phones, and their phone breaks so they buy a new one to clock in with, can it be bound?

If the DingTalk Attendance system recognizes the current phone as a new device, it can be bound as a new frequently-used phone. During binding, the employee is required to unbind the old phone.
</Note>

---

## 3. Employee Clock-In Verification

When an employee uses someone else's frequently-used phone, or exceeds the limit set by the admin, a security check is triggered. To continue clocking in, the employee must complete photo confirmation, facial recognition, or be blocked from clocking in, according to the admin's settings.

- Clocking in with a new phone automatically unbinds the original frequently-used phone.
- Clocking in with a non-frequently-used phone is intercepted and verified.

---

## 4. Admin Unbinding

In special cases where an employee wants to reuse a previously unbound phone, they can contact the admin to remove a bound frequently-used phone.

In line with the requirements of the Personal Information Protection Law and other relevant laws and regulations on protecting sensitive personal facial information, the DingTalk platform iterates promptly: for organizations that enable facial attendance clock-in, it upgrades facial base-image enrollment to require individual, separate authorization and clearly defines the scenarios and scope of data use. For existing facial photos (including facial clock-ins triggered by attendance exceptions), it optimizes data retention and performs deletion to protect sensitive personal information in accordance with the law.
