---
title: "Personal Attendance Statistics Notifications"
description: "About DingTalk personal attendance statistics notifications: push personal attendance statistics results and errors to employees, helping them stay informed about their own attendance in time."
---

At the start of each week and each month, DingTalk Attendance automatically sends attendance statistics to employees who participate in attendance. The content includes attendance results and errors. Notifications are delivered through **Service Assistant**, **Work Notifications**, and **DingMail**. Employees can choose whether to receive them.
