title: "Personal attendance statistics Notifications"
description: "DingTalk personal attendance statistics Notifications push individual attendance results and errors to employees, helping them stay informed of their attendance status in a timely manner."
polished: true
---

At the start of each week and each month, DingTalk Attendance automatically sends attendance statistics to employees enrolled in attendance. The content covers attendance results and errors. Notifications are delivered through **Service Assistant**, **Work Notifications**, and **DingMail**. Employees can choose whether to receive them.
