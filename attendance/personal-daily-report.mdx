DingTalk Attendance automatically sends attendance statistics to enrolled employees on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis. The content covers attendance results and errors. Delivery channels include **Service Assistant**, **Work Notifications**, and **DingMail**, and employees can choose whether to receive them.
