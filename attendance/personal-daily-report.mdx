DingTalk Attendance automatically sends attendance statistics to employees enrolled in attendance every day, every week, and at the start of each month. The content includes attendance results and errors. Delivery methods include **Service Assistant**, **Work Notifications**, and **DingMail**, and employees can choose whether to receive them.
