---
title: "Tips when check-in results are being calculated"
description: "An explanation of the blue notice \"Check-in recorded, calculating result\" that appears on the DingTalk Attendance check-in home page and personal statistics page, helping employees understand the status display while check-in results are being calculated."
---

<Note>
After you complete a check-in, do not worry when the "Check-in recorded, calculating result" screen appears. At this point, your check-in record has already been uploaded to the Attendance server, and the check-in result is being calculated. There may be some latency, so **there is no need to check in again**.
</Note>

Once the check-in result is calculated successfully, the home page and personal statistics page update automatically. Please check back later.
