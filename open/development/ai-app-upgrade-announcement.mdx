Hello, DingTalk developers. As part of a product brand upgrade, we will upgrade the DingTalk AI App Platform. This upgrade mainly covers Digital Employee and the Magic Wand app.

## **Upgrade Details**

### **The "Digital Employee" Brand Is Fully Upgraded to "AI Assistant"**

To better reflect the value proposition and future direction of DingTalk AI, the "Digital Employee" brand name will be fully renamed to "AI Assistant". After the brand upgrade, the user experience of AI Assistant will remain the same as the previous Digital Employee for now. We plan to upgrade the product features and experience of existing Digital Employees on February 1, 2024.

### The Magic Wand App Will Be Integrated with AI Assistant

The Magic Wand app will offer a brand-new AI Assistant interaction experience and is planned to be fully merged with AI Assistant later. For the detailed integration plan and progress, stay tuned for future announcements.

## **Upgrade Schedule**

Digital Employee upgraded to AI Assistant: expected to roll out to all users on January 9, 2024.

## **Upgrade Impact**

- Development experience: After the upgrade, the AI app development framework and development method remain unchanged.
- User experience: The product brand upgrade will not affect the use of existing services and features. You can still find and use "AI Assistant" (formerly "Digital Employee") in Contacts or in the search bar of the DingTalk Client-side. However, Digital Employees created before February 1, 2024, will differ from AI Assistant in the following ways, and these differences will also be aligned during the February 1, 2024 upgrade:

  - Existing Digital Employees can still be accessed from Search and Contacts, but cannot yet be accessed from the AI Assistant List.
  - Existing Digital Employees will remain in the department structure of the Organization Contacts, but will not count toward the Organization Contacts quota. After the February 1, 2024 upgrade, existing Digital Employees will move into the "AI Assistant" structure of the Organization Contacts and will not count toward the Organization Contacts quota.
  - The Card Template of existing Digital Employees differs from the AI Assistant Template Standard. A card design standard with an upgraded experience will be provided to developers later.
