## Usage Process

Before you start, make sure you have completed the preparations to [become a DingTalk developer](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/become-a-dingtalk-developer#). The process for developing a group app is as follows:

1. [Publish a third-party enterprise app](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/become-an-application-service-provider#). Develop the group app based on the published third-party enterprise app.
2. [Configure the basic information of the group app](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/configure-the-basic-information-of-the-group-application#). In the **Cool App Extension** of the third-party enterprise app, create the group app and configure its basic information. After the group app is created successfully, you can obtain the group app code.
3. [Set up message push](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/group-application-development-and-access#). In **Message Push**, develop and bind the bot.
4. [Develop interactive cards](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/interactive-card-development-and-access#). By binding the bot, you can send interactive cards and more.
5. [Submit for review and publish the group app](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/group-application-operations#). After the group app development is complete, submit it for review and contact the operations team to publish it.
6. [Enable and use the group app](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/enable-and-use-a-group-application-cool#). Once the group app is published to the Cool App Center, enable it.
