## Group App Publishing Process

## Usage Process

Before you start, make sure you have completed the preparations to [become a DingTalk developer](/open/dingstart/become-a-dingtalk-developer). The process for developing a group app is as follows:

1. Publish a third-party enterprise app. Develop the group app based on the published third-party enterprise app.
2. Configure the basic information of the group app. In the **Cool App Extension** of the third-party enterprise app, create the group app and configure its basic information. After the group app is created successfully, you can obtain the group app code.
3. Set up message push. In **Message Push**, develop and bind the bot.
4. Develop interactive cards. By binding the bot, you can send interactive cards and more.
5. Submit for review and publish the group app. After the group app development is complete, submit it for review and contact the operations team to publish it.
6. Enable and use the group app. Once the group app is published to the Cool App Center, enable it.
