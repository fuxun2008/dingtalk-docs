# Quick Approval Integration for Third-Party Systems: One-Click Approval in the To-Do Center and Message Cards

## Overview

This solution is for organizations whose approval tasks are spread across multiple business systems. It also suits managers who want to handle third-party approvals without leaving DingTalk. The OA Approval Process Center provides a custom quick approval API and a callback mechanism. Third-party business systems use them to sync approval tasks to the DingTalk Approval Center. Custom quick approval buttons then appear in the To-Do Center list and on OA Approval message cards. When an approver clicks a button, DingTalk calls back the third-party system. The system updates the task status and syncs it back to the DingTalk Approval Center. Approvers get a seamless, all-in-one approval experience.

### Background

Organizations generate large numbers of approval tasks in third-party business systems, such as expense control, procurement, and HR systems. Employees must sign in to each system and handle tasks one by one. The process is long and inefficient. This solution connects third-party approvals to the DingTalk To-Do Center and supports one-click approval in message cards. Employees handle all approvals in one place in DingTalk, without switching to the original systems.

Organizations commonly face the following pain points:

- **Repeated sign-ins across systems:** Employees sign in to 5+ business systems every day to handle approvals. Each approval takes an average of 3 minutes, wasting more than 40 minutes per day.
- **Opaque approval progress:** After submitting an approval, employees cannot track its status in DingTalk in real time. They must repeatedly sign in to the original system to check, and follow-ups rely on manual communication.
- **Poor mobile approval experience:** Most third-party systems are not optimized for mobile. Employees on business trips or working from home cannot handle urgent approvals in time, which delays business.
- **Hard-to-aggregate approval data:** Metrics such as approval volume and average processing time per department are scattered across systems. Managers cannot get a global view in DingTalk.

### Core Value

This solution delivers value in three areas:

- **One-click approval:** Quick approval buttons from third-party systems appear directly in the DingTalk To-Do Center list and on OA Approval message cards. Approvers complete simple approvals without switching to the business system.
- **Closed-loop sync:** When an approver clicks a quick button, DingTalk calls back the third-party system to update the task status. The third-party system then syncs the latest status back to the DingTalk Approval Center, completing the data loop.
- **Flexible customization:** Third-party systems can customize the label, icon, and visibility conditions of quick buttons. Configure different quick actions for different approval types based on business needs.

### Use Cases

This solution fits organizations with diverse approval sources and a high share of simple approvals. Typical use cases include:

- **One-stop approval for executives:** Executives such as the CEO and CFO handle approvals from multiple third-party systems every day. Many are simple confirmations that they want to complete with one click in DingTalk, without switching systems.
- **High-frequency, lightweight approvals:** Approvals such as expense reimbursement confirmations, leave extensions, and makeup clock-in requests are frequent and simple. Approvers can handle them directly in the To-Do list.
- **Instant approval from message cards:** After receiving an OA Approval message card notification, approvers complete the approval directly on the card without opening the details page.

## Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Quick Operational Approvals for a Hotel Chain (Hospitality and Tourism)

#### Pain Points

A national hotel chain operates 80+ properties. It uses an in-house PMS for room booking approvals, a procurement system for supply request approvals, and an HR system for staff scheduling and compensatory leave. Approval tasks run separately across the three systems. The group's operations director and store managers handle large volumes of approvals every day. The existing process has the following issues:

- **Time-consuming simple approvals:** Daily approval volume exceeds 60, and 40% are simple actions such as overbooking confirmations and restock confirmations. Each one requires signing in to the PMS or procurement system, finding the task, and clicking Accept, taking an average of 2 minutes.
- **Poor mobile experience:** Store managers handle approvals on their phones during property inspections. They click a link in a DingTalk message to open the PMS mobile page, which loads slowly and is cumbersome to use. Delayed responses to urgent overbooking confirmations hurt the guest experience.
- **No actions in message cards:** When a "Room overbooking requires confirmation" message card arrives, managers can only click to view details. They cannot confirm directly on the card and miss the best window to coordinate room changes.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated Workflow**

  The integration follows a five-step flow:

  1. **Multi-system integration** – connect official OA Approval, PMS, HR, and procurement systems.
  2. **Aggregated To-Do display** – a unified To-Do list with filtering by source system.
  3. **Quick button registration** – configure the Confirm/Reject actions and their callbacks.
  4. **In-card approval** – act directly on the card without opening a details page.
  5. **Closed-loop status sync** – callback events keep data consistent across systems.

  The OA Approval card shows the **Applicant**, **Application Time**, **Amount**, and **Reason** fields, plus embedded **Approve**, **Reject**, and **More** buttons.
- **Key Advantages**

  - **One-click efficiency:** Simple confirmation approvals drop from 2 minutes to 5 seconds, saving 40 minutes per day. Overbooking confirmation response time drops from 15 minutes to 30 seconds.
  - **Mobile-friendly:** Store managers approve directly from the DingTalk To-Do list on their phones, without opening the PMS mobile page. They handle urgent overbookings in real time during inspections.
  - **Direct card actions:** Quick buttons are embedded in OA Approval message cards. Managers confirm directly on the card when an overbooking notification arrives and never miss the window to coordinate room changes.

### Scenario 2: Multi-Party Approval Integration for Large Construction Projects (Construction and Engineering)

#### Pain Points

A large construction company runs 15 projects simultaneously. It uses an in-house project management system for construction progress approvals, a materials system for material delivery inspection approvals, and a safety system for hazard inspection and remediation approvals. The three approval processes run independently. Project managers and the safety director handle large volumes of approvals every day. The existing process has the following issues:

- **Inconvenient on-site approvals:** Project managers work on site. When the mobile signal is unstable, the project management system's mobile page loads slowly. Delayed material delivery confirmations slow down construction.
- **Slow response to safety hazards:** When a safety officer finds a hazard, they submit a remediation approval. The safety director must open the details page from the message card to view hazard photos and location before deciding. The traditional process takes too long, and missing the best remediation window can lead to accidents.
- **Inefficient multi-party collaboration:** A single material inspection approval requires confirmation from the project department, the materials department, and the supervising party, each in a separate system. Coordination takes up to 2 days.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated Workflow**

  The integration flow has five stages:

  1. **Multi-system access** — connect the project, materials, and safety systems to DingTalk.
  2. **Aggregated To-Do view** — pending items from all sources appear in one unified **To-Do Center**, filterable by **All**, **Project**, **Materials**, and **Security**.
  3. **Quick action button registration** — configure buttons for acceptance, recheck, and remediation actions.
  4. **Approve directly from the card** — act on the OA Approval card without opening a details page, using the **Approve**, **Reject**, and **More** buttons.
  5. **Real-time status sync** — callback events keep the source systems' data consistent.

  An approval card shows the request title, **Source**, applicant, submission time, and content.
- **Key Advantages**

  - **On-site efficiency:** Material inspection confirmations drop from 3 minutes to 5 seconds. Even with a weak signal on site, managers complete them quickly in DingTalk and avoid construction delays.
  - **Faster safety response:** Hazard remediation approvals are handled directly from message cards. Response time drops from 1 hour to 5 minutes, reducing the risk of safety incidents.
  - **Closed-loop collaboration:** Multi-party confirmation approvals use quick buttons and callbacks to sync status in real time. Coordination time drops from 2 days to 4 hours.

## Implementation Guide

### Technical Architecture

The technical architecture includes the following core components:

- **Authentication layer:** Manages app credentials and obtains access tokens to verify the identity of every API call.
- **Template sync layer:** Calls the API that saves external integrated approval templates in the Process Center. It syncs approval templates from third-party business systems to the DingTalk Approval Center and obtains the unique template code `processCode`.
- **Task creation layer:** Calls the API that creates or updates approval to-do tasks. It syncs approval tasks from third-party systems to the DingTalk Approval Center and registers custom quick approval buttons and the callback URL.
- **Callback handling layer:** The third-party system provides an HTTPS callback API. It receives quick approval callbacks from DingTalk, updates the local task status, and syncs the latest status back to the DingTalk Approval Center to close the data loop.
- **Event callback layer:** Listens for approval task status change events to keep the third-party system and the DingTalk Approval Center consistent.

### Prerequisites

- **App preparation:** Complete the [app creation and configuration](/open/dingstart/create-application) process.
- **Permission requirements:** Have DingTalk Organization Admin or Sub Admin permissions, and apply for the following API permissions:

  - `qyapi_aflow` (approval flow data management permission)
  - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission)
  - `Workflow.Form.Read` (workflow template read permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission)
  - `Premium.Workflow.ReadWrite.All` (OA Approval workflow read/write permission, exclusive to OA Premium Edition)
- **Development environment:** Install a Java development environment (JDK 1.6 or later) and the Maven build tool.
- **SDK preparation:** Download the DingTalk Server-side SDK, available for Java, Python, Go, and other languages.
- **Gateway preparation:** Register the third-party system's callback API as a gateway in DingTalk "Data Source Management". For details, see Data Source.

### Code Implementation

Step 1: Go to the app details page and get the app's Client ID and Client Secret.

Step 2: Apply for API permissions. Apply for the relevant "OA Approval" permissions.

Step 3: Get the app's access credential. See [Get the access token of an internal app](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app). When calling APIs, use the access token to authenticate the caller.

Step 4: Call the core APIs:

1. Register the callback API for custom quick approval buttons. All gateway callbacks to data APIs outside DingTalk must be registered as gateways in "Data Source Management". For details, see Data Source.
2. Call the new Server API that saves external integrated approval templates in the Process Center to get the unique template code `processCode`.
3. If you did not save `processCode`, call the [Get the template code](/open/development/obtain-the-template-code) API to get it.
4. After the approval template is created, go to the DingTalk OA Approval Admin Console. View your third-party approval form templates, view or search template data, and export or delete template data.
5. Use the template code `processCode` to call the API that saves external integrated approval instances in the Process Center. This initiates an approval instance and returns the approval instance ID `processInstanceId`.
6. After the approval instance is created, go to the DingTalk OA Approval Center. View the approval lists (Pending, Processed, Initiated, and Received), search approval instance data, and perform approvals.
7. Use the approval instance `processInstanceId` and the to-do list tasks to call the API that saves external integrated approval tasks in the Process Center. This syncs approval node information from the third-party system to DingTalk OA Approval, returns the taskId of each to-do item, and generates the corresponding DingTalk to-do task.
8. Call the [Query OA approval tasks integrated through the Process Center](/open/development/query-oa-approval-tasks-integrated-through-process-center) API to query a user's running approval tasks.
9. Use the approval instance `processInstanceId` and the approval to-do taskId to call the [Update Process Center task status](/open/development/update-process-center-task-status) API and sync the status of your own approval to-do items.

### Note

   In scenarios such as OR-sign approval, call the [Cancel multiple pending tasks in the Process Center](/open/development/cancel-multiple-oa-approval-tasks) API to set all running to-do items under an approval instance to CANCELED in a batch.
10. Use the approval instance `processInstanceId`, the instance status, the instance result, and other data to trigger events such as approval task start, end, and transfer, or approval instance start, end, termination, and deletion. These events update the instance status.
11. To clean up approval template data, call the [Delete template](/open/development/self-owned-approval-deletion-template) API. This deletes the approval template created for your organization, along with all instances and to-do tasks created from it.

## Implementation Steps

### **Step 1: Get the App Credential**

1. Sign in to the [DingTalk Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.io/).
2. Select the target app to open its details page.
3. Click **Basic Information** > **Credentials and Basic Information**.
4. Record the app's `Client ID` and `Client Secret`.

### Note

   Keep the `Client Secret` secure and never share it with third parties. Store it in environment variables or a key management system instead of hardcoding it.

### Step 2: Add API Permissions

1. On the app details page, click **Manage permissions**.
2. Enter the following permission identifiers in the permission search box and apply for them:

   - `qyapi_aflow` (approval flow data management permission): Updates Process Center task status.
   - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission): Gets the template code.
   - `Workflow.Form.Read` (workflow template read permission): Creates or updates approval instances.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission): Cancels pending tasks in the Process Center in a batch.
   - `Premium.Workflow.ReadWrite.All` (OA Approval workflow read/write permission, exclusive to OA Premium Edition): Gets a user's Pending, Processed, Initiated, and Received list data in the DingTalk Approval Center.

<Note>
Different business scenarios may require different permission combinations. Apply based on your actual needs.
</Note>

### Step 3: Get the Access Credential (Access Token)

Use the Client ID and Client Secret from Step 1 to get the app's access credential. See [Get the access token of an internal app](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app).

**Best practices**:

- **Caching strategy**: The `accessToken` is valid for 2 hours. Cache it in memory or Redis, and refresh it 5 minutes before it expires.
- **Concurrency control**: Prevent multiple threads from refreshing the token at the same time and causing conflicts. Use a distributed lock.
- **Error retry**: Retry automatically on network fluctuations, up to 3 times, with increasing intervals (1s → 2s → 4s).

## FAQ

- **Q: How do I handle callback API timeouts?**

  DingTalk's callback to the third-party system API times out after 5 seconds. If the third-party system does not return HTTP 200 within 5 seconds, DingTalk retries up to 3 times, at intervals of 1s, 2s, and 4s. Keep the callback API lightweight and process complex logic asynchronously.
- **Q: Do approvers need to confirm again after clicking a quick button?**

  No second confirmation is required by default. The action runs immediately on click. If your business scenario requires a second confirmation, return a specific status code from the third-party callback API. DingTalk then displays a confirmation dialog before running the action.
