This article introduces Cool Apps, Group Extension products, and how to enable and use group apps.

## What Is a Group Extension

Building on a comprehensive openness strategy, the DingTalk Open Platform fully opens up core scenarios to make DingTalk a true foundation for organizations. This strengthens the integration of the DingTalk ecosystem, jointly creates Cool Apps, drives new traffic, and delivers a brand-new user experience.

A Cool App is a new form of application. It combines capabilities such as quick entries, Workbench components, chat Bots, Interaction Cards, and OA Approval suites into DingTalk's various feature scenarios, bringing users a scenario-based, immersive experience. Cool Apps aim to upgrade traditional digitalization and eliminate the disconnect between OA and communication. They also improve the collaborative experience by solving problems such as fragmented communication scenarios with no accumulation, business scenarios without real-time communication, and tasks and people being in two separate places.

A **Group Extension** is a type of **Cool App**. It is an extended form of an app used within a chat and, based on the main app, extends the capabilities of quick entries, chat Bots, and Interaction Cards. **It enables an app not only to deliver immersive experiences such as multi-person, multi-role task collaboration, goal co-creation, business opportunity sharing, and battle report announcements in group chats, but also, from a technical standpoint, to integrate Cool Apps into Direct Message chats.**

### Features of Group Extensions

- **Immersive experience:** No need to leave the chat Context, so your current work is not interrupted.
- **Scenario-based collaboration:** Information collaborates in real time and efficiently around scenarios and specific matters.
- **Instant data analysis:** Structured Data generated through efficient collaboration is analyzed instantly to form actionable insights.
- **Metric-based operations:** Everyone participates and puts it into practice, and by combining it with organizational incentive mechanisms, digitalization truly takes root.

### Advantages of Group Extensions

- **App perspective:**

  An app can distill its most core functions and develop multiple group apps. Each group app can integrate chat Bots, Interaction Cards, pinned cards, and message menus to fully meet the capability needs of business scenarios.
- **Chat window perspective:**

  The chat window is a container. Multiple group apps can be installed in the group app bar of the chat container. These group apps can come from different main apps and can be combined in Custom ways according to the group scenario.

## How to Enable and Use Group Extensions

Go to **Internal Group > Cool App Bar > More Cool Apps** to enter the Cool App Center, then find and Enable the app.

After enabling a Cool App, use it from the Cool App bar. For example, click **Smart Form** to use the Smart Form app to add and Edit entries.

## Terminology

### Chat Container

DingTalk's chat container is Open and Allows organizations to customize it. This is a unique and primary collaborative office capability of DingTalk, providing users with collaborative services for scenarios such as sales, goal co-building, ticket assistance, and Direct Message communication.

The chat container includes areas such as the **chat title bar**, **pinned area**, **chat window, message menu**, **half screen**, **full screen**, **group app bar**, **toolbar**, **input box**, and **sidebar**.

### Group App

A group app is a form of app usage developed based on a main app. It is entered from the quick entry in the chat app bar. With chat Bots, Chats, pinned cards, and Interaction Cards as its main interaction methods, it creates a brand-new digital experience where communication is business and business is collaboration.

### Chat Bot

One of an app's interaction capabilities within a group. Every group app automatically associates a chat Bot. Through the chat Bot, you can Send information in a targeted, quantified, scheduled, and Content-specific manner, supporting both group chats and Direct Message chats.

### Chats

The most basic form of IM, including Text, Files, Images, Videos, and rich media.

### Interaction Card

An app's interaction capability within a group. An interactive message is a new type of message that is dynamic, interactive, and consistent across platforms. It can display information more richly and encourage users to communicate and interact instantly.

### Pinned Card

A type of Interaction Card with a relatively fixed usage scenario and method.
