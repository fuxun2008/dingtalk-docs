This article introduces two partnership models through which product solution providers can offer standard SaaS app services and customization services to enterprise users on DingTalk.

## Partnership Models

### Model 1: Standard SaaS App Service

After a standard SaaS app service product is published on DingTalk, it can be promoted in the following two ways:

- **App Market Promotion**: If you already have an app product or plan to develop one, and you have a clear business model that meets the DingTalk App Market publishing standards, you can publish it to the App Market for promotion after passing the review. Once the app is published, customers can search for, view, install, and purchase the app in the DingTalk App Market.
- **Own-Channel Promotion**: If you already have an app product or plan to develop one, you can use the API provided by DingTalk to generate an offline promotion QR Code, and promote the app through the service provider's own channels or channels outside DingTalk (such as external advertisements or third-party official websites). Customers can install the app by scanning the QR Code or clicking the link.

**Important**

Own-channel promotion is subject to a Strict review. Before onboarding, join the service group below to consult and confirm the details before submitting your onboarding and development requests, so as to avoid failing the review after development is complete.

### Model 2: Customization Service

After becoming a DingTalk product solution provider, you can use the data APIs and resources of the DingTalk Open Platform to provide personalized, project-based development or integration for DingTalk users. For example: customized Workbench, customized system integration, and other technical development services.

## Common Materials for Onboarding as a Service Provider

### DingTalk Organization Premium Authentication

- Business license of the organization
- Legal representative and their ID number
- Authentication authorization letter (Download it on the [DingTalk OA Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.com/index.htm#/chooseAuthType) > **Organization Premium Authentication** page)
- Note: Premium Authentication for some DingTalk organizations has been upgraded to annual review authentication (paid). Complete the annual review authentication as prompted on the authentication page.

### Onboarding Application

- Business license of the organization (original or copy with an official seal)
- Contact information and email of the business owner (does not need to be the legal representative; a DingTalk business owner is sufficient)
- Authorization letter (must bear the organization's official seal; [click here](https://files.alicdn.com/tpsservice/e2593e64e0597c07aff8f01be87af293.doc) to download the template)
- App product introduction proposal (PDF document recommended)
- Front and back photos of the contract signer's ID card
- Company Alipay account

### App Publishing Admission Review Standards

- Clear introduction to the app product positioning
- A clear and well-defined transaction model and pricing specifications
- App transactions must be integrated with DingTalk

  **Note**

  Transaction payments enter the Alipay escrow account opened by the service provider during onboarding.
- Personnel to handle online services
- Ability to serve business nationwide
- All app experience review items meet the standards
- The DingTalk Index score of the ISV's DingTalk organization is ≥ 1,000
- Completed deep co-creation with at least 3 customers and provided deep co-creation reports for 3 customers
- If the app is published to the App Market, it must also meet: in the past 6 months, the number of DingTalk paying customers ≥ 10 and GMV ≥ 100,000, or the number of paying customers ≥ 3 and GMV ≥ 500,000. (Business travel category: GMV generated on DingTalk in the past 6 months ≥ 20 million)

Note: The admission review standards were updated and took effect in April 2025.
