## **Dear DingTalk Developer**

To build a more stable, fair, and sustainable open platform ecosystem, and to encourage all developers to use platform resources efficiently and responsibly, we plan to adjust the API call quota policy on the DingTalk Open Platform. The existing "unlimited" policy (commonly known as the "PaaS Allowlist") will be revised, and all affected organizations will be given an ample transition period to adapt to the new rules.

### **Announcement on the PaaS Allowlist Policy Adjustment**

- **Effective September 1, 2025**, the original PaaS unlimited "Allowlist" policy for internal apps developed by organizations in select industries and individually protected organizations will end. (Unlimited quotas include: unlimited API calls, unlimited webhook and stream calls, and unlimited execution counts for Connector Flow execution nodes.)

### **Three-Month Optimization Transition Period**

- **From September 1 to November 30, 2025**, service will not be interrupted when usage exceeds the quota, and you can continue to use it.
- The DingTalk Open Platform will send overage warnings to the Organization Admin.
- We recommend that organizations optimize their code during this period or prepare to purchase additional usage packages.

### **Effective Date of the New Policy**

- **Effective December 1, 2025**, to prevent business disruptions caused by failure to scale up in time, the system will automatically provide a **buffer protection period of up to 5 days** once the monthly quota is exhausted. During this period, your app can still call APIs normally. In addition, we offer the right to a **15-day free trial of one Internal App Development Add-On Package**, which you can claim yourself in the Developer Console, or by scanning a QR code with your account manager.

To ensure your business runs smoothly after the policy adjustment, we strongly recommend that you assess and plan ahead. Sign in to the DingTalk Developer Console to review your app's API call activity over the recent period and estimate your future usage.

We understand that this adjustment may require you to invest time in adapting, so we provide up to three months of optimization buffer time, along with uninterrupted service and warning support during this period. If your estimated usage will exceed the Free quota, you can obtain a higher quota through the following options:

- **Option 1**: If you need a higher call count, purchase the Internal App Development Add-On Package separately.

- **Option 2**: Upgrade to DingTalk Pro or the Dedicated edition to receive a higher Free call quota and more value-added benefits within your plan.

Our goal is to grow together with you and build a healthier, more efficient development environment. If you have any questions, feel free to consult us through the Developer Console or contact your account manager.

This announcement takes effect on July 17, 2025. Thank you for your support and understanding.

DingTalk Open Platform Integrations

July 17, 2025

## **FAQ**

**Q1: I am a DingTalk developer. I heard that the "Allowlist" is going to be canceled. When exactly?**

A: Yes. To ensure the fairness and stability of platform services, we are upgrading the existing "unlimited" policy (commonly known as the "PaaS Allowlist") to a unified **Standard Quota Policy**. This adjustment will officially take effect on September 1, 2025, and we have prepared an ample transition plan for you.

**Q2: Will my service be stopped immediately on September 1, 2025?**

Absolutely not! We have set a three-month "transition and optimization period" from September 1 to November 30, 2025. During this period, even if your usage exceeds the new standard quota, your service will **continue to run normally without interruption**. We will send you warnings to help you understand your usage.

**Q3: What do I need to do during the transition period?**

A: This is the best time to prepare for the new policy. We recommend that you:

- **Check your usage:** Sign in to the Developer Console to review your current API usage.
- **Optimize your code:** Review your app to see whether there are any API call logic optimizations that can reduce usage.
- **Plan ahead:** If your business truly requires a higher quota, learn about the usage add-on package options in advance.

**Q4: When will the new policy officially take effect? What happens if I exceed the quota?**

A: The new policy will officially take effect on December 1, 2025. If you accidentally exceed the quota, don't worry—we provide a **5-day usage protection period**. During this protection period, your service will continue to run normally. You need to resolve the usage issue **within these 5 days**. If the issue is not resolved after 5 days, call requests exceeding the quota will be subject to corresponding restrictions.
