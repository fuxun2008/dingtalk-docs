## Overview of the Development Process for Internal AI Apps

### **Prerequisites**

You have obtained developer permissions. For details, see Obtain Developer Permissions.

### **Process Overview**

1. [Create an App](/open/dingstart/create-application)
2. Add AI App Capabilities
3. (Optional) Add Developers
4. Develop the App

   1. Build AI Capabilities
   2. Configure the Magic Wand App
   3. Configure Tabler
5. Publish the AI App

## Overview of the Development Process for Third-Party AI Apps

### **Prerequisites**

1. You have completed onboarding as a product solution provider.
2. You have obtained developer permissions. For details, see Obtain Developer Permissions.

### **Usage Limits**

The AI app development platform is currently in the invitation-only testing phase. You can only develop AI apps and try them out through a test organization, but you cannot publish them directly. To publish an AI app, you must jointly develop a mini program app or web app. For details, see (Optional) Test the App and Self-Test the App.

### **Process Overview**

1. [Create an App](/open/dingstart/create-application)
2. Add AI App Capabilities
3. (Optional) Add Developers
4. Develop the App

   1. Build AI Capabilities
   2. Configure the Magic Wand App
   3. Configure Tabler
5. Publish the AI App

   1. Monitor the App
   2. Deploy the App
   3. [Publish the AI App](/zh/open/development/publish-application)
   4. Configure Tabler
   5. Self-Test the App
