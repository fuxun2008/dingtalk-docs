## Overview of the Development Process for Third-Party AI Apps

### **Prerequisites**

1. You have completed onboarding as a [product solution provider](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/isvapp/become-an-application-service-provider).
2. You have obtained developer permissions. For details, see [Obtain Developer Permissions](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/get-developer-permissions#).

### **Usage Limits**

The AI app development platform is currently in the invitation-only testing phase. You can only develop AI apps and try them out through a test organization, but you cannot publish them directly. To publish an AI app, you must jointly develop a mini program app or web app. For details, see [(Optional) Test the App](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/optional-test-application#) and [Self-Test the App](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/self-test-application#).
