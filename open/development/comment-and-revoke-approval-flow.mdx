# Integrate OA Approval Workflows: Add Approval Comments and Terminate Approvals

## Overview

### Background

Business systems that organizations build or purchase often need to attach supporting materials to an approval instance during the approval workflow, such as scanned contracts or acceptance reports. These systems usually lack a unified attachment upload entry point. They also lack a standard way to revoke an approval when it was submitted in error or when requirements change. Connect approval comments and revocation to the DingTalk OA Approval Center so that users can add attachments and terminate approvals directly in DingTalk.

- **No entry point for attachments:** Approval initiators cannot add supporting materials outside the business system. Approval information stays incomplete, and decisions lack a solid basis.
- **Non-standard revocation:** Approval forms submitted in error are recalled through offline communication. No system-level revocation record or status sync exists.
- **Disconnected Drive spaces:** Approval attachments are scattered across personal Drive folders or local storage. They cannot be linked to approval instances, which makes audits difficult.
- **No schema confirmation:** Form structures are not validated before an approval is initiated. This causes runtime errors such as mismatched control IDs and failed data submission.

### Core Value

This solution reuses the Drive space management and comment engine of the official OA Approval. It integrates attachment upload, comment creation, and revocation into your business system to manage the full approval lifecycle.

- **Centralized attachment management:** All approval attachments are uploaded to a dedicated approval Drive space and bound to the instance. Download them in one click and trace them during audits.
- **Traceable comments:** Approval comments with attachments are kept permanently. They form a complete communication trail and prevent information loss.
- **Standardized revocation:** Trigger revocation through the API. The approval status automatically updates to "Terminated," and a synchronous callback notifies the business system.
- **Schema pre-validation:** Get the form schema before initiating an approval. Confirm that control settings match the template and reduce integration and debugging costs.

### Use Cases

This solution suits the following typical scenarios for integrating approval comments and revocation:

- A law firm needs to attach evidence and add lawyer review comments during a case approval process.
- An education and training organization needs to revoke a submitted course change approval after finding a scheduling conflict, then initiate it again.
- A medical organization needs to add supplier qualification documents as comment attachments during a drug procurement approval.
- A construction project needs to terminate the current acceptance approval after finding incorrect inspection data, then correct and resubmit it.

## Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Add Evidence to a Law Firm Case Approval

#### Pain Points

A law firm uses an in-house case management system to handle case approvals. After lawyers submit a case filing approval, they often need to add new evidence or legal opinions. The original process had the following issues:

- **No channel for adding evidence:** After a case filing approval starts, lawyers cannot attach newly collected evidence through the system. They email partners offline, so the approval materials remain incomplete.
- **Scattered communication records:** Lawyers' additional notes are spread across WeChat, email, and other channels. Approvers cannot view all communication in the approval form, which slows decisions.
- **Disorganized attachments:** Evidence files are stored on personal computers or shared network drives and are not linked to approval instances. Audits require manual matching.
- **Hard to correct erroneous approvals:** When filing information is wrong, no systematic revocation exists. Lawyers ask approvers to reject verbally, leaving no formal termination record or status sync.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated Workflow**

  The automated approval flow runs in five steps:

  1. A lawyer initiates case filing, enters case information, and uploads evidence materials.
  2. An **approval instance** is created and initialized through the **DingTalk OA** API.
  3. The partner reviews the case in DingTalk, where the Approval is routed and the form is displayed.
  4. The **approver** acts on mobile and selects **Reject** or **Approve**.
  5. The result is synced back to the system, which writes the status and synchronizes data.

  Compared with a manual process (cross-channel switching, out-of-sync status, and scattered notifications), the automated integration provides a unified entry point, real-time sync, and message delivery.
- **Key Advantages**

  - **Add evidence online:** Lawyers can attach evidence files to the approval form at any time through the approval comment API. Approvers view the complete materials in DingTalk and make decisions.
  - **Complete communication trail:** All notes and attachments are permanently linked to the approval instance through comments. This creates a complete case approval record for later audits.
  - **Standardized revocation:** When filing information is wrong, the revocation API formally terminates the Approval process. The system records the revocation reason and syncs the status, avoiding unclear responsibilities caused by verbal communication.

### Scenario 2: Revoke and Resubmit a Course Change Approval at a Training Organization

#### Pain Points

An education and training organization uses an in-house academic affairs system to manage course change approvals. After submitting a schedule change request, staff sometimes find a time conflict or a lack of classroom resources. The original process had the following issues:

- **Late conflict detection:** Time overlaps with other courses are found only after the approval is submitted. By then the approval is already in progress and cannot be recalled through the system.
- **Incomplete change information:** Approvers see only the change request form. They cannot view affected students, teacher schedules, or classroom usage in the approval form, which makes impact assessment difficult.
- **Non-standard revocation:** Erroneous requests can only be rejected by notifying approvers offline. No system-level revocation record exists, so academic logs do not match the actual status.
- **Inefficient resubmission:** After revocation, staff must refill the entire form to initiate a new approval. Data from the original form cannot be reused, which adds workload.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated Workflow**

  The end-to-end integrated flow runs in five steps:

  1. **Academic affairs initiates a schedule change:** Fill in the change request and tag affected students.
  2. **Create an approval instance:** Call the DingTalk OA Approval API to initialize the instance.
  3. **Review in the academic admin console:** Route the Approval form and run conflict detection. The form includes fields such as **Course name**, **Original time**, **New time**, **Classroom**, **Applicant teacher**, and **Affected headcount**.
  4. **Approver acts:** The approver reviews on mobile and taps **Approve** or **Reject**.
  5. **Sync the result back to the academic system:** Update the timetable and sync the data.
- **Key Advantages**

  - **Revoke conflicting schedules immediately:** When a change request is wrong, the revocation API immediately terminates the Approval process. The system records the revocation reason and prevents the erroneous approval from affecting teaching arrangements.
  - **Assess change impact online:** Staff can use comments to add affected student lists and classroom adjustment plans to the approval form. Approvers view all change information in one place in DingTalk.
  - **Real-time status sync:** Revocation results are written back to the academic system in real time, keeping the timetable consistent with the approval status. Staff can quickly correct data when initiating a new approval and reduce repeated data entry.

## Implementation Guide

### Technical Architecture

The technical architecture of this solution includes the following core components:

- **Authentication layer**: Manages App credentials and obtains access tokens to verify the identity of every API call.
- **Template management layer**: Creates and updates approval form templates and defines the structure and fields of the Approval process.
- **Instance execution layer**: Initiates, queries, and revokes approval instances to drive the Approval process.
- **Comment and attachment layer**: Manages the approval Drive space and adds comments to close the approval communication loop.

### Prerequisites

- **App preparation**: Complete [App creation and configuration](/open/dingstart/create-application).
- **Permissions**: Have DingTalk Organization Admin or Sub Admin permissions, and apply for the following API permissions:

  - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission)
  - `Workflow.Form.Read` (workflow template read permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (workflow instance read permission)
- **Development environment**: Install a Java development environment (JDK 1.6 or later) and the Maven build tool.
- **SDK preparation**: Download the DingTalk Server-side SDK, which supports Java, Python, Go, and other languages.

7. After reviewing the approval, use `instanceId` to call the new Server API [Revoke an Approval Instance](/open/development/revoke-an-approval-instance) to revoke the Approval form.

## Implementation Steps

### **Step 1:** **Get App Credentials**

1. Sign in to the [DingTalk Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.io/).
2. Select the target App to open its details page.
3. Click **Basic Information** > **Credentials and Basic Information**.
4. Record the App's `Client ID` and `Client Secret`.

### Note

   Keep the `Client Secret` secure and do not share it with third parties. Store it in environment variables or a key management system instead of hard-coding it.

### Step 2: Add API Permissions

1. On the App details page, click **Manage permissions**.
2. Enter the following permission identifiers in the permission search box and apply for them:

   - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission): Creates and updates approval form templates.
   - `Workflow.Form.Read` (workflow template read permission): Gets the form schema.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission): Initiates and revokes approval instances.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (workflow instance read permission): Queries approval instance details.

<Note>
Different business scenarios may require different permission combinations. Apply based on your actual needs. For example, batch import scenarios also require permissions for batch user operations.
</Note>

### Step 3: Get the Access Credential (Access Token)

Use the Client ID and Client Secret from Step 1 to get the App access credential. See [Get the Access Token of an Internal App](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app).

**Best practices**:

- **Caching**: The `accessToken` is valid for 2 hours. Cache it in memory or Redis and refresh it 5 minutes before it expires.
- **Concurrency control**: Prevent multiple threads from refreshing the token at the same time. Use a distributed lock.
- **Error retries**: Retry automatically on network fluctuations, up to 3 times with increasing intervals (1s → 2s → 4s).

## FAQ

- **Q1: What should I do if a 403 error is returned when I get the form schema?**

  Check that the `Workflow.Form.Read` permission has been granted and that the access token has not expired. Permissions take 1 to 2 minutes to take effect after approval.
- **Q2: Why are attachments not displayed after I add an approval comment?**

  Confirm that all required parameters are correctly obtained from the approval Drive space API and the Client-side JSAPI, and that the files are successfully uploaded to the corresponding Drive space.
- **Q3: Why is the status not updated after I revoke an approval instance?**

  Revocation runs asynchronously. Subscribe to the approval instance change callback event to monitor status changes, or poll the API for getting the details of a single approval instance to confirm.
- **Q4: Can I add comments to a revoked approval instance?**

  No. A revoked approval instance is in a final state and does not accept any comments or attachments. To add more information, initiate a new approval instance.
