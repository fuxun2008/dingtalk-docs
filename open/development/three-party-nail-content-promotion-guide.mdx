## **1. What Is the DingTalk Integration Score?**

The DingTalk Integration Score measures how deeply an app integrates with DingTalk. Apps achieve deep integration by connecting to DingTalk APIs, Connectors, Cool Apps, integrated suites, and more. Data shows that 80% of users choose apps with a higher DingTalk Integration Score.

## **2. Why Does the DingTalk Integration Score Matter?**

- The higher the score, the stronger the app's overall GMV performance. Apps with GMV above 10 million score an average of 70+, while apps with GMV below 500,000 score only in the teens.

  **Note**

  The X-Axis represents GMV, and the Y-Axis represents the DingTalk Integration Score.

- The higher the score, the more active the app's overall user base. Apps with high scores offer richer features, more diverse formats, and better data flow, delivering a superior customer experience and serving a broader audience. Data shows that apps scoring below 20 have almost no active users.

  **Note**

  The X-Axis represents the DingTalk Integration Score, and the Y-Axis represents app activity.

## **3. Scoring Rules for Third-Party Apps**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Scoring Dimension** | | **Definition** | **Scoring Rule** |
| **Capability Dimension** | **API** | Number of daily active APIs at L3 or above | **4** points per daily active API |
| **Connector** | Number of integrated and active Connectors (the core component of a Connector is the action, similar to an API) | **5** points per action of a daily active Connector |
| **Product Dimension** | **Cool App** | Whether the main app has a published Cool App, and its integration and activity across Cool App surfaces (banner, Bots, message menu, and so on) | **8** points per active surface, per day |
| **Integrated Suite** | Whether the app has integrated a first-party product suite—via co-development or third-party embedding—and the activity of the integrated surfaces | **10** points per active surface, per day |

## **5. How to Boost Your DingTalk Integration Score**

### **Connect More** [**L3+ APIs**](https://open.dingtalk.com/developer/old) **to Let DingTalk Empower People, Finance, Assets, Tasks, and the Supply-Sales Chain—and Raise Your Score**

- **BEFORE**

  - The app on DingTalk is just a **shortcut link pinned to the Workbench**, treating DingTalk as nothing more than an entry point—no different from opening it in a browser.
  - The app **only integrates DingTalk's account silent login** and the Contacts/org-structure APIs, treating DingTalk as a permission center. This uses only the most Basic capabilities DingTalk offers.
  - The app **only integrates DingTalk's Message notifications API**, without considering message diversity or delivery formats. This uses only one of DingTalk's most Basic capabilities.
- **AFTER**

  - Through **IM API integration, Bots, and interactive Cards**, the app achieves multidimensional Group Management, turning communication into business and business into collaboration.
  - Through **Event API** integration, the app deeply integrates your organization's internal Event management processes with DingTalk.
  - Through **APIs for attendance, Approval, To-Do, and more**, the app makes it easier to manage your organization's people, finance, assets, and tasks on DingTalk.
  - By connecting to DingTalk's **data assets**, the app becomes data-driven and comes alive.

### **Connect the** [**DingTalk Connector**](https://open.dingtalk.com/developer/connector) **to Eliminate Data Silos Across Heterogeneous Systems and Raise Your Score**

- **BEFORE**

  - After the app goes live on DingTalk, its **data is stored and accessed in isolation**—it's a silo.
  - Without joint development, third-party apps have no better path to interoperability. Without integration, silos form again, forcing customers to re-enter the same data repeatedly.
  - Submitting an ERP Approval form in an internal system requires separately notifying the relevant approver to confirm it in the ERP system—a cumbersome process.
- **AFTER**

  - The DingTalk Connector links DingTalk's official apps, third-party SaaS apps, and large external systems like Kingdee and Yonyou. **Integrate once, connect all data.**
  - By **integrating the Connector** in an internal system, submitting an expense reimbursement form automatically creates an Approval To-Do in DingTalk. Once the approver confirms it, the document Credential flows back to the finance system automatically through the Connector. The Connector makes reimbursement simple and efficient.

### **Connect** [**DingTalk Cool Apps**](https://open.dingtalk.com/coolapp) **to Drive Low-Frequency Apps with High-Frequency Surfaces and Raise Your Score**

- **BEFORE**

  - After the app goes live on DingTalk, using it every time means finding the app icon in the Organization Workbench, clicking in, and then locating the specific feature you need—a long path.
  - While sending and receiving resumes in a recruitment group, once an interviewer decides a resume looks good and tells HR, HR has to go back to the recruitment system to upload the resume, create the candidate, confirm times with the interviewer, and then schedule the interview. HR **keeps doing tasks that look routine but could be made far more efficient**.
- **AFTER**

  - By integrating DingTalk Cool Apps, publishing the app to the Workbench also lets you **distribute the app with one click to high-frequency entry points like Chat Quick actions, the message menu, and the Profile page**.
  - While sending and receiving resumes in a recruitment group, **resumes are automatically converted into candidate Cards**. When an interviewer decides a resume looks good and **clicks [Looks Good]**, a **half-screen floating window opens**, letting the interviewer select and confirm available interview slots. The **Card updates in real time**, so HR can follow the interviewer's interaction and contact the candidate directly to schedule the interview—fast and efficient.

### **Connect DingTalk Integrated Suites to Embed Your App Directly into First-Party Products and Raise Your Score**

- **BEFORE**

  - After the app goes live on DingTalk, **it's a standalone app on the Workbench**, and the Workbench is the only entry point.
  - The data the app needs is entered by customers in the app itself. **Using DingTalk's official data** is difficult beyond API integration, and the **data the app generates** stays isolated and closed, so it cannot be merged and displayed within DingTalk's official apps.
  - For example, when using both FXiaoke and DingTalk CRM at the same time, the data on both sides is completely separate.
- **AFTER**

  - By integrating with DingTalk's integrated suite development process, the app embeds directly into DingTalk's official scenarios. **It can be a standalone Tab Subpage or a standalone feature module**, with the first-party product serving as the entry point.
  - For example, FXiaoke embeds its standalone Work Order business directly into DingTalk's official CRM product, opening and switching within the same container and the same View. The **data of FXiaoke Work Orders and DingTalk CRM also stays synchronized in both directions**.
