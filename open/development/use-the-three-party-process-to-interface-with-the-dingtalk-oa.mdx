# Third-Party Workflow Approval Page Hosting: Integrate the Official Detail Page with a Custom Action Area

## Overview

This solution is for organizations that run their own workflow engine but have no mobile or H5 detail pages. These organizations want to reuse the official DingTalk OA Approval detail page style and customize the buttons in the approval action area. The approval page hosting API in the DingTalk OA Approval Process Center lets third-party business systems register two callback APIs when they create approval templates or instances. One callback renders the custom action-area buttons, and the other executes them. Approvers then open the official OA Approval detail page in DingTalk to process the workflow. Approvers get a consistent experience across devices. Third-party businesses can configure action buttons such as Accept, Decline, and Transfer, along with error messages.

### Background

Some complex approval scenarios, such as contract reviews and project initiation, require a custom business action area inside the DingTalk approval detail page. These scenarios also need the standard fields in the official DingTalk approval header. With page hosting, a single detail page combines standard DingTalk approval information with a dedicated business action panel. This approach delivers both standardization and flexibility.

Organizations often face the following pain points in daily operations:

- **Fragmented information display:** The standard DingTalk approval page cannot show business-specific information such as attachment previews, associated documents, or version history. Approvers must switch to other pages to view it.
- **Missing action buttons:** The approval detail page lacks business-specific entry points, such as "View Original Contract" or "Download Attachment Package." Approvers must remember the source system URL and open it manually.
- **Frequent style conflicts:** Embedded business pages are often incompatible with official DingTalk styles. This causes overlapping scroll bars, hidden buttons, and misaligned fonts, which degrade the approval experience.
- **Complex permission control:** Different roles need to see different action areas and data fields on the approval detail page. Existing hosting solutions cannot easily isolate permissions at this level of detail.

### Core Value

This solution delivers value in three areas:

- **Unified experience:** Standardizes the structure and style of approval forms from different business systems on the official DingTalk OA Approval detail page. Users get a consistent experience on mobile, PC, and tablet.
- **Zero-cost integration:** After hosting, approvals connect to native DingTalk features such as IM and To-Do at no extra cost. Users gain many features that improve approval efficiency, and third-party approvals work just like native ones.
- **Flexible customization:** Third-party businesses can customize the buttons in the approval action area of the detail page, such as Accept, Decline, and Transfer, to meet different business requirements.

### Use Cases

This solution fits organizations that run their own workflow engine but lack detail pages, and that want to reuse the official DingTalk style with a custom action area. Typical use cases include:

- **No mobile detail pages:** The company's approval system does not support mobile or H5 pages, so employees, especially managers, cannot approve in DingTalk. Approval hosting delivers a strong, unified DingTalk approval experience across devices at low cost.
- **Integration with native features:** Approvers want approvals to work better with native DingTalk features to improve efficiency. After hosting, features such as one-click group creation, sharing to chats, copying links to chats or documents, printing, and downloading are available at no extra cost.
- **Workflow efficiency governance:** Management wants to systematically analyze and improve the company's workflow efficiency. After hosting, efficiency diagnostics and process mining identify efficiency issues and unreasonable workflows. DingTalk then provides optimization suggestions based on its workflow governance practices.

## Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Approval Hosting for Store Operations at a Fitness Chain (Sports and Fitness)

#### Pain Points

A national fitness chain operates more than 120 stores. It uses an in-house store operations system for four approval workflows: equipment purchases, personal training schedules, membership refunds, and facility maintenance. All four workflows run separately, and none has a mobile or H5 detail page. Regional managers and store managers process many approvals every day. The original workflows have the following issues:

- **Fragmented experience across devices:** The chain handles more than 90 approvals per day. Approvers must sign in to the store operations system on a PC to view approval details. On mobile, they receive notifications but cannot view details or take action. When regional managers travel, delays in urgent purchase approvals disrupt store operations.
- **Missing native features:** When approvers receive an approval such as "Equipment failure requires urgent repair," they must manually copy the approval link and send it to the maintenance group. They cannot create a group or share the link to a chat with one click. Each action takes an extra 2 minutes.
- **Inconsistent interfaces:** The four types of approval detail pages each use a different style. New store managers need up to 3 weeks to adapt, and inconsistent workflows lead to an error rate of up to 8%.

#### Value Delivered

- **Automated Workflow**

  The automated integration flow has 5 steps:

  1. **Create approval template:** Obtain the **processCode**.
  2. **Register render callback:** Configure **PAGE_HOST**.
  3. **Sync approval instance:** Bind **featureConfig**.
  4. **Display the hosted detail page:** Official style plus custom buttons.
  5. **Execute custom action-area actions:** Callback execution and status sync.
- **Key Benefits**

  - **Unified across devices:** The detail page looks identical on PC, mobile, and tablet. Regional managers can view full approval details and take action on their phones while traveling. Response time for urgent purchases drops from 2 hours to 10 minutes.
  - **Native integration:** The approval detail page includes built-in features such as one-click group creation, sharing links to chats, printing, and downloading. Approvers no longer copy links manually, and communication time per approval drops from 2 minutes to zero.
  - **Flexible customization:** Equipment purchase approvals show "Confirm Purchase" and "Reject and Reselect" buttons. Membership refund approvals show "Approve Refund" and "Request More Documents" buttons. Each approval type has its own action-area configuration, and the error rate drops from 8% to 1%.

## Implementation Guide

### Technical Architecture

The technical architecture of this solution includes the following core components:

- **Authentication layer:** Manages App credentials and obtains access tokens to verify the identity of every API call.
- **Template hosting layer:** Calls the API that creates or updates third-party approval templates. It syncs approval templates from third-party business systems to the DingTalk Approval Center, obtains the unique template code `processCode`, and registers the callback URL that renders the custom action-area buttons in the template.
- **Instance hosting layer:** Calls the API that creates or updates approval instances. It syncs approval instances from third-party systems to the DingTalk Approval Center, obtains the approval instance ID `processInstanceId`, and registers the callback URL that executes the custom action-area buttons in the instance.
- **Callback rendering layer:** The third-party system provides an HTTPS callback API. It receives button rendering requests for the DingTalk approval action area and returns the custom button configuration. DingTalk uses this data to render the action area at the bottom of the detail page.
- **Callback execution layer:** The third-party system provides an HTTPS callback API. It receives button execution requests for the DingTalk approval action area, processes the user's approval action, and returns the result. DingTalk uses the result to update the approval status.
- **Task sync layer:** Calls the APIs that create, update, and query approval to-do tasks. It syncs approval to-do tasks from third-party systems to the DingTalk Approval Center to keep data consistent.

### Prerequisites

- **App preparation:** Complete the [app creation and configuration](/open/dingstart/create-application) process.
- **Permission requirements:** Have DingTalk Organization Admin or Sub Admin permissions, and apply for the following API permissions:

  - `qyapi_aflow` (approval workflow data management permission)
  - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission)
  - `Workflow.Form.Read` (workflow template read permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission)
  - `Premium.Workflow.ReadWrite.All` (OA Approval workflow read/write permission)
- **Development environment:** Install a Java development environment (JDK 1.6 or later) and the Maven build tool.
- **SDK preparation:** Download the DingTalk Server-side SDK, which supports Java, Python, Go, and other languages.
- **Gateway preparation:** Register the third-party system's callback APIs as gateways in DingTalk "Data Source Management." For details, see Data Source.

### Code Implementation

Step 1: Go to the app details page and get the app's client ID and Client Secret.

Step 2: Apply for API permissions. Apply for the relevant "OA Approval" permissions.

Step 3: Get the app's access credential. See [Get the Access Token of an Internal App](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app). When you call the API, the access token authenticates the caller's identity.

Step 4: Call the core APIs:

1. Register the callback API for custom quick-approval buttons. All gateway callbacks to external data APIs must be registered as gateways in "Data Source Management." For details, see Data Source.
2. Call the new Server API Save External Integrated Approval Template in Process Center to obtain the template's unique code `processCode`.
3. If you did not save the `processCode`, call the [Get Template Code](/open/development/obtain-the-template-code) API to obtain it.
4. After you create the approval template, users can go to the DingTalk OA Approval Admin Console to view third-party approval form templates, view and search template data, and export or delete template data.
5. Using the template code `processCode`, call the new Server API Save External Integrated Approval Instance in Process Center to start an approval instance and obtain the approval instance ID `processInstanceId`.
6. After the instance is created, users can go to the DingTalk OA Approval Center to view the four approval lists (Pending, Processed, Initiated, and Received), search approval instance data, and process approvals.
7. Using `processInstanceId` and `tasks`, call Save External Integrated Approval Task in Process Center to obtain the `taskId` of each to-do item and generate the corresponding DingTalk To-Do tasks.
8. After the pending task is created, call the [Query OA Approval Tasks Integrated Through Process Center](/open/development/query-oa-approval-tasks-integrated-through-process-center) API to query the user's running approval tasks.
9. Using the approval instance `processInstanceId` and the approval to-do task taskId, call the [Update Process Center Task Status](/open/development/update-process-center-task-status) API to sync status updates for your own approval to-do items. In scenarios such as OR-sign (any approver can approve), call the [Cancel Multiple Pending Tasks in Process Center](/open/development/cancel-multiple-oa-approval-tasks) API to set all running to-do items under the approval instance to CANCELED in a batch.
10. Using the approval instance `processInstanceId`, the instance status `status`, the instance result `result`, and other values, call the [Update Instance Status](/open/development/update-instance-status) or [Batch Update Instance Status](/open/development/self-owned-batch-update-of-instance-status) API to update the instance status.
11. To clean up approval template data, call the [Delete Template](/open/development/self-owned-approval-deletion-template) API. This deletes the approval template created for your organization, along with all instances and to-do tasks created under it.

## Implementation Steps

### **Step 1: Get the App Credential**

1. Sign in to the [DingTalk Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.io/).
2. Select the target app to open the app details page.
3. Click **Basic Information** > **Credentials and Basic Information**.
4. Record the app's `Client ID` and `Client Secret`.

### Note

   Keep your `Client Secret` secure and never share it with third parties. Store it in environment variables or a key management system instead of hard-coding it.

### Step 2: Add API Permissions

1. On the app details page, click **Manage Permissions**.
2. In the permission search box, enter the following permission identifiers and apply for them:

   - `qyapi_aflow` (approval workflow data management permission): Queries and updates Process Center task status.
   - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission): Obtains the template code.
   - `Workflow.Form.Read` (workflow template read permission): Creates and updates approval instances, and deletes templates.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission): Updates instance status.
   - `Premium.Workflow.ReadWrite.All` (OA Approval workflow read/write permission): Saves external integrated approval templates or approval tasks in Process Center.

<Note>
Different business scenarios may require different permission combinations. Apply for permissions based on your actual needs.
</Note>

### Step 3: Get the Access Credential (Access Token)

Use the Client ID and Client Secret from Step 1 to get the app's access credential. See [Get the Access Token of an Internal App](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app).

```java
public void getAccessToken() throws Exception {
  Config config = new Config();
  config.protocol = "https";
  config.regionId = "central";
  com.aliyun.dingtalkoauth2_1_0.Client client = new com.aliyun.dingtalkoauth2_1_0.Client(config);
  GetAccessTokenRequest accessTokenRequest = new GetAccessTokenRequest()
    .setAppKey("din*********hgn")
    .setAppSecret("9G_O************mBkhgGIO");
  GetAccessTokenResponse accessToken = client.getAccessToken(accessTokenRequest);
  System.out.println(JSON.toJSONString(accessToken.getBody()));
}
```

**Best practices**:

- **Caching:** The `accessToken` is valid for 2 hours. Cache it in memory or Redis, and refresh it 5 minutes before it expires.
- **Concurrency control:** Prevent conflicts caused by multiple threads refreshing the token at the same time. Use a distributed lock.
- **Error retries:** Retry automatically during network fluctuations, up to 3 times, with increasing intervals (1s → 2s → 4s).

1. **Register the callback APIs for the custom approval action-button area:** Register one callback that requests the action-area button definitions from the third-party system (button rendering). For protocol details, see [Approval Action Area Hosting API Protocol](/open/development/detailed-explanation-of-hosting-interface-agreement-in-approval-operation-area).
2. **Sync approval template data to DingTalk:** Call the new Server API Save External Integrated Approval Template in Process Center to obtain the template's unique code `processCode`.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: What is the difference between renderCallback and executeCallback?**

  - DingTalk uses `renderCallback` to request the action-area button definitions from the third-party system, such as button text, color, and visibility. DingTalk then renders the action area at the bottom of the detail page.
  - DingTalk uses `executeCallback` to submit the approval action to the third-party system after a user clicks a button. The third-party system processes the request and returns the result. The two callbacks have separate responsibilities and are not interchangeable.
- **Q: How are callback API timeouts handled?**

  DingTalk waits 5 seconds for a response from a third-party callback API. If the third-party system does not return HTTP 200 within 5 seconds, DingTalk retries up to 3 times at intervals of 1s, 2s, and 4s. Keep callback APIs lightweight and process complex logic asynchronously.
- **Q: Can I control the visibility of action-area buttons dynamically?**

  Yes. In the button configuration returned by `renderCallback`, use the `visible` field to control whether each button appears. Button visibility can change dynamically based on the approver's role, the approval status, business conditions, and more.
- **Q: How do I view workflow efficiency data after hosting the approval page?**

  After hosting, third-party approval flow data is collected in the official DingTalk approval efficiency Kanban. Management can view workflow efficiency reports through push notifications from the OA Approval assistant, or in the mobile app under OA Approval > Statistics, to fully assess the efficiency and soundness of the organization's approvals.
