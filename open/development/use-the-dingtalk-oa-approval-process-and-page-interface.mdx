# Official OA Approval: Integrate with DingTalk Processes and Pages

## Overview

### Background

Business systems that organizations build or purchase usually have their own approval processes and pages. Employees must switch between multiple systems to handle approvals. This fragments their work and reduces efficiency. Connect your business system's approvals to the official DingTalk OA Approval Center. Users can then view and handle all approvals from a single entry point in DingTalk with a standardized, one-stop experience. This approach addresses the following problems:

- **Inefficient switching between systems**: Employees switch back and forth between the business system and DingTalk to handle approvals. The process is tedious, and urgent items are easy to miss.
- **Out-of-sync approval status**: Data in the business system and DingTalk is inconsistent. Approval progress is not visible in real time, which delays decisions.
- **Scattered notification channels**: Approval notifications rely on each system's internal messages or email. They cannot use DingTalk's unified To-Do reminders and message push capabilities.
- **Difficult queries for managers**: Managers must sign in to multiple systems to check approval progress. They cannot track overall approval status in DingTalk.

### Core Value

This solution reuses the process engine and form components of the official DingTalk OA Approval to give organizations a standardized approval experience:

- **More standardized**: Use DingTalk OA processes and pages to connect seamlessly with DingTalk Chat, To-Do, and Notifications for efficient approvals.
- **One-stop experience**: Reuse the official DingTalk approval engine and form components to give users a unified OA Approval experience across all devices.
- **Deep integration**: Connect seamlessly with core DingTalk capabilities such as Chats, To-Do, and Drive. Approval results sync to the business system in real time through callback events.
- **Lower development costs**: Skip building your own approval engine and form designer. Reuse DingTalk's mature capabilities to launch approval processes quickly.

### Use Cases

This solution suits the following typical approval integration scenarios:

- **Production work order approval in manufacturing**: When the MES initiates a work order approval, it automatically creates a DingTalk approval instance. Approvers view work order details and take action directly in DingTalk.
- **Store operations approval in retail chains**: After store managers submit requests in the business system, the approval processes are automatically aggregated into the DingTalk OA Approval Center for unified management.
- **Cross-department collaboration in group organizations**: Headquarters and branches handle all types of business requests through the unified DingTalk approval entry point to improve collaboration.
- **Organizations with mature approval systems**: Reuse DingTalk's native approval engine and form components while keeping initiation and management in the business system.

## Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Integrate Production Work Order Approval in Manufacturing

#### Pain Points

A manufacturer uses an in-house MES to manage the flow of production work orders. Its original approval process relied on offline signatures and email confirmation, which was slow and hard to trace. Employees switched back and forth between the MES and DingTalk. Approval status was not synced in real time, which delayed production:

- Employees switched back and forth between the MES and DingTalk to handle approvals. The process was tedious, and urgent work orders were easy to miss.
- Approval status depended on manual sync. Data in the MES and DingTalk was inconsistent, which delayed production.
- Management could not view work order approval progress in DingTalk and had to sign in to multiple systems to check.
- Approval notifications relied on email or offline communication and could not use DingTalk's unified To-Do reminders and message push capabilities.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated process**

  The integration flow has 5 steps:

  1. The MES initiates a work order.
  2. The integration API **creates an approval instance** in DingTalk.
  3. The approval is handled **within DingTalk**. The work order approval form shows fields such as **Work Order No.**, **Work Order Type**, **Applicant Department**, **Priority**, and **Applicant**, plus a **Submit for Approval** button.
  4. The approver takes action on mobile: **Reject** or **Approve**.
  5. The result is **synced back to the MES**.

  Compared with the traditional manual flow (export the order > send an email > handle manually > send an email notice > record the result), the automated flow unifies the entry point, syncs in real time, and pushes message notifications.
- **Key advantages**

  - **Integrated production work order approval:** When the MES initiates a work order approval, it automatically creates a DingTalk approval instance. Approvers view work order details and take action directly in DingTalk without switching systems.
  - **Real-time visibility into approval processes:** DingTalk displays complete work order data, including the bill of materials, quality inspection requirements, and historical work orders. Approval progress is clear at a glance, with support for comments and reminders.
  - **End-to-end data sync:** Approval results are written back to the MES in real time. Rejections automatically cancel downstream To-Dos. This keeps data consistent and traceable on both ends and improves approval turnaround by more than 60%.

### Scenario 2: Store Operations Approval in Retail Chains

#### Pain Points

A retail chain operates hundreds of stores nationwide. Approvals for store renovations, promotions, and staff transfers are frequent. After store managers submitted requests, the approval processes were scattered across multiple systems. Regional managers and headquarters leads struggled to handle them in one place, and approval response times were long:

- Store managers submitted requests separately in multiple business systems. The process was tedious, and key information was easy to miss.
- Approval processes were scattered across different systems. Regional managers and headquarters leads had to sign in to multiple platforms and handle approvals one by one.
- Approval notifications relied on each system's internal messages and could not use DingTalk's unified To-Do reminders and message push capabilities.
- Management could not view approval progress for all stores in DingTalk and had to sign in to multiple systems to compile statistics.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated process**

  The end-to-end approval flow:

  1. The store submits a request.
  2. The request is aggregated into DingTalk through the API.
  3. The request enters the unified **Approval Center** in DingTalk OA.
  4. The regional manager approves.
  5. The headquarters lead approves.
- **Key advantages**

  - **Unified entry point for store approvals:** After store managers submit requests in the business system, the approval processes are automatically aggregated into the DingTalk OA Approval Center. Regional managers and headquarters leads handle all approvals in DingTalk.
  - **Real-time To-Do and message delivery:** DingTalk To-Do reminders and message notifications push approval tasks to approvers immediately, so urgent approvals are not missed.
  - **80% faster approval response:** Average approval response time dropped from 2 days to under 4 hours. This greatly improved store operations decision-making and supports efficient management of hundreds of stores.

## Implementation Guide

### Technical Architecture

The technical architecture of this solution includes the following core components:

- **Identity authentication layer**: Manages app credentials and obtains access tokens to verify the identity of all API calls.
- **Approval template management layer**: Creates, updates, and queries approval form templates, and syncs the business system's approval form structure to the DingTalk OA Approval Center.
- **Approval instance engine**: Initiates, revokes, and manages the status of approval instances, and controls the lifecycle of approval processes.
- **Approval task processing layer**: Queries task nodes and performs accept, decline, and transfer actions to move approval processes between nodes.
- **Attachment and comment service**: Authorizes the approval Drive space, uploads and downloads files, and manages approval comments to support rich-media approval interactions.
- **Event callback channel**: Pushes status change events for approval tasks and instances to the business system for real-time two-way sync of approval data.

### Prerequisites

- **App preparation**: Complete [app creation and configuration](/open/dingstart/create-application).
- **Permission requirements**: Have DingTalk Organization Admin or Sub Admin permissions, and apply for the following API permissions:

  - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (workflow instance read permission)
- **Development environment**: Install a Java development environment (JDK 1.6 or later) and the Maven build tool.
- **SDK preparation**: Download the DingTalk Server-side SDK. It supports multiple languages, including Java, Python, and Go.

### Code Implementation

Step 1: Go to the app details page and get the app's Client ID and Client Secret.

Step 2: Apply for API permissions. Apply for the relevant "OA Approval" permissions.

Step 3: Get the app access credential. See [Obtain the access token of an Internal app](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app). When you call an API, the access token authenticates the caller's identity.

Step 4: Call the Server-side OA Approval APIs.

1. Call the Server API [Create or update an approval form template](/open/development/create-an-approval-form-template) to create an official OA approval template and get its unique code, processCode.
2. Call the Server API [Create an approval instance](/open/development/create-an-approval-instance) to initiate an approval instance based on the template code and get the instance ID, instanceId.
3. Call the Server API [Get approval Drive space information](/open/development/obtains-the-information-about-approval-nail-disk) to get upload permissions for the Drive space and the spaceId.
4. Call the Client-side JSAPI uploadAttachmentToDingTalk to upload approval attachments to the Drive space.
5. Call the Server API [Add an approval comment](/open/development/official-approval-adds-approval-comments) to add comments and attachments to an approval instance.
6. Call the Server API [Authorize the download of approval Drive files](/open/development/download-the-approval-nail-file) to get download authorization for approval Drive files.
7. Call the Server API [Authorize the preview of approval attachments](/open/development/official-authorized-preview-approval-attachment) to get preview authorization for approval attachments.
8. Call the Server API [Download an approval attachment](/open/development/download-an-approval-attachment) to get the attachment download link, downloadUri.
9. Call the Server API [Get the details of a single approval instance](/open/development/obtains-the-details-of-a-single-approval-instance-pop) to get the approval instance details and the taskId of each task node.
10. Call the Server API [Accept or decline an approval task](/open/development/approve-or-reject-the-approval-task) to accept or decline a specified task node.
11. Call the Server API [Revoke an approval instance](/open/development/revoke-an-approval-instance) to revoke an initiated approval instance.
12. Subscribe to approval task change callback events to receive status change notifications such as task start, end, and transfer.
13. Subscribe to approval instance change callback events to receive status change notifications such as instance start, end, termination, and deletion.

## Implementation Steps

### **Step 1: Get the App Credentials**

1. Sign in to the [DingTalk Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.io/).
2. Select the target app to open the app details page.
3. Click **Basic Information** > **Credentials and Basic Information**.
4. Record the app's `Client ID` and `Client Secret`.

### Note

   Keep the `Client Secret` secure and never share it with third parties. Store it in environment variables or a key management system instead of hardcoding it.

### Step 2: Add API Permissions

1. On the app details page, click **Manage permissions**.
2. In the permission search box, enter the following permission identifiers and apply for them:

   - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission): Create and update approval form templates.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission): Initiate and revoke approval instances.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (workflow instance read permission): Query approval instance details.

<Note>
Different business scenarios may require different permission combinations. Apply based on your actual needs. For example, batch import scenarios also require permissions related to batch user operations.
</Note>

### Step 3: Get the Access Credential (Access Token)

Use the Client ID and Client Secret from Step 1 to get the app access credential. See [Obtain the access token of an Internal app](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app).

```java
public void getAccessToken() throws Exception {
  Config config = new Config();
  config.protocol = "https";
  config.regionId = "central";
  com.aliyun.dingtalkoauth2_1_0.Client client = new com.aliyun.dingtalkoauth2_1_0.Client(config);
  GetAccessTokenRequest accessTokenRequest = new GetAccessTokenRequest()
    .setAppKey("din*********hgn")
    .setAppSecret("9G_O************mBkhgGIO");
  GetAccessTokenResponse accessToken = client.getAccessToken(accessTokenRequest);
  System.out.println(JSON.toJSONString(accessToken.getBody()));
}
```

**Best practices**:

- **Caching**: The `accessToken` is valid for 2 hours. Cache it in memory or Redis, and refresh it 5 minutes before it expires.
- **Concurrency control**: Prevent conflicts caused by multiple threads refreshing the token at the same time. Use a distributed lock.
- **Error retries**: Retry automatically during network fluctuations, up to 3 times, with increasing intervals (1s → 2s → 4s).

## FAQ

- **Q1: What are the requirements for componentId in formComponents when I create an approval template?**

  componentId must be unique within the form. It has no business meaning and only identifies the component. To use a business identifier, use the bizAlias field instead.
- **Q2: What values does actionType in approvers support when I create an approval instance?**

  actionType supports values such as NONE (single approver), OR (any approver's acceptance approves the task), and AND (all approvers must accept). For details, see the API documentation.
- **Q3: How do I get a processCode that I did not save?**

  If you did not save the processCode returned when you created the approval template, sign in to the DingTalk OA Approval Admin Console to find it, or call the API that gets the template code.
- **Q4: Do I need to get the Drive space information before I upload approval attachments?**

  Yes. Before you add approval comment attachments, call the API that gets approval Drive space information to obtain the spaceId and upload permissions. Then upload the files to the Drive space through the JSAPI. Finally, call the API that adds an approval comment to associate the attachments.
- **Q5: How do I configure approval callback events?**

  Configure event subscription in the Open Platform console. Select the "Approval task change" and "Approval instance change" events, and enter the business system's callback URL. For details, see the guide on configuring the event push mode.
