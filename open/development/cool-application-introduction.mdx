## What Is a Cool App

**Cool App** lets users work with apps more conveniently on DingTalk, turning multiple steps into one—shifting from "**people looking for apps**" to "**apps reaching people**", from "**people looking for data**" to "**data reaching people**", and from "**passive work**" to "**proactive exploration**". First, get a quick overview of Cool Apps through videos and scenarios, and see how a Cool App makes working with apps more convenient on DingTalk.

**Scenario 1:** For example, several employees are on a business trip and submit different information through different systems, which involves many tedious steps. Employees first need to create a group and submit their information individually, book flights with a ticketing app, switch to another app to book hotels, and switch again to hail a car. If they need to report their itinerary or apply for funds, they must switch to the company system. Only after completing all steps do they notify the other employees.

With a Cool App, employees no longer need to switch between apps. They can quickly book flights, hotels, and cars with a simple tap in the group. If their itinerary matches a colleague who traveled earlier, they can tap +1 to travel together with one click. Itinerary data is also synced automatically with the company's internal system, so employees no longer need to do it manually.

**Scenario 2:** Employee Xiaoming is handling a project in a group chat, while colleague A is discussing the project with other colleagues, colleague B may be writing a project-related document, colleague C is on the DingTalk Workbench, and colleague D may be viewing the calendar. Xiaoming only needs to update any project data in the group chat, and colleagues A, B, C, and D can see it in real time and respond directly without leaving their current interface.

**Cool App** synchronizes data between apps, between apps and people, and between people, transforming how employees work and bringing value to more users. Cool App also encourages more employees to work with a digital approach and mindset, letting users see problems together, solve problems together, and achieve goals together through an immersive and impressive experience.

**Cool App** is a brand-new app form based on the concepts of immersion, scenario orientation, network collaboration, and inclusive data. Unlike traditional B2B apps, which consist of relatively complex features, forms, processes, and reports, a Cool App uses a very lightweight approach to deconstruct, fragment, and personalize complex features, processes, and reports, bringing these features into high-frequency collaboration scenarios such as Direct Messages, Group Chats, Events, Documents, Search, and the Workbench. ## Cool App Use Cases

### OKR Scenario

Enable the OKR Cool App in a Department Chat so that employees can use OKRs in their daily work. The OKR Cool App offers features such as one-click creation, smart reminders for alignment, and reminders to collaborators based on relevance when updates occur. It also provides weekly goal progress summaries and one-click weekly report generation, turning the progress of the week's main Objectives and Key Results into a concise weekly report that Group members can easily view.

### Recruitment Scenario

In the past, various recruitment processes faced many coordination problems when changes occurred. Many organizations use multiple systems or no system at all, and complete recruitment in parallel across multiple roles, stages, and systems (or no system), resulting in low collaboration efficiency. With the Recruitment Cool App, you can create a group for a specific recruitment position and have multiple roles collaborate in a single group, with structured data accumulated, greatly improving recruitment efficiency.

### Meal-Ordering Scenario

Ordering meals used to involve tedious steps: first open the Workbench, find the meal-ordering app, and then look for suitable food.

Ordering meals with a Cool App is more convenient. For a specific group of people, such as those eating healthy meals, you can create a group to remind one another to look after their health. You can also add the meal-ordering Cool App, order with one tap when it's time, and use a half-screen card that recalls your last order immersively without any redirect. The Cool App backend automatically generates the meal-ordering list, which can be printed to prepare and distribute meals. There's no need to say "I want a beef meal, no rice, extra cherry tomatoes" in chat or to fill out a form to order, freeing up restaurant staff as well.

## What Changes a Cool App Brings

### Striking a Balance Between Organizational Governance and Employee Experience

In recent years, there has been much discussion about organizational management or governance models, with various framings such as pyramid versus flat, control versus empowerment. These largely view past and future organizational design from opposing perspectives, treating them as two different management or governance models.

The future organization is no longer about simple control. Instead, under a clear business strategy, it involves organizing an organization's culture, mechanisms, and processes from the top down, and planning the digital system from that starting point. For example, whether to establish financial sharing or human resources sharing, upgrade the customer service center, launch OKRs, talent reviews, or an employee points incentive program, release a flexible fixed-asset management policy, or upgrade employee incentive and promotion systems based on different business attributes. These systems don't have to be all-encompassing either. For organizations with diversified operations, the design can be diversified and differentiated according to organizational and business attributes.

The future organization is also no longer about simple flattening or information transparency. The core is how to naturally integrate employees into the business and organizational systems, so that mechanisms, processes, and rules can be effectively implemented while employees do not feel obvious constraints from those mechanisms and rules. Built on DingTalk's organizational scale, high-frequency collaboration fields, and scenario-oriented Cool Apps, mechanisms, processes, and rules can become the invisible hand behind an organization's efficient and flexible operation, solving many of the challenges organizations currently face.

### Driving Low-Frequency Software Through High-Frequency Collaboration Fields to Accelerate Digital Penetration

In the past, organizations put in great effort to accelerate message delivery, data flow, and business advancement, building multiple siloed information systems for this purpose. DingTalk solves these problems. DingTalk is first of all an IM platform that solves the problem of communication and message delivery. It is also a collaboration platform, where the basis of collaboration is that data can flow freely and remain consistent, and low-code makes DingTalk an app development platform. A Cool App further opens up a high-frequency entry point for enterprise apps.

### Moving From Microservices to Micro Apps to Solve Scaling Challenges

Among all logical computation approaches, there are two ways of thinking: one is Top Down, and the other is Bottom Up. Top Down starts from the highest level and decomposes layer by layer, ultimately producing the entire blueprint. Most complex B2B apps are designed using the Top Down approach, such as the division of modules and the design of feature processes in an ERP. Scenario-based apps, however, need to adopt the Bottom Up approach.

To truly achieve immersion and high activity, a Cool App needs to organically combine the two. For mid- and back-office services, the Top Down approach is still needed to design and accumulate domain services. For the interaction design of a Cool App, the Bottom Up approach must be adopted, starting from users' actual collaboration scenarios. Ultimately, a Cool App will drive mid- and back-office services and client-side interaction innovation toward a completely different style.

## The Relationship Between Cool App and Low-Code/YiDA

The **low-code revolution** that DingTalk previously proposed was an innovation in how B2B apps are developed, while **Cool App** is an innovation in how B2B apps are used.

A Cool App deconstructs the traditional software system and reintegrates scenario-based requirements, making apps and tools readily accessible and bringing app and data capabilities to all employees. [YiDA](https://www.aliwork.com/) or DingTalk Da, from a low-code perspective, allows more people who understand the business to build apps in a low-barrier way. As the Cool App concept is promoted and widely recognized, low-code platforms such as YiDA and DingTalk Da will inevitably become Cool App factories, making it even easier to build Cool Apps.

## Introduction to Cool App Open Capabilities

[Group Extension](/zh/open/development/cool-application-overview) **is a type of Cool App**. It is an extension form used within chats, and it extends the main app with quick entry points, chat Bots, and interactive cards. This lets an app not only deliver immersive experiences such as multi-person, multi-role task collaboration, joint goal creation, business opportunity sharing, and battle report notifications in group chats, but also technically integrate a Cool App into Direct Message chats.

## How to Design a Cool App

The Cool App design source files are now available. They include Cool App design recommendations and general style specifications to help make the app "cool" experience even better. They also include design recommendations and design case templates for six fields: group pin, activity cards, Workbench, personal profile, the mobile half-screen container, and the desktop sidebar. Go to [Cool App Design Specifications](https://ding.design/#/cate/1/page/818) to view and download them.

### Cool App Design Guide

1. Clarify the app's most core value advantage.
2. Design an appropriate welcome experience.
3. Choose a suitable carrier for the Cool App and design the corresponding content.
4. Keep card information concise and easy to browse.
5. Have information change in real time as interactions occur.
6. Recommendations for button placement.
7. Cover multiple clients, with interactions across clients aligned to usage habits.
8. Adapt to Dark Mode.
