# OA Approval Integration: Download and Preview Approval Attachments

## Overview

### Background

Organization OA approval processes often involve attachments such as contracts, invoices, and qualification certificates. Approvers need to preview or download these attachments directly in DingTalk to review them. The OA Approval attachment APIs support online preview, secure download, and version management of approval attachments in DingTalk. Approvers no longer need to switch to the source system to view attachments, which improves approval efficiency and keeps the experience consistent.

Organizations commonly face the following pain points:

- **Long path to view attachments:** The approval details page shows only the attachment name and size. Approvers must switch to the business system to preview the content. Each approval takes about 2 extra minutes, which adds up to more than 30 minutes per day.
- **No mobile preview:** Most business systems do not support attachment preview on mobile devices. Approvers who are traveling or commuting cannot view key attachments such as contract terms or invoice details on their phones, so approval decisions are delayed.
- **Uncontrolled attachment downloads:** Anyone can download and forward sensitive attachments such as payslips and business contracts. Without watermarks, copy restrictions, or expiration limits, data leaks are a real risk.
- **Hard to distinguish attachment versions:** When an approval form contains multiple versions of an attachment, DingTalk cannot identify the version number or timestamp. Approvers may review an outdated file and reach the wrong decision.

### Key Benefits

- **End-to-end attachment automation:** APIs drive the entire process, from obtaining the Drive space and uploading files to creating approval instances and authorizing attachment downloads and previews. No manual steps are required.
- **Unified approval experience:** Approvers view attachment details and take action directly in DingTalk, without switching to external systems or downloading files locally.
- **Secure and controlled data:** The approval Drive space uses permission isolation, so attachments are accessible only within the authorized scope. Temporary download authorization and preview links protect sensitive files.

### Use Cases

This solution applies when you initiate approvals with attachments from your own business system and need to download or preview those attachments during the approval process. Typical use cases include:

- **Contract approval:** Attach scanned contracts, performance guarantees, and other legal documents.
- **Procurement approval:** Attach quotations, supplier qualification certificates, maintenance history, and other files.
- **Expense reimbursement approval:** Attach invoice images, expense details tables, and other financial vouchers.
- **Project acceptance:** Attach acceptance reports, site photos, inspection reports, and other materials.

## Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Construction Contract Approval (Construction Industry)

#### Pain Points

A large construction group uses an in-house project management system to manage construction contract approvals. When submitting a contract for approval, project managers must attach multiple files, such as the scanned contract, the counterparty's business license, and a performance guarantee. The original process had the following issues:

- **Inefficient attachment delivery:** Paper contracts had to be scanned and emailed to approvers at each level, which took 2-3 business days on average. Urgent contracts could not move forward in time.
- **Disorganized version management:** Contract versions from multiple revision rounds were scattered across different users' mailboxes. Approvers struggled to confirm the latest version and risked signing the wrong one.
- **Difficult compliance review:** Legal and finance approvers could not view attachments directly in the approval interface. They had to print the materials and check them one by one, which was slow and prone to missing key clauses.
- **No audit trail:** Manually delivered attachments had no action logs. Auditors could not reconstruct how files moved through the approval process or who was responsible.

#### Value Delivered

- **Automated process**

  End-to-end automated contract approval flow:

  1. The project management system initiates the contract approval (uploading files such as building drawings and the contract file).
  2. An API integration layer handles the interface calls, covering **Authentication**, **Authorization**, **Routing**, and **Logging**.
  3. The approval form is displayed inside DingTalk, showing the contract attachments, the counterparty's qualification details, and historical signing records.
  4. The approver reviews and acts on the request (**Reject** / **Approve**) from the DingTalk mobile client, with optional approval comments.
  5. The result is written back to the project management system, with data synced bidirectionally.

  Automated integration flow: **Upload from project management system** > **DingTalk Approval Center** > **Approver reviews in-app** > **Result written back in real time**.
- **Key advantages**

  - **One-click approval with attachment details:** Approvers view contract details and take action directly in DingTalk, without switching systems.
  - **Real-time visibility into the approval process:** DingTalk displays complete contract data, including the attachment list, counterparty qualifications, and historical signing records. Approval progress is clear at a glance, and comments and reminders are supported.
  - **End-to-end data synchronization:** Approval results are written back to the project management system in real time. When a request is rejected, the applicant is automatically notified to provide additional materials. Data stays consistent and traceable on both ends, and the contract approval cycle is shortened by more than 60%.

### Scenario 2: University Research Funding Reimbursement Approval (Education Industry)

#### Pain Points

A leading university uses a research management system to process faculty research funding reimbursements. When submitting a reimbursement request, the principal investigator must attach supporting materials such as scanned invoices, business trip approval forms, and meeting invitation letters. The original process had the following issues:

- **Tedious receipt verification:** Finance staff had to switch repeatedly between the research system and email to verify invoice authenticity and amounts. Reviewing a single reimbursement took more than 40 minutes.
- **Hard to ensure complete materials:** Attachment requirements vary widely across research projects. Approvers could not quickly check online whether all materials were provided, and the return rate for missing documents reached 35%.
- **Delayed cross-campus collaboration:** The main campus finance office and branch campus research secretaries relied on offline delivery of paper documents. Reimbursements at remote campuses arrived 5-7 days later than at the main campus.
- **Weak budget monitoring:** Reimbursement approvals were disconnected from project budget limits. Overspending was discovered only after the fact, with no mechanism to block it in advance.

#### Value Delivered

- **Automated process**

  The automated reimbursement flow runs in five stages:
  1. **Finance Initiates Reimbursement** — receipt capture and expense entry.
  2. **Automatic System Validation** (via **API**) — budget verification and material review.
  3. **In-DingTalk Approval** — multi-level approval with form display.
  4. **Approver Action** — mobile approval and quick processing.
  5. **Result Synced to Finance** — status write-back and data synchronization.

  Compared with the traditional manual flow (**manual form** > **email** > **manual reconciliation** > **paper filing**), the automated flow provides a unified entry point, real-time synchronization, and in-app message delivery.
- **Key advantages**

  - **Receipts linked to budgets online:** Approvers view invoice images, remaining project budget, and reimbursement history directly in DingTalk, and complete compliance checks in one place.
  - **Smart prompts for complete materials:** The system automatically lists the required attachments based on the project type and highlights missing items, reducing unnecessary returns.
  - **Zero-delay cross-campus approval:** All attachments are stored centrally in the approval Drive space. Branch campus staff approve online without waiting for paper documents, and reimbursement turnaround improves by more than 70%.

## Implementation Guide

### Technical Architecture

The technical architecture of this solution includes the following core components:

- **Authentication layer:** Manages App credentials and obtains access tokens to verify the identity of every API call.
- **Drive management layer:** Obtains the approval Drive space and uploads files, providing secure storage and permission isolation for attachments.
- **Instance execution layer:** Initiates, queries, and tracks approval instances to drive the approval process.
- **Attachment authorization layer:** Authorizes downloads and previews of approval attachments so that sensitive files are used only within the authorized scope.

### Prerequisites

- **App preparation:** Complete the [App creation and configuration](/open/dingstart/create-application) process and obtain the client ID and Client Secret.
- **Permission requirements:** Have DingTalk Organization Admin or Sub Admin permissions, and apply for the following API permissions:

  - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (approval instance write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (approval instance read permission)
- **Development environment:** Install a Java development environment (JDK 1.6 or later) and the Maven build tool.
- **SDK preparation:** Download the DingTalk Server-side SDK, which supports Java, Python, Go, and other languages.

### Code Implementation

Step 1: Go to the App details page and obtain the client ID and Client Secret.

Step 2: Apply for API permissions. Apply for the required "OA Approval" permissions.

Step 3: Obtain the App access credential. See [Obtain the Access Token of an Internal App](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app). The access token authenticates the caller's identity when calling APIs.

Step 4: Call the OA Approval APIs and Client-side JSAPIs:

1. Call the Server API [Obtain Approval Drive Space Information](/open/development/obtains-the-information-about-approval-nail-disk) to get the approval Drive space `spaceId`.
2. Call the Client-side JSAPI for uploading attachments to Drive or selecting files from Drive to get the basic file information.
3. Get the unique template code `processCode`.

   - Open the approval template in the [DingTalk Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io/index.htm?lang=en_US#/microApp/microAppListNew) under OA Approval to get it.
   - Call the Server API [Create or Update an Approval Form Template](/open/development/create-an-approval-form-template) to create an approval template and get the `processCode`.
4. Call the Server API [Create an Approval Instance](/open/development/create-an-approval-instance) to initiate an approval and get the approval instance `instanceId`.
5. Using the approval instance `instanceId`, call the Server API [Obtain the Details of a Single Approval Instance](/open/development/obtains-the-details-of-a-single-approval-instance-pop) to get the `fileId` from the approval instance details.
6. Perform approval attachment actions:

   1. Download approval attachments

      1. Call the Server API [Authorize Download of Approval Drive Files](/open/development/download-the-approval-nail-file) to authorize access to the approval Drive file.
      2. Call the Server API [Download an Approval Attachment](/open/development/download-an-approval-attachment) to get the file link `downloadUri` and download the file. Third-party enterprise apps cannot call this API at this time.
   2. Preview approval attachments:

      1. Call the Server API [Authorize Preview of Approval Attachments](/open/development/official-authorized-preview-approval-attachment) to enable preview of the Drive file.
      2. Call the Client-side JSAPI for previewing Drive files to preview the file.

## Implementation Steps

### **Step 1: Obtain the App Credential**

1. Sign in to the [DingTalk Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.io/).
2. Select the target App to open the App details page.
3. Click **Basic Information** > **Credentials and Basic Information**.
4. Record the App's `Client ID` and `Client Secret`.

### Note

   Keep your `Client Secret` secure and never share it with third parties. Store it in environment variables or a secrets management system instead of hardcoding it.

### Step 2: Add API Permissions

1. On the App details page, click **Manage permissions**.
2. Enter the following permission identifiers in the permission search box and apply for them:

   - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission): Used to create and update approval form templates
   - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (approval instance write permission): Used to initiate and execute approval instances
   - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (approval instance read permission): Used to query approval instance details

<Note>
Different business scenarios may require different permission combinations. Apply based on your actual needs.
</Note>

### Step 3: Obtain the Access Credential (Access Token)

Use the Client ID and Client Secret from Step 1 to obtain the App access credential. See [Obtain the Access Token of an Internal App](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app).

**Best practices**:

- **Caching strategy:** The `accessToken` is valid for 2 hours. Cache it in memory or Redis, and refresh it proactively 5 minutes before it expires.
- **Concurrency control:** Prevent multiple threads from refreshing the token at the same time and causing conflicts. Use a distributed lock.
- **Error retry:** Retry automatically on network fluctuations, up to 3 times, with increasing intervals (1s → 2s → 4s).

## FAQ

- **Q: Why must I obtain approval Drive space information before calling the attachment upload JSAPI?**

  The Obtain Approval Drive Space Information API grants the user access to the Drive space. Uploading without this authorization fails the permission check.
- **Q: Can third-party enterprise apps call the Download an Approval Attachment API?**

  No. Third-party enterprise apps cannot call the Download an Approval Attachment API at this time. Only Internal apps can use it. Third-party apps can view attachments through the preview authorization API.
- **Q: How long does approval attachment authorization remain valid?**

  Download and preview links use temporary authorization. The validity period is controlled by the token returned by the API. Complete the action within the validity period. If the authorization expires, authorize again.
- **Q: How do I upload multiple attachments to the same approval instance?**

  In the formComponentValues of the Create an Approval Instance API, put the JSON objects for all attachments into a single array. Each object contains the spaceId, fileName, fileSize, fileType, and fileId fields.
