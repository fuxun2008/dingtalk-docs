# OA Approval Integration: Accept or Decline Approval Processes

## Overview

### Background

In-house and third-party business systems often have their own approval processes and pages. Employees must switch between multiple systems to handle approvals, which fragments their work and lowers efficiency. Connect your approval processes to the DingTalk OA Approval Center so users can view and handle all approvals from a single entry point in DingTalk.

- **Frequent system switching:** Employees switch back and forth between business systems and DingTalk. The long paths make it easy to miss urgent approvals.
- **Out-of-sync approval status:** Data in business systems and DingTalk does not match, which makes approval progress hard to track and manage in real time.
- **Scattered approval processes:** Managers cannot view all approval progress in DingTalk and must sign in to each system separately.
- **Delayed notifications:** Approval notifications rely on email or offline communication and do not use DingTalk's unified To-Do reminders and Message push.

### Core Value

This solution reuses the process engine and form components of the official OA Approval in DingTalk. It integrates your business system's approval processes into DingTalk for unified management and efficient collaboration.

- **Unified approval entry point:** Users view and handle approvals from all in-house systems in the DingTalk approval list, with less switching between systems.
- **Low integration cost:** Complete the integration with only a few API calls. No need to rebuild existing business processes or page logic.
- **Real-time status sync:** Approval results are written back to the business system in real time, keeping data consistent and traceable on both sides.
- **Instant message delivery:** DingTalk To-Do reminders and Message notifications push approval tasks to approvers immediately.

### Use Cases

This solution suits the following typical approval integration scenarios:

- In-house ERP, OA, or other business systems that need to connect with the DingTalk Approval Center.
- Retail chain store operation approvals, such as renovation requests and promotion approvals.
- Logistics and transportation dispatch approvals, such as vehicle repair requests and route change approvals.
- HR-related approvals, such as leave requests, expense reimbursements, onboarding, and offboarding.

## Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Medical Equipment Procurement Approval (Healthcare)

#### Pain Points

A top-tier hospital uses an in-house HIS system to manage medical equipment procurement. After a department submits a procurement request, it goes through three levels of approval: Equipment Department → Finance Department → Vice President in charge. The original process has the following issues:

- **Slow procurement response:** Urgent equipment purchases wait for paper forms to be signed level by level, causing an average delay of 3–5 days and slowing down clinical care.
- **Incomplete approval information:** Approvers see only the equipment name and budget. They cannot view technical specifications, supplier qualifications, or procurement history in DingTalk, which makes accurate decisions difficult.
- **Unclear approval accountability:** Multi-level approvals lack a clear order and deadlines, leading to buck-passing or duplicate approvals and longer decision cycles.
- **Severe data silos:** Procurement status in the HIS system is out of sync with DingTalk approval progress. Departments cannot get approval results in real time and must call repeatedly to check.

#### Value Delivered

- **Automated Workflow**

  Integration flow:

  1. The HIS system initiates a procurement request.
  2. An approval instance is created in DingTalk (via API).
  3. The procurement request is approved within DingTalk.
  4. The approver reviews details and takes action (**Approve** or **Reject**).
  5. The result is synced back to the HIS system.

  The DingTalk approval card displays fields including applicant, requesting department, procurement name, amount, and supplier.
- **Key Benefits**

  - **Online verification of equipment specifications and qualifications:** When equipment is entered in the HIS system, a DingTalk approval instance is created automatically. Approvers view equipment details and take action directly in DingTalk without switching systems.
  - **Transparent, traceable multi-level co-signing:** DingTalk displays complete equipment data, including technical specifications, supplier qualifications, and procurement history. Approval progress is clear at a glance, with support for comments and reminders.
  - **Fast track for urgent procurement:** Approval results are written back to the HIS system in real time. When a request is declined, downstream to-dos are canceled automatically. Data stays consistent and traceable on both sides, and approval turnaround improves by more than 50%.

### Scenario 2: University Research Fund Reimbursement Approval (Education)

#### Pain Points

A leading university uses an in-house research management system to manage fund reimbursements. After a faculty member submits a reimbursement request, it goes through three levels of approval: Project Lead → School → Finance Office. The original process has the following issues:

- **Long reimbursement cycle:** Paper forms are passed manually between approvers, taking an average of 7–10 business days and putting financial pressure on faculty who pay upfront.
- **Difficult invoice review:** Approvers cannot view original invoices or expense details in DingTalk. They must print paper documents and check them one by one, which is inefficient.
- **Poor cross-department collaboration:** Project leads, school secretaries, and finance reviewers lack a real-time communication channel. Returned requests must go through the entire process again.
- **High compliance risk:** Manually filled reimbursement forms are prone to account errors and over-limit claims. Violations found in later audits require accountability and rectification.

#### Value Delivered

- **Automated Workflow**

  The integrated research-funding reimbursement flow runs end to end in five steps:

  1. The research system initiates the reimbursement (applicant submits the reimbursement request).
  2. An **approval instance** is created via the integration API.
  3. The reimbursement form flows into DingTalk for in-app review, where its details are displayed.
  4. The **approver** acts on mobile, choosing **Reject** or **Approve**.
  5. The result is written back and synced to the research system.

  Compared with a traditional manual flow (submit > email > manual handling > email notification > manual entry), the automated integration gives a single entry point, real-time sync, and consolidated message delivery.
- **Key Benefits**

  - **Intelligent invoice image recognition and verification:** When an invoice is uploaded to the research system, a DingTalk approval instance is created automatically. Approvers view invoice images and take action directly in DingTalk without switching systems.
  - **Real-time budget balance validation:** DingTalk displays complete reimbursement data, including invoice images, expense details, and budget balance. Approval progress is clear at a glance, with support for comments and reminders.
  - **Closed-loop return and correction:** Approval results are written back to the research system in real time. When a request is declined, the applicant is notified automatically to provide additional materials. Data stays consistent and traceable on both sides, and the reimbursement cycle is shortened by more than 60%.

## Implementation Guide

### Technical Architecture

The technical architecture of this solution includes the following core components:

- **Authentication layer:** Manages App credentials and obtains access tokens to verify the identity of every API call.
- **Template management layer:** Creates and updates approval form templates and defines the structure and fields of the Approval process.
- **Instance execution layer:** Initiates, queries, and executes approval instances to drive the Approval process.
- **Event callback layer:** Receives status change events for approval tasks and instances to enable two-way sync between the business system and DingTalk.

### Prerequisites

- **App preparation:** Complete [App creation and configuration](/open/dingstart/create-application) and obtain the Client ID and Client Secret.
- **Permission requirements:** Have DingTalk Organization Admin or Sub Admin permissions, and apply for the following API permissions:

  - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (approval instance write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (approval instance read permission)
- **Development environment:** Install a Java development environment (JDK 1.6 or later) and the Maven build tool.
- **SDK preparation:** Download the DingTalk Server-side SDK, available in Java, Python, Go, and other languages.

### Code Implementation

Step 1: Go to the App details page and get the client ID and Client Secret.

Step 2: Apply for API permissions. Apply for the relevant "OA Approval" permissions.

Step 3: Get the App access credential. See [Get the access token of an Internal app](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app). Use the access token to authenticate the caller when calling APIs.

Step 4: Call the OA Approval APIs:

1. Call the new Server API [Create or update an approval form template](/open/development/create-an-approval-form-template) to get the unique template code `processCode`.
2. Using `processCode`, call the new Server API [Create an approval instance](/open/development/create-an-approval-instance) to initiate an approval instance and get the approval instance `instanceId`.

### Note

   If you did not save the approval instance `instanceId`, call the new Server API [Get a list of approval instance IDs](/open/development/obtain-an-approval-list-of-instance-ids) to get the `instanceId` values for the time range in which the instances were initiated.
3. Using `instanceId`, call the new Server API [Get the details of a single approval instance](/open/development/obtains-the-details-of-a-single-approval-instance-pop) to get the approval instance details and the `taskId` of each task node.
4. Using `instanceId` and the corresponding `taskId`, call the new Server API [Accept or decline an approval task](/open/development/approve-or-reject-the-approval-task) to act on the approval task. The Approval form is approved once all approval nodes accept it. This step does not support Third-party enterprise apps.

## Implementation Steps

### **Step 1:** **Get App Credentials**

1. Sign in to the [DingTalk Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.io/).
2. Select the target App and go to the App details page.
3. Click **Basic Information** > **Credentials and Basic Information**.
4. Record the App's `Client ID` and `Client Secret`.

### Note

   Keep your `Client Secret` secure and never share it with third parties. Store it in environment variables or a key management system instead of hardcoding it.

### Step 2: Add API Permissions

1. On the App details page, click **Manage permissions**.
2. Enter the following permission identifiers in the permission search box and apply for them:

   - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission): Creates and updates approval form templates.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (approval instance write permission): Initiates and executes approval instances.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (approval instance read permission): Queries approval instance details.

<Note>
Different business scenarios may require different permission combinations. Apply based on your actual needs.
</Note>

### Step 3: Get the Access Credential (Access Token)

Use the Client ID and Client Secret from Step 1 to get the App access credential. See [Get the access token of an Internal app](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app).

**Best practices**:

- **Caching strategy**: The `accessToken` is valid for 2 hours. Cache it in memory or Redis and refresh it proactively 5 minutes before it expires.
- **Concurrency control**: Prevent multiple threads from refreshing the token at the same time and causing conflicts. Use a distributed lock.
- **Error retry**: Retry automatically on network fluctuations, up to 3 times, with increasing intervals (1s → 2s → 4s).

## FAQ

- **Q1: What are the requirements for componentId in formComponents when creating an approval template?**

  componentId must be unique within the form and has no business meaning. It only identifies the control. To use a business identifier, use the bizAlias field instead.
- **Q2: What values does actionType in approvers support when initiating an approval instance?**

  actionType supports values such as NONE (single approver), OR (any-sign: approved when any one approver accepts), and AND (co-sign: all approvers must accept). See the API documentation for details.
- **Q3: How do I get a processCode I did not save?**

  If you did not save the processCode returned when creating the approval template, sign in to the DingTalk OA Approval Admin Console to find it, or call the API that retrieves the template code.
- **Q4: What are the requirements for actionerUserId when executing an approval task?**

  actionerUserId must be the userId of a User assigned to the current approval node; otherwise, the action fails.
- **Q5: How does the business system detect status changes after an approval completes?**

  Subscribe to the approval instance change callback event to detect status changes in real time, or periodically call the approval instance details API and poll the status field.
