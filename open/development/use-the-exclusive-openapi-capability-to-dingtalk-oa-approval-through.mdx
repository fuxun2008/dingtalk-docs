# Deeply Integrate DingTalk OA Approval: Exclusive OpenAPI and AppLink Protocol Integration

## Overview

This solution targets organizations that have already connected multiple business systems to the DingTalk OA Approval Process Center and need further customization. It uses the exclusive OpenAPI of DingTalk OA Approval Premium Edition and the front-end AppLink protocol to integrate self-built apps deeply with DingTalk official OA Approval. The solution provides exclusive capabilities such as Approval Center list queries, batch processing of approval tasks, workflow handover, and approval page redirection. Use it to build a one-stop, immersive approval workbench that supports filtering by source, batch actions, and Admin-initiated transfers.

### Background

Approval requests from core business systems, such as ERP, CRM, and finance systems, must integrate deeply with DingTalk OA Approval. The goal is a closed loop: start the approval in DingTalk, process it in the business system, and write the result back to DingTalk. Exclusive OpenAPI and AppLink protocol integration embeds business approvals seamlessly into the DingTalk approval system while balancing user experience and data security.

Organizations commonly face the following challenges in daily operations:

- **Broken approval chains**: Approval forms generated in business systems must be recreated manually in DingTalk. The two records cannot be associated, and results do not sync back to the business system automatically.
- **Exposure of sensitive data**: Approval details appear directly in native DingTalk forms. Sensitive fields such as contract amounts and customer information are at risk of leakage.
- **Limited custom approval logic**: Native DingTalk approval components cannot handle complex business validation rules, such as budget linkage or multi-level countersign conditions. Business departments must fall back on offline communication.
- **Difficult audit tracing**: Approval action logs are split between DingTalk and the business system. Compliance audits require manually piecing together timelines, which is time-consuming and error-prone.

### Core Value

This solution delivers value in three areas:

- **Deep customization**: Exclusive OpenAPI exposes Approval Center lists, batch processing, workflow handover, and more. Fully customize pages and feature logic based on your business logic.
- **Rich capabilities**: The AppLink protocol redirects users from a self-built app directly to the DingTalk official OA Approval home page, instance creation page, instance details page, and more, reusing the native DingTalk experience.
- **Tailored responses**: Support multiple business solutions, including a self-built Approval Center, workflow handover, and approval page integration, to meet more personalized business needs.

### Use Cases

This solution applies to organizations that have completed basic OA Approval integration and need deeper customization. Typical use cases include:

- **One-stop immersive approval**: Company executives handle a large volume of approvals daily. They want to consolidate approval workflows from multiple third-party systems and DingTalk into a self-built app for unified batch processing.
- **Admin workflow handover**: When an employee leaves or changes roles, the Admin must transfer that employee's pending approval tasks in batches to other active members of the organization.
- **Deep approval page integration**: Certain business modules in a self-built app need to open the DingTalk official OA Approval home page, instance creation page, instance details page, and more directly.

## Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Approval Aggregation for Large Retail Chain Store Operations (Retail Industry)

#### Challenges

A nationwide retail chain operates more than 500 stores. It uses an in-house POS system for store replenishment requests, SAP for supplier purchase orders, and an in-house HR system for staff scheduling and compensatory leave. Each system has its own approval workflow. Regional managers and the head-office operations director must sign in to three systems every day to review and process approvals. This causes the following problems:

- **Frequent system switching**: More than 80 approvals per day are spread across three systems. Each switch requires signing in again and finding To-Do items, which takes about 25 minutes.
- **No batch actions**: Similar approvals, such as bulk replenishment or bulk scheduling adjustments, cannot be processed at once. Approvers must click Accept on each item, leading to repetitive work and fatigue.
- **Fragmented approval context**: The POS system does not show related supplier inventory status, and SAP does not show historical store sales data, so decision-making information is incomplete.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated workflow**

  The automated integration flow runs in five stages:

  1. **Multi-system access**: Connect official OA, self-built OA, and third-party systems (POS, SAP, HR).
  2. **Aggregated To-Do display**: Show all pending items in a unified view with source and type filters.
  3. **Batch Action**: Select multiple items of the same type and Accept them in one click.
  4. **Immersive detail view**: Open an item's details page with associated data (such as inventory and sales history) for decision support.
  5. **Real-time status sync**: Write the result back to each source system through callback events to keep data consistent.
- **Key advantages**

  - **Multi-source aggregation**: POS replenishment, SAP procurement, and HR scheduling approvals appear in one unified view. No system switching is required, saving 25 minutes per day.
  - **Batch efficiency**: Select similar approvals and accept them in a batch. Clicks per session drop from 80 to 3, improving efficiency in high-frequency scenarios by more than 90%.
  - **Complete context**: The details page of the self-built Approval Center embeds data APIs from each system. Approvers view related inventory, historical sales, and other decision-support information on a single page.

### Scenario 2: Approval Integration for a Nationwide Logistics Hub Park (Logistics and Warehousing Industry)

#### Challenges

A nationwide logistics hub park hosts more than 200 organizations, each using different ERP/WMS systems to manage warehouse operations. The park management committee must centrally supervise approvals for workplace safety, environmental compliance, and fire inspections. The original supervision model had the following problems:

- **Fragmented supervision**: With more than 200 different organizational systems, the committee cannot view the park's overall safety and compliance status. Hazard detection relies on self-reporting, resulting in high rates of concealed and missed reports.
- **Difficult rectification tracking**: After a violation is found, the committee must issue rectification notices offline, follow up by phone, and verify on site. Without digital tracking, the rectification completion rate is only 65%.
- **Slow emergency response**: Emergencies such as hazardous chemical leaks or fire alarms require multi-department collaborative approval. Traditional phone coordination takes too long and misses the best response window.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated workflow**

  The integrated automation flow runs in five stages:

  1. **More than 200 organizations onboarded**: Security compliance with unified aggregation.
  2. **Graded hazard alerts**: Automatic grading and real-time push notifications, with four severity levels: **Major Hazard**, **Significant Hazard**, **General Hazard**, and **Low Risk**.
  3. **One-click rectification**: Command dispatch and progress tracking.
  4. **Emergency collaborative approval**: Automatically triggered, with multi-department coordination through an emergency command center.
  5. **Real-time rectification rate sync**: Rate increases from 65% to 95%, with data kept consistent.
- **Key advantages**

  - **Full risk visibility**: The safety and compliance status of more than 200 organizations appears on a single screen. Hazards are graded and flagged automatically, reducing the rate of concealed and missed reports to below 5%.
  - **Closed-loop rectification**: Issue rectification orders for violations in one click. The system tracks progress automatically and reminds organizations with overdue items, raising the rectification completion rate from 65% to 95%.
  - **Emergency coordination**: Emergencies automatically trigger multi-department collaborative approval. Response time drops from 2 hours to 10 minutes, capturing the best response window.

## Implementation Guide

### Technical Architecture

The technical architecture of this solution includes the following core components:

- **Authentication layer**: Manages app credentials and obtains access tokens to verify the identity behind every API call.
- **Data aggregation layer**: Calls the exclusive APIs of DingTalk OA Approval Premium Edition to aggregate To-Do data from multiple sources, such as official OA Approval and self-built OA Approval, into a unified list.
- **Batch processing layer**: Distinguishes official and self-built approval tasks by the approval type `processType`, and calls the corresponding batch update API for each to sync status.
- **Workflow handover layer**: Lets Admins query the pending task list of a specific employee and transfer those tasks in batches to other active members of the organization.
- **Page integration layer**: Uses the AppLink protocol to redirect from a self-built app to the DingTalk official OA Approval home page, instance creation page, instance details page, and more.
- **Event callback layer**: Listens for callback events such as approval task start, end, and transfer, as well as approval instance status changes, to keep the self-built Approval Center data up to date.

### Prerequisites

- **App preparation**: Complete [App creation and configuration](/open/dingstart/create-application).
- **Permission requirements**: Hold DingTalk Organization Admin or Sub Admin permissions, and apply for the following API permissions:

  - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (write permission for workflow instances)
  - `Premium.Workflow.ReadWrite.All` (read/write permission for OA Approval workflows (exclusive to OA Premium Edition))
  - `qyapi_aflow` (approval workflow data management permission)
- **Development environment**: Install a Java development environment (JDK 1.6 or later) and the Maven build tool.
- **SDK preparation**: Download the DingTalk Server-side SDK, which supports Java, Python, Go, and other languages.

### Code Implementation

Step 1: Go to the app details page and get the app client ID and Client Secret.

Step 2: Apply for API permissions. Apply for the relevant "OA Approval" permissions.

Step 3: Get the app access credential. See [Get the access token of an internal app](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app). When calling APIs, use the access token to authenticate the caller.

Step 4: Call the OA Approval APIs:

1. Select an integration method:

   - **Self-built OA Approval integration:** Connect each third-party business system to DingTalk self-built OA Approval. For details, see [Self-built OA Approval: Third-party process and page integration](/open/development/use-three-party-process-and-page-docking).
   - **Official OA Approval integration:** Connect third-party business systems to DingTalk official OA Approval. For details, see [Official OA Approval: DingTalk process and page integration](/open/development/use-the-dingtalk-oa-approval-process-and-page-interface).
   - **User-initiated:** Users submit approval workflows directly in the DingTalk official OA Approval app. Use the two methods above to centralize scattered business workflows in the DingTalk OA Approval Center.
2. **Get Approval Center data:** Use the Premium Edition exclusive APIs to get the user's pending, processed, submitted, and received approval lists from the DingTalk Approval Center.
3. **Batch process approval tasks:** After the self-built Approval Center app retrieves the user's task list, use the approval type `processType` returned by the pending list API (0: official OA Approval, 1: self-built OA Approval) to batch update official and self-built OA Approval tasks separately.

   - **Batch update self-built OA Approval tasks:** Based on the approval instance `processInstanceId` and `taskId`, call the [Update Process Center task status](/open/development/update-process-center-task-status) API to sync the status of self-built approval To-Do items. In scenarios such as OR-sign, call the [Batch cancel pending tasks in the Process Center](/open/development/cancel-multiple-oa-approval-tasks) API to set all running To-Do items under an approval instance to CANCELED.
   - **Batch update official OA Approval tasks:** Based on the approval instance `processInstanceId` and `taskId`, the business system calls the new Server API for batch accepting or declining approval tasks to process a batch of tasks with different approval instance IDs and task node IDs.
4. When the status of an approval form changes, OA Approval pushes callback events, such as approval task status changes and approval instance status changes, to the business system. This enables deeper integration between organization apps and the DingTalk platform for information sharing and business collaboration. For details, see [Event subscription guide](/open/development/event-subscription-overview).
5. Call the API for Admins to query the pending task list of a specific employee. The Admin (`managerUserId`) queries all pending OA Approval tasks of the current approver's UserId.
6. Call the API for Admins to batch transfer the pending tasks of a specific employee. The Admin transfers the tasks returned by the query API in batches to other active members of the organization.

## Implementation Steps

### **Step 1: Get the App Credentials**

1. Sign in to the [DingTalk Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.io/).
2. Select the target app to open the app details page.
3. Click **Basic Information** > **Credentials and Basic Information**.
4. Record the app's `Client ID` and `Client Secret`.

### Note

   Keep the `Client Secret` secure and never share it with third parties. Store it in environment variables or a key management system instead of hardcoding it.

### Step 2: Add API Permissions

1. On the app details page, click **Manage permissions**.
2. Enter the following permission identifiers in the permission search box and apply for them:

   - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (write permission for workflow instances): Used to batch cancel pending tasks in the Process Center.
   - `Premium.Workflow.ReadWrite.All` (read/write permission for OA Approval workflows (exclusive to OA Premium Edition)): Used to get the user's pending, processed, submitted, and received lists from the DingTalk Approval Center.
   - `qyapi_aflow` (approval workflow data management permission): Used to update Process Center task status.

<Note>
Different business scenarios may require different permission combinations. Apply based on your actual needs.
</Note>

### Step 3: Get the Access Credential (Access Token)

Use the Client ID and Client Secret from Step 1 to get the app access credential. See [Get the access token of an internal app](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app).

**Best practices**:

- **Caching strategy**: The `accessToken` is valid for 2 hours. Cache it in memory or Redis, and refresh it proactively 5 minutes before it expires.
- **Concurrency control**: Prevent multiple threads from refreshing the token at the same time and causing conflicts. Use a distributed lock.
- **Error retry**: Retry automatically on network fluctuations, up to 3 times, with increasing intervals (1s → 2s → 4s).

## FAQ

- **Q: What is the maximum number of tasks supported by the batch accept or decline approval tasks API?**

  A single batch action supports up to 10 tasks. If there are more than 10 pending tasks, call the API in batches of no more than 10 tasks each to avoid timeouts or rate limits.
- **Q: How does the processType parameter distinguish official OA Approval from self-built OA Approval?**

  Each task object returned by the Approval Center list query APIs includes a `processType` field. A value of 0 indicates official OA Approval, and a value of 1 indicates self-built OA Approval. During batch processing, call the corresponding update API based on this field.
