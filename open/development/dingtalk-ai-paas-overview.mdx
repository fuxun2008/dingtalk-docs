With AI PaaS, you and your organization can develop your own AI apps quickly and at low cost, and build your own dedicated large model. AI PaaS lowers the barrier to developing AI apps, enabling more organizations to fully leverage AI technology.

## Model Scheduling Platform

The Model Scheduling Platform is a perception system. It senses input and context from the user side and schedules the corresponding skills based on demand. Skills are capabilities built on large models to complete specific tasks, such as the meeting summary skill and the translation skill.

## Model Training Platform

The Model Training Platform is a cognition system. It combines a large model with an organization's dedicated data to generate a dedicated model that better understands the organization and its industry. Acting like a brain, it analyzes user information from the Model Scheduling Platform and can also integrate with an organization's own systems.

## Plugin Development Platform

The Plugin Development Platform is an action system. Organizations can register services from their own systems as plugins so that the Scheduling Platform can call them to perform specific tasks. Acting like hands and feet, it executes the tasks that the brain has interpreted.

## Generate Intelligent Apps

Using the Model Scheduling Platform, Model Training Platform, and Plugin Development Platform, you can quickly develop a wide range of skills and ultimately generate Magic Wand apps and Tabler.
