This topic covers the background of the Approval Process Center, what it is, the user experience after integration, and the developer integration guide.

## Background of the Approval Process Center

Digital transformation among Chinese organizations has entered a new stage. Demand is growing for business digitization, system interconnection, and digital collaboration across the upstream and downstream industry chain. To better meet customer needs, DingTalk has positioned itself as a PaaS platform. This positioning requires DingTalk to become increasingly open.

OA Approval is one of the DingTalk capabilities best suited to a PaaS model. In line with this positioning, OA Approval has been rebuilt as a PaaS offering. Its form and process capabilities are now open, and a Process Center integration solution is available to meet the needs of partners across different business models.

The **Process Center** supports a range of built-in and extensible Approval process categories, including **Low-code process**, **Contract**, **Custom process**, **Onboarding/Transfer/Departure**, **Procurement**, **Asset**, and **Reimbursement**. It is open to integration with third-party process and form providers.

The Process Center integration delivers three unified capabilities: **Unified Chat experience** (developer apps can communicate and collaborate in real time), **Unified To-Do** (handle all work to-dos in one place), and **Unified data insight / efficiency diagnosis** (data asset accumulation).

## What Is the Approval Process Center

DingTalk OA Approval can receive approval tasks pushed from external systems, whether built in-house or purchased from third parties, into the DingTalk Approval app. Approval tasks reach users in real time on PC, mobile, and tablet, and open in the embedded form side panel of DingTalk Approval. Users can view and process all approvals in one place in DingTalk Approval for a one-stop approval experience. Combined with chat Message push, one-tap forwarding to chats, daily To-Do reminders, and an efficiency Kanban, the Process Center improves approval efficiency across submission, processing, and reporting.

### Use Cases

In mid-sized and large organizations, approvals are often scattered across different business systems. Approvers spend time every day switching between systems to process approvals, which is tedious and inefficient. Approvers also tend to forget pending approvals, which delays business processing and lowers management efficiency. Senior executives face more approval types and higher volumes, so the problem is often even worse for them.

### Admin View

- View and Search Process Center templates in the Admin Console

  In the OA Approval Admin Console, open **Form Management** > **System Hidden Forms** to see Approval forms integrated from other apps. For each form, the Admin Console supports **Search** of Approval forms and **View Data** of approval instances. Use the **View Data** page to export and manage this data.
- View and Search approval instance data of Process Center templates in the Admin Console
- Delete/Export Process Center Approval forms in the Admin Console

  In the OA Approval **Admin Console**, open the **Data Viewing** page to manage Process Center data. ### Process Center Integration Solution

DingTalk Process Center can be integrated through several entry points:

1. **DingTalk native Approval** (preset templates, Custom forms, built-in business processes, and suites) connects directly.
2. **Self-built systems** connect either through the **Process Engine** via **API**, directly via **API**, or through a service provider.
3. **Third-party apps** (such as Landray, Weaver, Kingdee) connect through the **Official Connector**.

The **Process Center** provides the following capabilities: **Approval Center**, **IM Dynamic Card**, **DingTalk To-Do**, **Search/Filter**, **Data Management (import/export)**, **Process Efficiency**, **Approval task deletion/cancellation**, and **Process Intervention**.

### Process Center Integration Sequence Diagram

The integration sequence involves four participants: **DingTalk User**, **Developer**, **DingTalk Open Platform**, and **DingTalk OA Approval**.

1. **Create/update approval template**: The Developer calls the Open Platform, which saves the template to OA Approval and returns an **approval template Code**.
2. **Manage approval templates**: In the OA Approval Admin Console, the Developer can view templates, view/search data, and export/delete data.
3. **Create approval instance**: The Developer creates an instance via the Open Platform (which returns an **approval instance ID**), and a CC Card is sent to the DingTalk User.
4. **Manage Approval forms**: In the DingTalk Process Center, the DingTalk User can view the four Approval Lists, search data, and execute approvals.
5. **Create an approval task that redirects to the original form** (DingTalk Approval redirects to the third-party form page for execution): The Open Platform returns an **approval task ID**, a task Card is sent, the task is executed on the original form, and the task Card is updated.
6. **Create an approval task executed inside DingTalk Approval** (integrated execution): After execution, DingTalk sends an execution message, the result is pushed via **RDS** or **HTTPSYNC**, and the task Card is updated.
7. **Update approval instance**: The Developer updates the instance through the Open Platform, gets the update result, and the CC Card is updated.

### How to Integrate

Internal apps and Third-party apps call the Process Center APIs under self-built OA Approval on the DingTalk Open Platform to sync approval data to the DingTalk Approval Process Center, giving the Organization a unified entry point for processing approvals. For the detailed integration solution, see [Self-built OA Approval: Third-Party Process and Page Integration](/open/development/use-three-party-process-and-page-docking).

1. Create an approval form.

   The **Internal app** interacts with **DingTalk Approval** through two operations: **Create or update template** and **Delete template**.
2. Sync data.

   The Integration flow runs between the **Internal app** and **DingTalk Approval**. The app drives DingTalk Approval through four interactions: **Create Instance**, **Create Task**, **Sync Task Status**, and **Sync Instance Status**.
