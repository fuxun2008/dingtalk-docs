## Introduction

The DingTalk Custom Workbench provides the following six major capabilities:

- **Unified Security**: Built and deployed on the DingTalk Workbench foundation to keep the workbench secure and controllable.
- **Unified Experience**: Follows the same strict component specifications as official DingTalk, and is based on the Mini Program framework to ensure a high-quality experience.
- **Unified Service**: Provides productized service capabilities to ensure service quality.
- **Tailored for Every Industry**: Leverages DingTalk's deep enterprise service capabilities to build professional, exclusive workbenches for industries such as education, government, film and television, Party building, e-commerce, and event management.
- **Tailored for Every Individual**: Based on DingTalk's organizational collaboration capabilities, users in different roles can be granted exclusive workbench page authorizations to enable business collaboration.
- **Ecosystem Online**: A rich ecosystem marketplace provides high-value workbench solutions and rapid component integration capabilities, truly bringing business online.

## Custom Workbench Editions

To meet customer needs, DingTalk offers two Custom Workbench editions: the Custom Workbench Professional Edition and the Custom Workbench Tailored Edition.

The two Custom Workbench editions differ in functionality as follows:

| Comparison Item | **Custom Workbench Professional Edition** | **Custom Workbench Tailored Edition** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trial Period | 7 days | 30 days |
| Organization Member Limit | Unlimited | Tiered limit |
| Self-Built Components | Not supported | Supported |
| Third-Party Data | Not supported | Supported |
