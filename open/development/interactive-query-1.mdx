This article introduces the best practices for interactive queries with DingTalk Bots.

## Basic usage — Automated Q&A through cards

In many business scenarios, you need to use a Q&A format to resolve user issues. Previously, users had to ask multiple questions to clarify the problem and identify a solution. Within DingTalk, after a user's question helps locate the issue, you can leverage the refresh capability of DingTalk Cards to let users query and confirm information through click interactions.

## Advanced usage — Conversational service powered by AI

By integrating the bot dialogue service from Alibaba DAMO Academy, developers can build a Direct Message conversational Q&A service similar to Tmall Genie, helping resolve vague questions raised by users.
