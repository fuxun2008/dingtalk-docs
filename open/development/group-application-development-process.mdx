## Group Extension Development Process

## Introduction to the Group Extension Development Process

Before you start, make sure you have completed the preparations in [Become a DingTalk Developer](/open/dingstart/become-a-dingtalk-developer). The process for developing a group app is as follows:

1. Develop an Internal App. Group extension development must be based on an internal app. If you do not have an internal app, create an app. If you already have one, use the existing app directly.
2. Configure Message Push. In **Message push**, configure and develop the bot binding.
3. Configure the Basic Information of the Group Extension. In the **Cool App Extension** section of the internal app, enable the group extension and configure the basic information. After it is created successfully, you can obtain the group extension app code.
4. Develop an Interactive Card (optional). Bind a bot to send interactive cards and more.
5. Enable the Group Extension App. After the group app is developed, **Add it to a group** and enable it in the Cool App Center.
6. Use the Group Extension App. In the group app bar of your organization, view and use the Cool App.
