## Group Extension Development Process

## Introduction to the Group Extension Development Process

Before you start, make sure you have completed the preparations in [Become a DingTalk Developer](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/app/become-a-dingtalk-developer#topic-2024337). The process for developing a group app is as follows:

1. [Develop an Internal App](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/org/create-orgapp#topic-2024342). Group extension development must be based on an internal app. If you do not have an internal app, [create an app](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/org/create-orgapp#topic-2024342). If you already have one, use the existing app directly.
2. [Configure Message Push](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/org/configure-push-settings#topic-2199272). In **Message push**, configure and develop the bot binding.
3. [Configure the Basic Information of the Group Extension](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/org/configure-the-basic-information-of-the-group-application#topic-2199273). In the **Cool App Extension** section of the internal app, enable the group extension and configure the basic information. After it is created successfully, you can obtain the group extension app code.
4. [Develop an Interactive Card](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/org/interactive-card#topic-2199274) (optional). Bind a bot to send interactive cards and more.
5. [Enable the Group Extension App](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/org/install-group-applications#topic-2199277). After the group app is developed, **Add it to a group** and enable it in the Cool App Center.
6. [Use the Group Extension App](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/org/install-group-applications#topic-2199277). In the group app bar of your organization, view and use the Cool App.
