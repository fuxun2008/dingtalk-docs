This topic describes the capabilities of the Attendance open APIs and explains how to integrate them.

## What Is Attendance

Attendance is an official DingTalk app that provides organizations with an employee attendance management solution. It shows your team's status anytime, anywhere, gives **an at-a-glance view of who is present**, and **calculates attendance data automatically** without manual checks. For more information, see [Quick Start for Attendance Administrators](/attendance/admin-quick-start).

Key features:

- **Multiple clock-in methods for various attendance scenarios**: Clock in by GPS location, Wi-Fi location, and more.
- **Unified management of in-office and field work for easier statistics**: Field clock-in tracks attendance for employees who are temporarily out of the office or on business trips.
- **Integrated leave and attendance approval for one-table attendance statistics**: Leave, overtime, outing, and business trip approvals are summarized and calculated automatically, so you no longer need to cross-check multiple reports.

## How to Enable Attendance

Attendance is an official app that DingTalk installs by default. Employees open and use it from Workbench in DingTalk.

- Mobile: DingTalk mobile client > Workbench

  On the DingTalk mobile client:
  1. Tap **Workbench** in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Open the **Attendance** app.
- Desktop: DingTalk desktop client > Workbench

  On the DingTalk client, open **Workbench**. Under the **All Staff** section, select the **Attendance** app to begin setup.

## Glossary

### Attendance Group

An attendance group is a set of attendance rules that the Organization Admin configures for employees. Create different types of attendance groups for the different job types in your organization.

<Note>
Due to differences between the legacy and new API specifications, an attendance group is uniquely identified by either group_id or groupKey. Call the [Convert groupKey to groupId](/open/development/convert-groupkey-to-groupid) API to convert a groupKey value to its corresponding group_id.
</Note>

There are three types of attendance groups:

- **Fixed shift attendance group**: For employees with fixed working and rest hours, such as 9:00 to 18:00 daily with fixed weekends off.
- **Shift-based attendance group**: For employees who work early and late shifts, whose shifts vary by day, or whose days off must be scheduled.
- **Flexible working hours attendance group**: For employees with flexible working hours.

Sign in to the [DingTalk Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io/) and go to **Workbench > App Management > Attendance > Attendance Group Management** to add an attendance group.

### Leave Rule (leave_code)

A leave rule is uniquely identified by leave_code.

In the DingTalk Admin Console, go to **Leave Management** > **Leave Rules** to view and configure leave rules. Click **Add Leave** to create a leave rule. Each leave rule has the following attributes:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Leave Name | Name of the leave type (e.g., Personal Leave, Time-off in Lieu, Sick Leave, Annual Leave, Maternity Leave, Paternity Leave, Marriage Leave) |
| Leave Unit | Minimum booking unit: by hour, by half-day, or by day |
| Duration Calculation Method | How leave duration is counted (e.g., by working day) |
| Paid | Whether the leave is paid or unpaid |
| Balance Rule | Balance limit (e.g., unlimited balance) |
| Scope | Who the rule applies to (e.g., the entire organization) |
| Actions | Edit or delete the rule |

### Shift

Bind a shift when you create an attendance group. A shift defines the clock-in times and working hours for employees in the attendance group. Customize attendance shifts to fit your organization's needs, including flexible clock-in, late arrival with late departure, early arrival with early departure, late arrival, and absenteeism settings.

Sign in to the [DingTalk Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io/) and go to **App Management > Attendance > Shift Management** to add a shift.

On the **Shift Management** page, click **Add Shift** to create a new shift. Existing shifts are listed with the following columns:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Shift Name** | Name of the shift (e.g., Default Shift) |
| **Attendance Time** | Working time range (e.g., 09:00–18:00) |
| **Actions** | **Edit** or **Delete** the shift |
