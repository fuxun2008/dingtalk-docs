## What Is a Group Extension

The DingTalk Open Platform, together with its comprehensive openness strategy, aims to make DingTalk a true foundation for organizations. It fully opens up core scenarios, strengthens DingTalk ecosystem integration, and jointly builds [Cool Apps](/zh/open/development/cool-application-introduction) to drive new traffic and deliver a brand-new user experience.

A [Cool App](/zh/open/development/cool-application-introduction) is a new form of app. It combines DingTalk app capabilities such as shortcut entries, Workbench components, chat Bots, interactive Cards, and OA Approval suites across multiple DingTalk feature scenarios, delivering a scenario-based, immersive experience to users. Cool Apps are designed to upgrade traditional digitalization and bridge the gap between OA and communication. They also improve the collaboration experience by solving problems such as fragmented communication scenarios with no accumulation, business scenarios lacking real-time communication, and tasks and people being located in two separate places.

**A group extension is a type of** [Cool App](/zh/open/development/cool-application-introduction). It is an extension form used within chats and, based on the main app, extends the capabilities of shortcut entries, chat Bots, and interactive Cards. This allows an app not only to deliver an immersive experience for multi-user, multi-role task collaboration, goal co-creation, business opportunity sharing, and battle reports in group chats, but also, from a technical standpoint, to integrate Cool Apps into direct message chats.

### Features of Group Extensions

- **Immersive experience:** Stay within the chat Context without interrupting your current work.
- **Scenario-based collaboration:** Information is collaborated on in real time and efficiently around scenarios and specific matters.
- **Instant data analysis:** Structured data generated from efficient collaboration is analyzed instantly to form effective insights.
- **Metric-driven operations:** Everyone participates and truly applies the tool, combined with organizational incentive mechanisms, so that digitalization is genuinely implemented.

### Advantages of Group Extensions

- **App perspective:**

  An app can distill its most core features and develop multiple group apps. Each group app can integrate a chat Bot, interactive Cards, pinned Cards, and a Chats menu, fully meeting the capability requirements of business scenarios.
- **Chat box perspective:**

  The chat box is a container. Multiple group apps can be installed in the group app bar of the chat container. These group apps can come from different main apps and can be combined in a Custom way based on group scenarios.

## How to Enable and Use a Group Extension App

Go to **Internal Group > Cool App Bar > More Cool Apps** to enter the Cool App Center, then find and enable an app. After you enable a Cool App, use it from the Cool App bar. For example, click **Smart Form** to add and edit content with the Smart Form app.

## Glossary

### Chat Container

DingTalk's chat container is open and Allows organizations to Customize it. This is a unique and primary collaborative office capability of DingTalk, providing users with collaboration services based on scenarios such as sales, goal co-creation, ticket assistance, and direct message communication.

The chat container includes the following areas: **chat title bar**, **pinned area**, **chat box, Chats menu**, **half-screen**, **full-screen**, **group app bar**, **toolbar**, **input box**, and **sidebar**.

### Chat Bot

One of an app's interaction capabilities within a group. Every group app is automatically associated with a chat Bot. Through the chat Bot, you can send information to specific targets in a specific quantity, at a specific time, and with specific content. This supports both group chats and direct message chats.

### Chats

The most basic form of IM, including Text, Files, Images, Videos, and rich media.

### Interactive Card

An app's interaction capability within a group. Interactive Chats are a new type of message that is dynamic, interactive, and unified across platforms. They display information in a richer way and encourage users to communicate and interact immediately.

### Pinned Card

A type of interactive Card, with relatively fixed use scenarios and methods.
