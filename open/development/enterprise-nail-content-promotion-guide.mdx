## **1. What Is the DingTalk Integration Level?**

The DingTalk integration level measures how deeply an app is integrated with DingTalk. Apps integrate deeply with DingTalk through DingTalk APIs, Connectors, Cool Apps, and more. Data shows that organizations using apps with a high DingTalk integration level see a 35% increase in app activity.

## **2. Why Does the DingTalk Integration Level Matter?**

- An app with a high DingTalk integration level offers richer features and greater value, making business digitalization more accessible to everyone.
- An app with a high DingTalk integration level lets live data flow securely, and flowing data gives the app more vitality.
- An app with a high DingTalk integration level takes on diverse, scenario-based forms and can be used seamlessly in a more immersive environment.
- An app with a high DingTalk integration level breaks down the barriers between enterprise apps and official apps, letting them work together for a better user experience.

## **3. Scoring Rules for an Enterprise App's DingTalk Integration Level**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Integration Level Scoring Dimension** | | **Dimension Definition** | **Scoring Rules** |
| **Capability Dimension** | **API** | Counts the number of daily active APIs at Level L3 or above | **4** points per daily active API |
| **Connector** | Counts the number of integrated and active Connectors (an action is a Connector's core component, similar to an API) | **5** points per action of a daily active Connector |
| **Product Dimension** | **Cool App** | Checks whether the main app has an extended Cool App, and its integration and activity across Cool App scenarios (Top Banner, Bots, message menus, and more) | **8** points per active scenario each day |

## **5. How to Improve Your DingTalk Integration Level**

### **Integrate More** **L3+ APIs** **to Let DingTalk Empower People, Finance, Assets, Operations, Production, Supply, and Sales, and Raise Your Integration Level**

- **BEFORE**

  - The app on DingTalk is just a **quick link pinned to the Workbench**, treating DingTalk merely as an entry point—no different from opening it in a browser.
  - The app **only integrates DingTalk's account silent login** and the interfaces for retrieving Contacts and organizational structure, treating DingTalk merely as a permission center. This is only the most basic capability DingTalk provides.
  - The app **only integrates DingTalk's message notification interface**, without considering the diversity of messages or how they reach users. This, too, uses only one of DingTalk's most basic capabilities.
- **AFTER**

  - Through **IM API integration, Bots, and interactive Cards**, the app achieves multidimensional Group Management, turning communication into business and business into collaboration.
  - Through **Event API** integration, the app deeply integrates your organization's internal event management processes with DingTalk.
  - Through **Attendance, Approval, To-Do, and other API** integrations, the app makes it easier to manage your organization's people, finance, assets, and operations on DingTalk.
  - Integrate DingTalk's **data assets** to drive with data and bring your DingTalk app to life.

### **Integrate** [**DingTalk Connectors**](/open/yida/guide/concept/connector) **to Eliminate Data Silos Across Heterogeneous Systems and Raise Your Integration Level**

- **BEFORE**

  - After the app goes on DingTalk, its **data is stored and accessed independently**—creating a silo.
  - To share data between enterprise apps, there is no better path than joint development between them. Without integration, silos re-emerge, and the same data must inevitably be entered repeatedly.
  - Submitting an ERP approval form in an internal system requires separately notifying the relevant approver to confirm it in the ERP system, which is cumbersome.
- **AFTER**

  - DingTalk Connectors integrate official DingTalk apps, third-party SaaS apps, and large external systems such as Kingdee and Yonyou. **Integrate once, and connect your data.**
  - **Integrate a Connector** into your internal system. When an expense reimbursement form is submitted, the Connector automatically creates an approval To-Do in DingTalk. After the approver confirms it, the form credentials flow back to the finance system automatically through the Connector. The Connector makes the reimbursement process simple and efficient.

### **Integrate** **DingTalk Cool Apps** **to Let High-Frequency Scenarios Drive Low-Frequency Apps and Raise Your Integration Level**

- **BEFORE**

  - After the app goes on DingTalk, every time you want to **use it** you must find the app icon in the enterprise Workbench, click into it, and then locate the feature you need—**a long path**.
  - When sending and receiving resumes in a recruiting group, the interviewer decides a resume looks good and tells HR. HR then has to return to the recruiting system, upload the resume, create the candidate, confirm the time with the interviewer, and schedule the interview. HR **keeps doing tasks that seem routine but could actually be made far more efficient**.
- **AFTER**

  - Integrate a DingTalk Cool App. When the app is published to the Workbench, you can also **distribute it with one click to high-frequency entry points such as Chat Quick actions, message menus, and the Profile page**.
  - When sending and receiving resumes in a recruiting group, the **resume is automatically converted into a candidate Card**. The interviewer decides a resume looks good and **taps [Looks Good]**. A **half-screen floating window opens**, where the interviewer selects an available time slot and confirms. The **Card updates in real time**, HR follows up on the interviewer's interactions, and HR contacts the candidate directly to schedule the interview—fast and efficient.
