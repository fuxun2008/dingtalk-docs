Solution providers can independently expand their customer base and use the **targeted app distribution** feature to install third-party enterprise apps in bulk, based on clearly defined target organizations and a fixed number of organizations.

**Note**

If you have a need for targeted, quantified app distribution, first join the "DingTalk ISV Communication Group" organization ([ISV Communication Group application link](https://wx.dingtalk.com/invite-page/index.html?bizSource=____source____&corpId=dinga22b5311e4211323&inviterUid=B5B939A3299331EF99372A3FA1525DE6&encodeDeptId=0054DC2B53AFE745)).

## Procedure

1. Sign in to the [Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.com/?).
2. Click **App Development**, and then select **Third-party enterprise app**.
3. Click the target app to open its details page, and then click **Targeted App Distribution**.
4. Click **Create Distribution Request**.

5. On the **Create App Distribution Request** page, enter the following information.

   - **Project Name**: Enter a name for the request.
   - **DingTalk Support Contact**: Enter the mobile number of your DingTalk support contact.
   - **App Installation Method**:

     - Authorized installation: The app is installed with the authorization of the customer's Organization Admin, who must accept the authorization.
     - Silent installation: You must submit a **written authorization letter for app installation** between the distributing primary organization and the receiving organizations, stamped with a legally valid seal.

       **Note**

       The uploaded authorization letter must not exceed 10 MB and supports formats such as PDF, JPG, and PNG.
   - **Import List**: Upload the list following the format of the template file.

     The list includes the following content:

     - Customer organization ID: For example, ding0ed2fxx.
     - Customer organization name: For example, Open Platform App Review Team.
     - Customer Organization Admin's mobile number: For example, 138xxxx3120.
6. After you complete the information, click **Submit** to submit it for review.

7. After the request is approved, select the project with the status **Approved**, and then click Details to open the distribution page.

8. On the distribution page, you can deploy the distribution using the following methods.

   - Click **App Distribution** to deploy.

     1. Click **Start Distribution** to deploy to all organizations under **Distribution Information** at once. After the deployment succeeds, different organizations display different deployment statuses.
