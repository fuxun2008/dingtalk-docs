Tabler builds on the intelligent capabilities of the Magic Wand app and adds human-like abilities such as joining Contacts, one-on-one and group chats, writing documents, and participating in audio and video meetings. It integrates deeply into more DingTalk scenarios to serve users in your organization more efficiently.

## **Background Information**

Once you create a Magic Wand app, it automatically appears in the Tabler configuration options. A Tabler supports only one Magic Wand app.

## **Prerequisites**

- You have added the Tabler app capability. For details, see [Add AI App Capabilities](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/add-ai-application-capabilities#).
- You have configured a Magic Wand app. For more information, see [Configure the Magic Wand App](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/configure-the-magic-wand-application#).

## **Procedure**

1. Review and confirm that the selected skills in the Magic Wand app meet your expectations.
2. Configure the basic information, and then click **Save**.

   |  |  |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Configuration Item** | **Description** |
   | Tabler avatar | Upload a Tabler avatar. The icon must be in JPG or PNG format, at least 240px \* 240px, 1:1 aspect ratio, no larger than 2 MB, and without rounded corners. For detailed specifications, see [Tabler Avatar Design Specifications](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/design). |
   | Tabler name | Enter a Tabler name of up to 20 characters. The name is displayed in Contacts and can be modified by the Contacts admin. |
   | Tabler description | Provide a further description of the Tabler, up to 50 characters. The description is displayed in the Tabler profile and can be modified by the Contacts admin. |

## **Next Steps**

After you save successfully, you must publish the Tabler before it can be used. For more information, see [Publish an Enterprise Internal AI App](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/publish-enterprise-internal-ai-applications#) or [Publish a Third-Party Enterprise AI App](https://open.dingtalk.com/document/ai-dev/monitoring-applications#).
