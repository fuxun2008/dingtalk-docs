## 1. Definition of DingTalk Integration Level

The DingTalk Integration Level measures how deeply an app is integrated with DingTalk. An app achieves deep integration with DingTalk by connecting to DingTalk API, Connectors, Cool Apps, and more.

- **API**

  The DingTalk Open Platform provides APIs covering different standards and development scenarios, such as OA Approval, Message notifications, attendance, New Education, Exclusive DingTalk, and customer management. You can also find developer guides, development samples, and tool resources to quickly integrate your app with DingTalk. For details, see API Overview.
- **Connector**

  Using zero-code methods such as visual drag-and-drop configuration and one-click subscription, you can simply and efficiently achieve data interconnection and integration among DingTalk, internal systems within your organization, well-known vendor systems, and DingTalk third-party apps. For details, visit the [Connector website](/open/yida/guide/concept/connector).
- **Cool App**

  A Cool App is a new type of scenario-based app that merges app features with multiple product scenarios in DingTalk. It delivers an immersive experience, creates new interaction methods, and enhances data empowerment. It shifts from users finding apps to apps finding users, building a new scenario-based collaboration experience. For details, visit the Cool App website.
