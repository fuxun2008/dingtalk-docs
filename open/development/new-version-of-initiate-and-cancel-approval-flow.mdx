# OA Approval Workflow Integration: Initiate and Revoke Approval Instances

## Overview

### Background

In-house and third-party business systems often have their own approval processes and pages. Employees must switch between multiple systems to handle approvals, which fragments their work and lowers efficiency. Connect your approval processes to the DingTalk OA Approval Center so users can view and handle all approvals from a single entry point in DingTalk.

- **Frequent system switching:** Employees switch back and forth between business systems and DingTalk. The workflow is long, and urgent approvals are easy to miss.
- **Out-of-sync approval status:** Data in business systems and DingTalk does not match, so approval progress is hard to track and manage in real time.
- **Scattered approval processes:** Managers cannot view all approval progress in DingTalk. They must sign in to each system separately.
- **Delayed notifications:** Approval notifications rely on email or offline communication and cannot use DingTalk's unified To-Do reminders and Message push.

### Key Benefits

This solution reuses the process engine and form component capabilities of the DingTalk official OA Approval. It integrates business system approval processes seamlessly into DingTalk for unified management and efficient collaboration.

- **Unified approval entry:** Users view and handle all approvals from their own systems in the DingTalk approval list, reducing system switching.
- **Low integration cost:** Integrate with only a few API calls. No need to rebuild existing business processes or page logic.
- **Real-time status sync:** Approval results are written back to the business system in real time, keeping data consistent and traceable on both sides.
- **Instant notification delivery:** DingTalk To-Do reminders and Message notifications push approval tasks to approvers immediately.

### Use Cases

This solution suits the following typical approval integration scenarios:

- In-house CRM, ERP, OA, or other business systems that need to connect with the DingTalk Approval Center.
- Store operations approvals for retail chains, such as renovation requests and promotion approvals.
- Production work order approvals in manufacturing, such as quality inspection reports and material requests.
- HR approvals, such as leave requests, expense reimbursements, and onboarding and offboarding.

## Typical Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: CRM Customer Follow-up Approval

#### Pain Points

A technology company uses an in-house CRM system to manage customer follow-ups. After a sales rep enters a prospect's information, a supervisor must approve it before the rep can claim pooled leads or start business negotiations. The original process had these problems:

- **Slow response to opportunities:** After a lead is entered, the rep waits for the supervisor to sign off offline. The average delay is 2–3 days, so competitors often reach high-intent customers first.
- **Incomplete customer information:** Approvers see only the customer name and contact details. They cannot view communication history, requirements, or competitor analysis in DingTalk, which makes accurate decisions difficult.
- **Unclear approval responsibility:** Joint approvals lack a defined order and deadline. This leads to buck-passing or duplicate approvals and longer decision cycles.
- **Severe data silos:** Customer status in the CRM is not synced with approval progress in DingTalk. Sales reps cannot see approval results in real time and must repeatedly call or email their supervisors.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated Workflow**

  End-to-end flow:

  1. **CRM system**: A sales rep enters customer information, which triggers the next step in real time.
  2. **API integration**: A DingTalk **approval instance** is created automatically.
  3. **DingTalk Approval Center**: The approver reviews the customer profile and history on desktop or mobile, then taps **Reject** or **Approve**.
  4. **Data write-back**: The approval result is synced back to the CRM system in real time.
- **Key Advantages**

  - **One-click customer approval:** After a customer is entered in the CRM system, a DingTalk approval instance is created automatically. Approvers view customer details and act directly in DingTalk without switching systems.
  - **Real-time approval visibility:** DingTalk displays complete customer data, including contact details, requirements, and follow-up history. Approval progress is clear at a glance, with support for comments and reminders.
  - **End-to-end data sync:** Approval results are written back to the CRM system in real time. When an approval is rejected, downstream To-Dos are canceled automatically, keeping data consistent and traceable on both sides.

### Scenario 2: Manufacturing Production Work Order Approval

#### Pain Points

A manufacturer uses an in-house MES system to manage production work orders that involve multiple process approvals, such as first-article inspection, process handover, and finished goods warehousing. The original process relied on paper forms and verbal confirmation from team leaders, which caused these problems:

- **Long approval chains:** Work orders require three levels of approval: team leader → quality inspector → workshop director. Paper forms move slowly, making it hard to respond quickly to rush orders.
- **Scattered inspection data:** Inspection reports are stored as PDFs or photos in local folders. Approvers cannot view complete inspection records or compare historical work orders in DingTalk.
- **Poor production line coordination:** Upstream and downstream processes lack real-time status sync. A downstream process may start before the upstream process is approved, causing rework and waste.
- **High traceability costs:** When quality issues occur, staff must manually search paper forms to find responsible parties and approval nodes. This is time-consuming and prone to missing key information.

#### Value Validation

- **Automated Workflow**

  End-to-end integration flow:

  1. The MES system creates a production work order.
  2. The DingTalk OA Approval API is called to create an approval instance.
  3. ERP checks material inventory.
  4. The workshop supervisor approves in DingTalk.
  5. The approval result is synced back to all three systems (MES, ERP, QMS).

  The approval result updates the work order status in MES, reserved inventory in ERP, and the linked quality record in QMS.
- **Key Advantages**

  - **Integrated work order approval:** When the MES system initiates a work order approval, a DingTalk approval instance is created automatically. Approvers view work order details and act directly in DingTalk without switching systems.
  - **Real-time approval visibility:** DingTalk displays complete work order data, including bills of materials, inspection requirements, and historical work orders. Approval progress is clear at a glance, with support for comments and reminders.
  - **End-to-end data sync:** Approval results are written back to the MES system in real time. When an approval is rejected, downstream To-Dos are canceled automatically, keeping data consistent and traceable on both sides. Approval turnaround improves by more than 60%.

## Implementation Guide

### Technical Architecture

The technical architecture of this solution includes these core components:

- **Authentication layer**: Manages App credentials and obtains access tokens to verify the identity of every API call.
- **Template management layer**: Creates and updates approval form templates and defines the structure and fields of approval processes.
- **Instance execution layer**: Initiates, queries, transfers, and revokes approval instances to drive approval process execution.
- **Event callback layer**: Receives status change events for approval tasks and instances, enabling two-way sync between business systems and the DingTalk platform.

### Prerequisites

- **App preparation**: Complete [App Creation and Configuration](/open/dingstart/create-application).
- **Permissions**: Hold DingTalk Organization Admin or Sub Admin permissions, and apply for the following API permissions:

  - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission)
  - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (workflow instance read permission)
- **Development environment**: Install a Java development environment (JDK 1.6 or later) and the Maven build tool.
- **SDK**: Download the DingTalk Server-side SDK, available for Java, Python, Go, and other languages.

### Code Implementation

Step 1: Go to the App details page and get the client ID and Client Secret.

Step 2: Apply for API permissions. Apply for the relevant "OA Approval" permissions.

Step 3: Get the App access credential. See [Get the Access Token of an Internal App](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app). When calling APIs, use the access token to authenticate the caller.

Step 4: Call the OA Approval APIs:

1. Call the new Server API [Create or Update an Approval Form Template](/open/development/create-an-approval-form-template) to get the unique template code `processCode`.
2. Use the template code `processCode` to call the new Server API [Create an Approval Instance](/open/development/create-an-approval-instance) and get the approval instance `instanceId`.
3. Use the approval instance `instanceId` to call the new Server API [Get the Details of a Single Approval Instance](/open/development/obtains-the-details-of-a-single-approval-instance-pop) and get the approval instance details.
4. Use the fields from the approval instance details to call the new Server API [Transfer an OA Approval Task](/open/development/transfer-the-oa-approval-task) and transfer the approval instance.
5. After reviewing the approval, use the approval instance `instanceId` to call the new Server API [Revoke an Approval Instance](/open/development/revoke-an-approval-instance) and revoke the Approval form.

## Implementation Steps

### **Step 1: Get the App Credentials**

1. Sign in to the [DingTalk Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.io/).
2. Select the target App to open the App details page.
3. Click **Basic Information** > **Credentials and Basic Information**.
4. Record the App's `Client ID` and `Client Secret`.

### Note

   Keep the `Client Secret` secure and never share it with third parties. Store it in environment variables or a key management system instead of hardcoding it.

### Step 2: Add API Permissions

1. On the App details page, click **Manage permissions**.
2. In the permission search box, enter the following permission identifiers and apply for them:

   - `Workflow.Form.Write` (workflow template write permission): Creates and updates approval form templates.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Write` (workflow instance write permission): Initiates and revokes approval instances.
   - `Workflow.Instance.Read` (workflow instance read permission): Queries approval instance details.

<Note>
Different business scenarios may require different permission combinations. Apply based on your actual needs. For example, batch import scenarios also require permissions for batch User operations.
</Note>

### Step 3: Get the Access Credential (Access Token)

Use the Client ID and Client Secret from Step 1 to get the App access credential. See [Get the Access Token of an Internal App](/open/development/obtain-the-access-token-of-an-internal-app).

**Best practices**:

- **Caching**: The `accessToken` is valid for 2 hours. Cache it in memory or Redis and refresh it proactively 5 minutes before it expires.
- **Concurrency control**: Prevent multiple threads from refreshing the token at the same time and causing conflicts. Use a distributed lock.
- **Error retry**: Retry automatically on network fluctuations, up to 3 times, with increasing intervals (1s → 2s → 4s).

## FAQ

- **Q1: What are the requirements for componentId in formComponents when creating an approval template?**

  The componentId must be unique within the form and carries no business meaning. It only identifies the control. To use a business identifier, use the bizAlias Field instead.
- **Q2: What values does actionType in approvers support when creating an approval instance?**

  actionType supports values such as NONE (single approver), OR (either-or approval: the request passes when any approver accepts), and AND (joint approval: all approvers must accept). See the API documentation for the full list.
- **Q3: How do I retrieve a processCode that I did not save?**

  If you did not save the processCode returned when creating the approval template, sign in to the DingTalk OA Approval Admin Console to find it, or call the API that gets the template code.
- **Q4: What are the requirements for operateUserId when transferring an approval task?**

  operateUserId must be the userId of a User on the current approval node. Otherwise, the transfer fails.
- **Q5: How is the approval status synced after an approval instance is revoked?**

  After a successful revocation, the approval instance status changes to "Terminated". Subscribe to the approval instance change callback event to detect status changes in real time.
