---
title: "Add a bot to a group"
---

To add a bot to a group, follow the steps in this document.

## Prerequisites

1. Complete the [Configure an enterprise bot](/open/dingstart/configure-the-robot-application) process.
2. Complete the app publishing process.

## Procedure

1. Sign in to the DingTalk client-side and create a group chat:

   - If you already have a group chat, skip this step.
   - If you do not have a group chat, do the following:

     1. Click the "➕" icon in the upper-right corner and select New Chat.

     2. Click **Select Contact** and configure the group information.

        | **Item** | **Description** |
        | --- | --- |
        | Group name | Enter the group name. |
        | Group scenario | Select Internal Group. **Note**: The group scenario must be Internal Group. |
        | Group organization | Must be consistent with the organization used in [Configure an enterprise bot](/open/dingstart/configure-the-robot-application). |
     3. Click Confirm to complete the group chat creation.
2. Enter the group chat and click the **Group settings** icon in the upper-right corner.

3. Click **Group Management** > **Bots** > **Add Bot**.
4. In the Enterprise bot list, select the bot that you configured and published in the prerequisites.

5. After selecting the bot to add, click Add > Complete.
