Before you begin development, the organization admin must grant developer permissions to developers. Developers with different identities can develop different types of apps.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Developer** | **App Type Developed** | **Associated Organization** |
| Internal developer | Internal app built by the organization | Develop internal apps built by the organization as an internal employee. |
| Developer from a solution provider | Third-party enterprise app  Develop internal apps on delegation | To develop third-party enterprise apps or custom apps for organizations as a developer from a solution provider, your organization must complete solution provider authentication. For details, see the [Full Cooperation Process Guide](/zh/open/development/isv-cooperation-guide). |

## Procedure

The organization admin grants developer permissions to developers by following these steps:

**Note**

After applying for developer permissions, developers can sign in to develop, upload, and publish apps.

1. The organization admin signs in to the [OA Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.com/index.htm#/safety/subAccount).

2. In the Admin Console, choose **Security & Permissions** > **Manage Permissions**, and then click **Management Groups** to open the **Management Groups** page.

3. Click **Add Management Group**.

4. On the **Edit Management Group** page, fill in the following fields, and then click **Save**.

- **Management group name:** Enter the name of the management group.
- **Users:** Assign users to the management group.
- **Management scope:** Select the management scope.
- **Assign permissions:** Select the corresponding permissions.

5. Developers sign in to the [Developer Console](https://open-dev.dingtalk.com/#/index) to develop apps.
