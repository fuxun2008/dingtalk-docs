Group owners and admins can open **Group Management** to manage advanced group controls.

## Open Group Management

1.  Open the group chat.
    
2.  Click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.
    
3.  Select **Group Management**.

## Advanced settings you can find

Group Management includes settings for admins, mute controls, member permissions, join permissions, group tools, and group-related features.

| Setting | What it is used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** / **Add** | Add group admins. |
| **Mute Settings** | Open mute-related controls. |
| **Group permission settings** | Control who can use selected group features. |
| **Group Quick Bar** | Manage the group quick bar. |
| **Join group permission** | Manage how new members can join. |
| **How long can new members view their chat history** | Manage how much previous history new members can view. |
| **Group Calendar** | Open group calendar settings. |
| **Group can receive mails** | Manage whether the group can receive mail. |
| **Turn on automatic synchronization to Ding Drive** | Manage automatic synchronization to Ding Drive. |
| **Turn off Private Chat** | Manage private chat access from the group. |
| **Transfer Group Ownership** | Transfer ownership to another member. |

## Group permission settings

Open **Group permission settings** when you need to control who can use specific group features.

Available permission entries include:

*   **Who can @ Everyone**
    
*   **Who can send DING in the group**
    
*   **Who can put the group message on top**
    
*   **Who can pin the group news**
    
*   **Who can upload files, folders, and Ding Drive files?**
    
*   **Who can initiate video and voice meetings in the group**
