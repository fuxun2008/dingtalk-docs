---
title: "Start a Video Conference from Messages"
description: "Start a video conference from the message composer in a direct message, or from the group chat toolbar to bring multiple group members into the meeting."
---

When a chat discussion needs a live conversation, you can start a video conference directly from **Messages**.

## Start from the message composer

1.  Open a conversation.
    
2.  Click **+** in the message composer.
    
3.  Select **Video Conference**.
    

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/ZWGl05mrGkp0Mn34/img/92efe9d3-32b9-4e32-a900-9cb413714eee.png)

DingTalk opens the video conference flow from the current conversation.

## Start from a group chat toolbar

In a group chat, you can also use the video meeting icon in the upper-right corner of the conversation.

Use this entry when the meeting is related to the group discussion and you want to continue with the same members or topic.
