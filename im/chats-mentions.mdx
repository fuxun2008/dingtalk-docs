title: "Manage @Everyone Permissions"
description: "Manage @Everyone permissions in a group: change who can use @Everyone, understand what changes for members, and adjust related notification settings."
---

In busy groups, @Everyone can notify many people at once. Group owners and admins can control who is allowed to use it.

## Change who can use @Everyone

1.  Open the group chat.
    
2.  Click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.
    
3.  Select **Group Management**.
    
4.  Open **Group permission settings**.
    
5.  Select **Who can @ Everyone**.
    

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/J9LnW6jvkDrBalvD/img/d4319d1e-2af8-4006-bccc-31634a0c520e.png)

Use this setting to reduce unnecessary broad notifications in large or active groups.

## What changes for members

After this permission is updated, the selected permission rule controls who can notify everyone in the group with @Everyone.

## Related settings

Other group notification and message controls are also available under **Group Management** > **Group permission settings**, such as who can put group messages on top or pin group news.
