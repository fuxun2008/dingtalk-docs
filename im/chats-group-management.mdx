---
title: "Manage Group Members and Permissions"
description: "View and add group members, appoint group admins, configure group permissions, manage how people join the group, clear messages, and quit a group when no longer needed."
---

Group owners and admins can manage members, admins, invitations, and permissions from **Group Settings**.

## View group members

1.  Open the group chat.
    
2.  Click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.
    
3.  In **Members**, view the member count or open **View all group members**.
    

Use the member list when you need to check who is currently in the group.

## Add members

1.  Open the group chat.
    
2.  Click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.
    
3.  Click **+** next to the member list.
    
4.  Select people from the contact selector.
    
5.  Click **Confirm**.
    

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01usANpBQmhaI4N9W8_!!6000000001280-2-tps-2154-1322.png)

New members are added to the group after you confirm.

## Add group admins

Use admins when more than one person needs to help manage a group.

Open the group chat > gear icon > **Group Management** > **Admin** > **Add**

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01H7EI08SqYjH6Rg2r_!!6000000000436-2-tps-3175-1322.png)

## Manage group permissions

Open the group chat > gear icon > **Group Management** > **Group permission settings**.

From this page, you can manage who can use @Everyone, send DING in the group, put group messages on top, pin group news, upload files, and start video or voice meetings.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01MZTCX1DkXjE4N9W8_!!6000000001361-2-tps-2154-1322.png)

## Manage how people join

Open the group chat > gear icon > **Group Management** > **Join group permission**.

Use this setting to manage how new members can join the group.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01Y4cNWaxtJIL4N9W8_!!6000000001599-2-tps-2154-1322.png)

## Clear messages

Open the group chat > gear icon > **Clear Messages**.

This clears messages from your local view of the conversation.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01Qw4Dz6qyqUB4N9W8_!!6000000000440-2-tps-2154-1322.png)

## Quit a group

Open the group chat > gear icon > scroll down > **Quit**.
