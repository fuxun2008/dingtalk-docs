title: "Manage Message Notifications"
description: "Manage message notifications by muting or unmuting conversations, marking as unread, opening notification settings, and following tips to balance focus with availability."
---

You can reduce interruptions by muting individual conversations or changing desktop notification settings.

Notification settings affect how DingTalk alerts you while you are working on desktop.

## Mute a conversation

Use either method:

*   Open the conversation, click the gear icon, then turn on **Mute Notifications**.
    
*   Right-click the conversation in the conversation list, then select **Message Do Not Disturb**.
    

Muted conversations still remain in your message list.

You can still open a muted conversation and read new messages at any time.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01KQjXb6HOuCF4N9sa_!!6000000001573-2-tps-2154-2714.png)

## Unmute a conversation

Open the conversation, click the gear icon, then turn off **Mute Notifications**.

## Mark a conversation as unread

Right-click the conversation in the conversation list, then select **Mark as unread**.

Use this when you have opened a conversation but want to come back to it later.

## Open notification settings

Open **Settings** > **Notifications**.

From this page, you can review options such as **Message Notifications**, **Message Tone**, **Ringtone Settings**, **Message Desktop Notifications**, **Show Number of Unread Messages on Icon**, **Feature Notifications**, **Schedule Desktop Notifications**, **Mailbox Desktop Notifications**, and **Mailbox Navigation Notifications**.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01tzvlTg1WLCF4N9W8_!!6000000007410-2-tps-2154-1322.png)

## Tips

*   Mute only the conversations that do not need immediate attention.
    
*   Use **Mark as unread** when you need a visual reminder to return to a conversation.
    
*   Review **Message Desktop Notifications** if you want to adjust how message alerts appear on your desktop.
