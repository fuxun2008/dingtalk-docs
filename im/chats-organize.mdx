---
title: "Manage the Conversation List"
description: "Organize the conversation list by pinning chats at the top, marking conversations as unread, muting notifications, hiding conversations, clearing local chat history, and choosing the right action."
---

Use the conversation list menu to control how conversations appear in **Chats**. These actions help you keep important chats easy to find and reduce distractions during daily work.

## Keep a conversation at the top

Right-click a conversation, then select **Set-top session**.

The conversation stays near the top of your list, making it easier to return to active work.

## Mark a conversation as unread

Right-click a conversation, then select **Mark as unread**.

Use this when you have opened a conversation but want to remind yourself to return to it later.

## Mute a conversation

Right-click a conversation, then select **Message Do Not Disturb**.

Muted conversations remain in your list but stop sending regular notification alerts.

## Hide a conversation

Right-click a conversation, then select **Do not display this session**.

Use this to remove a conversation from the list without clearing its history.

## Clear local chat history

Right-click a conversation, then select **Empty chat history**.

This clears the local chat history for that conversation.

## Choose the right action

| Goal | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Keep a high-priority conversation visible | **Set-top session** |
| Come back to a conversation later | **Mark as unread** |
| Stop notification interruptions | **Message Do Not Disturb** |
| Remove a conversation from the list | **Do not display this session** |
| Clear local message history | **Empty chat history** |
