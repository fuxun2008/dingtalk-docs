title: "Create and Manage a Group Chat"
description: "Create a group chat with multiple contacts, add members directly or via Group Settings, open the Group Settings panel, manage group permissions, and leave a group."
---

Create a group chat when multiple people need to discuss the same topic, project, or team update.

## Create a group chat

1.  Click **+** in the upper-right corner of DingTalk desktop app.
    
2.  Select **New Chat**.
    
3.  In **Select Contact**, search for people or choose from **Friends**, **Organization**, **Groups**, or **Import**.
    
4.  Select the people you want to add.
    
5.  Click **Confirm**.
    

After the group is created, it appears in your Messages list.

## Add members to a group

1.  Open the group chat.
    
2.  Click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.
    
3.  Click **+** next to the member list.
    
4.  Select the people you want to add.
    
5.  Click **Confirm**.
    

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/r4mlQ5bJkLb4klxo/img/483d8137-7354-492d-8523-bb26150243bf.png)

## Invite people from Group Settings

Open the group chat, click the gear icon, then select **Group Invitation**.

Use this entry when you want to invite more people from the group settings page.

## Open Group Settings

Open the group chat and click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.

From Group Settings, you can manage group information, members, invitations, notification preferences, and group-level controls.

| Area | What you can manage |
| --- | --- |
| Members | View members or add people to the group. |
| Group Chat Information | Update **Name**, open **Notice**, or use **Group Invitation**. |
| Personal settings | Set **My Nickname in Group**, **Group Alias**, **Stick to Top**, or **Mute Notifications**. |
| Group Management | Manage admins, permissions, quick bar settings, join permissions, messages, and group exit. |

## Manage group permissions

Open the group chat > gear icon > **Group Management** > **Group permission settings**.

Use this page to control permissions such as who can use @Everyone, send DING in the group, put group messages on top, pin group news, upload files, and start meetings.

## Leave a group

Open the group chat > gear icon > scroll down > **Quit**.

After you quit, the group is removed from your active conversations.
