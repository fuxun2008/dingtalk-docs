## Messages

### How do I start a direct message?

Click the search bar at the top of DingTalk desktop app, search for the person, and select the result to open the conversation.

### How do I create a group chat?

Click **+** in the upper-right corner, select **New Chat**, choose people in **Select Contact**, then click **Confirm**.

### How do I format a message?

Click **Aa** in the message composer to open the rich text toolbar.

### How do I send a message without interrupting others?

Type your message, click the dropdown arrow next to **Send**, then select **Set silent**.

### How do I start a video conference from Messages?

Open a conversation, click **+** in the message composer, then select **Video Conference**.

## Group Settings

### Where is Group Invitation?

Open a group chat, click the gear icon, then select **Group Invitation**.

### Where can I control @Everyone permissions?

Open the group chat > gear icon > **Group Management** > **Group permission settings** > **Who can @ Everyone**.

### Where can I find advanced group settings?

Open the group chat > gear icon > **Group Management**.

### How do I leave a group?

Open the group chat > gear icon > scroll down > **Quit**.

## Notifications

### How do I mute a conversation?

Open the conversation > gear icon > **Mute Notifications**, or right-click the conversation > **Message Do Not Disturb**.

### How do I mark a conversation as unread?

Right-click the conversation > **Mark as unread**.

### Where are notification settings?

Open **Settings** > **Notifications**.

### How do I mute a service conversation?

Open the service conversation, click the gear icon, then turn on **Mute Notifications**.

## Search

### How do I search within a conversation?

Open the conversation, click the search icon in the upper-right corner, keep **Chat Records** selected, enter a keyword, and use **Filter** if needed.

### How do I browse files in a conversation?

Open the conversation and click the file/folder icon in the upper-right corner.
