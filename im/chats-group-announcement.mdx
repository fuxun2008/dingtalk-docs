Group notices are shared updates that group members can view from **Group Settings**. Use this page when you need to check the latest notice for a group.

Notices are useful for information that should stay easy to find, such as team rules, project reminders, or important group updates.

## Open group notices

1.  Open the group chat from **Messages**.
    
2.  Click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.
    
3.  Select **Notice**.
    

The Notice page opens from Group Settings.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/3BMqYyb9WzdmEqwZ/img/b4efef19-f94a-43e1-ab73-a3af26f4a3a5.png)

## When to use Notice

Use Notice for information that members may need to refer back to later. For quick discussion or one-time updates, send a normal message in the group chat instead.

## Related settings

Group notices are part of **Group Settings**. From the same settings panel, you can also open **Group Invitation**, manage **Members**, or go to **Group Management**.
