Some conversations in **Messages** are created by DingTalk services, apps, or product features. These conversations can send updates or cards related to the service.

You can manage a service conversation from its settings panel.

## Open service conversation settings

1.  Open **Messages**.
    
2.  Select the service conversation.
    
3.  Click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.
    

The service conversation settings panel opens.

## Mute a service conversation

In the settings panel, turn on **Mute Notifications**.

When this is enabled, DingTalk no longer sends push messages for this service conversation.

## Keep a service conversation at the top

In the settings panel, turn on **Stick on Top**.

Use this when the service conversation contains updates you need to check frequently.
