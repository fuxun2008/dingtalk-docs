Use **Group Settings** to manage group information, your personal preferences, and group-level controls.

To open Group Settings, open a group chat and click the gear icon in the upper-right corner.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01kma392VuadL4N9W8_!!6000000001089-2-tps-2154-1322.png)

## Group information

Use the group information section to manage basic group details.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | View or edit the group name. |
| **Notice** | Open group notices. |
| **Group Invitation** | Open group invitation options. |

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01J8XsR3h8ZaF4N9W8_!!6000000000760-2-tps-2154-1322.png)

## Personal group settings

These settings affect only your own experience in the group.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **My Nickname in Group** | Set your display name in this group. |
| **Group Alias** | Set your local alias for the group. |
| **Stick to Top** | Keep the group near the top of your conversation list. |
| **Mute Notifications** | Mute notifications for this group. |

## Group Management

Open **Group Management** when you need to manage admins, permissions, joining rules, or group-level features.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** / **Add** | Add group admins. |
| **Mute Settings** | Open group mute settings. |
| **Group permission settings** | Control member permissions for group features. |
| **Group Quick Bar** | Configure the group quick bar. |
| **Join group permission** | Configure how new members can join. |
| **How long can new members view their chat history** | Configure how much previous history new members can view. |
| **Group Calendar** | Open the group calendar setting. |
| **Group can receive mails** | Configure whether the group can receive mail. |
| **Turn on automatic synchronization to Ding Drive** | Synchronize group files to Ding Drive. |
| **Turn off Private Chat** | Disable private chat entry points from the group. |
| **Transfer Group Ownership** | Transfer ownership to another member. |

## Clear messages or quit the group

*   To clear local messages, open Group Settings > **Clear Messages**.
    
*   To leave the group, open Group Settings > **Quit**.
