An enterprise has 100 GB of free DingTalk Drive storage. If the space is used up, the enterprise can expand capacity. If the enterprise does not want to pay for additional capacity, it can also clean up storage regularly to resolve DingTalk Drive capacity issues.

Open DingTalk Drive, click **More** in the lower-left corner, then click **Enterprise storage capacity** to view the enterprise's current used capacity.

Click **View details** to see detailed usage by app.

![截屏2026-06-11 23.54.51.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/NpQlK5jBd0130qDv/img/a2966856-70d2-46f9-adec-8e1e41b07ccc.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_69,w_3456,h_2165/ignore-error,1)

Click **Manage** to open the enterprise admin console and view detailed storage capacity information.
