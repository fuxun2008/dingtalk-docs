title: "How to Batch Download, Move, or Delete DingTalk Drive Files"
description: "Select multiple DingTalk Drive files or folders on desktop to batch download, move, copy, or delete in one go — cutting repetitive work and making file organization and knowledge management faster."
---

DingTalk Drive files and folders support batch operations such as moving, copying, and deleting. This reduces repetitive work and makes file organization and knowledge management faster and more efficient.

## Desktop

Open the DingTalk Drive home page, **Team Files** on the Docs home page, or **My Files (from DingTalk Drive)** under My Docs. Select multiple files or folders, then use the toolbar to batch download, move, delete, and more.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01gj9SRms7WGC713Mj_!!6000000007813-2-tps-3456-2165.png)

To download files from DingTalk Drive, click **Download** in the toolbar and choose a local save path.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01PUXkGIUgL6G713Mj_!!6000000000593-2-tps-3456-2165.png)

To move files in DingTalk Drive, click **Move** in the toolbar. You can move files to different locations such as a knowledge base, knowledge group, My Files, or Team Files. After a file is moved, it will no longer appear in the original location.

If you want to keep the file in the original location, choose **Add to** in the toolbar to create a copy, then save it to a knowledge base, knowledge group, My Files, Team Files, or another location.

Select **Delete** to move files to the recycle bin. You can restore them from the recycle bin or permanently delete them there.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01omigjkdq7OH713Mj_!!6000000004160-2-tps-3456-2165.png)
