---
title: "How to Configure Capacity Management by Role in DingTalk Drive"
description: "Open the Enterprise Admin Console, find DingTalk Drive via Workbench or search, and configure capacity quotas by role so different employee roles each receive the appropriate storage allocation."
---

Open the **Enterprise Admin Console**. In Workbench or through search, find **DingTalk Drive** and open the DingTalk Drive capacity management page.

On the **My Files** page, you can configure whether roles such as enterprise administrators, cloud drive administrators, and specified people are allowed to use **My Files**. You can also set capacity limits for those roles.
