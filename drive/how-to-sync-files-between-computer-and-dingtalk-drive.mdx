# What Is Local Sync?

DingTalk Drive Local Sync keeps your files automatically synced across your computer, phone, and tablet. No more manual uploads or downloads — your files stay up to date on every device in real time. Changes to **My Files** and **Team Files** sync instantly, so you can access and edit your files from anywhere.

*   **Auto-upload to the cloud**: Edits, additions, moves, and deletions made locally are automatically synced to the cloud.
    
*   **Auto-download from the cloud**: Edits, additions, moves, and deletions made in the cloud are automatically synced to your local device.
    
*   **Free up local storage**: Release unused files from local storage to save space, and download them again when needed.
    

# How to Enable Local Sync

### System requirements

*   **DingTalk desktop**: Version 8.2.0 or later (download from [**dingtalk.io**](https://www.dingtalk.io/download/))
    
*   **Windows**: Windows 10 (64-bit), version 1709 or later
    
*   **Mac**: Not yet available. Stay tuned for updates
    

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Local Sync">
    You can enable local sync for root-level folders in **Team Files**, and for **My Files**. Select the folders you want to use locally, and turn on sync.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01xcYn61zUsSK3thBc_!!6000000002864-2-tps-1920-1140.png" />
    </Frame>
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN012C6M7eWV4eG3thBc_!!6000000004995-2-tps-1920-1140.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Synced Folders on Your Computer">
    Once sync is enabled, a **DingDrive** entry appears in File Explorer, where you can find all your synced folders.

    Windows: Look for **DingDrive** in the left pane of File Explorer to access your DingTalk Drive sync folders.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01yHcQmp1FZtB3thBd_!!6000000006070-2-tps-1920-1141.png" />
    </Frame>

    Mac: Not yet available. Stay tuned for updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two-Way Sync Between Local and Cloud">
    *   Files you add, edit, or delete in the local sync folder are automatically synced to DingTalk Drive.
        *   You need the appropriate permissions to sync changes to DingTalk Drive.
    *   Files that anyone adds, edits, or deletes in the cloud folder are automatically synced to your local sync folder.
        *   You need download permission for the corresponding files to sync to your local device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# How to Check Sync Status

*   You can view all sync activity in **DingTalk Drive > Sync Management > Sync Records**.
    

![image.png](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01ZqtmQcF61ZI1DX0m_!!6000000002749-2-tps-600-900.png)

# How to Cancel Sync

*   In **DingTalk Drive > Sync Management > Synced Folders**, find the folder you want to stop syncing, right-click it, and select **Cancel Sync**.
    

![image.png](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01J94RwB4Hl7F3thBd_!!6000000006151-2-tps-1920-1141.png)

# How to Change the Sync Path

*   Go to **DingTalk Drive > Sync Management > Settings** to change the local sync path.
    

![image.png](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN0132lrbbNzzVH3thBc_!!6000000005796-2-tps-1920-1140.png)

# How to Restrict Members from Using Local Sync

*   Enterprise administrators can restrict which members can use local sync.
    
*   In the Enterprise Admin Console, go to **Docs and Storage Management > Permissions and Security > Permission Settings** to configure who can use local sync.
    
*   Members who are restricted will not see the **Enable Local Sync** option in DingTalk Drive.
