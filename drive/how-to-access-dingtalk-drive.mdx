---
title: "How to Access DingTalk Drive"
description: "Two ways to open DingTalk Drive on desktop — click the DingTalk Drive icon in the left-side menu, or use the alternative entry point — so you can quickly reach your files from anywhere in DingTalk."
---

## Method 1

On DingTalk desktop, click the **DingTalk Drive** icon in the left-side menu to access DingTalk Drive.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01J5poEJ3dPyJ713Mj_!!6000000004095-2-tps-3456-2165.png)

## Method 2

On the DingTalk home page, open **Docs** from the left-side menu. In **My Docs** and **Team Files**, you can view team or personal files from DingTalk Drive.

![](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01X9pJ5G81AiG713Mj_!!6000000003368-2-tps-3456-2165.png)
