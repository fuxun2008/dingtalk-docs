## Method 1

On DingTalk desktop, click the **DingTalk Drive** icon in the left-side menu to access DingTalk Drive.

## Method 2

On the DingTalk home page, open **Docs** from the left-side menu. In **My Docs** and **Team Files**, you can view team or personal files from DingTalk Drive.
