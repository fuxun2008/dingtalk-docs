## 1. Role overview

<Note>
In DingTalk OA Approval, non-admin users such as supervisors and specialists who can manage data are called data managers.

A data manager's action permissions: view, export, and delete data within the permission scope.

Data managers can perform data management actions in **OA Approval** > **Data Center** and **OA Approval** > Form > **History**.

**Note**: The management scope and action permissions of a data manager are configured by the approval admin in the form.
</Note>

## 2. Prerequisites

By default, data permissions in an approval app only allow users to "**view**" the approval forms they "**initiated or participated in**". To grant a broader data scope and more action permissions, the admin must **create a permission group** in the form.

Go to Form > **Edit** > **Advanced settings** > **Manage permissions**, click **Add permission group**, and set the **Management scope** and **Action permissions**.

<Frame>

![Add permission group settings](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01YhCgMqug3ZF5bBcc_!!6000000003845-2-tps-2758-1736.png)
</Frame>

**Management scope**

| Management scope | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Owned by me | Forms submitted by the permission group members themselves |
| Entire company | Forms submitted by anyone in the company |
| Same tier | Forms submitted by all users who report to the same supervisor as the permission group members |
| Subordinates | Forms submitted by the permission group members and their subordinates |
| Custom | Forms submitted by the permission group members and specified departments or users |

**Action permissions**

| Action permissions | Description |
| --- | --- |
| View | Users with this permission can view the list and details of approval forms within the management scope in the OA Approval app > **Data management** and Form > **History**. |
| Export | Users with this permission can export the detailed list of approval forms within the management scope in the OA Approval app > **Data management** and Form > **History**. |
| Delete | Users with this permission can delete approval data within the management scope in the OA Approval app > **Data management**, Form > **History**, and on the form itself. |

## 3. Core actions of a data manager

### 1. View data

To view all form data, go to **OA Approval** > **Data management**.

<Frame>

![Data management page in OA Approval](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01GLqR426tvkL5agOy_!!6000000007927-2-tps-2754-1730.png)
</Frame>

To view data for a specific form, go to **OA Approval** > Form > **History**.

<Frame>

![Form history page](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN019bKMnlSTxHJ5bREO_!!6000000002121-2-tps-2760-1736.png)
</Frame>

### 2. Query and export data

You can query data by form name, approval status, initiator, approval number, submission time, and completion time. The maximum time span is six months.

<Frame>

![Data query filters](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01iOjZXeDAGqF5bwRu_!!6000000006970-2-tps-2764-1734.png)
</Frame>

You can export the data to a DingTalk Spreadsheet or download it to your local device.

<Frame>

![Export options for approval data](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01Iki3bxIIdsK5bBca_!!6000000007998-2-tps-2758-1734.png)
</Frame>

If you choose to download the file locally, the spreadsheet is delivered via Work Notifications.

<Frame>

![Spreadsheet delivered via Work Notifications](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01hX7TkrbP34F5bRES_!!6000000004597-2-tps-2760-1740.png)
</Frame>

### 3. Delete data

After filtering or querying, you can select all records to delete, or select specific records and click **Delete selected**.

<Frame>

![Delete selected records](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01bh9tLCmpWuK5bBcY_!!6000000001979-2-tps-2758-1732.png)
</Frame>

Because deletion has a significant impact, you must confirm the action with your own signature.

<Frame>

![Signature confirmation for deletion](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN018FdeDrIRASF5bBcS_!!6000000006035-2-tps-2758-1726.png)
</Frame>

### 4. Download

Click **View** to open the form details, then click **Download** in the upper-right corner to save the current form as a PDF for archiving. You can also print it directly via cloud printing.

<Frame>

![Download form as PDF](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN011OkCmHYm4TK5bREU_!!6000000005174-2-tps-2760-1742.png)
</Frame>

### 5. View activity log

In the activity log, you can view the export, print, and deletion records of **the operator themselves**.

<Frame>

![Activity log records](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01esvE1RqKnTD5bgq6_!!6000000005672-2-tps-2762-1732.png)
</Frame>
