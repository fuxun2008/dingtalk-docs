## 1. Role descriptions

<Note>
In DingTalk OA Approval, operators in the Approval process include approvers, handlers, and CC recipients.

Approver permissions:

Receive Approval forms pending Approval.

Perform actions such as Accept, Decline, transfer, return, sign request, and Comments.

Download Approval form details within their management scope.

Set Approval delegates.

Handler permissions:

Receive Approval forms pending handling.

Perform actions such as Submit, transfer, return, sign request, and Comments.

Download Approval form details within their management scope.

Set Approval delegates.

CC recipient permissions:

Receive CC'd Approval forms.

Perform Comments actions.

Download Approval form details within their management scope.

**Note**: The Approval process is configured by the organization's Approval Admin.
</Note>

*   Handler: Receives pending Approval forms in the Approval Center, Work Notifications, and To-Do.

*   CC recipient: Receives CC'd Approval forms in the Approval Center and Work Notifications.

### 2. Actions

| **Operator** | **Core Action** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| approver | Accept |   The current Step is passed and the process moves on to the next Step.<br />    <br />If it is the last Step, the current Approval is passed and the status changes to Approval Complete.<br />    <br />After you accept, the Approval form moves from Pending to Processed. |
| Decline |   The current Step is not passed and the current Approval is declined.<br />    <br />After you decline, the Approval form moves from Pending to Processed. |
| transfer |   When the approver's position changes but the process has not yet been adjusted, the approver can transfer manually.<br />    <br />After you click transfer, Select a User from the Contacts within the Organization to receive the current Approval Step.<br />    <br />After the transfer, the Approval form moves from Pending to Processed. |
| sign request |   When an approver needs to be added temporarily, the approver can initiate a sign request manually.<br />    <br />After you click sign request, choose pre-sign or post-sign.<br />    <br />Pre-sign: Add the sign request before the current approver; the current Approval form moves from Pending to Processed.<br />        <br />Post-sign: Add the sign request after the current approver; the current Approval form does not move, and after Approval is complete, it flows to the sign-request recipient automatically.<br />        <br /> |
| return |   When a previous approver or the initiator needs to supplement information, the approver can return the form manually.<br />    <br />After you click return, choose the Step to return to.<br />    <br />Once the approver or initiator at the returned Step completes their Approval, the form must be re-approved level by level.<br />    <br />After the return, the Approval form moves from Pending to Processed.<br />    <br /> |
| Comments | Add Comments on the Approval form. Comments are interleaved with the Approval Steps. |
| handler | Submit |   The current Step action is complete and the process moves on to the next Step.<br />    <br />If it is the last Step, the current Approval ends and the status changes to Approval Complete.<br />    <br />After you submit, the Approval form moves from Pending to Processed. |
| Save |   The current Step action is saved temporarily and remains at the current Step.<br />    <br />After you save, the Approval form does not move. |
| CC | Read | Once the CC recipient views the Approval details, the status changes to Read. |

### 3. Setting an Approval delegate

<Note>
While you are on leave, if someone temporarily needs to approve on your behalf, use **Set Approval Delegate**.
</Note>

In the "..." menu at the top right of **OA Approval**, click **Set Delegate**.

<Frame>

![Set Delegate entry in OA Approval](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1X3lE5j84PMMXlJb/img/f89814ec-f567-462b-b665-54f6fc670696.png)
</Frame>

Set the delegate, delegation period, and the Approval forms to delegate. You can set different delegates for different Approval Types.

<Frame>

![Delegate configuration page](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1X3lE5j84PMMXlJb/img/3b4d39c5-8230-4b4d-a555-bf4dcfe93d1d.png)
</Frame>

Once configured, scroll to the bottom and click **Submit**.

<Frame>

![Submit button at the bottom of the delegate page](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1X3lE5j84PMMXlJb/img/544f5810-2785-4d69-8b97-1894daa8deff.png)
</Frame>

After a successful Submit, you are redirected to **Delegate Records**. If your leave plan changes on short notice, click Undo.

<Frame>

![Delegate Records page with Undo option](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/1X3lE5j84PMMXlJb/img/b6ab94ec-2a97-4327-8739-f56744db2108.png)
