## 1. Capability overview

<Note>
Value in one sentence: speak to initiate, or copy text and paste images to fill forms.

Applicable roles: all employees (especially frequent initiators)

Supported devices: mobile | desktop

Core capabilities: voice initiation | text pasting | image recognition → auto-fill forms
</Note>

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| Typing on mobile is slow | Use voice input, copy text from Chats, or take screenshots directly in the chat interface to fill forms in seconds |
| Documents and Spreadsheets have to be manually copied into forms | Copy the text, paste it into the recognition box, and fill the form in seconds |
| Screenshots could only be attached to forms, and content had to be re-entered | Drop in an image for automatic recognition and instant form filling |

<Frame>

![Form AI Capability Configuration page in Admin Console System Management, with global toggles for desktop and mobile AI form filling](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/4EZlweZBkEKezqxA/img/b89b025c-adc5-40cd-9cf3-ab788adbd738.png)

*   When the global toggle is off, you can still turn on AI form filling for a specific form.

*   When the global toggle is on, you can turn off AI form filling for a specific form.

## 4. FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Why do some forms not offer AI form filling?">

Currently, AI form filling does not support certain control groups or forms that contain certain controls. When a form contains any of the following, AI form filling is unavailable:

*   Unsupported control groups: leave request / compensatory leave, business trip, HR contract approval, batch payment, all finance & tax control groups, all legal management control groups, other third-party control groups (engineering, assets, vehicle use, etc.)

*   Unsupported controls: cascading / classification, engineering project, customer, budget request, associated contract

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why do some forms not support text form filling?">

When the form contains a detail control, only image-based form filling is currently supported.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will AI leak my data?">

No. All voice, image, and text is sent to the model only during the call, and nothing is stored or logged afterward — meeting organization data security requirements.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
