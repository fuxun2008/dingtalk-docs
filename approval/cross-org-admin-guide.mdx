## 1. Overview

Cross-organization approval lets you start approval processes with external organizations. It covers a wide range of needs across management, communication, collaboration, and business chains between enterprises and government-related organizations, helping you better connect with suppliers, distributors, platform merchants, and other business partners.

## 2. Use cases

*   Cross-regional collaboration projects

*   Supplier contract management

*   Visitor registration

*   Other applicable scenarios

## 3. Procedure

### 1. Create a scenario

<Steps>
<Step title="Open OA Approval">

As an admin with the required permissions, go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > App Management > OA Approval, and click Open:

<Frame>

![App Management page in the Admin Console, with the Open button next to the OA Approval app](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/dc33141b-8bd8-45dc-be9a-830110cbaf09.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Create a scenario">

Click Cross-Organization Management > Create Scenario, enter the scenario name and description, and click Confirm.

<Note>
Note: The system provides preset profiles and process forms for three scenarios—customer service, visitor, and supplier. After a scenario is created, a Contact profile form is generated automatically.
</Note>

<Frame>

![Create Scenario dialog on the Cross-Organization Management page, where you enter the scenario name and description and confirm](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/df50feb4-e583-4df0-bade-7bda84d9582a.png)
</Frame>

</Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Design the profile form

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter editing mode">

As an admin with the required permissions, go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > App Management > OA Approval, and click Open > Cross-Organization Management > Edit:

<Frame>

![Cross-Organization Management page, with the Edit entry next to the Contact profile](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/0288e3d5-ac3d-4be4-829a-ea4f1efec61c.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Design the form">

<Note>
Notes:

1. The User Authorization control is added to the form by default. This control cannot be deleted and is used to collect DingTalk profile information from users outside the organization.

2. Drag controls from the left panel into the form as needed. For details, see [approval form controls](/approval/admin-controls).
</Note>

<Frame>

![Contact profile form design page, with the User Authorization control added by default and other controls available to drag in from the left](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/e3c104d7-ac84-4486-a4b6-6d52eb74be6d.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Publish">

After editing, click Publish in the upper-right corner.

<Note>
Note: Save the QR Code (valid permanently by default) or copy the link and share it with external users so they can fill out the form.
</Note>

<Frame>

![Contact profile published dialog, where you can save the QR Code or copy the link to share with external users](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/5aa71179-b9df-4648-af82-43d95531cc52.png)
</Frame>

</Step>
</Steps>

### 3. Design the process form

<Steps>
<Step title="Create an Approval form">

As an admin with the required permissions, go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > App Management > OA Approval, and click Open > Cross-Organization Management > Create Approval Form:

<Frame>

![Cross-Organization Management page, with the Create Approval Form entry](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/6595b8c1-9146-4107-be57-dc0a3dd6b686.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Select a process form">

In the Create Approval Form dialog, select Process Form:

<Frame>

![Create Approval Form dialog, where you can choose the process form or data form type](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/b7186b70-3165-4501-a8fe-0f42a1336ee2.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Basic settings">

Enter the form name, description, and other basic information:

<Frame>

![Basic settings page of the process form, where you enter the form name and description](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/2009cceb-d16f-4fc4-a85b-78bea7dfebeb.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Design the form">

Drag controls from the left panel to design the form fields:

<Frame>

![Form design page of the process form, where you drag controls from the left panel and configure the approver field](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/cfb612a1-3fb1-4286-9286-5f8948f25aee.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Design the process">

Configure the approval steps, including the external approver step:

<Frame>

![Process design page of the process form, where you can configure initiator, approver, and External contacts steps](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/47c0fe88-3fc0-4cb1-b654-ae4ccf6534b5.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Premium settings">

Configure Premium options such as automatic deduplication and withdrawal rules as needed:

<Frame>

![Premium settings page of the process form, where you can configure rules such as automatic deduplication and withdrawal](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/0a490f06-477a-49f1-99da-eab87a2db7f5.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Publish">

Once the design is complete, click Publish:

<Frame>

![Process form published dialog, where you can save the QR Code or copy the link](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/e34def02-dc41-4b73-86d9-e3e506d2e0ce.png)
</Frame>

</Step>
</Steps>

### 4. Link form data

#### Mobile

Open DingTalk Mobile > Workbench > OA Approval > More in the lower-right corner > Cross-Organization Approval:

<Frame>

![Path in DingTalk Mobile to access Cross-Organization Approval: Workbench, OA Approval, More, Cross-Organization Approval](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/e8a7bfbb-5d77-44c1-86cd-8059a34ab85a.png)
</Frame>

Tap Profile Form to view all customer information in the scenario. Tap an entry to open Profile Details, where you can edit or share it. Tap ··· to delete or print.

<Note>
Note: To export or bulk delete data, use the desktop client.
</Note>

<Frame>

![Contact profile list and profile details pages on mobile, supporting edit, share, delete, and print](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/aeddd9b4-844a-4d69-b3cc-725c61233a4c.png)
</Frame>

Tap Invite External User to invite external users to fill out the profile via QR Code, DingTalk sharing, or a copied link:

<Frame>

![Invite External User on the Contact profile page on mobile, which generates an invitation QR Code](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/47e15e4d-80cd-4fa9-b953-c94cf242434d.png)
</Frame>

Tap Submit for Approval to share the form directly:

<Frame>

![Submit for Approval on the Cross-Organization Approval page on mobile, where the form can be shared directly with external users](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/480a7411-36b1-41d6-9937-d89a2cd20ebd.png)
</Frame>

Tap a form to submit an approval directly. Tap View Data to see all form data:

<Frame>

![Cross-Organization Approval page on mobile, where tapping a form starts a submission or opens the full data view](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/vBPlN5jVJrjAzOdG/img/8c2e2506-5edb-451c-9e72-98d1d572ba7a.png)
