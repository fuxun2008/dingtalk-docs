## 1. Get to know DingTalk OA Approval

DingTalk OA Approval is an out-of-the-box, one-stop approval platform that solves complex management challenges with simple operations. Through pre-installed templates, it helps organizations quickly build a collaborative platform and digitize attendance, HR, finance, legal, and other business approvals. Combined with DingTalk's native capabilities, it improves efficiency, connects business management, and ultimately brings the entire business online.

## 2. Core roles and corresponding actions

### 1. Core roles

<Card title="Approval Admin">
Has operation Permissions in the Approval Admin Console, and can Create, Edit, and manage Forms they have Permissions for within the Organization.

In the Admin Console, can query, Export, and Delete Form Data they have management Permissions over.

The Admin is typically a User in the Organization responsible for business process management, such as an HR, administrative head, or IT head.
</Card>
<Card title="Approval initiator">
Based on the settings of a specific Form, can initiate applications from entry points such as the Approval app, Organization Workbench, and Group Chat Plugins.

Within the Approval app, can view Data within the Form's scope.

The Submitter is usually every User in the Organization, but based on the actual scenario, only some Users can be selected as Submitters.
</Card>
<Card title="Approval handler">
Based on the handlers set in the process, can handle approvals from entry points such as To-Do, Approval Center, Direct Message, and Work Notifications.

Within the Approval app, can view Data within the set scope.

Handlers are typically department managers in the Organization, or the person in charge of, executing, or collaborating on a specific matter.
</Card>
<Card title="Data manager">
Can perform Data management according to the Permissions set for a specific Form.

Within the Approval app, can query, Export, Delete, and perform other actions on specific Forms.

The data manager is typically the executor of a specific matter within the Organization who, in daily work, needs to run statistics and aggregation on specific approval Data.
</Card>
