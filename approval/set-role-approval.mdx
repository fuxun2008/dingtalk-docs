## 1. Overview

<Note>
Admins can set a role as an approver.

A role represents a professional category within a team, such as HR, Administration, or Finance. Each role can include one or more members.

When a role is set as the approver and a member of that role leaves or changes position, you only need to replace the approver within the role and adjust the management scope. The process continues to run normally.

Role changes take effect immediately on approvals that have not yet reached the current node, as well as on the process settings within approval templates.
</Note>

### 3.2 Configure the role's management scope

<Note>
When a role is set in the approval process and its management scope is configured in Contacts, after the initiator submits the approval, the system **precisely** matches the corresponding approver in the role based on the initiator's department.
</Note>

In the role member list, click **Set Management Scope** to the right of the member, and select the departments covered by that member's management scope in the dialog:

### 3.3 Default role management

Default roles include Person in Charge, Manager, Super Admin, and Sub Admin. You cannot add users to them from the role list.

The Super Admin of the organization must configure this in DingTalk Mobile > Contacts > Organization Management > Person in Charge Settings.

## 4. FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="If role-based approval is set and the role has multiple members, what is the approval order?">
    If a role has multiple members with no management scope configured, and the approval form is set to be approved by that role, the members approve in random order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many roles can be added to a single approval node?">
    The Standard edition supports 1 role per node. The Premium edition supports up to 10 roles per node.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the approval process sync automatically after a role is modified?">
    Yes. Role changes take effect immediately on approvals that have not yet reached the current node, as well as on the process settings within approval templates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a role and the role corresponding to a department field in the form?">
    The difference lies in the **management scope**:

    - **Role**: When the approver is set to a role, after the initiator submits the approval form, the role's **management scope** is determined by the initiator's department.
    - **Role corresponding to a department field in the form**: When a department field is added to the form and the approver is set to the role corresponding to that field, after the initiator submits the approval form, the role's management scope is determined by the department selected in the form.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
