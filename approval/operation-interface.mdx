## 1. Common interface

### 1) Fixed entries

#### 1. Desktop

<Frame>
![Desktop fixed entries](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN015sWOQNUm39I5cC3g_!!6000000001924-2-tps-2766-1734.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
<Step title="Manage approval forms">
Shortcut to the OA Approval Admin Console.

**Permissions**: Only Admins can access the Admin Console.
</Step>

<Step title="Set a delegate">
When you take a short leave, use this entry to set a delegate. Pending approval forms will then be handled by the delegate on your behalf.

**Permission rules**:
- All Users can set a delegate.
- You can only set a delegate for yourself.
- Delegates can be set per form.
</Step>

<Step title="Delegation records">
Keeps delegation records from the past 6 months.

**Visibility**: Visible to all Users.
</Step>

<Step title="Help Center">
Opens the official DingTalk help manual.

**Visibility**: Visible to all Users.
</Step>

<Step title="Release notes">
Opens the latest release notes for Approval.

**Visibility**: Visible to all Users.
</Step>
</Steps>

### 3) Approval Center

| **Entry** | **Description** | **Details** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form categories | Four categories are supported: "Pending", "Processed", "Submitted by me", and "CC'd to me". | "Pending" forms: approval forms where you are the handler at the current Step.<br /><br />"Processed" forms: approval forms where you were the handler at a previous Step.<br /><br />"Submitted by me" forms: forms you submitted as the initiator.<br /><br />"CC'd to me" forms: approval forms where you are a CC recipient. |
| Form Card | Use filters to quickly locate a form Card. | Supports precise filtering by form name, approval status, initiator, Submission time, and completion time.<br /><br />Fuzzy Search is also supported.<br /><br />**Note:** With precise filtering by initiator, if an Employee **left and rejoined the company** and their **original Employee Number was not reused** on rejoining, the system treats them as two different Employees, and filtering by Employee will fail to find their historical approval forms. Use fuzzy Search to retrieve all forms, or reuse the original Employee Number. |
| Form details | View the approval form's basic information, approval details and process, and complete approval actions. | Basic information: includes the approval Number (Copy supported), form name, initiator's Department, and approval form status.<br /><br />Approval details: form Content is visible based on Field permissions.<br /><br />Process: includes the Approval process, approval Steps, and approvers.<br /><br />**Note:** The Approval process cannot be viewed for approval forms with **dynamic flow** or **parallel branches** turned On. |
