## 1. Common problems when using approvals

These "small problems" quietly eat away at your Team's time and patience every day:

### 1. When initiating

*   Filling out Forms is inconvenient during business travel.

*   Information sits in Spreadsheets, and when submitting an Approval, you must paste it into the Form one entry at a time.

*   Some Approval matters are complex, and drafting the explanation requires repeated thought and polishing.

*   Information is entered incorrectly and not checked carefully, forcing a resubmission after the request is sent back.

### 2. When approving

*   In scenarios such as on-site construction inspection Approvals, images and other information in the Form must be checked manually.

*   In scenarios such as financial applications, Forms submitted over a period of time need to be compared to assess whether they are reasonable.

*   In scenarios such as makeup clock-in Approvals, many people Submit Daily, but the risks are low, so they can be quickly approved in bulk.

## 2. What can Approval AI do for you?

Our AI is not a flashy concept — it genuinely helps initiators handle repetitive, tedious, and error-prone tasks with ease. It does not make decisions for approvers, but helps them gather information and offer suggestions, making Approvals safer and more convenient.
