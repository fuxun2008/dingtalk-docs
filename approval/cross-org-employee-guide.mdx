## 1. Feature overview

Cross-organization approval lets you run approval processes with external organizations, addressing enterprise and government-enterprise needs across management, communication, collaboration, and business chains. It helps you connect more effectively with suppliers, distributors, platform merchants, and other business partners. This guide explains **how employees use the cross-organization approval feature**.

## 2. Applicable scenarios

*   Your Organization uses cross-organization approval, and employees need to work with the feature.

*   Other scenarios that require cross-organization approval.

## 3. Operation process

### 1. Mobile

<Steps>
<Step title="Open cross-organization approval">

On mobile DingTalk, go to **Workbench** > **OA Approval** > **More** (bottom right) > **Cross-Organization Approval**:

<Frame>

![Path to open cross-organization approval on mobile DingTalk: Workbench, OA Approval, More, Cross-Organization Approval](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01eLCL59LDzSE2DSoD_!!6000000007248-2-tps-1092-589.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="View customer profile">

Tap **Customer Profile** to view all customers you invited in this scenario (that is, customers for whom you are the follow-up person). Tap a customer to view profile details. To change the follow-up person, use the desktop client:

<Frame>

![Mobile customer profile list and details page, showing customers for whom you are the follow-up person](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01PtkkxFCcSrH1nmg4_!!6000000003521-2-tps-888-640.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Invite external organization users">

Tap **Invite External Organization Users** to invite external users to fill out the profile by sending a QR Code, sharing to DingTalk, or copying the Link:

<Frame>

![Tap Invite External Organization Users on the mobile customer profile page to generate a shareable invitation QR Code](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN014euHt2T1SOC1Blg4_!!6000000004729-2-tps-586-634.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Share the form">

Tap **Initiate Approval** to share the Form directly:

<Frame>

![Tap Initiate Approval on the mobile cross-organization approval page to share the Form directly with external users](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01PDBxp6UnC1K1CGtf_!!6000000001861-2-tps-590-637.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Initiate approval and view data">

Tap a Form to initiate the Approval directly. Tap **View Data** to view every process Form Data record related to you (both submitted and processed by you):

<div style={{maxWidth: '400px', margin: '16px auto'}}>
<Frame>

![Mobile customer ticket View Data page, listing process Form Data records related to you](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01AKbtwMpplhL0cWO5_!!6000000002662-2-tps-306-659.png)
</Frame>
</div>

</Step>

<Step title="View Approval form details">

Tap an Approval form to view details. Tap **···** at the bottom to resubmit or Download the File:

<Frame>

![Mobile Approval form details page, with more actions at the bottom for resubmitting and downloading the File](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01I7EdtggXZnH1rR18_!!6000000000286-2-tps-917-661.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Initiate a new approval">

Tap the **+** Button in the bottom right to initiate an Approval:

<div style={{maxWidth: '400px', margin: '16px auto'}}>
<Frame>

![Tap the plus Button in the bottom right of the mobile View Data page to initiate a new cross-organization approval](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01DxTHf1WcuuJ0ZsTh_!!6000000001331-2-tps-285-612.png)
</Frame>
</div>

</Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Desktop

<Steps>
<Step title="Open cross-organization approval">

On desktop DingTalk, go to **Workbench** > **OA Approval** > **Cross-Organization Approval**:

<Frame>

![Desktop OA Approval cross-organization approval Home, showing profile forms and process forms for each scenario](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01Qc6n7gwI4NB2Fr69_!!6000000003415-2-tps-1111-594.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="View customer profile">

Click the profile Form. You can only view customer profiles in this scenario that you invited by sharing the Form — that is, profiles for which you are the follow-up person:

<Frame>

![Desktop customer profile List page, showing customer profiles for which you are the follow-up person](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01JZnobZ86NLK2Eir6_!!6000000002678-2-tps-1102-590.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>

![The desktop customer profile List supports filtering by Created time and approval status](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01YIuYG4FDovC2ZUdv_!!6000000002140-2-tps-1267-680.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="View profile details">

Click a profile to view its details and change the follow-up person:

<Frame>

![Desktop profile details side panel, for viewing profile Content and changing the follow-up person](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN010mAWPU48K4H2Dy1k_!!6000000006212-2-tps-1096-588.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Invite external organization users">

Click **Invite External Organization Users** in the top right to invite external users to submit profiles by saving the QR Code, sharing to a DingTalk Chat window, or copying the Link:

<Frame>

![Desktop Invite External Organization Users dialog — save the QR Code, Share to DingTalk, or Copy the Link](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN01VgQdACcz8cB2Kdg9_!!6000000007705-2-tps-1149-612.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Initiate or share a process form">

Click **Process Form** to initiate an Approval directly, or Share it with external users to fill out:

<Frame>

![Process Form entry on the desktop cross-organization approval Page](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN012SYdsANpWMH21d9w_!!6000000004102-2-tps-998-534.png)
</Frame>

Fill out the Form directly to initiate the Approval:

<Frame>

![Desktop process Form fill-out Page — fill in the fields and initiate a cross-organization approval directly](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01ItSIEpmOqiE21NYB_!!6000000003530-2-tps-996-535.png)
</Frame>

Or Share it with external users to fill out:

<Frame>

![Desktop Share to External Organization dialog — Share the process Form with external users to fill out](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i2/O1CN01WhcMQbjI4QF1uSHw_!!6000000004906-2-tps-941-507.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="View data">

Click **View Data** to open the Data Page, where you can view every process Form Data record related to you (both submitted and processed by you). Click a record to view its details:

<Frame>

![Desktop View Data Page, listing process Form Data records related to you along with their details](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i3/O1CN01zTgo7EryIXE2XE9m_!!6000000005403-2-tps-1249-669.png)
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Initiate a new approval">

Click **Create** in the top right to initiate an Approval:

<Frame>

![Click Create in the top right of the desktop Data Page to initiate a cross-organization approval](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i1/O1CN013Jt1z0t6GnG2W5uh_!!6000000002790-2-tps-1240-663.png)
</Frame>

</Step>
</Steps>
