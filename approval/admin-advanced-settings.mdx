## 1. Overview

<Note>
Admins can configure advanced settings.

Advanced settings apply to the **current single form**.

Available options include initiator settings, approver settings, data title and summary settings, permission settings, fill-in reminders, and more.
</Note>

## 2. Prerequisites

*   Only Approval Admins can perform process design. You must first become an Approval Admin.

If you are not yet an Approval Admin, open DingTalk OA Approval, click **Settings**, click **Create Approval Form**, click **Apply for Admin permissions**, and then click **Submit**.

## 3. Steps

### 3.1 Open advanced settings

On the **Approval Admin Console** > **Form management** page, click **Edit** in the Actions column of the form.

<Frame>

![Edit action in Form management](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/jP2lRYjZmyDkLO8g/img/0aa4e5e9-e662-4552-866b-60163ba741b8.png)
</Frame>

Click **Advanced settings** to open the main advanced settings page.

<Frame>

![Advanced settings entry](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/YdgOk2b0JJJNXq4B/img/c73049bd-d1e4-4786-84e6-3ae69c77790e.png)

### 3.2 Initiator settings

<Note>
All options in this section apply only to the **initiator and commenter** of the **current form**.
</Note>

<Frame>

![Initiator settings page](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jJXRgRYO01/img/fe5dac6c-4a18-42af-abe5-126ef4f75d84.png)
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Withdrawal settings">

Allow initiators to withdraw approved approval forms.

Default: Not allowed.

Range: Within 1–180 days after approval.

Allow initiators to withdraw approval forms that are in progress.

Default: Allowed.

Range: Not configurable, up to 180 days.

Allow commenters to withdraw comments on a form.

Default: Allowed, not editable.

Range: Within 1–30 minutes after commenting.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Modification settings">

Allow initiators to modify approved approval forms.

Default: Not allowed.

Range: Within 1–180 days after approval, with 1–3 modifications.

When enabled: Initiators can modify approval forms.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Select approvers after submission">

Allow initiators to submit an approval form first and then select approvers.

Default: Allowed.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Send to Chat">

Allow initiators to send approval forms to a Group Chat or Direct Message.

Default: Allowed.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Multi-department options">

Set the default department when an initiator belongs to multiple departments.

Default: Manual selection at submission.

Can be set to use the primary department from the **Smart HR** app.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Submit on behalf of others">

Allow the current process to be initiated by another person on behalf of someone else.

Default: Not allowed.

When enabled: Others can submit on behalf.

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### 3.3 Approver settings

<Note>
All options in this section apply to approvers.
</Note>

<Frame>

![Approver settings page](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/KM7qeobab3aWklpj/img/d266ee56-6213-4dcb-963d-7942bcb75430.png)
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Deduplicate approvers">

When the same approver appears multiple times, deduplicate them.

Default: No deduplication.

Options:

*   When the approver appears multiple times in the process, keep only the first occurrence.

*   Deduplicate automatically only when they appear consecutively.

When the approver and the initiator are the same person, approve automatically.

Default: Off.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Approval comments">

Require approvers to enter approval comments.

Default: Off.

Range: You can set a prompt for entering approval comments.

Approver comments are visible only to the admin and the approver.

Default: Off.

When enabled: Approval comments are hidden from the initiator.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Allow adding approvers">

Control whether approvers can add more approvers during the approval process.

Default: Allowed.

Allow initiators to add approvers.

Default: Not allowed.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Signature">

Control whether approvers must provide a handwritten signature before approving.

Default: Off.

Options:

*   Reuse previous signature: After signing once, the same signature can be reused in later approvals.

*   Sign again each time: A new handwritten signature is required for every approval.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Approval-related message notifications">

When an approval is declined, notify previous approvers who approved.

Default: On.

When an approval is declined, notify other OR approvers at the same node.

Default: On.

When an approval is declined, notify other AND approvers at the same node.

Default: On.

When an approval is accepted, notify other OR approvers at the same node.

Default: On.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Return approval">

Choose whether the approval needs to restart after being returned and resubmitted.

Default: Restart the approval from the beginning.

Paid capability: Skip already-approved nodes and return to the node where the form was returned.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Time-limited approval">

Paid capability.

Default: Off.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Display user contact job title">

Paid feature.

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### 3.5 Manage permissions

<Note>
Permission settings apply only to viewing data within the app. They do not apply to the Admin Console.

Note: If you remove an admin's data viewing and operation permissions here but set them as unable to manage in **Form admins** under Basic settings, the admin can no longer operate data within the app. However, the admin can **still view and delete data in the Admin Console**.
</Note>

**Entry points**

Within the OA Approval app: **Data management**.

<Frame>

![Data management entry in OA Approval](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jJXRgRYO01/img/656a00f9-0241-4d11-9000-14c0aad392e3.png)
</Frame>

Form: **History**.

<Frame>

![History entry on the form](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jJXRgRYO01/img/3084b536-f29b-4cf6-9a1d-2ed3a7b8aec2.png)
</Frame>

**Default permission group: All OA Approval admins**

They can view, export, and delete approval data for the entire company.

<Frame>

![Default permission group settings](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jJXRgRYO01/img/274762ad-1afd-4563-9989-edb1308313a7.png)
</Frame>

**Management scope**

| Scope | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Own | Forms submitted by permission group members themselves. |
| Entire company | Forms submitted by everyone in the company. |
| Same tier | Forms submitted by permission group members and everyone under the same manager. |
| Subordinates | Forms submitted by permission group members and their subordinates. |
| Custom | Forms submitted by permission group members and specified departments or users. |

**Action permissions**

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| View | Organization members with this permission can view the list and details of approval forms within their management scope in the OA Approval app under **Data management** and in the form under **History**. |
| Export | Organization members with this permission can export the details list of approval forms within their management scope in the OA Approval app under **Data management** and in the form under **History**. |
| Delete | Organization members with this permission can delete approval data within their management scope in the OA Approval app under **Data management**, in the form under **History**, and on the form itself. |
