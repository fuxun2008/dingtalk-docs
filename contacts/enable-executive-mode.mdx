### Executive Mode

After Executive Mode is enabled, the executive's mobile number is hidden from employees. Ordinary employees cannot send DING messages or business calls to the executive.

#### Desktop

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Member Management > find the specified employee > Executive Mode.

![](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/Lk3lbmb2xzQRJOm9/img/0fc1ce8d-416e-4c89-a50c-9261250aeb11.png)

Currently, 10 different blocking methods are supported.

The Executive Mode allowlist can be selected by department, role, or person.
