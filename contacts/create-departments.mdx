---
title: "Create Departments"
description: "Add a single department or build a multi-level hierarchy from the Admin Console—rename, reorder, or nest departments to mirror your real-world org chart."
---

Administrators can go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Department Management.

#### Create one department

Click Add Department, enter the department name, and save.
