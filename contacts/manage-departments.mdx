## 1. Feature Overview

As an enterprise grows and its organization structure becomes more complex, efficiently managing departments and related groups becomes important for team collaboration. This guide provides administrators with a structured method for managing departments and department groups.

## 2. Procedure

### View a department ID

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Department Management > select the target department > click Edit Selected Department to view the department ID.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/768a5491-a123-46a0-b901-e0ff16dd9b48.png)

### Set department leader

There is currently no limit on the number of department supervisors.

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Department Management > select the target department > click ... > Set Department leader.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/c66beafb-713e-44f0-89ff-b0b218eeb640.png)

### Set department introduction and phone number

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Department Management > select the target department > Edit Selected Department > set Department Introduction / Phone Number.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/c41db7d2-0d19-488a-8feb-deefaa59be05.png)

### All-member group settings

An all-member group can be created only after a team or enterprise is created. It must be created by a primary administrator or a sub-administrator with Contacts permissions. Ordinary members cannot create an all-member group.

#### Create an all-member group

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Member Management > click ... after the root department > Edit Department Information > Create Group Chat.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/8b9dda4c-dc4c-4008-8268-5de9598a46c0.png)

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/bbe8d61a-5b31-4509-9822-333d35eca984.png)

#### Delete an all-member group

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Member Management > click ... after the root department > Edit Department Information > Delete the group.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/beb9d0a5-4d82-41f2-8e4e-7afacec81c8c.png)

#### Set the group owner

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Member Management > Edit Department > select Group Owner.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/dfbdcf7f-2cdb-406c-b65e-ed5e9557ef3a.png)

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/aeca052f-aa92-42c2-8ee7-b0a973e16358.png)

#### Transfer the group owner on mobile

Open DingTalk on mobile > Messages > enter the all-member group > ... in the upper-right corner > Group Management > scroll to the bottom and select Transfer Group Ownership.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/37b3ae47-220a-4f37-819a-4faac94f5c7d.png)

### Department group settings

#### Create a department group

A department group can be created only after a team or enterprise is created. It must be created by a primary administrator or a sub-administrator with Contacts permissions. Ordinary members cannot create a department group.

##### Desktop

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Department Management > select the target department > Edit Selected Department > Create Group Chat.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/ad055b6b-2f1d-411b-b698-e7bcaba6d0d2.png)

#### Delete a department group

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Department Management > select the target department > Edit Selected Department > Delete This Group.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/83fc1e75-ec24-4b02-97d0-cd15b8e60fac.png)

#### Set the department group owner

Go to [Admin Console](https://oa.dingtalk.io) > Contacts > Department Management > select the target department > Edit Selected Department > select Group Owner.

![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/oJGq75kVjEzxLlAK/img/83fc1e75-ec24-4b02-97d0-cd15b8e60fac.png)
