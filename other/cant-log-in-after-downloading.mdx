If you can't sign in after downloading and installing DingTalk — for example, the app says your account doesn't exist — you may have installed DingDing, the version for mainland China, instead of the international DingTalk app. Follow the steps below to check and fix the issue.

## DingTalk and DingDing are two separate apps

- **DingTalk** is the international version, available at dingtalk.io, for users outside mainland China.
- **DingDing (钉钉)** is the version for users in mainland China.

The two apps have separate account systems. A DingTalk account cannot sign in to DingDing, and a DingDing account cannot sign in to DingTalk. Because the names look similar, searching for "DingTalk" in an app store or on the web can lead you to DingDing instead.

## Check which app is on your phone

- **App name:** the international version appears as **DingTalk**; the mainland China version appears as **钉钉**.
- **Download source:** the official download links for the international version are on <a href="https://www.dingtalk.io/download/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">dingtalk.io/download</a>.

If your app shows DingDing (钉钉), install the international DingTalk app using the official links below, then sign in with your dingtalk.io account.
